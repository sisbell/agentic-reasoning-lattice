# Why I'm importing this doc

ASN-0126 (Substrate Shape Framework) converged at review-109 with four of
its open questions explicitly deferred to "the successor note that layers
operational semantics on this framework": idem semantics at emit (OQ1),
the behavior catalog (OQ2), default predicates (OQ3), and standard
registrations (OQ4). This note is that successor. It also settles OQ7
(sterilization containment) via an operation-surface policy on the
standard retraction registration, and posts explicit open-question fences
on the territory ASN-0126's review trail repeatedly probed out-of-scope
(registry evolution, multi-app registry composition, retraction-attribution
query semantics).

The draft was authored against ASN-0126's final converged text (version
.95+, post review-109 revise): it extends the registry value to the
registration record (shape, idem, behaviors) — the "successor registry"
ASN-0126's OQ1/OQ3 gesture at — commits coverage equality as the idem
criterion of sameness, cites the Nullify_Binary contract / FrontierUnification
/ Corollary RangeSterilization / B3 path transfer by their final names, and
derives DR (DisciplineRestoration): on surface-disciplined substrates the
disciplined-domain wp simplification ASN-0126 abandoned as layer-scoped is
restored as a guarantee of this operation surface.
