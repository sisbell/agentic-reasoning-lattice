# Attribution

**Base:** ASN-0034

The motif's construct — distinct allocation events ⟹ distinct addresses — has its
defining primitives (allocator discipline T10a, partition independence T10, per-stream
forward allocation T9, prefix divergence via T3/T4) already owned by ASN-0034, which
literally states and proves GlobalUniqueness as a theorem. ASN-0036's S4 and ASN-0040's
B8 both explicitly derive their uniqueness claims from ASN-0034's GlobalUniqueness; they
are downstream consumers, not owners of the vocabulary. ASN-0034 is the deepest cited
note whose vocabulary covers the construct's primitives, satisfying the "cited note that
owns the defining vocabulary" rule.
