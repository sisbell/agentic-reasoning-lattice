Defines the carrier set T of tumblers as all nonempty finite sequences of natural numbers, equipped with a length operator and component-projection functions. Establishes three foundational axioms governing T: comprehension (every length-and-component-map pair with length ≥ 1 yields a tumbler in T), nonemptiness (every tumbler has at least one component), and extensionality (length and pointwise component equality uniquely determine a tumbler). Together these axioms guarantee that T is fully populated by exactly the sequences they describe, with no hidden structure beyond length and components.
