# Review of ASN-0128

This note was checked claim-by-claim against the foundation statements (ASN-0043, ASN-0086, ASN-0126). The load-bearing derivations were re-derived rather than taken on faith; the results of those checks are summarized here because a CONVERGED verdict on a note this dense should show its work.

**Checks performed and passed:**

- **RP apparatus.** The projection `ρ` is verified sound: (i) `ρ(Σ_init)` satisfies ASN-0126's C0 (keys preserved, every projected value a bare shape by R-C0); (ii) gate-verdict agreement holds because `Sh-conf` reads only `(F, G)` and the shape component; (iii) the induction is standard. The RP-a/RP-b/RP-c split correctly mirrors ASN-0126's B1–B3 discipline, including the critical separation of per-state transfer from successor-quantified transfer — RangeSterilization and R6a/R6c are routed through RP-b, never RP-a, which is the distinction that would be easy to fumble. RP-c's necessity is correctly diagnosed: had dedup been pushed into the relation, P5 would fail at any state holding an active duplicate; keeping idem at the surface (I1) is what preserves the lift.
- **I0's minimal-elements identity.** Both inclusions check out. (⊆): a ≼-minimal coverage element `t` lies in some denoted `r`'s subtree with `r` itself in the coverage, forcing `t = r` by minimality; minimality among denoted addresses follows since a denoted `r' ≺ t` would sit in the coverage. (⊇): symmetric, using reflexivity of ≼ and absorption. The recovery direction (finite denoted set, every element descends to a minimal one, subtrees absorbed) is sound. The information-loss bound — a hit suppresses exactly non-minimal listings — is therefore exact as stated.
- **I1a.** The induction is correct, including the two subtle cases: a deposit through `Emit_K` is necessarily the miss branch (a hit takes no step), so the deposit's I0-class had no active member at the pre-state; and the K ~ R wrapper case correctly observes that nullification of another class only shrinks it, preserving at-most-one. Immutability (L12 via B2/RP-b) closes the no-class-migration gap.
- **I6's wp.** Necessity and sufficiency both verified per branch. The excluded cases all falsify POST: rejection returns no address (POST exhibits `a★`), and a born-nullified miss leaves `a★`'s only tuple outside `A_K^{Σ'}` with SliceUniqueness blocking any other witness at that address. The disciplined-domain reduction correctly rests on DR's C3-vacuity plus S3's wrapper-only routing for C2.
- **DR's C3-vacuity derivation.** The two-part argument is complete: equality fails because `f = a_emit(Θ, d) ∉ dom(Θ.L)` while the target is resident (both P-tgt branches place it in the link domain no later than the retraction's post-state, monotone thereafter); strict prefix fails because every strict prefix of a chain slot violates L1 or L1b, which the resident target satisfies. The note's choice of the state-local computation over the prospective-step R0a instantiation is the right one, and it honestly records the alternative.
- **DR's wrapper hit branch.** The residence argument (subtree root determined uniquely by mutual ≼; that root was a P-tgt-valid target, resident ever since; the self-emit candidate is fresh — so a hit is admitted by the first disjunct) is airtight, and the re-establishment of nullification, single-tuple scope (via R0a's antichain), and persistence at the unchanged state is correctly done from the pre-existing tuple rather than from a step. The attainability-convention declaration is load-bearing exactly where the note says it is — the P0-failing-call-against-resident-target case would otherwise falsify necessity — and the note catches its own asymmetry with I6 (whose POST falsifies under rejection for free).
- **`retract_stale`'s batch semantics.** The once-at-entry P0 locus is correctly argued (the interleaved-K.σ partial-execution scenario is real, and entry evaluation forecloses it); P0/P-tgt persistence by domain monotonicity holds; the already-retracted case split (same-document hit vs. cross-document or nullified-prior fresh deposit) is correct against I0's from-fill identity and I2. Intra-batch constituents cannot dedup against each other (distinct targets, antichain, distinct subtree coverages), so order-independence of the final nullified set holds though unclaimed.
- **D2/D3 bridge.** The equality of `targets_under` with the coverage-keyed alternative over address-denoting tuples verifies via `addr ∈ coverage(F) ⟺ (∃x ∈ addrs(F) : x ≼ addr)`, and the claim that `targets_of` is not recoverable from `targets_under` is correct (the union forgets source attribution).
- **BH2 termination and verdicts.** The walk bound is sound (extensions are pairwise-distinct members of the finite vertex set; the head needs no vertex membership); self-loop and two-cycle edge cases produce the documented ⊥.
- **Boundary cases.** Multi with `G = ∅` (gate passes, dedup on `coverage(∅) = ∅` — coherent); Unary re-mark dedup; one-element chains; the gate-first observability example (two presented addresses, coverage-equal to a one-address incumbent by absorption, rejected on `|F| = 2`) — all consistent.
- **Self-containment.** All numbered references are to the three foundations. The concrete example section exercises every shape-behavior cell the shipped registrations leave open, including the born-nullified case with its off-discipline reachability correctly flagged.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: The serializing authority behind I4
**Why out of scope**: I4 correctly stipulates that `→_sh` is sequential and that concurrency is resolved by an ordering authority *ahead of* the relation, then derives first-to-commit consequences. A model of that authority — who serializes, what fairness or atomicity it provides, whether two `Emit_K` calls can observe the same pre-state at all — is a genuine layer below/beside this note, not a gap in it. The note draws the boundary cleanly; formalizing the other side is a future ASN.

### Topic 2: Derivation-relative discipline hypotheses
**Why out of scope**: "Surface-emitted K-history" (I1a) and "surface-disciplined substrate" (DR) are properties of how a state was reached, not predicates of the state — strictly, the induction establishes the conclusion for states reached by *some* disciplined derivation. The note operationalizes both clearly (no raw `K.λ_sh` bypass) and flags the quantifier where it bites ("the quantifier is real"). A future note could reify discipline as a checkable state property (e.g., a shape-level characterization of disciplined `L_R` contents, related to the note's own Open question 3), but the present phrasing follows ASN-0126's layer-reachability precedent and creates no soundness gap.

### Topic 3: Predicate composition, registry evolution, and multi-app merge
**Why out of scope**: The note's own deferrals (its What-this-note-doesn't-cover list and Open questions 5–8) are correctly scoped: composition algebra over the shipped predicates, post-`Σ_init` registration paths, and the protocol by which several apps' declarations merge into one registry are each new machinery, not omissions in the operational semantics committed here. C0's key uniqueness states the merge *constraint*; the achieving protocol is successor territory.
