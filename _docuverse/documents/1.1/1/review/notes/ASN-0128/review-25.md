# Review of ASN-0128

This note is in late-cycle condition: the load-bearing derivations hold up under pressure. I checked the I0 endpoint-equality argument (TA-strict puts the lesser endpoint inside one interval and not the other; TA-LC cancels the shared start), I0a's two inclusions, I1a's induction including the K ~ R wrapper instantiation, I6's wp in both directions (and the note itself correctly distinguishes why I6's address-returning POST needs no attainability convention while DR's pure-state postcondition does — that distinction is drawn explicitly and accurately), DR's antichain argument for C3-emptiness (freshness at Θ, R0a at Θ', post-state reachable regardless of C3), the hit-branch residence argument ruling out self-emit hits under SD, BH2's termination bound, BH4's age arithmetic (`f_d^Σ − 1 − j` counts exactly the postdating deposits), and the D3 `targets_under` equality at F-denoting states. All correct. The remaining issues are one mechanism mis-attribution in the example and two duplication accretions of the kind the anti-bloat classifier targets.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: The born-nullified example grounds the exclusion in the covering retraction's *activity*, which the mechanism does not use
**ASN-0128, An abstract registry example, "Born-nullified case (I3)"**: "Suppose a pre-existing active R-tuple has to-endset covering some address `a*`…" and "By ASN-0086's active-subset semantics, `a*` is covered by an active retraction, so the new tuple is not in `A_{fanout}^{Σ'}`".
**Problem**: The operative condition is C3's existential over the **audit slice** `L_R^Σ` — exactly as I3 states it, as ASN-0126's WP states it, and as `nullified` is defined (ASN-0086). The covering R-tuple's own status is immaterial: R6b is explicit that the membership test consults `L_R`, not `A_R`, and that retraction-of-retraction is non-fixpoint — a covering retraction tuple that has itself been nullified still bars the deposit. The note's own text is consistent everywhere else (I2's caveat carries no activity qualifier; DR's sufficiency argument quantifies over `L_R^Θ`). The example is the one place the qualifier appears, twice, and in a section whose purpose is to verify the mechanism against a concrete scenario. An implementer who validates a born-nullified check against this example can implement the test against `A_R^Σ`, pass the example, and diverge from R6b the first time a retractor is itself retracted.
**Required**: Drop both "active" qualifiers (or state explicitly that the covering tuple's status is immaterial, citing R6b), and name C3 over `L_R^{Σ}` as the operative test. The "By ASN-0086's active-subset semantics" citation is right for the fanout side (`A_K = L_K` minus `nullified`) but should not be made to carry the retraction-side hypothesis.

### Issue 2: SD's step classification is stated three times in different words
**ASN-0128, SD / Standard registrations (pre-DR) / DR closing paragraph**: SD defines "*surface-disciplined* iff every `L_R`-growing step along it … is a `Nullify_Binary` invocation"; the paragraph introducing DR adds "— equivalently, by SD's equivalence, a derivation whose every `L_R`-growing step is wrapper-routed (SD, Idem operational semantics)"; DR's closing paragraph opens "The quantifier is real, and the hypothesis it carries is exactly SD's step classification: a derivation with an `L_R`-growing step that is no wrapper invocation — pre-policy history or a raw `K.λ_sh` bypass — is not surface-disciplined, the states from that step onward fall outside DR's scope".
**Problem**: The second occurrence re-glosses SD's definition verbatim — "equivalently, by SD's equivalence" derives nothing SD has not already stated. The third occurrence re-explains the definition a second time before reaching its genuinely new content (the two defeat modes), and "The quantifier is real" is a defensive clause addressed to a skeptical reader, not part of the argument. This is the accretion pattern the classifier names: the same content restated across sections, and prose asserting that a hypothesis matters rather than advancing what it says.
**Required**: State the classification once, at SD. At the DR intro and DR tail, cite SD bare and keep only the new content — the intro keeps "buys back what ASN-0126's gated layer lost, at every state a surface-disciplined derivation reaches (SD)"; the tail keeps the defeat-mode case split (range-G defeats C3; unit-depth ghost-target bypass defeats the hit branch), which is valuable and should survive the trim intact.

### Issue 3: The lens re-gloss duplicates the view definitions stated immediately above it
**ASN-0128, Denotation and views, closing paragraph**: "The lenses answer different questions — what was ever asserted (audit), what stands unretracted (active), what stands unretracted and unfiltered (default) — and the escape hatches differ accordingly…"
**Problem**: The first clause restates, in different words, the three bullet definitions given a few lines earlier (audit = "every K-tuple ever deposited, nullified or not"; active = "deposited and not nullified"; default = "active view after the read-filter rewrite"). Two passages in the same section saying the same thing. The second half of the sentence is real content — the asymmetry of the escape hatches (filtering undone by selecting `active`; nullification undone by nothing, R6c, only resurfaced in the audit view) is a commitment stated nowhere else.
**Required**: Keep the escape-hatch commitment; delete the re-gloss or fold the asymmetry directly into the three bullets.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Rejection-cause observability
The surface collapses every rejection — gate failure, the `K ≁ R` exclusion, invalid `d` at a `d`-reading branch, P-tgt failure at the wrapper — into the single outcome "no step, no address." Whether a caller can distinguish causes, and through what error channel, is unspecified.
**Why out of scope**: The rejection *semantics* are fully pinned (which calls reject, with what state effect); cause reporting is an operational-surface concern for a successor note, not a gap in this one's contracts.

### Topic 2: The serializing authority I4 presupposes
I4 commits concurrent-emit semantics relative to "a serializing authority orders the two calls before either becomes a step," inheriting ASN-0086's sequential interleaved model. Who serializes, under what protocol, and across what node topology is unaddressed.
**Why out of scope**: The four-component state deliberately has no concurrency machinery; I4 correctly reduces the question to call order and answers everything downstream of that reduction. The authority itself is new territory for a concurrency note.
