The remaining findings are one citation-discipline gap and the accretion patterns the anti-bloat classifier asks for.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: `age`'s well-definedness is pattern-matched, not derived
**ASN-0128, BH4 (age-staleness), Provides**: "for an active event tuple at `a = chain_d(j)`, `d = home(a)`: `age(a) → ℕ` — `f_d^Σ − 1 − j`"
**Problem**: The definition assumes every active tuple's address *has* the form `chain_d(j)` for its home and a *unique* index `j`. That is L-ContiguousPrefix (ASN-0086) — a single-state C/M/L result that reaches this note's extended-record reachable states only through ASN-0126's B2 and this note's RP-a — and BH4 cites neither; it cites only HomedChain and FrontierUnification, with no transfer clause. The note's own discipline names the crossing at every comparable use (I2: "FrontierUnification, ASN-0126, at extended-record states by RP-a"; DR names B2/RP-b per step). Uniqueness of `j` — needed for `age` to be a function — is also unstated; it follows from the chain's strict T1-ascent under sibling advance, but nothing says so.
**Required**: One clause before the formula: every `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` lies in its home's contiguous initial segment at a unique chain index (L-ContiguousPrefix via B2 and RP-a; uniqueness by strict ascent of `chain_d`), so `age` is total on active tuples and single-valued.

### Issue 2: The range-valued-endsets scope decision is stated twice
**ASN-0128, AD**: "The narrowing is this surface's, not the substrate's: Gregory's link-creation surface is range-valued end to end — every endpoint a span set whose widths are semantically live — and that generality persists beneath this surface (L4 untouched; raw `K.λ_sh`), deferred rather than denied (What this note doesn't cover)." — and **What this note doesn't cover, Range-valued endsets**: "endsets whose spans carry *extent* … remain expressible at the substrate (L4's generality is untouched), but no predicate this note ships enumerates them. A range-aware predicate layer is deferred."
**Problem**: The same deferral, with the same L4 citation and the same Gregory grounding, in two places, one pointing at the other — the flagged pattern of two paragraphs saying the same thing in different words across sections.
**Required**: State it once. The doesn't-cover bullet is the right slot; AD should keep only what AD needs — the surface encodes finite address sets as unit-depth spans — and drop the narrowing-defense sentence.

### Issue 3: Consolidation scar tissue around SD/DR and section-relating narration in the idem section
**ASN-0128, SD**: "The discipline's wp consequence is stated and proved once, as DR (Standard registrations)." **I6, opening**: "The pieces above — gate-first order, hit, miss, home validation (I1), born-nullified landings (I3), the first-commit reading (I4) — consolidate into one caller-facing contract…" **I6, closing**: "This is I4's closing fact in wp form — on the shipped surface every admitted miss lands active — and the Case-2 counterpart to the Case-1 consolidation DR states for the wrapper." **Exposed signature, heading**: "the surface's one normative statement, once for both idem values."
**Problem**: These clauses narrate document organization rather than advance claims: "stated and proved once" justifies where a proof lives (the residue of consolidating SD into DR); the I6 opening is an inventory of upstream sources; the I6 closing relates lemmas to each other ("counterpart to") instead of asserting anything; the heading clause likewise. Four sites now defer to DR (SD, I4, I6, the example) — the bare citations in I4/I6 and the example are fine and load-bearing; the organizational framing around them is the noise, and it is exactly the accretion the classifier names.
**Required**: Reduce SD to its definition plus a bare "(DR)" citation; delete I6's opening inventory clause (start at "The contract:" or equivalent) and its closing cross-section sentence; trim the heading to "The exposed signature."

### Issue 4: Points stated twice within their own sections
**ASN-0128, BH4 Effect**: "The time reference is *ordinal, not temporal*: the state has exactly four components … none of which holds a clock, and R1 forbids the registry from acquiring one. The implementation evidence is emphatic on the same point…" — and **BH4, closing**: "Calendar staleness — seconds rather than slots — needs a time source the state does not have… a real extension this note declines to make." Also **I0**: "— the same line Gregory's intersection-decided matching and Nelson's query-time link equivalence draw (I1)" against **I1, closing**: "(Nelson's architecture likewise: links are never merged, equivalence among them computed at query time, never stored as identity)."
**Problem**: BH4 makes the no-clock point at its opening and again at its close; the closing paragraph's one novel datum is that apps wanting calendar time carry timestamps as tuple content. I0 forward-invokes the Gregory/Nelson evidence that I1 then states in full — the same authority cited twice for the same conclusion, with I0's instance being a pointer into I1.
**Required**: BH4: keep the Effect statement, cut the closing paragraph to the app-supplied-timestamps clause. I0: drop the forward invocation; the rejection of the finer criterion stands on the no-query-can-select argument alone, and the evidence lands once, at I1.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE
