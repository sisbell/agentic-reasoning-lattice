# Review of ASN-0128

The mathematics held up under checking — I verified I0a's two directions, I1a's induction, DR's antichain derivation, the I6 wp necessity/sufficiency per branch, and the wrapper hit-branch re-establishment, and found no technical hole. What remains is one missing-consequence item (Issue 6) and a cluster of duplication/structure findings this note's anti-bloat classifier exists to catch: the same rationale stated in two or three places, with each site pointing at the others.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: I0a is a named lemma with no block of its own, inside a definition grown into an essay
**ASN-0128, Idem operational semantics, I0**: "The information de-duplication forfeits is thereby bounded, by an identity — **I0a (MinimalElementsIdentity)** — that needs both directions shown: for an address-denoting endset `e`, the ≼-minimal elements of `coverage(e)` are exactly the ≼-minimal elements of `addrs(e)`."
**Problem**: I0 is one unbroken paragraph carrying: the criterion (two sentences), an extended rejected-alternative analysis (denoted-set equality), two authority citations, a closing maxim ("The gate and idem thus measure different things on principle") — and, mid-stream, a named lemma with its full (⊆)/(⊇) proof spliced into running prose by em-dashes. I0a is cited by name from I1 ("which by I0a is the ≼-minimal set of its coverage"); a reader chasing that citation lands in the middle of a paragraph and must reconstruct where the proof starts and ends. The proof itself is correct — both directions check — but the packaging defeats it. Compounding this, the `t` / `t.x` separating pair is narrated in full in I0 and again in I1's hit clause ("a presented G with unit-depth spans at `t` and `t.x` against an active G' with the span at `t` alone, coverage-equal by subtree absorption (I0)") — the same example twice, before its third, legitimate appearance in the registry example.
**Required**: Extract I0a as its own labeled claim block with the two directions as displayed proof steps. Let I0 state the criterion and the one-sentence ground (a tuple asserts its coverage; sameness is sameness of assertion). State the `t`/`t.x` pair once — in I0a or I1, not both — and cite it from the other site.

### Issue 2: The wrapper's both-branches-read-`d_retr` rationale is stated verbatim twice
**ASN-0128, I1 (home validation)**: "(The retraction wrapper, by contrast, checks its P0 on every call, hits included, by the same principle pointing the other way: its from-fill puts `subtree(d_retr)` into the I0 identity itself, so `d_retr` is read on both branches — DR, Standard registrations.)"
**ASN-0128, Standard registrations, DR (Preconditions — uniform)**: "Uniform P0, against I1's branch-local home validation for a general `Emit_K`, is the same validate-where-read principle: the wrapper's from-fill puts `subtree(d_retr)` into its I0 identity, so `d_retr` is read on both branches."
**Problem**: The same sentence, nearly word for word, in two sections, each deferring to the other. This is prior-cycle content surviving in two locations rather than one.
**Required**: Keep the DR statement (the wrapper's contract lives there); delete the I1 parenthetical. I1's contrast needs at most "the wrapper checks P0 uniformly (DR)".

### Issue 3: The RP-c trailing paragraph justifies the design instead of stating the claim, and duplicates I1
**ASN-0128, The registration record, after RP-c**: "RP-c is trivial because this note adds no precondition to `K.λ_sh`. Had de-duplication been built into the relation, P5 would be *false* at any state holding an active I0-duplicate of an idem=⊤ type; instead the relation is ASN-0126's verbatim, and idem lives in the surface operation `Emit_K` (I1)."
**Problem**: This is a counterfactual explaining why the locus decision was made — and the same commitment is stated operationally in I1 ("The locus keeps ASN-0126 intact: a raw `K.λ_sh` step depositing an I0-duplicate remains a step of the relation — the surface merely declines to take it — so P5 (GateRealizability) survives (RP)") and a third time in the commitments bullet ("never a refinement of the relation itself (I1)"). Three statements of one fact; the RP-c paragraph is the one that explains why rather than what. Adjacent to it, R-VAL closes with "Construction builds the registry side alone; the C/M/L side is inherited, never built" — a restatement of the sentence immediately before it ("`Σ_init.C`, `Σ_init.M`, `Σ_init.L` are `Σ_init^{0086}`'s components verbatim").
**Required**: Cut the RP-c paragraph to its first clause (or delete it; I1 carries the load). Drop one of R-VAL's two closing restatements.

### Issue 4: I6 and DR each announce the same Case-1/Case-2 correspondence, deferring to each other; DR narrates the review process
**ASN-0128, DR (closing)**: "…closing the wp Case-1 parallelism ASN-0126's review trail left open. The Case-2 counterpart — the consolidated contract and wp of the general idem=⊤ `Emit_K`, whose inherited analyses the surface likewise invalidates — is I6 (Idem operational semantics); its disciplined-domain reduction rests on the C3 vacuity established here."
**ASN-0128, I6 (closing)**: "This is I4's closing fact in wp form — on the shipped surface every admitted miss lands active — and the Case-2 counterpart to the Case-1 consolidation DR states for the wrapper."
**Problem**: The wrapper-wp ↔ I6-wp correspondence is stated in both sections, each pointing at the other — the mutual-deferral pattern. And "ASN-0126's review trail left open" is process narration: the system specification should not cite the revision history of another document as motivation.
**Required**: State the correspondence once, in either I6 or DR (one sentence; the technical dependency "I6's reduction rests on DR's C3 vacuity" is the part worth keeping). Delete the review-trail clause.

### Issue 5: The reach-withholding rationale appears in full twice; the adjudication-to-the-reader point three times
**ASN-0128, BH2 Effect**: "A general `reach(x, y)` over the denoted digraph would be finite and decidable by the bounds above; withholding it keeps the substrate on the authorities' side of the line — multi-hop traversal and adjudication belong to the reader — with the deferral recorded in What this note doesn't cover."
**ASN-0128, What this note doesn't cover**: "`reach(x, y)` over the denoted digraph would be finite and decidable by the same L-fin bounds, but both authorities place multi-hop traversal outside the system — Gregory's machinery answers every link query in a single pass…"
**Problem**: The full rationale — decidability bound, both authorities, the line being held — is stated in BH2 and restated in the deferral section, with an explicit pointer from one to the other. Separately, the claims-accumulate/adjudication-belongs-to-the-reader point appears in BH2's Effect ("adjudicating among competing claims belongs to readers, not the back end"), in BH2's Adopted-by line ("which claim to honor is the reader's adjudication, not the substrate's"), and again in S2 ("leaving adjudication to the reader").
**Required**: Full reach rationale once — the deferral section is the natural home — with a one-line pointer in BH2. Adjudication point once (S2 or BH2's Effect), cited from the other sites.

### Issue 6: `stale(h)` compares ages denominated in different homes' traffic, and the consequence is unstated
**ASN-0128, BH4**: "`stale(h) → set of event-addrs` — `{a : (a, F, G) ∈ A_K^Σ ∧ age(a) > h}` for an ordinal horizon `h ∈ ℕ`"
**Problem**: BH4 is explicit that age is per home chain ("The chain interleaves every type homed at `d`, so age counts the home's subsequent link traffic, not K-events alone"), but `stale(h)` unions over all active K-tuples regardless of home, applying one horizon to ordinals measured in different units. Two events asserted at the same moment can have arbitrarily different ages; the same `h` expires a lease instantly under a busy home and never under a quiet one, and `retract_stale` inherits the skew wholesale. The Compatibility paragraph's lease reading ("renewal is re-emission, the renewed assertion's age restarts at zero, the unrenewed one ages out") is sound only home-locally. The definitions are precise; the cross-home consequence is the first thing a lease-protocol caller will hit, and the note neither acknowledges it nor restricts the predicate.
**Required**: One committed sentence: either acknowledge that the horizon is home-relative and that callers homing K-events across documents get per-home expiry semantics by design, or parameterize `stale`/`retract_stale` by home.
