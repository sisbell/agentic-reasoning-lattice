The address arithmetic in the worked illustration checks out (the chain `...2.3` → `...2.4 = g` → half-open coverage `[...2.4, ...2.7)` with three sterilized slots and `...2.7` first past the range is all correct), the wp conjunct anatomy is complete (enabledness contributes `(i) ∧ (ii) ∧ C1`; landing contributes `C2 ∧ C3`; arity, L3, `K ∈ T_admissible`, and freshness are correctly accounted as contributing no conjunct), the abutting-spans witness for span-count/coverage divergence is fully derived, RegisteredAdmissible properly closes the gap between the stored representative and the emitted type slot, and the R-Scope port is done at its native transition with the post-state framing argument made explicit rather than smuggled through B2. ## REVISE

### Issue 1: Corollary RangeSterilization applies R6c without the transfer license the note's own framework demands
**ASN-0126, Worked illustration (Born nullified — "The poisoning is a block, not a slot")**: "the retraction tuple `(a_R, [c₁], G_rng)` persists in the audit slice at every later state (L12 across each `→_sh`-step by B2's transition-invariant scope; equivalently R3, projected stepwise), `nullified` consults `L_R`, never `A_R` (R6b) — so even nullifying the retractor `a_R` lifts nothing — and R6c bars every nullified tuple from every future active subset."

**Problem**: R6c's conclusion is quantified over `→*`-successor states (`A Σ' : Σ →* Σ' :: (a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{Σ'}`). The note's own import framework licenses exactly two routes — B2 for predicates over a single `→*`-reachable state, and single-step transition invariants across a genuine `→_sh`-step — and R6c fits neither: it is path-quantified, the very category B2 explicitly excludes ("B2 yields no `→_sh`-successors"). The corollary applies it to `→_sh*`-future states bare, in the same sentence where the adjacent imports *are* licensed (L12 "by B2's transition-invariant scope"; R3 "projected stepwise"). The note polices this distinction scrupulously elsewhere ("This is R-Scope at its native transition, *not* a B2 transfer"), so the bare R6c cite is an unlicensed transfer by the note's own discipline. R6b in the same sentence is also cited without its B2 license, though it at least qualifies (single-state).

**Required**: Either (a) state the path-level license: ProjectionBridge's step-mapping clause gives, by induction on the derivation, `π(Σ) →* π(Σ'')` for every `→_sh*`-successor `Σ''`, so R6c applies along the projected path, and B1's shared L-component carries `(a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{π(Σ'')} = A_K^{Σ''}` back; or (b) drop the R6c citation and argue per-state with licenses already in hand: the retractor persists in `L_R^{Σ''}` (the L12/R3 stepwise transfer already cited), the covered address remains in `A_rel^{Σ''}` and in the fixed `coverage(G_rng)`, so it lies in `nullified(Σ'')` by the definition evaluated at `Σ''` via B1, hence outside `A_K^{Σ''}` by ActiveSubset's definition. Two lines either way.

### Issue 2: Duplicated full statements (anti-bloat)
**ASN-0126, Retraction as an attributed Binary, final sentence / Worked illustration, Corollary RangeSterilization**: "a gate-clearing range to-span that covers *unfilled* slots of a home's link chain sterilizes them permanently — every future emission landing there, of any registered type, is born nullified, beyond restoration (Corollary RangeSterilization, Worked illustration)."

**Problem**: This sentence states Corollary RangeSterilization's entire content — sterilization, permanence, type-blindness, irrestorability — one section ahead of the corollary, which then restates it in near-identical words ("irreversibly sterilizes every unfilled link-chain slot its to-span covers... each later emission homed at `d` that lands in the block, of any registered type, is born nullified"). This is the preview-then-restate pattern the anti-bloat classifier targets: two paragraphs in different sections saying the same thing, joined by a pointer. A second, smaller instance sits in Reachable conformance, which opens "We lift P3's single-step guarantee to an invariant holding at every reachable state" and two sentences later re-says it as "This is the state-level closure of P3's single-step half" — the same sentence twice, and "P3's single-step half" has no referent: P3 is not divided into halves (the halves belong to P6's own step case — newly deposited tuple versus persisting tuple).

**Required**: One authoritative statement per fact. In the Retraction section, compress the closing sentence to a bare cross-reference ("the first gap's cost extends to unfilled chain slots — Corollary RangeSterilization, Worked illustration") and let the corollary carry the content. In Reachable conformance, delete "This is the state-level closure of P3's single-step half" or fold it into the opening sentence with the dangling "half" removed.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Registry provisioning and shape evolution
The framework fixes the registry at `Σ_init` and proves it immutable (P1), but says nothing about who discharges C0 at construction (key-uniqueness is decidable pairwise via CoverageEqualityDecidable, but no obligation-holder is named) or what an app does when a registered class's shape must evolve — a Unary type that later needs targets, say. Re-registration under a fresh coverage class plus app-level migration, versus registry versioning, is a real design fork.
**Why out of scope**: Immutability is this note's deliberate commitment — P1 is the point, not an oversight — and the lifecycle machinery around construction and evolution is successor-note territory adjacent to Open Questions 1 and 4, neither of which covers it. New territory, not an error here.
