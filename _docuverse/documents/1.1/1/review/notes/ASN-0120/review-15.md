# Review of ASN-0120

## REVISE

### Issue 1: The worked example's discoverability check ignores the type endset's residence
**ASN-0120, "A worked example"**: "A type address `θ₁` is held somewhere stable." … "From the home `C`: the link's endsets reference only `A`- and `B`-content, none of it in `ran(Σ'.M(C))` … So `coverage(eᵢ) ∩ ran(Σ'.M(C)) = ∅` for all `i`, and the link is *not* discoverable from its own home."
**Problem**: The conclusion quantifies over all three endsets, but the example never says which document's arrangement holds `θ₁`. "Held somewhere stable" is not a state assertion. If `θ₁ ∈ ran(Σ.M(C))` — nothing stated excludes it — then `coverage(e₃) ∩ ran(Σ'.M(C)) ⊇ {θ₁} ≠ ∅` and the link *is* discoverable from `C`, falsifying the example's punchline. Moreover the sentence "the link's endsets reference only `A`- and `B`-content" is false on the example's own terms: `e₃` references `θ₁`, which is neither `A`- nor `B`-content as far as the example specifies. The example also never exercises endset-symmetry (ML5/ML9's "reachable from the type-regions alike") even though it is the one place a concrete check of the third endset would cost a single line.
**Required**: Name `θ₁`'s source document explicitly (e.g., `θ₁ = D.0.s_C.1` with `θ₁ ∈ ran(Σ.M(D))`, `D ∉ {A, B, C}`, or stipulate `θ₁ ∉ ran(Σ.M(C))`), correct the "only A- and B-content" sentence, and check `discoverable_from(a, D, Σ')` to witness the type-side symmetry ML9 claims.

### Issue 2: The stored link value is underdetermined — "fix the canonical representation" is immediately retracted, and ML2 overstates unobservability
**ASN-0120, "What the endset arguments name…"**: "We fix the *canonical* representation: each I-address `aₖ ∈ ρ(R, Σ)` is recorded by its unit-depth span … (ASN-0058 supplies the block decomposition that may merge adjacent runs into a wider span where the content is contiguous in I-space; the merge is a representation choice and changes nothing below)." And ML2: "the stored span-set's cardinality is a representation matter, with no abstract observable sensitive to it."
**Problem**: These two sentences contradict each other as a postcondition. If the representation is fixed, `Σ'.L(a)` is a determinate value; if merging is permitted, the operation is nondeterministic in its stored value. That matters because the foundation treats link values as first-class compared entities: endsets are sets of spans, link equality is component-wise tuple equality (L6, ASN-0043), and L12/ML7 freeze "the value" forever — `{(a₁, δ(1)), (a₂, δ(1))}` and `{(a₁, δ(2))}` are *distinct* link values with equal coverage. So ML2's claim that *no* abstract observable is sensitive to the choice is false at the model's own value level (value equality is exactly such an observable, and L11b-style claims quantify over it); what is true is the narrower statement that projection (LP21), type matching (L8), and every claim in *this* ASN are coverage-functions. Note also that ML9 Fact (a) is argued from "each `eᵢ` is a union of canonical unit-depth spans" — under the merge allowance that premise is literally false (the spans are width-`n` canonical), and the argument survives only because LP-Fin Corollary covers `δ(n, #s)` generally; the text should not rest the proof on a premise the same section licenses violating.
**Required**: Choose one: (a) commit to the unit-depth canonical form and delete the merge allowance, making `Σ'.L(a)` deterministic; or (b) state explicitly that the postcondition determines each `Σ'.L(a).eᵢ` only up to coverage equivalence, acknowledge that value-level link equality is therefore deliberately not pinned by MAKELINK, and restate ML2 as "no observable *of this model's operations* (projection, type matching) is sensitive to the decomposition" rather than "no abstract observable." In either case rephrase ML9 Fact (a) to invoke canonical spans of arbitrary width.

### Issue 3: J1'★'s vacuity is mis-attributed, and ML10's frame omits E and R
**ASN-0120, "The substrate we build on"**: "The coupling constraints hold *vacuously*: MAKELINK allocates no content (`Σ'.C = Σ.C`, ML10), so there is no fresh content address (J0 vacuous) and no content-subspace range-new I-address (J1★, J1'★ vacuous)."
**Problem**: The justification is correct for J0 and J1★, whose antecedents quantify over fresh/range-new content addresses. But J1'★'s antecedent is `(a, d) ∈ R' \ R` — a *new provenance entry*. Absence of range-new content makes J1'★'s *consequent* unsatisfiable; the constraint then holds only if the antecedent is also false, i.e., only if `R' = R`. That fact is available (K.λ and K.μ⁺_L both carry `R' = R` in their ASN-0047 frames) but is never stated: ML10's frame lists only `Σ'.C`, `Σ'.M(d')` for `d' ≠ d`, and existing `Σ.L` entries, omitting `E' = E ∧ R' = R` even though the ASN works inside ASN-0047's extended state `(C, L, E, M, R)` and invokes ValidComposite★.
**Required**: Add `E' = E ∧ R' = R` to ML10's frame (inherited from the two elementary frames), and discharge J1'★ from `R' = R` rather than from the absence of range-new content addresses.

### Issue 4: The discharge of K.μ⁺_L's `a ∉ ran(M(d))` covers only the link-subspace half of the range
**ASN-0120, "Residence, and its independence…"**: "`a` is link-subspace and fresh (`a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` before `K.λ`), so since the link-subspace range of `M(d)` lies entirely in `dom(Σ.L)` (S3★/CL-OWN) we have `a ∉ ran(M(d))`."
**Problem**: `ran(M(d))` has two parts under S3★: link-subspace images in `dom(Σ.L)` and content-subspace images in `dom(Σ.C)`. The quoted argument excludes `a` from the first part only. The second part needs `a ∉ dom(Σ.C)` — available from K.λ's freshness against `dom(C) ∪ dom(L)` (or from SD at the intermediate state, since `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` there) — but the step is skipped. As written, the conclusion `a ∉ ran(M(d))` does not follow from the stated premises.
**Required**: Add the content-subspace half: `a ∉ dom(Σ.C)` (freshness / SD), hence `a` is in neither image set, hence `a ∉ ran(M(d))` at the intermediate state.

### Issue 5: `wf`'s prose asserts a depth condition the predicate does not contain
**ASN-0120, "What the endset arguments name…"**: "Each `σ_j` thus names an allocated source … lies in the *content subspace* (`subspace(u_j) = s_C`) at the common content V-position depth `m = #u_j ≥ 2` in `d_j` (ASN-0058)."
**Problem**: The formal `wf(R, Σ)` requires only `#u_j ≥ 2`; no conjunct ties `#u_j` to `d_j`'s S8-depth common depth `m_{s_C}(d_j)` (which is moreover undefined when `V_{s_C}(d_j) = ∅`). The gloss therefore claims more than the predicate enforces. The mismatch is not innocuous either way: if depth-match is intended (paralleling ASN-0058's ContentReference condition (iii)), the predicate is missing a conjunct; if it is not intended — and the partial-span generalization suggests it is not, since `ρ` filters to active positions and resolves correctly even with `#u_j ≠ m` — then the prose asserts a false identification.
**Required**: Make prose and predicate agree: either add the depth-match conjunct to `wf` (with the empty-subspace case handled), or rewrite the gloss to claim only `#u_j ≥ 2` and note explicitly that `#u_j` need not equal the arrangement's common depth, with `ρ` still confined and contained by the T5 argument.

### Issue 6: ML1's covering-surplus argument applies `#E` where it is undefined
**ASN-0120, "What the endset arguments name…"**: "A proper descendant of such an address has `#E ≥ 3`, so it is no store address and in particular not in `dom(Σ.C)`." (Repeated in the worked example: "their proper descendants having `#E ≥ 3`.")
**Problem**: `E(·)` is a T4b projection, defined only on T4-valid tumblers. A proper descendant of `aₖ` in the subtree `{t : aₖ ≼ t}` may fail T4 (e.g., a zero extension with `zeros = 4`), and for such `t` the assertion `#E(t) ≥ 3` is not false but *meaningless*. The ASN itself handles the same point correctly later ("We establish this over store membership, not over the value of `subspace_I` at arbitrary covered tumblers (where it need not be defined…)") — ML1's earlier wording should be the same argument: any `t ∈ dom(Σ.C)` is in `F` (LP-Sub) with `#E(t) = 2` exactly, while `aₖ ≺ t` forces, *for `t ∈ F`*, an element field of length ≥ 3 — contradiction; descendants outside `F` are excluded by LP-Sub directly.
**Required**: Restate the surplus argument over store membership (case split: `t ∉ F` excluded by LP-Sub; `t ∈ F` excluded by the prefix-forced `#E ≥ 3` against `F`'s `#E = 2`), matching the careful version already present in ML9 Fact (a).

### Issue 7: Meta-prose accretion (anti-bloat)
**ASN-0120, several sections**:
**Problem**: Instances of the flagged patterns: (a) the closing paragraph "These ten claims are not independent demands … refracted through the permanence of the I-address space" is essay content in a structural slot — and miscounts: ML0–ML10 is eleven claims, not ten; (b) defensive justifications that do not advance the argument: "We diverge from `resolve` in one deliberate respect *and name it as such*", "this generalization is required, not incidental", "the sporgl layout itself is implementation, not an abstract claim"; (c) "The ordinal-displacement conjunct is load-bearing: it forces … and is exactly what lets resolution land in the content store" explains why the conjunct is needed before the derivation that shows it — the derivation two sentences later makes the preview redundant; (d) the Gregory CREATELINK divergence ("Gregory's CREATELINK does *not* enforce this: …") sits inline in normative ML6 prose while every other piece of implementation evidence is segregated into blockquoted implementation notes — placement, not existence, is the issue.
**Required**: Delete or fold (a)–(c) (fixing the count if the sentence survives); move (d) into a blockquoted implementation note alongside the others.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Direct I-address endset arguments (ghost types, foreign endsets)
**Why out of scope**: The ASN correctly restricts MAKELINK-via-V-specs to content-backed endsets and notes that reaching L9/L4 generality requires a different argument shape; specifying that argument shape is a future operation variant, not an error here.

### Topic 2: Empty from/to resolution semantics and link-subspace endset arguments
**Why out of scope**: Both are already posed as Open Questions; the operation as specified is sound without resolving them (L3 constrains only slot 3), and answering them belongs to a successor ASN.

### Topic 3: Link deletion by the owner
**Why out of scope**: ML7 correctly scopes permanence to "no one else's edit can break it"; whether and how an owner withdraws a link is a separate operation (the ASN says so), not a gap in MAKELINK.
