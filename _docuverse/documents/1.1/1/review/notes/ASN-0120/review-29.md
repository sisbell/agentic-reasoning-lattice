## REVISE

### Issue 1: The operation's contract is not consolidated into the claims — the seating effect, the `m = 2` convention, and `enabled` exist only in prose
**ASN-0120, "Residence, and its independence from what the link connects" / Claims Introduced**: "We fix it by convention: `m = 2`, the least depth S8a admits, giving first link V-position `v_a = [s_L, 1]`. Any fixed `m ≥ 2` would serve equally — no claim of this ASN consults the link-subspace depth — but the operation must name one."

**Problem**: Three pieces of normative, operation-level content never reach the Claims Introduced table, which is the ASN's contract surface. (i) The home-seating effect — `Σ'.M(d) = Σ.M(d) ∪ {v_a ↦ a}`, with the two-branch determination of `v_a` (`shift(max(V_{s_L}(d)), 1)` when `V_{s_L}(d) ≠ ∅`; the first-link form otherwise) — appears in the table only negatively, inside ML10 ("a source coinciding with the home gains only the link-subspace seating `v_a ↦ a`"), with `v_a` undefined at the table level. (ii) The `m = 2` first-link depth convention is a specification decision this ASN itself makes — the prose is explicit that the choice falls to MAKELINK, not to K.μ⁺_L, and that "the operation must name one" — yet it is recorded in no claim; an implementer reading only the extracted claims cannot determine the first link's V-position. (iii) ML9's table entry states the wp as `enabled(makelink) ∧ …`, but `enabled(makelink(d, R₁, R₂, R₃)) ≡ d ∈ dom(Σ.M) ∧ (A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ 3 : wf(R_i, Σ)) ∧ ρ(R₃, Σ) ≠ ∅` is defined only mid-derivation in the ML9 prose; the table row is unexpandable on its own, and the operation's full precondition is otherwise scattered (wf in the resolution section, the type conjunct in ML6, `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` implicit in ML0). The body's derivations are sound — this is a contract-surface gap, not a soundness gap, but the claims table is what downstream extraction carries forward, and these three items vanish from it.

**Required**: Add an operation-definition claim (DEF, operation) carrying the signature `makelink(d, R₁, R₂, R₃)`, the consolidated precondition (the `enabled` expansion), and the effect — the link-store entry per ML0/ML1 plus the arrangement seating `v_a ↦ a` with both determination branches and the `m = 2` convention — with a frame pointer to ML10. Equivalently: fold the seating rule and convention into ML0 or ML10, and inline the `enabled` expansion into ML9's table entry.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Endset arguments supplied as I-addresses directly (ghost and foreign endsets)
**Why out of scope**: The ASN explicitly restricts MAKELINK's arguments to content-subspace V-specs, so it cannot exercise L4's full generality or L9's ghost types; reaching addresses outside the content store is, as the ASN says, "a distinct argument shape." That is a future operation variant, not an error here.

### Topic 2: Link-targeting endsets and the assertional content of the empty one-sided endset
**Why out of scope**: Both are the ASN's own Open Questions. The well-formedness conditions for resolving a spec into the link subspace, and what `(∅, e₂, e₃)` asserts about a connection beyond the definedness/legality/inertness already settled, are new territory rather than gaps in the present contract.
