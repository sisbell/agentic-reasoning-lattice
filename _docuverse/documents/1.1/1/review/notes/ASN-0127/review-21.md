# Review of ASN-0127

The mathematics here is largely sound — I verified the derivations of F-IMG-MONO/CONTR/SWING, F-UDIST through F-VDIST, F-CIL and its per-link weakening, F-LAMBDA, the E-lane, D-CWP's biconditional, and every arithmetic step of the worked illustration's six scenarios, and found no broken proof. What remains is one unsupported premise in the worked illustration, one ambiguous quantification domain, and a cluster of accretion patterns the anti-bloat mode exists to catch.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: The worked illustration's `a_θ` incomparability premise is asserted, not discharged
**ASN-0127, Worked illustration, "Coverage of the endset shorthand"**: "`a_θ` is a type address in a distinct subspace (`s_L`), so it diverges from every content address at or before the subspace component and is prefix-incomparable with each — a fortiori `a_θ ∉ {a_1, a_2, a_3}`."
**Problem**: The premise for `a_1, a_2, a_3` is properly discharged (siblings on `A_C(d)` by ChainMembershipForOrigin, incomparable by T10a.2). For `a_θ` the note infers prefix-incomparability from subspace difference alone — but no foundation claim delivers that. T7 and L1d(a) give only *distinctness* (`a ≠ b`) for cross-subspace element-level addresses, which is strictly weaker than `a_θ ⋠ a_i`. The actual argument is multi-step: one must show that `a_θ ≼ a_i` would force all three zeros of `a_i` into the agreement prefix, making `E(a_i)₁` coincide with `E(a_θ)₁` and contradicting `s_C ≠ s_L`. The note neither carries this out nor can cite it (T10a.5 doesn't apply: `a_θ` may be a ghost type, L9, hence on no allocator's chain). Worse, `a_θ`'s home document and length are never pinned, so the claim quantifies over a family of addresses the example never fixes. Every slot-3 computation — Phase 2, the contraction bullet, both swing variants, and the K.λ bullet — consumes `subtree(a_θ) ∩ I = ∅` through this premise.
**Required**: Pin `a_θ` concretely, e.g. `a_θ = [d, 0, s_L, 1]`: each `a_i = [d, 0, s_C, kᵢ]` has the same length `#d + 3` (FirstEmission's `#E = 2` propagated by `inc(·, 0)`, TA5(c)), so incomparability is two cited steps — distinctness by T7, and Prefix's `p ≺ q ⟹ #p < #q` ruling out any prefix relation between distinct equal-length tumblers. Alternatively, stipulate prefix-incomparability explicitly in the setup, or state and prove the general cross-subspace incomparability lemma.

### Issue 2: F-INERT's quantification domain is ambiguous, and one reading falsifies the claim
**ASN-0127, F-INERT**: "For every transition in `V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~} ∖ {K.λ}` and every `I ⊆ T`: `findlinks(I, Σ) = findlinks(I, Σ')`."
**Problem**: `∪` and `∖` carry no agreed precedence. Read as `V_atomic ∪ ({K.μ~} ∖ {K.λ}) = V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~}`, the domain includes K.λ — under which F-INERT is false (F-LAMBDA exists precisely because K.λ moves the result). The intended `(V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~}) ∖ {K.λ}` is recoverable from context, but a formal claim's quantification domain cannot depend on the reader choosing the reading that keeps the claim true.
**Required**: Parenthesize `(V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~}) ∖ {K.λ}`, or reuse F-PRES's explicit enumeration `{K.α, K.δ, K.μ⁺, K.μ⁺_L, K.μ⁻, K.ρ}` plus `K.μ~`.

### Issue 3: Dangling label "(Q3)"
**ASN-0127, D-CWP, boundary case**: "The uniform characterization over arbitrary transitions and regions remains open (Q3)."
**Problem**: The Open questions section is four unnumbered prose paragraphs; the label `Q3` is defined nowhere. The reader must guess that it means the third paragraph.
**Required**: Number the open questions, or replace "(Q3)" with a textual pointer ("see Open questions").

### Issue 4: The two-keystone split is narrated three times, twice with deferrals to the same downstream site
**ASN-0127, "The stability keystone" / "Operational consequences" / "Existence anchoring"**: (a) "This note runs on *two* keystones… It propagates to F-INERT and, through its per-link weakening F-CIL-perlink, to F-LAMBDA, and thence to everything D-NONMONO and D-CWP draw from F-INERT. It does *not* reach the existence-anchoring lane… named as the second keystone in the existence-anchoring section below." (b) "…— for the store-fixed lane. (The existence lane is governed instead by LP13; see below.)" (c) "LP13 is *this* lane's keystone, standing where F-CIL stood for the store-fixed lane… The three claims below all descend from it."
**Problem**: The same architectural fact — F-CIL's hypothesis `Σ.L = Σ'.L` fails on K.λ-admitting paths, so the existence lane rests on LP13 — is stated in three sections, in three registers. Site (a) is mostly a propagation inventory (consumer enumeration) plus a forward deferral; site (b) defers to the same downstream location again; site (c) restates (a). The substantive content is already encoded where it is used: E-INV's derivation derives from LP13 and even explains why LP3★ is insufficient. Additionally the section heading "The stability keystone" (singular) contradicts the paragraph it introduces ("two keystones"). This is the deferral/duplication accretion pattern compounding across cycles.
**Required**: Carry the split once — one sentence at F-CIL scoping its hypothesis, with the LP13 grounding left where E-INV derives it. Delete the propagation inventory and both "see below"-style parentheticals; reconcile the heading.

### Issue 5: The "unrestricted union law" rationale appears three times; F-LAMBDA pre-narrates the discovery section
**ASN-0127, F-UDIST / F-VDIST / F-LAMBDA**: F-UDIST ends "The unrestricted form is what Phase 1 needs: images of two disjoint V-regions need not be disjoint I-sets… (M13/M14, ASN-0058)." F-VDIST's derivation says "The middle step is exactly where F-UDIST must be unrestricted: even when `W₁ ∩ W₂ = ∅`, the two images may overlap… (M13/M14, ASN-0058)…" and then adds "F-VDIST is the Phase-1 payoff F-UDIST exists to enable…". F-LAMBDA closes with "The scope is essential: this is a statement about the fixed-`I` comprehension… For the latter, K.μ⁺, K.μ⁻, and K.μ~ on the query document all move the result… (D-NONMONO)."
**Problem**: The same content-sharing rationale with the same citation sits in two adjacent claims, and F-VDIST restates it a third time in purpose-justification register ("exists to enable") — the definition-slot consumer-enumeration pattern. F-LAMBDA's last two sentences forward-summarize D-PRES and D-NONMONO, which the discovery section then states in full; only its first sentence (K.λ as unique single-step change source for fixed `I`) is a corollary this section owns.
**Required**: Keep the overlap rationale once, at the consuming step (F-VDIST's middle step); cut F-UDIST's "what Phase 1 needs" sentence and F-VDIST's "payoff" sentence (and the table annotation echoing it). In F-LAMBDA, retain the uniqueness corollary and drop the two forward-summary sentences.

### Issue 6: Dead slot-constraint apparatus, and an open question hanging off undefined machinery
**ASN-0127, F-CIL-perlink, second bullet**: "for every slot constraint `(i, J)`, the per-link conjunct `i ≤ |Σ.L(a)| ∧ coverage(Σ.L(a).eᵢ) ∩ J ≠ ∅` evaluates identically at `Σ` and `Σ'`." **And Open questions, second paragraph**: "Under what filter-set constraints over `findlinks` does union-distributivity (F-UDIST) preserve into the filtered form, and where does the per-slot universal vs the per-link existential distinction matter…"
**Problem**: No claim in the note consumes the `(i, J)` bullet — F-LAMBDA, E-INV, D-NONMONO, and D-CWP all use only the `matches` bullet. Correspondingly, the open question refers to "the filtered form" (definite article) and a "per-slot universal" — neither a filtered `findlinks` nor any per-slot-universal query is defined anywhere in the note. Both look like residue of a trimmed filtered-query treatment: unconsumed apparatus in a lemma and a question dangling off machinery that isn't there.
**Required**: Either delete the slot-constraint bullet and recast the open question to say in one sentence what query form it contemplates, or introduce the filtered query the bullet serves. Do one or the other; the current half-presence is the defect.

### Issue 7: D-NONMONO's K.μ~ clause embeds Phase-1 witnesses that belong with F-IMG-SWING
**ASN-0127, D-NONMONO, K.μ~ clause**: the shrink witness ("`Σ.M(d_q) : v₁ ↦ a, v₂ ↦ b, v₃ ↦ b` with `W = {v₁, v₂}` under `π(v₁) = v₃, π(v₂) = v₁, π(v₃) = v₂` shrinks it (`{b} ⊊ {a, b}`)") and the four-position incomparable witness ("`v₁ ↦ a, v₂ ↦ b, v₃ ↦ c, v₄ ↦ a` … `image(W, d_q, Σ') = {a, c}`, `⊆`-incomparable with `{a, b}`").
**Problem**: Both witnesses are pure image-level facts — no link, no discovery set appears in them — substantiating the availability taxonomy of image motion (what injectivity does and doesn't permit), which is F-IMG-SWING's subject. Parked mid-bullet, they bury the clause's actual discovery-level argument (containment ⟹ F-IMONO via F-INERT; incomparable ⟹ read off directly; refutation by the illustration's swing). There is also a statement/witness mismatch this placement creates: F-IMG-SWING's statement asserts the image "may additionally gain **or lose** members," but locally witnesses only the gain — the loss witness lives three sections away. The examples are correct and verified; the placement is the finding.
**Required**: Move the two witnesses (and the containment-vs-incomparable availability taxonomy they support) to F-IMG-SWING, completing its own "gain or lose" claim; have the D-NONMONO clause cite them and keep only the discovery-level inheritance argument.
