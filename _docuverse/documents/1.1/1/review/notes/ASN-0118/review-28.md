# Review of ASN-0118

The core of this ASN is sound. CP0's per-position grounding of `expand` through the run lockstep is correct; the displacing-case decomposition into K.μ⁻ + K.μ⁺ is faithful (and the observation that no K.μ⁺ can vacate a position is exactly right); the three-branch J1★/J1'★/P4★ provenance discharge is careful and correct, including the reachable "range-new yet already recorded" configuration; the tiling argument is done from TS1/TS2/TS3/TS4 rather than waved at; the wp for link discoverability is non-trivial and correctly derived from the range equation; the worked example checks out numerically (I verified the resolution, the shift `[1,2] + 3 = [1,5]`, the origin projections, and the range-new classification via S4). CP3c/CP12/CP8-⊆ close every state component in both directions, so `Σ'` is uniquely determined by the clauses — a real improvement over postcondition-only specifications. The remaining findings are one under-discharged conjunct in the composite exhibition and three instances of prose accretion of the kind the anti-bloat classifier targets.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: K.μ⁺'s image-membership conjunct is asserted "uniformly discharged" but never shown for the displaced trailing bindings
**ASN-0118, "COPY as a valid composite"**: "so ValidComposite clause 1 (each step's elementary precondition at its intermediate state) is discharged uniformly across the K.μ⁻, K.μ⁺, and K.ρ steps alike."
**Problem**: The explicit reasoning in that paragraph covers only K.ρ's precondition (`cᵢ ∈ dom(C)` via CP0(a) plus the content frame, `d ∈ E_doc`). K.μ⁺'s amended precondition additionally requires *every* newly added mapping's image to lie in `dom(C)`. For the `W` placement positions this is covered by the same CP0(a) chain. For the re-added displaced trailing positions — bound to images `Σ.M(d)(min+i)` for `j ≤ i < N` — membership in `dom(C)` at the intermediate state `Σ₁` is never exhibited. It is true (S3★ at `Σ` gives `Σ.M(d)(min+i) ∈ dom(Σ.C)`, and K.μ⁻'s frame gives `Σ₁.C = Σ.C`), but "discharged uniformly … alike" is a proof-by-similarly over a conjunct that was never instantiated for one of the two kinds of added mapping.
**Required**: One sentence in the displacing case discharging K.μ⁺'s image clause for the displaced bindings: S3★ at `Σ` places the trailing images in `dom(Σ.C)`, and K.μ⁻'s content frame carries `dom(Σ₁.C) = dom(Σ.C)` to the state where K.μ⁺ fires.

### Issue 2: the closure-bounding rationale is stated three times in three sections
**ASN-0118, operation section (CP3c, CP6, CP12)**: CP3c's paragraph: "so each text V-position carries exactly one binding and `d`'s per-state invariants — S2 functionality among them — are dischargeable from the postconditions alone, not only through the exhibited composite (CP6's domain-equality conjunct does the same for the non-text subspaces)." CP6's gloss: "the domain-equality conjunct pins `d`'s non-`s_C` domain to exactly its pre-state value — the non-text instance of CP3c's closure principle." CP12's paragraph: "without them a transition could satisfy every other clause while adding entities to `E` or spurious pairs to `Σ.R`. They play, for `E` and `Σ.R`, the closure role CP3c plays for the arrangement domain."
**Problem**: This is why-the-clause-is-needed prose, not content of the clauses — and it is repeated in three places. Each clause (CP3c, CP6's domain-equality conjunct, CP12, CP8-⊆) says what it pins; the justification that pinning prevents under-specified components is one design observation, currently smeared across the operation section. This is the accretion pattern the anti-bloat classifier names: prose around a postcondition explaining why it exists rather than what it says, duplicated across sections.
**Required**: Keep all four clauses as stated. State the closure rationale once — a single sentence where CP3c is introduced suffices — and remove the echoes at CP6 ("the non-text instance of CP3c's closure principle") and CP12 ("without them a transition could…"; "They play … the closure role CP3c plays").

### Issue 3: the redundant-K.ρ/J1'★-indifference argument is duplicated verbatim in the worked example
**ASN-0118, "COPY as a valid composite" and "A worked assembly from two sources"**: composite section: "A redundant K.ρ recording the pair would be a no-op on the relation — the pair already stands in `Σ.R`, so the step contributes nothing to `Σ'.R ∖ Σ.R`, and J1'★, which constrains only pairs genuinely new to `R`, is indifferent to it." Worked example: "a redundant K.ρ would be a no-op on the relation — the pair contributes nothing to `Σ'.R ∖ Σ.R`, so J1'★, which constrains only pairs genuinely new to `R`, is indifferent to it."
**Problem**: The example's varied-spec-set paragraph restates the general argument in near-identical words instead of instantiating it. A worked example earns its place by checking claims against concrete values; re-deriving the general branch logic is two paragraphs saying the same thing in different words.
**Required**: In the example, instantiate and cite: `x₁ ∈ ran(Σ.M(d))|_{s_C}`, so the not-range-new branch applies — `(x₁, d) ∈ Σ.R` by P4★, carried by P2 — and delete the repeated no-op/J1'★ sentence.

### Issue 4: the partial-binding decision is stated twice in adjacent paragraphs
**ASN-0118, "What a spec-set names, and what resolution recovers"**: spec-set paragraph tail: "a precision-of-boundary mechanism, not a demand that every named position be occupied"; then the `act` paragraph: "a V-spec whose span names positions the source does not bind is admitted, and `act` resolves it by restriction to the bound subset … it acts on whatever the boundaries determine."
**Problem**: The first paragraph is about ordering and exclusion (what the "exactly" quote means); its final clause pivots to occupancy and anticipates the next paragraph's thesis. The occupancy decision — partial binding admitted, full-binding well-formedness not required — is then made properly, with its Nelson grounding, in the `act` paragraph. The anticipatory clause is a duplicate, and reads like relocated response-to-a-finding prose rather than argument.
**Required**: Make the occupancy point once, in the `act` paragraph where the semantics is defined; end the spec-set paragraph at the exclusion reading of "exactly."

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Authorization — who may transclude whose content
**Why out of scope**: COPY deliberately carries no permission precondition; CP5's ownership-invariance is the right boundary for this ASN, and whether a destination's owner may transclude a given source's content is ownership/principal territory (ASN-0042 and a future operation-authorization ASN), not an error here.

### Topic 2: The listed open questions (partial-binding width shortfall, cross-depth assembly, post-COPY undiscoverability, the correspondence relation, link-subspace transclusion)
**Why out of scope**: Each is genuinely new territory — width guarantees under partial binding would need new claims about spec-set semantics, and link-subspace transclusion is a different operation; the ASN correctly poses these as open rather than half-claiming them.
