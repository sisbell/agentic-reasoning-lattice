# Review of ASN-0118

The operation's architecture is sound: the resolution/placement decomposition, the K.μ⁻+K.μ⁺+K.ρ composite exhibition, the provenance branch analysis, the wp for link discoverability, and the worked two-source assembly are all genuine and check out arithmetically. Four issues remain — one falsifiable claim, one lemma applied outside its hypotheses, and two smaller precision/bloat items.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: S8a well-formedness of the span start is asserted but no longer derivable
**ASN-0118, "What a spec-set names, and what resolution recovers"**: "Whatever depth `ℓ` has, the span's start `s` is a well-formed V-position (ASN-0036, S8a): `zeros(s) = 0`, `#s ≥ 2`, every component of `s` positive."
**Problem**: This is presented as a fact with a citation, but it is false under the ASN's own preconditions. In ASN-0058's setting the start is S8a-valid because full-binding well-formedness forces `u ∈ dom(M(d_s))` (since `u ∈ ⟦σ⟧` by TA-strict), and S8a then applies. ASN-0118 explicitly drops full binding ("COPY thus does not require ASN-0058's optional full-binding well-formedness condition"), which severs that derivation. Counterexample satisfying every stated V-spec constraint: source `d_s` with `V_1(d_s) = {[1,1], [1,2]}` (depth 2), spec `σ = ([1,0], [0,2])`. Level-uniform (`#s = #ℓ = 2`), T12-valid (`Pos([0,2])`, `actionPoint = 2 ≤ 2`), condition (iii) (`#ℓ = #u = m = 2`), condition (i) (`V_1(d_s) ≠ ∅`). Its denotation `{t : [1,0] ≤ t < [1,2]}` yields `act = {[1,1]}`, so content-residence holds — yet `zeros(s) = 1 ≠ 0`. The sentence is a leftover from the well-formed setting that the partial-binding design no longer supports.
**Required**: Delete the sentence or condition it ("when the start is itself bound, S8a applies"). Nothing downstream consumes it — resolution uses only `act(ρ, Σ)`, whose members get S8a from membership in `dom(Σ.M(d_s))`, and the placement-position observation concerns `p`, not span starts. Alternatively, if the design intends to restrict boundary tumblers to well-formed V-positions, state it as an explicit precondition and acknowledge it narrows Nelson's "designation by boundaries" reading quoted two paragraphs earlier.

### Issue 2: I3-VP / I3-VD invoked outside their hypotheses
**ASN-0118, "COPY as a valid composite," displacing case**: "the displaced trailing positions `{(min+i)+W : j ≤ i < N}` are *shifted* content, so I3-VP (PostInsertionWellFormedness, ASN-0082) and I3-VD (PostInsertionDepthUniformity) apply; the placement positions … are *gap-fill*, not shifted content, so I3-VP/I3-VD do not cover them"
**Problem**: I3-VP and I3-VD are lemmas about ASN-0082's post-insertion arrangement, whose domain closure I3-CS characterises that arrangement as left ∪ shifted *only* — no gap-fill block exists in their setting, and both lemmas' conclusions quantify over the whole `dom(M'(d))` of *that* operation. COPY's displacing-case post-arrangement contains the placement block, so it is not an I3 post-state and the lemmas cannot be instantiated on it — not even "for the shifted subset," since a lemma about one arrangement's full domain cannot be applied to a subset of a different arrangement. The ASN's own exemption of the gap-fill positions concedes the hypotheses fail. The needed facts are one-liners the ASN already deploys for gap-fill: OrdShiftHom(b) (S8a closed under shift) and the shift length identity `#shift(v, W) = #v` (OrdinalShift, ASN-0034).
**Required**: Discharge the shifted positions' S8a and depth obligations directly via OrdShiftHom(b) and the shift length identity at base `v` — the same route the placement-position observation uses at base `p` — and drop the I3-VP/I3-VD citations (or generalize the placement-position observation to any S8a-valid base and apply it to both blocks).

### Issue 3: The exhibited composite's K.ρ inventory is indeterminate
**ASN-0118, "COPY as a valid composite" and Claims table CP8**: "the composite appends one K.ρ provenance step per range-new address" — versus, in the range-new-yet-recorded branch, "*no K.ρ step is required*. A redundant K.ρ recording the pair would be a no-op," and the table's "(re-COPY of deleted content, K.ρ optional)."
**Problem**: The exhibition first commits to one K.ρ per range-new address, then retracts to K.ρ only for range-new addresses not already in `Σ.R`, without saying which step sequence is the exhibited composite. Both variants discharge the couplings, so existence is unharmed, but an exhibition is a specific witness: an implementer reading this cannot tell whether the canonical COPY emits the redundant step.
**Required**: One sentence fixing the canonical inventory — e.g., "the composite includes one K.ρ per range-new address not already in `Σ.R`" — with the redundant variant, if kept, noted as an admissible alternative rather than interleaved with the canonical one.

### Issue 4: Repeated deferral to the composite section, with a near-verbatim duplicated sentence
**ASN-0118, "The substrate we build on" / "The COPY operation" / "COPY as a valid composite"**: three pointers to the same downstream section — "(we exhibit its decomposition below)," "COPY's decomposition into atomic steps is exhibited in the next section," and "That the membership is *produced* rather than merely *required* — and the bound respected — is established in the composite exhibition that follows the frame clauses (the next section)" — with the composite section then reopening: "We must show that CP8's membership is *produced* rather than merely *required*, that its bound is respected…"
**Problem**: This is the flagged accretion pattern: multiple paragraphs deferring to one downstream location, and two adjacent sections stating the same obligation in nearly identical words. The first two pointers carry small load (boundary-precondition justification; `→*` notation discipline); the CP8 pointer carries none — its content is restated in full twenty lines later.
**Required**: State the CP8 obligation once, in the composite section where it is discharged; compress the CP8-paragraph forward pointer to nothing or to a bare "(discharged below)."
