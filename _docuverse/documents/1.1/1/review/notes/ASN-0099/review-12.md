# Review of ASN-0099

## REVISE

### Issue 1: F4 (MatchFormulaUniqueness) witnesses reference unrealizable coverage shapes

**ASN-0099, F4 proof, three explicit witness clauses**:
- "Witness: a slot `i` at link `a` with `coverage(Σ.L(a).eᵢ) = {α, β}` (any two distinct addresses) and `I = {α}`."
- "Witness: a slot `i` at link `a` with `coverage(Σ.L(a).eᵢ) = {α}` (singleton coverage) and `I = {α, γ}` for any `γ ∉ coverage(Σ.L(a).eᵢ)`."
- "Witness: any slot `i` and `I` arranged so that `coverage(Σ.L(a).eᵢ) ∩ I = {α}` (singleton intersection — produced, for instance, by either of the two witness shapes above)."

**Problem**: Endset coverage is defined as a union of T1 half-open intervals `{t ∈ T : s ≤ t < s ⊕ ℓ}` (T12, ASN-0034). By PrefixSpanCoverage (ASN-0043), the canonical span `(α, δ(1, #α))` has coverage `{t : α ≼ t}` — infinite, containing α plus every extension α.0, α.1, α.0.0, … . T is densely populated within prefix subtrees (the immediate T1-successor of any tumbler s is s.0 by T1 case ii). No span construction produces a singleton or two-element coverage; the literal "= {α, β}" and "= {α}" witness shapes are unrealizable.

The justification "Each witness exists in the abstract because F1's coverage space is `𝒫(T)`" is incorrect. L4 (ASN-0043) establishes that endset spans may reference any address in T; it does not extend to "endsets may have any coverage shape in `𝒫(T)`". Coverage space is the subset of `𝒫(T)` generated by unions of T1 half-open intervals — a strict subset of `𝒫(T)`.

The fourth clause ("any other refinement") inherits the defect by appealing to singleton-intersection witnesses constructed by the prior two unrealizable shapes.

**Required**: Replace the literal "= {α, β}" and "= {α}" witnesses with realizable coverage shapes that exhibit the required overlap/non-overlap properties. The proof's logical structure is sound and preserves verbatim; only the witness exhibits need adjustment. Suggested replacements:

- For `coverage(eᵢ) ⊆ I` strengthening: canonical span `(α, δ(1, #α))` with I = {α}. Coverage = `{t : α ≼ t}`, so coverage ∩ I = {α} ≠ ∅ (F1 fires), but coverage ⊄ I because α.0 ∈ coverage \ I (strengthening fails).
- For `I ⊆ coverage(eᵢ)` strengthening: same canonical span with I = {α, γ} where γ is any address not extending α (e.g., a same-length sibling differing only at the last component). F1 fires (∩ = {α}); I ⊄ coverage because γ uncovered.
- For threshold `|coverage(eᵢ) ∩ I| ≥ k` with k > 1: same canonical span with I = {α}. Singleton intersection witnesses F1 but defeats the threshold for every k > 1.

These witnesses are realizable through the actual span machinery and the proof's conclusion stands.
