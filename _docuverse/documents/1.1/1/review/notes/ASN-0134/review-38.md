## REVISE

### Issue 1: Forward-reference inventory prose that catalogs where things are handled instead of advancing a claim
**ASN-0134, §4 (opening) and A5/W4**: "The conflict analysis that follows quantifies over allocation steps; H3 (stated after G1) then re-admits registration to the confluence result, lifting G1 to full executions that contain K.σ — freshness and register-before-allocate doing for registration what the frontier discipline does for allocation — while the contract of §9 rests on per-step atomicity and the per-home facts directly: its safety theorem SAFE cites H1/H2 and the clauses, never full-execution confluence, so it needs no registration clause regardless."
**Problem**: The *content* this paragraph carries that is load-bearing — `K.σ` is scoped out of the conflict analysis; freshness and register-before-allocate are assumed preconditions from an excluded layer — is one or two sentences. The rest is an inventory of *where in the document* each downstream consumer handles registration (H3 here, §9 there, SAFE needs no clause). A reader following the conflict argument must skip it. The same pattern recurs in the twin deferrals to Open Question 5: A5 ("we defer it to Open Question 5") and W4 ("the reader gap it leaves open is Open Question 5") — two paragraphs in different sections pointing at the same downstream slot, exactly the accretion the classifier targets.
**Required**: Reduce the §4 opening to the scoping commitment itself ("`K.σ` is scoped out; freshness and register-before-allocate are assumed preconditions"), and let H3/§9 speak for themselves where they appear. Collapse the two OQ5 deferrals to a single pointer.

### Issue 2: A "synthesis" paragraph that restates per-instance conclusions already established

**Problem**: Each of these three conclusions is already stated where the instance is analyzed (the target-residence-race paragraph states emit-before-retract excludes it; instance (i)+G2 state clause 8 suppresses it; instance (ii) states neither discipline reaches it). The synthesis adds no new claim — it re-says them. The same restatement-for-labeling shows up in W1 ("The contribution here is only the model-intrinsic label; the disjointness is H1's") and W2 ("The serialization-borne label is the contribution; the collision is H2's") — two whole claims whose self-described content is a one-word classification attached to H1/H2.
**Required**: If a roadmap of the three families is wanted, one sentence keyed to the per-instance results suffices; drop the recap of their resolutions. Fold the model-intrinsic / serialization-borne *labels* of W1/W2 into the §5 partition prose rather than standing them up as separate claims that restate H1/H2.
