# Review of ASN-0077

## REVISE

### Issue 1: Worked example re-derives foundation K.μ~ admissibility
**ASN-0077, "A worked example" (Σ₁ → Σ₁' alternative path)**: the K.μ~ witness discharges the precondition plus all five admissibility clauses *and* six invariant sub-checks (S8a, S8-depth, D-CTG★, D-MIN★, S3★, S3★-aux) for a two-position swap, then is abandoned ("Returning to the main chronology…").
**Problem**: O14 needs only *a* valid K.μ~ transition in which origins shift to an incomparable set. That a valid K.μ~ preserves S8a/S8-depth/D-CTG★/D-MIN★/S3★/S3★-aux is guaranteed by the operation's own definition in foundation ASN-0047 — re-verifying each clause inline re-derives foundation invariant-preservation and does not advance the O14 witness. The reader must wade through a full admissibility audit to reach the one load-bearing fact (`{d₁}` vs `{d₃}`).
**Required**: Reduce the K.μ~ witness to what O14 requires — a valid swap and the resulting incomparable origin sets — citing K.μ~'s ASN-0047 admissibility rather than re-discharging its invariant package.

### Issue 2: Worked example over-verifies near-trivial claims
**ASN-0077, "A worked example"**: the example verifies essentially every introduced claim (O4, O5★, O6★, O7, O8, O9, O10, O11, O11', O11★★, O13, O14, both wp's, F1≡F2≡F3), several as standalone paragraphs for near-trivial corollaries — e.g. O10 idempotence ("invoked twice in succession both yield {d₁}"), O8/O9 each restated against the scenario.
**Problem**: The review standard asks for one concrete scenario checking *key* postconditions. Exhaustively replaying ~16 claims, including trivial ones, is over-supply the precise reader must skip past. This is the patch-over-supply pattern: load-bearing demonstrations buried among speculative auxiliaries.
**Required**: Keep the load-bearing demonstrations (the multi-origin result; the O13 admissibility-loss and O14 non-preservation negative witnesses; one wp evaluation) and drop the paragraph-per-claim recap of trivial corollaries.
