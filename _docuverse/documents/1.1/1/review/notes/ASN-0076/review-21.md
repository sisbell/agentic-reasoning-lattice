**Why out of scope**: The ASN explicitly defers the τ_sup designation convention to a future ASN on type-endset conventions, properly cited in Open Questions. E4 stands as a structural witness without requiring this convention.

**Why out of scope**: E6's discussion of who may invoke K.λ on which document references an authorization layer the ASN explicitly defers. The Alice/Bob/Carol naming is acknowledged as informal application-layer terminology.

### Topic 3: Cycle invariants on supersession structure
**Why out of scope**: Whether supersession chains may contain cycles is a separate invariant question deferred to Open Questions; the link model formally permits cycles.

### Topic 4: Retraction / counter-claim semantics
**Why out of scope**: The "Counter-claim" discussion in E9's commentary is properly framed as motivation; formal retraction semantics belong to a future ASN.

### Topic 5: Multi-link supersessions
**Why out of scope**: Whether one supersession may relate more than two links is properly listed in Open Questions.
