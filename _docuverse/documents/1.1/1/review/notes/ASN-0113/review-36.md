# Review of ASN-0113

## REVISE

### Issue 1: W4's exactness is proved once, then re-derived a second way
**ASN-0113, "The extent of a single subspace" (W4 derivation)**: After the direct derivation closes — "These are exactly the elements `[S,1,…,1,k]` ... — which is `V_S(d)` by D-SEQ★. So the span omits no active position (completeness) and includes no inactive V-slice tumbler (exclusivity)." — three further sentences re-establish the same result: "The relation the member span bears ... is therefore definitional, not approximate ...", "The load-bearing invariant here is contiguity ...", and "A span's denotation is order-convex (T12; S0 of ASN-0053), so any single span covering both extremes ... is exact precisely because no such between-tumbler is ever inactive."

**Problem**: Exactness is fully discharged by the D-SEQ★/T5 derivation. The "definitional, not approximate" sentence merely restates the conclusion, and the order-convexity sentence is a parallel re-derivation of the same fact in different words — the anti-bloat "two paragraphs say the same thing in different words" pattern. Only the middle sentence (the D-CTG★ dependency, which grounds the first open question) advances anything.

**Required**: Keep the one-sentence contiguity-dependency note; delete the "definitional, not approximate" restatement and the order-convexity re-derivation.

### Issue 2: Open Questions 2 and 6 overlap on the same vintage/comparability concern
**ASN-0113, "Open Questions"**: Q2 asks "under what conditions is 'subspace absent' safely read as 'extent zero' rather than 'subspace not yet supported'?"; Q6 asks, on extending the subspace convention, "what must the operation guarantee so that the kind-list remains fixed and the report stays comparable across documents of different vintages?"

**Problem**: Q2's "subspace not yet supported" case is precisely the convention-extension scenario Q6 governs. Both pivot on "comparable across documents of different vintages" when the kind-list changes — two questions circling one future concern (convention extension breaking absent-member interpretation).

**Required**: Merge into a single open question, or scope Q2 to consumer-side interpretation and Q6 to the operation-side guarantee with an explicit pointer, so the shared concern is stated once.
