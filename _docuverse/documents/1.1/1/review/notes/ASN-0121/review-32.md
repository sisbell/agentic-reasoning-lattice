# Review of ASN-0121

## REVISE

### Issue 1: FL-SND is weaker than the soundness the forcing argument demands, and the surrounding claims contradict each other
**ASN-0121, "The answer is forced" / FL-SND / FL-CUR / Claims table**: The forcing argument demands "*Soundness*: `(A a : a ∈ R : a ∈ addressable(Σ) ∧ sat(a, q, Σ))`", but the recorded claim is "**FL-SND (soundness).** `(A a : a ∈ findlinks(q, Σ) : sat(a, q, Σ))` … There are no false positives," followed by "they are the load-bearing guarantees an alternative implementation must independently demonstrate."
**Problem**: FL-SND drops the addressability conjunct. The ASN's own forcing paragraph constructs `R_max = { a ∈ dom(Σ.L) : sat(a, q, Σ) }` to show that without that conjunct the answer is not forced — and `R_max` satisfies both FL-SND and FL-CMP as recorded. So the pair recorded as "the load-bearing guarantees an alternative implementation must independently demonstrate" does not pin the operation down: an implementation demonstrating exactly FL-SND + FL-CMP may legally return retracted links. "There are no false positives" overstates FL-SND for the same reason. The document then contradicts itself: FL-CUR's body correctly notes "FL-SND alone delivers only the `sat` conjunct, so the addressability half rests on FL-DEF, not FL-SND," while FL-CUR's claims-table row calls it "the conjunction of FL-SND and FL-CMP against `addressable(Σ)`." FL-CMP's gloss "The result is *exactly* the satisfying subset" likewise overstates its one-direction formal statement.
**Required**: Strengthen FL-SND to the demanded form — `a ∈ findlinks(q, Σ) ⟹ a ∈ addressable(Σ) ∧ sat(a, q, Σ)` — which makes the "load-bearing guarantees" sentence and the FL-CUR table row true and removes FL-CUR's corrective aside; or, alternatively, repoint the implementation-obligation language at FL-CUR and trim the FL-SND/FL-CMP glosses to their formal content. Either way, the four statements must agree on whether soundness includes addressability.

### Issue 2: Foundation citations mis-stated in the FL-WP scope paragraph and the transition-vocabulary paragraph
**ASN-0121, FL-WP "Scope of the wp" / "The answer is forced" (vocabulary paragraph)**: "matching ASN-0086's wp Case 2 (EmitKWeakestPrecondition), which likewise carries the operation's applicability predicate (`enabled(K.μ⁻[d, R])`) as a wp conjunct"; "the step's own applicability predicate `enabled(K.λ)` — K.λ's freshness `ℓ ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, its L3 well-formedness …, and `home(ℓ) ∈ dom(Σ.M)` (ASN-0093 K.λ)"; "We take `→` to be the full atomic transition vocabulary of ASN-0047: … and the named composite K.μ~."
**Problem**: Three foundation-fidelity slips. (i) `enabled(K.μ⁻[d, R])` is ASN-0098's LP12a (ContractionDiscoverabilityWP) predicate for a different operation; ASN-0086's wp Case 2 carries Emit_K's own applicability conjunct `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`. The cited precedent and the quoted predicate do not match. (ii) ASN-0093's K.λ binding precondition pins `ℓ` to the determinate sub-allocator frontier (`[d.0.s_L.1]` or `inc(ℓ_prev, 0)`); freshness `ℓ ∉ dom(Σ.L)` is a derived lemma (FirstEmissionFreshness/SubsequentEmissionFreshness), not a precondition conjunct, and the frontier-position constraint — the actual content of `enabled(K.λ)` — is omitted. Since FL-WP declares "the full weakest precondition is `enabled(K.λ) ∧ ⟨displayed conjunction⟩`," mis-defining `enabled` makes the full-wp claim wrong relative to the foundation. (iii) ASN-0047's ValidComposite states "The named composite K.μ~ is not atomic"; listing K.μ~ inside "the full atomic transition vocabulary" is internally contradictory (harmless mathematically, since F-PRES covers it, but a misquote of the foundation).
**Required**: Cite LP12a (or quote wp Case 2's actual `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` conjunct) as the precedent; gloss `enabled(K.λ)` as ASN-0093 states it — home allocated, frontier-position binding, L3 value shape — noting freshness as its consequence; phrase `→` as the atomic vocabulary plus the named composite, not as all-atomic.

### Issue 3: The "three ways" enumeration's completeness is asserted, not shown
**ASN-0121, "The only result-changing transition"**: "A single K.λ step can change the answer in three ways, and we compute the weakest precondition of each."
**Problem**: The enumeration is implicitly a completeness claim, and neither half of its complement is discharged: that no *existing* link can *enter* the answer across a K.λ step, and that no existing match can *leave* across an *ordinary* K.λ. Both follow in one line from facts the ASN already holds — an existing non-member has either `¬sat` (constant by L12 and address-fixity of `home`) or `a ∈ nullified(Σ)` (monotone by R6a), so it stays out; and case (a)'s `L_R^{Σ'} = L_R^Σ` leaves `nullified` fixed on `dom(Σ.L)`, so ordinary steps cause no exits — but neither sentence appears. Per the standards, an enumeration claimed exhaustive must show its exhaustiveness.
**Required**: Add the two-line completeness argument immediately after the enumeration, so "three ways" is a theorem rather than a framing.

### Issue 4: Duplicated derivation prose inside FL-WP
**ASN-0121, FL-WP cases (a), (b), (c)**: Case (a) establishes `L_R^{Σ'} = L_R^Σ` twice, near-verbatim — once in the case statement ("membership of an *existing* address in `L_R` reads its stored value …, so with `ℓ` the only address `Σ.L` gains, no tuple enters or leaves `L_R`") and again in the derivation ("The ordinariness condition above — `ℓ ∉ L_R^{Σ'}` — is exactly what leaves the retraction relation fixed: … so `L_R^{Σ'} = L_R^Σ`"). Case (c) makes the R6b-direction point twice ("R6b supplies only ⊇" and later "R6b discharges only the ⟸ (⊇) half — … while the displayed split supplies the ⟹ (⊆) half"). Cases (b) and (c) carry mutual scope-deferrals about the same self-retraction term ("the term case (c) below sets aside by scope" / "excluded by scope … the subject of case (b) above").
**Problem**: The same argument stated twice in the same case, and two paragraphs deferring to each other to make one scoping point — the duplication patterns the anti-bloat mode flags. The reader must verify each restatement says nothing new.
**Required**: State the `L_R`-fixity argument once in case (a), the R6b one-direction point once in case (c), and keep a single one-directional cross-reference for the self-retraction term's home.

### Issue 5: Meta-prose accretion in the request-grammar and addressability passages
**ASN-0121, "What is being matched" / "The answer is forced"**: "This is the full well-formedness condition on a request: there is no further typing constraint on any component"; "There is exactly one kind of request in this grammar; the arrangement-mediated *V-spec* phrasing discussed later (under editing stability) is a separable front-end convenience … not a second kind of request. So the qualifier 'I-address request,' wherever it appears below, is simply every `q` the grammar admits"; "The foundation already supplies exactly this notion, so we use it rather than reinventing it"; "We do not posit a fresh retraction set nor assume its monotonicity; we inherit `nullified` and its R6a guarantee. (The *mechanism* of retraction is ASN-0086's concern; here we need only its effect on addressability.)"
**Problem**: Exhaustiveness claims, terminology defense, and sourcing apologia rather than definition content. The V-spec-is-a-front-end point is made here with a forward deferral and then made again, in full, in the FL-STB section — two paragraphs deferring to the same downstream location. The two sentences around `nullified` explain why the definition is inherited rather than what it says. These are the accretion patterns this note's anti-bloat classifier targets.
**Required**: Define the grammar once (each component an endset or `∗`; convention on rooting noted in one sentence); keep the V-spec contrast solely in the editing-stability section; cite ASN-0086's `nullified`/R6a without the defensive framing.

### Issue 6: Claims-table rows carry derivation essays and off-topic content
**ASN-0121, Claims Introduced, FL-WP and FL-WILD rows**: The FL-WP row reproduces the body's sub-case taxonomy, the discipline commentary ("carried explicitly absent a retraction discipline; cf. ASN-0086 wp Case 2"), the exhaustiveness note, and parenthetical dependence remarks ("which depends only on slot 3 and to-coverage, hence is unaffected by `F_b`"). The FL-WILD row carries "under a *constrained* request a link is matched on its first three endsets `e₁, e₂, e₃` alone (slots `e₄ … eₙ` never enter `sat`)" — higher-arity matching under constrained requests, which is not wildcard semantics and duplicates the "What is being matched" paragraph — plus a remark about how `nullified` is computed.
**Problem**: Essay content in structural slots. The table should state each claim; the body holds the derivations. Duplicating body prose into rows guarantees drift between the two.
**Required**: Reduce FL-WP's row to the claim ("per-case wp's for the three result-changing K.λ cases, partitioned by retraction-relation membership; displayed in cases (a)–(c)") and FL-WILD's row to wildcard semantics (unit of the conjunction; all-wildcard returns `addressable(Σ)`), relocating or deleting the rest.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Result ordering and pagination
Nelson's source quote says the operation "returns a list," while the ASN specifies a set. Enumeration order, cursors, and counts are FINDNEXTNLINKSFROMTOTHREE / FINDNUMOFLINKSFROMTOTHREE territory, explicitly excluded by the scope list.
**Why out of scope**: The set-vs-list delta is a property of the paginated variants, not an error in the membership specification.

### Topic 2: Disciplined-domain simplification of FL-WP
Under an ASN-0086-style layer commitment (unit-depth retraction discipline), the ghost-pre-coverage conjunct in FL-WP cases (a)/(b) would discharge vacuously, paralleling ASN-0086's disciplined-domain simplification.
**Why out of scope**: The ASN deliberately works over the full undisciplined ASN-0047 vocabulary and explicitly declines the discharge; specifying a discipline and re-deriving the simplified wp is a future layer ASN.

### Topic 3: Path-level conservation law
A two-sided characterisation of `findlinks(q, Σ') ∖ findlinks(q, Σ)` and the reverse difference over arbitrary `Σ →* Σ'` — exactly the created-and-matching links minus the newly nullified — would be the analog of ASN-0127's E-CONS for the filtered query.
**Why out of scope**: The ASN supplies single-step wp's and one-sided results (FL-MON, FL-RET); the path-level ledger is new claim territory, not a gap in those.
