# Review of ASN-0129

## REVISE

### Issue 1: Default-view rewrite scope sweeps V-TUP projections in, contradicting V-TUP, PD0, and PC3
**ASN-0129, FP (ReadFootprints) / UV / V-TUP**: FP: "*the default-view increment, codomain-uniform*: under `v = default`, every *collection-valued* atom — `℘_fin(T)`- or `Seq_fin(T)`-valued, whatever its family: `members`, `targets_of`, `succs`, `sources_to`, `chain`, `stale` — has as its footprint its active-view footprint plus, for each BH1 type J, the footprint of `is_filtered_J`". UV's Collections clause is likewise headed by codomain: "*Collections* (`℘_fin(T)`, `Seq_fin(T)`): the result drops exactly the elements `x` with `filtered(x)`".
**Problem**: `addrs_F(x)` and `addrs_G(x)` are `℘_fin(T)`-valued atoms — `V_atom` explicitly includes V-TUP — so the codomain-driven reading of UV/FP ("every collection-valued atom, whatever its family") rewrites them at `v = default`. That contradicts three other claims: (i) V-TUP's "For a fixed binding of `x`, every V-TUP read is constant across states, the stored value being immutable (L12)" — `is_filtered` is state-dependent, so a rewritten `addrs_F` is not constant; (ii) PD0's classification of "V-TUP reads of a bound tuple" as step-constants in `ST ∩ SF` — a bare `retired` deposit would flip a rewritten read; (iii) PC3's "Cross-view readings are derivable now, **inside a term of any view**... `⋃(L_K, addrs_F)` rebuilds `members(K, audit)`" — false in a default-view term if `addrs_F` is rewritten. Note also that UV's rewrite predicate `filtered(x)` is keyed to `K_queried` (`J ≠ K_queried`), and a V-TUP projection of a bound tuple has no queried K — the rewrite is not even well-defined on these atoms, which confirms the intended scope excludes them but is nowhere stated.
**Required**: One explicit sentence in UV: V-TUP projections are raw stored-value reads at every view, never rewritten (they are value reads of an in-hand tuple, not result surfaces). Scope FP's increment clause to the named template/behavior collection atoms instead of "whatever its family".

### Issue 2: UV's rewrite of `chain`'s returned sequence contradicts the foundation's "BH1 filtering does not rewrite the walk"
**ASN-0129, UV**: "*Collections* ...: the result drops exactly the elements `x` with `filtered(x)` — ..., the sequence `chain` returns, and BH4's `stale` alike."
**Problem**: ASN-0128's BH2 defines the returned list *as* the walk — "`chain(addr) → ordered list of addrs` — the maximal *determinate* walk from `addr`: the longest sequence..." — and then commits: "BH1 filtering does not rewrite the walk." ASN-0128's BH1 further commits "Nothing else is rewritten". Under the foundation's own identification of walk = returned sequence, UV's default-view deletion from `chain`'s result directly contradicts the BH2 sentence. The note's only cover is an appeal to ASN-0128's Open Question 1 ("left the behavior surfaces open"), never quoting or scoping the two foundation sentences it overrides. UV does contain the materials for a reconciliation (its traversal clause — "a retired mid-chain element is traversed but not shown in the returned sequence" — preserves the walk-as-traversal reading; BH1's "nothing else" is explicated by its colon list — active equations, membership predicates, raw `Observe_K` — all of which UV preserves), but it never performs it. V-PRIM's `elems` parenthetical ("at `default` the rewritten sequence's length, strictly shorter whenever a traversed element is filtered") depends on which way this resolves.
**Required**: Explicit reconciliation in UV: quote BH2's sentence and state the reading adopted (the walk = traversal and verdicts, preserved unrewritten; the returned sequence's default-view presentation is what OQ1 left open), and dispose of BH1's "Nothing else is rewritten" by its colon-explicated scope — or else exempt `chain`'s sequence from the collection rewrite (treating the walk like a verdict, consistent with `is_in_chain` reading the unrewritten walk) and adjust the `elems` parenthetical. Silently overriding the natural reading of a foundation claim is not acceptable.

### Issue 3: `Map_fin`'s carrier is never defined
**ASN-0129, COD (Codomains)**: "`Codom = {Bool, T, ℘_fin(T), T ∪ {⊥}, Seq_fin(T), Map_fin, ℕ, ℕ ∪ {⊥}}` — Boolean, address, finite address set, optional address, finite address sequence, finite map, natural number, optional natural number."
**Problem**: Every other entry's carrier is fixed; `Map_fin`'s key and value types appear nowhere in the note. They are recoverable only by inference from V-PRIM's lookup signature (`·[K] : Map_fin → T ∪ {⊥}`, K a registered class) and ASN-0128's BH3 — but COD's stated job is to pin the codomain set ("PC0–PC2a compose within `Codom` and introduce no codomain beyond it"), and a codomain whose elements are undefined undercuts that.
**Required**: One line in COD: `Map_fin` = finite partial maps from registered coverage classes to `T`, introduced by `targets_keyed`, eliminated only by V-PRIM's `·[K]`.

### Issue 4: Intro paragraph 2 and the commitments list say the same thing twice (anti-bloat)
**ASN-0129, opening section**: "The properties proved here are what such machinery needs: purity (PC4) and termination (PC5) make predicates safe to evaluate concurrently and guaranteed to halt; the closure ceiling (PC6) makes the language's limits knowable; and the dynamics classification (PD0–PD2) tells a protocol author how a predicate's truth *moves* across transitions" — immediately followed by the commitments bullets: "**Evaluation guarantees** (PC4, PC5): purity and termination for every PL term at every reachable state"; "**Predicate dynamics** (FP, PD0, PD1, PD2): ... the stability theory a protocol author needs to choose trigger and termination predicates soundly."
**Problem**: The same per-label inventory with near-identical glosses appears in two adjacent structural slots. This is the "two paragraphs say the same thing in different words" pattern flagged for this note's anti-bloat mode.
**Required**: Keep one. Let the commitments list carry the inventory; trim the intro paragraph to the consumers/extension-language framing it uniquely contributes.

### Issue 5: PC6's "What the relativization costs" carries conjecture-implication essay content in the theorem's slot (anti-bloat)
**ASN-0129, PC6**: "The relationship between that unrestricted ceiling and C-reach is one-directional, and the polarity matters: an unrestricted ceiling claim ... would *entail* ¬C-reach ... Nor does the converse hold: ¬C-reach ... would not establish the unrestricted ceiling ... The parity of `count(L_dom)` is the candidate ... A vocabulary-axis escape suffices for the non-implication, the unrestricted class computing parity a fortiori."
**Problem**: This is an analysis of mutual implications between two non-theorems (an unstated "unrestricted ceiling" claim and the C-reach conjecture), placed inside PC6's already very long paragraph rather than with C-reach/Open Question 6 where the conjecture's status lives. The three-restriction pricing earlier in the same passage is load-bearing (it explains where the base is fixed and why); the entailment/non-implication polarity chain and the a-fortiori parity elaboration are essay content in a theorem slot. Relatedly, V-TUP's coverage-test bullet forward-defers to the same site ("PC6's base granularity, where Gregory's read-path evidence for this line is given") — the granularity treatment is split across two locations with a pointer.
**Required**: PC6 keeps its statement, the two fixed parameters, both proof directions, and a tightened pricing of the three restrictions (the reach loop, plus one sentence that the unrestricted class strictly exceeds PL — parity as the witness candidate). Move the entailment/non-implication polarity analysis to C-reach or Open Question 6, or cut it to a sentence. Trim V-TUP's forward-pointer to the bare granularity statement.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Completing the dynamics classification
PD0 deliberately restricts to enumerated forms ("we keep the class to the enumerated forms"): `is_K` at audit view is ⊤-stable but classifiable only via its V-AUD re-spelling as a quantifier over `L_K`; audit-view `targets_of(x)` is grow-only in fact but not in the grow-only class; the guarded T1-extremum lower bound is acknowledged ⊤-stable but excluded. **Why out of scope**: the note's Open Question 5 already names mechanical, more complete classification as open; widening the sufficient-condition classes is successor work, not an error in this note's sound-but-incomplete classes.

### Topic 2: Binary set algebra and ℕ subtraction
V-PRIM admits indexed union `⋃(D, f)` but no binary `∪`/`∩`/`∖` on set-valued terms and no ℕ subtraction. Union-cardinality *predicates* across types are recoverable by inclusion–exclusion in inequality form (`count(M_K1) + count(M_K2) ≥ c + count({x ∈ M_K1 : x ∈ M_K2})` — the intersection is a QD filter with a V-PRIM membership body), but a union-total *value* is not a term. **Why out of scope**: a deliberate vocabulary omission of exactly the kind PC6's vocabulary-axis discussion prices — closable by a V-PRIM-style admission in a successor if a consumer forces it.

VERDICT: REVISE
