This is a strong note: the C-reach demotion from theorem to conjecture is exemplary honesty, H-init is correctly surfaced as a hypothesis rather than smuggled in as a citation, the worked trace actually computes the PD1 flip on named states, and the V-IDX vacuity argument checks out against S1–S3 and R-C0/R-C1 (BH1 fails at the two Binary designates, BH2/BH3 fail at `retired`, BH4 fails at all three idem=⊤ entries). The remaining issues are two precision defects and the anti-bloat accretion the review mode flags.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: V_atom's displayed definition omits two of its four announced additions
**ASN-0129, "The atomic vocabulary," V**: "The atomic vocabulary is `V_atom = ⋃_{K registered} Tpl(record(K))`"
**Problem**: The template families contain only the core and BH1–BH4 atoms. V-TUP's projections and V-PRIM's primitives are announced as vocabulary ("all four extend the vocabulary itself") but appear in no `Tpl`, so the displayed union excludes them. Downstream uses patch this silently: V-STAT's proof argues staticity of "the template families and V-AUD/V-TUP" as though V-TUP were inside `V_atom`, and PC6 must correct the generator set parenthetically — "the least class containing `V_atom` (V-AUD's readings, V-TUP's projections, and V-PRIM's primitives included)". For a note whose own converse argument insists that "an unadmitted ambient leaf … would silently break" the leaf enumeration, the official alphabet formula failing to match the announced inventory is exactly the slip its standards condemn.
**Required**: Define the alphabet once: `V_atom = ⋃_K Tpl(record(K)) ∪ V-TUP ∪ V-PRIM`, state V-AUD's status explicitly (a third view-reading of existing core symbols, not new symbols), and drop the corrective parentheticals at V-STAT and PC6.

### Issue 2: the parity example is a vocabulary-axis escape mislabeled as feedback
**ASN-0129, PC6, "What the relativization costs"**: "the parity of `count(L_dom)` is computable by a feedback enumeration of `dom(Σ.L)`"
**Problem**: The same paragraph defines feedback as "control flow that re-drives base reads from the evaluation's own accumulating output … whose depth no syntax fixes." Parity is computable by a single pass over the enumerated domain with a one-bit accumulator: the reads are fixed by the collection, the depth by syntax — exactly the shape of PL's admitted folds. What excludes it from the syntax-directed class is the *node vocabulary* (the bit-flip fold is not among PC1/PC2a's folds), i.e., the vocabulary axis — the axis the paragraph classifies as "benign … closable by V-PRIM-style admission." The example therefore contradicts the paragraph's own claim that the three restrictions "fail differently when dropped," and it strains the "benign" verdict, since admitting arbitrary fold accumulators is a categorically larger extension than admitting one node combinator like multiplication (order-dependent folds over T1-sorted sets would admit arbitrary finite-state passes).
**Required**: Relabel parity as a fold-vocabulary escape (a vocabulary-axis escape already suffices for the converse non-implication being argued), and qualify the "benign" classification to node combinators as distinct from new fold forms — or replace parity with an example that genuinely requires feedback.

### Issue 3: admission rationales repeated at every definition site, all deferring to PC6's converse
**ASN-0129, V / V-TUP / V-PRIM / COD / QD-fin / PD0** (anti-bloat): The same justification shape — "this admission exists because PC6's converse needs an exhaustive leaf enumeration" — recurs at six or more sites: V's "This division is load-bearing three ways: it is where PC6's evaluation class draws its line, it is the grammar fact PC6a states, and it is the honest framing of C-reach"; V's "PC6's leaf enumeration counts vocabulary, so the bookkeeping counts four"; V-TUP's closing "V-TUP is also what closes PC6's converse"; V-PRIM's "load-bearing twice over: … which an unadmitted ambient leaf — a bare `⊤`, an undeclared numeral, an unowned definedness test — would silently break"; the Map_fin lookup "admitted so that `Map_fin` has a consumer: without one … dead vocabulary in an algebra whose converse direction enumerates its forms exhaustively"; COD repeating that same Map_fin rationale ("admitted for exactly that reason"); and PC6's converse then making the V-TUP point a third time ("This is why V-TUP must be vocabulary: …"). The defensive parentheticals in QD-fin ("the induction spares the B2/RP-b transfer chain a bare L-fin citation would owe"), PD0 ("Registry constancy, R1, contributes no leaf here: …"), and V ("but composition does not otherwise lack one: …") are the same pattern — pre-rebutting objections rather than advancing definitions.
**Problem**: These are the flagged accretion patterns: downstream-consumer inventories at definition sites, defensive justification prose, and four-plus separate paragraphs deferring to the same downstream location (PC6's converse). The technical content of every one of these rationales is established exactly once, in PC6's converse, where the leaf normalization is actually proven.
**Required**: State each admission as a bare definition; carry the "needed for the converse" argument once, at PC6's converse (where the V-TUP necessity sentence already lives); delete the per-site rationale prose, the bookkeeping sentence, and the duplicated Map_fin justification in COD.

### Issue 4: front matter re-argues body content at full technical depth
**ASN-0129, intro and "What this note commits"** (anti-bloat): The PC6 relativization with its polarity argument is told three times — intro ¶1 ("relative to the substrate's read base as PC6 fixes it — atom-granular … no richer and no poorer"), commitments bullet 5 ("an unrestricted ceiling would *entail* ¬C-reach, so this note's conjecture of C-reach is implicitly a conjecture that the unrestricted ceiling fails"), and PC6's body, which repeats that sentence in substance verbatim. The V-IDX vacuity mechanism is argued in commitments bullet 2 ("the mandatory designated entries defeat universal attachment at every constructible registry") and again as V-IDX's bold claim. "Settling ASN-0128 Open Question 2" appears in both the commitments and PC2a; "settles ASN-0128 Open Question 1" in both the commitments and UV; "four admissions … each fenced where introduced" in both the commitments and V.
**Problem**: This is the "two paragraphs say the same thing in different words" pattern, compounded to three tellings for PC6. The commitments section should index the body's claims, not re-derive their multi-clause arguments; as written, the reader meets each load-bearing argument before its definitions exist and then must recognize it again at the proof site.
**Required**: Reduce each commitments bullet to a one-line index (claim labels plus a one-clause hook); keep each argument's single full statement at its body site; trim intro ¶1's restatement of PC6's parameter-fixing to the claim name.
