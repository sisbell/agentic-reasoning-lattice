This is a mature note — the algebra is sound where I probed it. I verified the worked trace state-by-state (Σ₀–Σ₄, both views, including the C3/DR discharge at Σ₄ and the frontier-landing addresses), the QD-fin induction, the PD0 soundness argument rule-by-rule, FP's footprints against the upstream atom definitions (including the `targets_keyed` cross-type footprint and BH4's home-wide footprint), and PC6's converse normalization at its non-trivial leaf (`Observe_K` as a QD filter over V-TUP tests). ## REVISE

### Issue 1: PD0's aggregate clause contradicts itself on which class `count(D) ≤ c` inhabits
**ASN-0129, Predicate dynamics, PD0 (AuditMonotonicity), Aggregates bullet**: "Equality and wrong-polarity bounds are in *neither* class: `count(L_K) ≤ 3` is the canonical non-member of `ST` — true while the slice is small, flipped ⊤→⊥ for good by the fourth K-deposit; it lives in `SF`, where that flip is the licensed direction — and `count(D) = c` lives nowhere"
**Problem**: The headline asserts that wrong-polarity bounds are in *neither* class. This is contradicted twice within its own context: the rule one sentence earlier grants `count(D) ≤ c ∈ SF` outright, and the example's own continuation says `count(L_K) ≤ 3` "lives in `SF`". Only equality is in neither class; each inequality is in exactly one. In a section whose entire content is a membership classification — and which PD2 and Open Question 5 treat as the mechanical input to protocol checking — a reader applying the headline literally concludes `count(D) ≤ c ∉ SF` and loses the ⊥-stability the rule just granted. The intended meaning (an inequality read at the *wrong* polarity is not in *that* class) is recoverable only by discarding the sentence's literal claim.
**Required**: Split the classification so the headline matches the rules: equality is in neither class; each inequality bound belongs to exactly one class — `count(D) ≥ c` to `ST` only (its ⊤→⊥ flip is unlicensed there is false; its ⊥→⊤ flip is the licensed direction), `count(D) ≤ c` to `SF` only. Keep the `count(L_K) ≤ 3` example as the illustration of why the `ST` reading fails.

### Issue 2: Conjecture cross-pointer accretion across PC6, C-emit, QD-audit, and Open Question 6
**ASN-0129, PC6 / C-emit / QD-audit / Open Question 6**: PC6 states "the age-bearing case is a separate obligation of any proof — frontier-derived data, to be closed by the same kind of invariance argument as the self-emit conjecture (C-emit, below)"; C-emit restates it — "PC6's parity candidate carries the same age-bearing obligation, to be closed by the same invariance machinery"; Open Question 6 then mentions it a third time ("assessed at PC6, the age-bearing obligation included") and decorates its other two conjectures with self-locating parentheticals — "C-reach (stated and assessed in full at C-reach)" and "(stated and assessed in full at C-emit)". QD-audit separately defers with a contents-inventory of another section: "the grammar fact, its grounds, and the design conclusion it carries are C-emit's (The ceiling)."
**Problem**: This is the forward-reference accretion pattern the review mode names: the same proof obligation is stated in three places in slightly different words, and multiple sections defer to the same downstream location with prose that describes what the target section contains rather than advancing any claim. "C-reach (stated and assessed in full at C-reach)" points a label at itself — the parenthetical is pure reviewer-directed reassurance ("in full") with zero content for the next reader. QD-audit's deferral inventories C-emit's contents ("the grammar fact, its grounds, and the design conclusion it carries") when all the audit needs is the fact itself: the self-emit disjunct has no PL spelling. These pointers compound: a reader following them traverses PC6 → C-emit → OQ6 → back to PC6 and learns the one fact three times.
**Required**: State the age-bearing obligation once, at its home (PC6's parity passage or C-emit, not both), with the other site reduced to a bare cross-reference if any is needed. Strip Open Question 6's "(stated and assessed in full at …)" parentheticals — naming the three conjectures suffices, since each name is its location. Reduce QD-audit's deferral to the minimal statement plus pointer ("the self-emit disjunct has no PL spelling — see C-emit").

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Finer stability rules for extremum-built terms
PD0 excludes the T1-extrema from both classes wholesale ("the ⊥-at-empty verdict and the extremum's movement under growth make their polarity conditional"). The exclusion is sound but conservative: over a grow-only domain, `max_{T1}(D)` is T1-nondecreasing once the domain is non-empty, so guarded tests of the form "defined and ≥ a" are in fact ⊤-stable. **Why out of scope**: refining the classification toward completeness is exactly the territory Open Question 5 reserves; PD0's syntactic classes are explicitly sound-not-complete, and a sound exclusion is not an error.
