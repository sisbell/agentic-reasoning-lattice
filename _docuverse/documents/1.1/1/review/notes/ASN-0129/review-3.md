# Review of ASN-0129

The architecture is sound — the four fenced admissions, the QD/PL mutual induction, the honest demotion of C-reach to conjecture, and the polarity-typed PD0 class are all careful work. The remaining issues are precision failures in load-bearing scope statements, one internal inconsistency between FP and its consumer PD2, and a missing concrete evaluation. All are fixable.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: "exactly as conjectural as C-reach" misstates the logical relationship — with inverted polarity
**ASN-0129, PC6 (and the commitments bullet, which repeats it)**: "If C-reach holds, feedback computation strictly exceeds PL; against unrestricted base-read computation, the ceiling claim is therefore exactly as conjectural as C-reach, and PC6 does not assert it."
**Problem**: The relationship is one-directional and the polarity inverts. C-reach — which the note *conjectures true* — would *refute* the unrestricted ceiling (reach is feedback-computable, so reach ∉ PL forces unrestricted-class ≠ PL). So under the note's own conjecture, the unrestricted ceiling is conjectured **false**, not "as conjectural"; a reader of the commitments bullet can come away believing the note tentatively endorses the unrestricted ceiling, when it is committed to the opposite. The converse direction also fails: ¬C-reach (reach ∈ PL) would not establish the ceiling, since other feedback-computable predicates may escape PL independently of reach — e.g., "count(L_dom) is even" is computable by a feedback enumeration of dom(Σ.L), while PL's ℕ fragment (comparisons and + against literals, no quantification over ℕ, no recursion) gives no plausible expression of parity. "Exactly" asserts an equivalence the note nowhere argues and that is false in at least one direction.
**Required**: Replace both occurrences with the precise statement: the unrestricted-ceiling claim *entails* ¬C-reach; since the note conjectures C-reach, it implicitly conjectures the unrestricted ceiling fails; PC6 asserts the ceiling for syntax-directed evaluation only.

### Issue 2: FP's per-atom table contradicts PD2's use of it for behavior atoms at the default view
**ASN-0129, FP**: "BH2 and BH3 atoms of K: `L_K` and `L_R` (active-view reads, ASN-0128)" — stated unconditionally; **PD2**: "(A default-view term adds every BH1 type's active slice to its footprint, FP, so its non-interference set shrinks by Φ as well.)"
**Problem**: Under `v = default`, UV rewrites the collection-valued results of the behavior atoms too — `succs`, `sources_to`, the sequence `chain` returns, and `stale` are all in UV's collections bullet — and computing the rewrite evaluates `is_filtered_J` for every BH1 type J, so those atoms' default-view evaluation footprint includes `⋃_{J ∈ Φ}(L_J ∪ L_R)`. FP records this increment only in its row for "core atoms of K at view default"; the BH2/BH3/BH4 rows carry no default-view variant. PD2's blanket parenthetical is the *correct* claim, but it cites FP for support FP's table only provides for core atoms. The note advertises the dynamics classes as "footprint facts"; the table and its consumer must agree.
**Required**: Add the default-view increment to FP uniformly for every collection-returning atom (the verdict-, Boolean-, and `Map_fin`-valued atoms correctly take no increment under UV — say so), or restate the default rule once, atom-uniformly. PD2's citation then matches.

### Issue 3: QD-fin cites L-fin at extended-record states without the transfer chain the note requires of itself elsewhere
**ASN-0129, QD-fin**: "dom(Σ.L) is finite by L-fin (ASN-0043)" (and again: "M_K is finite by D1's bound and V-AUD's (L-fin plus finitely many spans per endset, AD)").
**Problem**: L-fin is an invariant of ASN-0043's reachable states; QD-fin needs it at `→_sh*`-reachable *extended-record* states. The note's own citation discipline names the carriage everywhere else — PD0's ground says "carried across extended-record steps by ASN-0126's B2 with RP-b, ASN-0128", and QD-fin itself, for the step effects, writes "as extended by R-TR, ASN-0128" — but the L-fin appeal skips the bridge entirely. The fact holds; the discharge is absent. A shorter self-contained discharge is sitting in the same proof: the note already invokes `Σ_init.L = ∅` (R-VAL, in the H-init paragraph), and each derivation step adjoins at most one link key, so dom(Σ.L)-finiteness follows from the very reaching-derivation induction the proof already runs for dom(Σ.C) and dom(Σ.M).
**Required**: Either cite L-fin together with its transfer chain to extended-record states, or fold dom(Σ.L) into the existing induction (base `Σ_init.L = ∅` per R-VAL, step: one fresh key per K.λ_sh).

### Issue 4: PC6's converse discharges registry-lookup leaves "by admission," but no registry-reading atom is admitted
**ASN-0129, PC6 (converse)**: "The remaining leaves — domain memberships, registry lookups, V-PRIM primitives — are vocabulary by admission, so every syntax-directed evaluation normalizes into the grammar."
**Problem**: Registry lookups are not vocabulary. No PL atom returns registry data — `shape(K)`, the behavior set, or registration status; the only registry read PL contains is the QD base `Reg` (plus the per-K *existence* of template families, fixed at construction). The correct discharge for a registry-lookup leaf is not admission but R1 constant-folding: the lookup is state-constant, so the computation containing it is extensionally equal to one with the value inlined — a V-PRIM constant, a statically taken branch, or a `Reg` expansion. The proof omits this step and instead asserts an admission the vocabulary section never makes. The same looseness recurs in PD0's step-constants list ("registry lookups (R1)"), which names a term form PL does not contain.
**Required**: State the constant-folding clause for registry-lookup leaves explicitly (the cheap fix), or admit a registry-reading atom and account for it in the "exactly four admissions" bookkeeping. Align PD0's step-constant list with whichever is chosen.

### Issue 5: V-IDX's static expansion is undefined when the body's vocabulary is not attached at every registered class
**ASN-0129, V-IDX**: "in `(∀ K ∈ Reg :: Φ(K))` the body applies atom families at the bound class — `M_K`, `is_K`, `L_K` with K a variable — a term former that would otherwise be undefined. It is defined by *static expansion*."
**Problem**: The expansion is well-defined only when every instance `Φ(K₀)` is a PL term, and the available vocabulary is non-uniform across `Reg`: a body using a behavior-family atom (`succs`, `tip`, `age`) or the keyed lookup has no denotation at registered classes lacking the behavior or the shape — the instance simply is not in PL. V-IDX neither restricts the body to the core family nor states the well-formedness condition, and the closing well-typing remark ("signature unification against COD plus registry lookup") does not address class variables. This is a boundary case of the note's own definitional machinery, left unhandled.
**Required**: One clause: a `Reg`-quantified term is well-formed iff each of its finitely many expansion instances is — equivalently, behavior-family atoms under a class variable require the behavior (and shape) attached at every registered class; decidable at construction since the registry is static.

### Issue 6: no composed predicate is ever evaluated at a concrete state; the PD1 oscillation is asserted, not traced
**ASN-0129, Worked composition / PD1**: the section constructs terms (`OPEN(t)`, `quiescent(t)`, `head_live(t)`) and checks their typing, but no term is evaluated against an explicit state; PD1's central protocol-facing warning is given only generically ("flips ⊥→⊤ on a K-deposit and ⊤→⊥ on a retraction whose target carries the last witness").
**Problem**: The note's key deliverables — evaluation semantics and the dynamics classification — are verified against no concrete scenario. A claim of the form "a fire-until-Q loop can un-terminate" should be exhibited, not described; the registrations for `cmt` and `res` are already on the page and make the trace three steps long.
**Required**: One explicit trace: Σ₁ with a single cmt tuple c₁ targeting `t` (compute `OPEN(t) = {c₁}`, `quiescent(t) = ⊥`); Σ₂ after emitting a res tuple resolving c₁ (`OPEN(t) = ∅`, `quiescent(t) = ⊤`); Σ₃ after `Nullify_Binary` on the res tuple's address (the existential loses its witness in `A_res`; `OPEN(t) = {c₁}`, `quiescent(t) = ⊥`) — verifying the PD1 flip sequence ⊥→⊤→⊥ on the named term. Optionally evaluate one audit-view PD0 term (e.g., `(∃ x ∈ L_res :: ⊤)`) along the same trace to show it locks ⊤ at Σ₂ and stays ⊤ at Σ₃.

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Characterization of PL's numeric fragment
**Why out of scope**: Which properties of counts are expressible (parity-style boundaries; the quantifier-free-Presburger shape of the ℕ fragment) would sharpen the ceiling and feed the C-reach proof obligation, but it is new theory adjacent to Open Questions 3 and 6, not an error in this note — except insofar as Issue 1 leans on its absence, which the Issue 1 fix resolves without the characterization.

### Topic 2: Audit-view analogues of the behavior atoms
**Why out of scope**: The note deliberately leaves BH1–BH4 fixed-view ("their slice part of their meaning"), and audit-side reconstructions of the core readings are derivable through `L_K`. An audit-time walk (a `chain` over the audit graph) would be a new admission with its own footprint and dynamics rows — a successor's territory if a need ever materializes.
