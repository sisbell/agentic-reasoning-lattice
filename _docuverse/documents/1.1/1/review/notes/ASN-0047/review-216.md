# Review of ASN-0047

This is a technically strong note. The proof obligations are discharged with unusual care — GlobalLineage's inc-chain induction, the D-SEQ★ case split at m=2/m≥3, the K.μ~ necessity/sufficiency argument, and the FrontierEquivalence biconditional all check out, and the boundary cases I probed (full-document clearance, constant-valued `dom_C`, empty link subspace, fork with `V_{s_C}(d_src) = ∅`) are handled correctly. The findings below are about accretion and internal consistency, not correctness of the core results. The note carries the `review-mode.anti-bloat` / forward-reference classifiers, and several flagged patterns are present.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: Worked-example steps list vacuous P7a checks, contradicting the stated carry-forward convention
**ASN-0047, *Worked example: fork with subsequent insertion* (and the delete/reorder/link steps that follow)**: The "Vacuity conventions" block states convention (3): "Any Class (a) per-state invariant not explicitly verified at a step is inherited from the prior state by the relevant frame or restriction. **Each step below lists only its non-vacuous checks.**"
**Problem**: Nearly every step then lists a vacuous P7a check anyway — e.g. fork: "*P7a:* dom(C₂) = dom(C₁)...both a₁ and a₂ have provenance entries"; the K.μ⁻ delete step: "*P7a:* dom(C₄) = dom(C₃) and R₄ = R₃ (frame)"; the K.μ~ reorder step: "*P7a:* dom(C₅) = dom(C₄) and R₅ = R₄ (frame)"; and again in all four link-allocation steps ("*P7a:* dom(C) is unchanged..."). In each of these, dom(C) and R are framed, so P7a is exactly the carry-forward case convention (3) says to omit. (P7a is also a *composite-boundary* property, not Class (a), so its appearance in per-step verification is doubly odd.) The convention and the listings disagree.
**Required**: Either drop the vacuous P7a lines from the frame-only steps (per the stated convention), or, if they are kept for emphasis, amend convention (3) to say P7a is always shown. The steps where dom(C) genuinely grows (the insert step adding a₃; the interior-replacement step adding a₂') correctly carry a non-vacuous P7a check and should stay.

### Issue 2: Rationale/proof-architecture prose occupying structural slots
**ASN-0047, *Extended reachable-state invariants* preamble**: The paragraph "This distinction reflects Nelson's design: at the docuverse layer, compound user actions decompose into sequences of elementary commands whose intermediate states are conceptually observable to other agents (LM 4/63 — FEBE commands return acknowledgments individually...). Only CREATENEWVERSION is atomic at the protocol level; other compound flows expose intermediate states by design."
**Problem**: This is protocol-rationale essay explaining *why* the per-state vs composite-boundary distinction is drawn, sitting in the slot that should state the invariant content. The distinction itself is already fully specified by the two bullet definitions immediately above; the Nelson-observability justification adds no proof obligation and is exactly the "essay content / explains why rather than what" pattern the anti-bloat mode flags. Related instances: the K.δ k=1 aside "the (a')/(b') dispatch on t's parent allocator...surfaces only in the child-spawn verification, not in the operation itself" (proof-architecture commentary), and FrontierEquivalence's "which is itself the frontier-maximality condition on t, not a separate precondition clause" (defensive clarification about clause status).
**Required**: Cut the protocol-observability paragraph (or compress to a one-line pointer if the LM citation is load-bearing elsewhere). Drop the "surfaces only in..." and "not a separate precondition clause" asides — they describe where arguments live rather than advancing them.

### Issue 3: Inherited link invariants stated three times
**ASN-0047, *Link store and extended system state* vs *Inherited from foundation* table**: L0, L1, L1a, L3, L12, L14 are each given a full formal statement in the body section ("For self-containment, we restate the definitions and invariants needed"), then restated in full again in the *Inherited from foundation (restated for narrative continuity)* table, and several (L0, L1, L3, L14) also carry a third formal statement in their verification-matrix prose rows.
**Problem**: This is the "two paragraphs say the same thing in different words" pattern at scale. The *Inherited from foundation* table already exists precisely to restate foundation invariants for continuity with their source citations; the earlier body block duplicates that role. A reader reconciling L3's body statement, its table statement, and the matrix-row statement must verify they agree (they do, but the verification is forced work).
**Required**: Keep one canonical restatement site — the *Inherited from foundation* table, since it pairs each statement with its ASN-0093 source. Replace the body-section formal restatements with a single pointer ("L0, L1, L1a, L3, L12, L14 are inherited unchanged from ASN-0093; see the *Inherited from foundation* table"), retaining only the genuinely new local content there (e.g. the empty-endset semantics note under L3, which is not in the foundation).
