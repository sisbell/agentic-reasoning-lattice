# Review of ASN-0122

## REVISE

### Issue 1: X2's reachability construction is not assembled from valid composites
**ASN-0122, "What 'Correspond' Must Mean," X2 (CoincidenceExclusion)**: "let two allocation events deposit the same `v ∈ Val` at `a₁` and `a₂`. … Arrange each by K.μ⁺ (its precondition `a_i ∈ dom(C)` holds) in documents `d₁, d₂`."
**Problem**: The claim is "There are reachable states…", and in the foundation's transition model a state is reachable only via elementary transitions drawn from *valid composites*. A composite that allocates `a_i` (K.α) and installs it into the content-subspace range of `M(d_i)` (K.μ⁺) is valid only if it also records provenance: J1★ demands `(a_i, d_i) ∈ R'` for every range-new content address, and J0 demands the allocation and arrangement co-occur within one composite. The construction as written names K.α and K.μ⁺ only; the sketched sequence violates J1★ and therefore does not witness a reachable state.
**Required**: Package each allocation explicitly as a valid composite K.α + K.μ⁺ + K.ρ (noting `d₁, d₂` pre-exist or are created by K.δ), and conclude reachability from composite validity. One or two sentences suffice; the conclusion of X2 is unaffected.

### Issue 2: X9's justification overstates what the discarded pairs reveal
**ASN-0122, "Which Positions May Participate"**: lead-in "the link subspace, if admitted, would contribute only what is decidable from the operand syntax alone"; X9's conclusion "discards only pairs whose membership was already decidable without consulting the arrangements — no information about the documents."
**Problem**: The forced diagonal is `{((d,v),(d,v)) : (d,v) a link instance in P ∩ Q}`, and whether `(d, v)` is an instance at all is arrangement information — `v ∈ dom(M(d))` is exactly what the clipping consults. A caller supplying link-subspace spans would receive, via the diagonal, precisely which of those positions are currently arranged — document information, not derivable from operand syntax. What the three sub-arguments (CL-OWN, S3★+SD, CL-UNIQ) actually establish is narrower and sufficient: for any pair involving a link instance, the `corr` predicate reduces to instance equality *without consulting the resolution map*, so the link subspace contributes no cross-position or cross-document correspondence. The proof is right; the gloss it is wrapped in is false as stated, and X12 leans on that gloss ("lossless by X9").
**Required**: Restate the lead-in and X9's conclusion precisely: the discarded contribution is the forced self-diagonal, determined by `P ∩ Q` independently of `res`; it carries arrangement-domain information but no correspondence (sharing) information, which is the sense in which the content-subspace restriction is lossless for this operation.

### Issue 3: R3 conflates determinism with canonicity, and the conformance clause then retracts R3
**ASN-0122, "The Operation," X12**: "(R3) *determinism* — pairs are maximal and listed in canonical lexicographic order, first-operand-major" versus, one paragraph later, "it may emit finer pairs than the maximal ones — granularity is free — so long as the denotation equals `corr`. Completeness, soundness, confinement, and the determinism of whatever presentation it fixes are non-negotiable."
**Problem**: Two defects. (a) Mislabeling: maximality plus canonical order is *canonicity* — a particular output; determinism (same inputs, same output) is already X5's consequence and holds for any fixed presentation. (b) Normative inconsistency: an implementation emitting finer-than-maximal pairs violates R3 as written yet is declared conformant by the very next paragraph — and the ASN's own udanax-green adjudication ("one pair per arrangement-index leaf … the particular count is implementation granularity") applies the denotational standard, not R3. The same ambiguity touches R4, which the implementation section also relaxes ("The packing is representation, not content"). As a contract, X12 mixes binding postconditions (R1, R2) with reference-presentation properties (R3, R4) under one heading and equal force.
**Required**: Separate the contract into (i) binding conformance requirements — soundness, completeness, confinement, deterministic presentation — and (ii) the canonical reference presentation `CANON` (maximal pairs, lexicographic order, minimal record), stating explicitly that (ii) defines the reference result and is not required of conforming implementations. Rename R3 accordingly.

### Issue 4: Empty-document and empty-region boundaries are unstated, and σ_full carries an implicit nonemptiness precondition
**ASN-0122, "State, Instances, and Spec-Sets"**: "Whole-document comparison is not a separate notion. When `V_{s_C}(d) ≠ ∅`, D-SEQ★ gives … `σ_full = ([s_C, 1, …, 1], δ(n, m))` … and nothing we prove about windows needs a whole-document special case."
**Problem**: `δ(n, m)` requires `n ≥ 1`, so `σ_full` does not exist for an empty document — "whole-document comparison is not a separate notion" silently excludes exactly the case where the document arranges nothing, and the ASN never says what the whole-document operand is then (the empty span-set is the only witness). More broadly, the mandatory boundary cases are nowhere stated: an empty spec-set (`j = 0`), a span-set clipping to the empty region, and one or both regions empty. They all follow from the definitions (`corr = ∅`, X11 partitions the empty relation vacuously, `CANON = ⟨⟩` conforms), but a specification must state its empty cases, not leave the reader to verify that the maximal-chain machinery degenerates correctly.
**Required**: One paragraph: the whole-document operand for an empty document is `(d, ∅)`; empty region(s) yield `corr = ∅` and the canonical report `⟨⟩`; X11's existence/uniqueness holds vacuously.

### Issue 5: No concrete worked example
**ASN-0122, whole document**: No key postcondition is verified against a specific scenario. The implementation-observations section is qualitative; the only computation with explicit tumblers is the `σ_full` clipping check.
**Problem**: The ASN's hardest machinery — fan-out under repetition (X8(b), X11's tie-break), window clipping of a maximal pair (X4c), and canonical ordering — is never exercised on data. Per the depth standard, postconditions without a concrete verification are a REVISE item, and this operation is precisely where an example would catch off-by-one and ordering errors.
**Required**: One worked example with explicit arrangements, e.g. `M(d₁): [1,1]↦a, [1,2]↦b, [1,3]↦c, [1,4]↦b` and `M(d₂): [1,1]↦b, [1,2]↦c`: compute `corr` (five elements, including the fan-out from the repeated `b`), the maximal pairs (`(d₁,[1,2]; d₂,[1,1]; 2)` plus the two width-1 pairs from `[1,4]`), the canonical order including the tie-break, then verify X4 on a window and X8(c) on disjoint windows of `d₁` (which must surface the `b`–`b` internal sharing).

### Issue 6: X6(b)'s composition premises are unstated, and X7's wp formulas are silently one-sided
**ASN-0122, "Stability," X6(b)–(c) and X7(i)–(ii)**: "Steps compose: after `k` steps the composite `φ_k ∘ … ∘ φ₁` is again injective and res-preserving, so the endpoints correspond exactly on the transported material, with a bound independent of `k`"; "wp(K.μ⁻[d₁, n′], (p, q) ∈ corr) ≡ enabled ∧ p surviving ∧ (p, q) ∈ corr"; "corr_{Σ′} = corr_Σ ∩ (Surv × Q)".
**Problem**: (a) The endpoint conclusion of X6(b) needs premises the one-line proof omits: `d⁰`'s restriction must persist from the chain's start to the evaluation state (each step's frame gives this only in the pure-chain scenario), and edits to an intermediate `d^i` *between* its incoming and outgoing steps are absorbed only by composing that edit's X7 position map into `ψ` — which X6 never says. Since (c) advertises that intermediates "may meanwhile be rearranged, contracted to nothing," the reader is invited to interleave edits with steps, and as written (b) does not cover that interleaving. (b) The wp displays in X7(i) and (ii), and the contraction equation `corr_{Σ′} = corr_Σ ∩ (Surv × Q)`, are valid only when the second foot lies off the edited document; the both-feet-on-`d₁` case (self-comparison, the case X8 makes central) requires pulling back or testing survival of *both* feet. The prose note about instantiating `τ` and `υ` from the same map covers X-T, but the displayed formulas carry no such qualifier.
**Required**: State X6(b)'s premises explicitly (endpoint frames persist; intervening intermediate edits enter the composite via their position maps), and either qualify the wp formulas as the one-foot case or display the two-sided forms (`π⁻¹` on both feet; both feet surviving).

### Issue 7: X10(a) cites TS2 for distinctness across shift amounts
**ASN-0122, "The Pair," X10(a)**: "Both feet sets `{u + k : 0 ≤ k < n}` and `{w + k : 0 ≤ k < n}` have cardinality exactly `n`, since shift is injective and strictly increasing (TS2, TS4)."
**Problem**: TS2 is injectivity in the *base* at a fixed amount (`shift(v₁,n) = shift(v₂,n) ⟹ v₁ = v₂`); it does not deliver `k₁ ≠ k₂ ⟹ u + k₁ ≠ u + k₂`. Distinctness across amounts needs strict monotonicity in the amount: for `1 ≤ k₁ < k₂`, `u + k₂ = shift(shift(u, k₁), k₂ − k₁) > shift(u, k₁)` by TS3 then TS4 (or TS5 directly), with the `k₁ = 0` case by TS4 alone. In a series that prides itself on per-step citation discharge, the wrong lemma is a defect.
**Required**: Replace the citation with TS3+TS4 (or TS5, plus TS4 for the `k = 0` base).

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Asserted semantic equivalence (translations, paraphrases) as a typed link
**Why out of scope**: The ASN correctly excludes human-judged sameness from this operation ("an asserted relation owned by its asserter"); specifying such a link type is new territory for a future ASN, not a gap here.

### Topic 2: Consistency contract for derived correspondence indices or cached reports
**Why out of scope**: X5's memorylessness pins the semantics; what a conforming cache must guarantee across edits is already flagged in Open Questions and belongs in its own ASN.

### Topic 3: N-way alignment composed from pairwise reports
**Why out of scope**: X6(d) gives the pairwise composition law; the guarantees needed for sound n-way assembly are listed as an open question and are future work.

VERDICT: REVISE
