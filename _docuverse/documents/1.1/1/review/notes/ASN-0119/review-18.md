# Review of ASN-0119

I verified the two worked examples arithmetically (pivot `A B C D E ↦ A C D E B` with `π: 1↦1,2↦5,3↦2,4↦3,5↦4`; swap `A B C D E F ↦ A E F C D B` with middle displacement `+1 = w_β − w_α`; the four contiguity outcomes; the atomicity composite reaching the same net `π` through the divergent intermediate `A C D B E`). All check out. The S3★ derivation (via `π⁻¹`), the inline P7a transport (via P1 rather than LP11), and the P7c run-structure argument (via R-COMM constant displacement) are sound. The findings below are about framing, labelling, and one internally inconsistent open question — not about the core derivations.

## REVISE

### Issue 1: Open question rests on a premise the model excludes
**ASN-0119, Open Questions**: "What must REARRANGE guarantee when a cut falls on a V-position that is shared, through transclusion, by more than one document, so that the cut is a boundary in one arrangement but interior to another?"
**Problem**: A V-position is a key in *one* document's arrangement `M(d)`. Transclusion shares I-addresses (content identity), not V-positions — this ASN's own Document Isolation section states it directly: "each document is its own V→I mapping over the common, immutable content… A document `d'` that transcludes some of `d`'s content holds an *independent* mapping `M(d')` over the same I-addresses." So no V-position is ever shared across documents; only `ran(M(d)) ∩ ran(M(d'))` can be non-empty. The open question's premise ("a V-position shared… by more than one document") contradicts the model and the note's own P9 analysis.
**Required**: Reframe in terms of a shared *I-address*: a content boundary that is a cut in one document's arrangement but falls interior to another's, given the two arrangements are independent maps over the same Istream. As written the question is incoherent and should not seed a future ASN.

### Issue 2: Introduced claim labels P0–P9 collide systematically with ASN-0047's P-scheme
**ASN-0119, Claims Introduced**: P1 (IdentityCorrespondence), P2 (Permutation), P3 (VExtentConservation), P6 (LinkStoreFrame), P7a (FootprintTransport), P8a/P8b.
**Problem**: ASN-0047 — the model this note explicitly works in and cites on nearly every page (S2, S3★, D-CTG★, D-SEQ★, S8-depth, …) — already defines P0 (ContentPermanence), **P1 (EntityPermanence)**, **P2 (ProvenancePermanence)**, **P3 (ArrangementMutabilityOnly)**, **P6 (ExistentialCoherence)**, **P7a (ProvenanceCoverage)**, **P8 (EntityHierarchy)**. This note reuses those exact labels for unrelated statements (P1, P2, P3, P6, P7a all collide on meaning; P0 reuses the *name* "ContentPermanence" for a strictly stronger statement `Σ'.C = Σ.C`). Because ASN-0047's invariants are interleaved with the note's own P-labels throughout, a reader cannot tell whether "P7a" means FootprintTransport or ProvenanceCoverage without re-deriving context. The other foundation ASNs avoid this by taking a distinctive prefix (ASN-0084 `R-*`, ASN-0098 `LP-*`, ASN-0058 `M-*`).
**Required**: Adopt a distinctive prefix for introduced claims (e.g. `RA-*`) so none collides with the ASN-0047 P-scheme the note depends on. (Also fix the table ordering P7a/P7c/P7b.)

### Issue 3: REARRANGE's status in ASN-0047's *transition* model is left unresolved, and the atomicity claim depends on it
**ASN-0119, The two streams / Links**: "We work in the extended state Σ = (C, L, E, M, R) of ASN-0047's transition model" … "REARRANGE_K is fixed by its destination equations, not as a K.μ~ composite — on symmetric content it yields `M'(d) = M(d)`, which fails K.μ~'s non-triviality clause, so LP11's stated hypothesis is not available for every REARRANGE."
**Problem**: The note treats REARRANGE as an *atomic* transition of ASN-0047's model (the whole atomicity section, P8a/P8b: the document "passes from one canonical total order directly to another"). But ASN-0047's only bijective-rearrangement mechanism, K.μ~, is explicitly **"not atomic"** — a `K.μ⁻ + K.μ⁺` composite that necessarily passes through a contracted (content-removed) intermediate. The LP11 paragraph raises K.μ~ only to justify a derivation choice and leaves REARRANGE's model status half-stated: it records that REARRANGE isn't *always* a K.μ~ (the trivial case), but omits the positive fact that **every non-trivial REARRANGE satisfies all five K.μ~ admissibility conditions (i)–(v) and the K.μ~ precondition** — it *is* a K.μ~ instance. So the reader is left unable to place REARRANGE: if it is the K.μ~ composite, P8's "directly to another" is false (it traverses the K.μ⁻ contraction); if it is ASN-0084's atomic primitive (single `π`, the reading P8 actually needs), then it is *not* in ASN-0047's atomic vocabulary and "we work in ASN-0047's transition model" overclaims. The note also never discharges the model's composite-validity obligations — J0, J1★, J1'★ — which here are vacuous (no content allocated, `ran(M'(d)) = ran(M(d))` by P1, no provenance recorded) but should be stated for a transition asserted to live in that model.
**Required**: Position REARRANGE explicitly as ASN-0084's *atomic* arrangement-rearrangement primitive — distinct from, and not reducible to, ASN-0047's non-atomic K.μ~ composite even though non-trivial REARRANGE realizes the same *net* arrangement change — and qualify the framing to "ASN-0047's state, with REARRANGE as an atomic primitive." Complete the LP11 discussion with the positive characterization (non-trivial ⟹ a K.μ~; trivial ⟹ a no-op), which both backs the model claim and *fully* motivates the uniform inline derivation (it covers the trivial no-op that a bare LP11 citation cannot). Note the coupling constraints are vacuously satisfied. This also lets the defensive "we discharge this inline rather than cite… whose three lines are the ones above" be replaced by the substantive statement.

### Issue 4: Minor meta-prose (anti-bloat)
**ASN-0119, The two streams / What is preserved**: "we cite the starred forms throughout because the operative model is ASN-0047's"; "These are exactly the 'hardest to maintain' tiling conjuncts; here they are the *easiest*, precisely because REARRANGE touches no key."
**Problem**: These sentences justify a citation choice and editorialize rather than advance the argument. The substantive content (the starred forms *coincide* with ASN-0036's on `s_C`; the tiling invariants hold because the key set is unchanged) is already carried by the surrounding prose.
**Required**: Keep the substantive clause (key set unchanged ⟹ key-only invariants inherited), drop the choice-justification and the hardest/easiest color. (Low priority relative to Issues 1–3.)

## OUT_OF_SCOPE

### Topic 1: Generalization beyond text-subspace depth 2
The note restricts to `s_C` at `#v = 2`, matching ASN-0084's REARRANGE_K (CS4). ASN-0047's D-SEQ★ admits subspace depths `m_S ≥ 2`, so a deeper text subspace is conceivable.
**Why out of scope**: ASN-0084's closed-form permutations are established only at depth 2; lifting REARRANGE to general depth requires ASN-0084 to extend first. This is new territory, not a defect here. (The note correctly disclaims it: "We make no claim about other subspaces or other depths.")

### Topic 2: Implementation defects (uniform-displacement collision, subspace-boundary crossing)
The well-definedness section's discussion of Gregory's `diff[2]`, the green-implementation collision when `w_β > w_α`, and the boundary-crossing hazard.
**Why out of scope**: These are framed as *evidence* that the abstract tiling spec is correct and a uniform-offset implementation is not — they do not specify REARRANGE, and the boundary-preservation guard is correctly deferred to a future refinement ASN (Open Q5). No drift; no META.

VERDICT: REVISE
