## REVISE

### Issue 1: The K.μ~ aside in the P4a discharge does not advance the Case-2 argument

**ASN-0119, §"What is preserved: I-address correspondence" (the P4a/TraceWitnessing discharge)**: "The non-atomic K.μ~ composite (K.μ⁻ + K.μ⁺) realizes the same net π and reaches the same Σ', ending in its K.μ⁺ half — but it is only one such route (a standalone K.μ⁺ adding a single content position reaches Σ' as well), an illustration rather than a characterization of the alternatives."

**Problem**: The surrounding Case-2 argument is already fully general — it discharges P4a for *any* non-REARRANGE final composite `Σ'' →* Σ'` from "the single hypothesis that P4a holds at its pre-state Σ''." That generality is precisely what makes Case 2 correct: every trace to `Σ'` whose final composite is not a REARRANGE step is handled, whether or not any specific such route is exhibited. The dichotomy is clean — Case 2 is either non-vacuous (handled by the general statement) or vacuous (trivially fine) — so no particular alternative route needs to be named, and naming one then disclaiming it ("only one such route... an illustration rather than a characterization") contributes nothing the general statement does not already carry. Worse, the sentence re-opens the REARRANGE/K.μ~ relationship the intro already settled — the opening paragraph states REARRANGE is "distinct from and not reducible to ASN-0047's own non-atomic K.μ~ composite" and "realizes the same net arrangement change as that composite without ever vacating content." The aside's informational content (K.μ~ is `K.μ⁻ + K.μ⁺`, same net change, reaches the same state) duplicates that. This is the residue of the "fix K.μ~ over-claim" revision: a prior over-claim (K.μ~ as *the* route) was softened into a hedge rather than removed. The hedge is now a detour a precise reader must skip to follow the argument, which runs cleanly from "...it does not factor through Σ via REARRANGE, and need not" directly to "For *any* such final composite Σ'' →* Σ', ASN-0047's P4a argument...".

**Required**: Delete the sentence. The Case-2 argument needs no specific alternative route named; the general statement and the combined induction discharge it. The intro carries the REARRANGE/K.μ~ distinction that is actually load-bearing (it justifies adding REARRANGE as a *new* atomic primitive to ASN-0047's vocabulary), so nothing is lost.
