### S7 attribution proof (main body) type-confuses `dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯`; inconsistent with the per-claim contract
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline) — quantified "for every allocator `A ∈ 𝒯` and every `t ∈ dom(A)`"; instantiation at `A := A_element, t := a` requires two memberships of *distinct types*: `A_element ∈ 𝒯` (allocator in the tree) and `a ∈ dom(A_element)` (address in its domain). Per AllocatedSet/T10a, `𝒯` is the set of *allocators* and `dom(A) ⊆ T` is a set of *tumblers* — disjoint types.
**ASN**: The main "## Structural attribution" narrative carries the premise as a single chained subset. S7a: *"every `a ∈ dom(Σ.C)` satisfies `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` … This membership is the premise that … licenses T10a.4's preservation invariant to be instantiated at `a`."* And the S7 well-definedness proof: *"instantiating it at `A := A_element` and `t := a` — legitimate precisely because S7a places `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` — yields that `a` is T4-valid."* The per-claim sections already carry the correct form: S7a — *"two memberships of distinct types — `A_element ∈ 𝒯` … and `a ∈ dom(A_element)`"*; S7 proof — *"S7a places `A_element ∈ 𝒯` and `a ∈ dom(A_element)`, the two memberships of distinct types the instantiation requires"*; and S7's Formal-Contract preconditions — *"`A_element ∈ 𝒯 ∧ a ∈ dom(A_element)`."*
**Issue**: `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` asserts `dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` — that the address domain (a set of tumblers, `⊆ T`) is a subset of the allocator set. That is a type error: tumblers are not allocators. T10a.4's instantiation genuinely needs the two separate memberships, not a chain through `⊆`. The singular "This membership is the premise" (vs. the per-claim "These two memberships are the premises") confirms the conflation: only the `t`-side membership is being tracked, and the `A_element ∈ 𝒯` site is silently dropped. This is the partial-fix pattern — the per-claim Formal Contracts were corrected to the two-membership form, but the two narrative sites in "## Structural attribution" still carry the old type-confused phrasing, so the body now contradicts its own contract and a reader following the main proof hits a false subset claim. (The conclusion "`a` is T4-valid" is correct; the justification is not.)
**What needs resolving**: Replace `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` at both main-body sites (S7a narrative and the S7 well-definedness step) with the two-membership form already present in the per-claim contracts (`A_element ∈ 𝒯` and `a ∈ dom(A_element)`), and change "This membership is the premise … instantiated at `a`" to the plural "these two memberships are the premises … instantiated at `A := A_element, t := a`," matching the per-claim S7a/S7 text.
