### "document" is its tumbler in S5 but a pre-address abstract entity in S7/S7d's uniqueness routing
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: GlobalUniqueness (ASN-0034); cross-claim — Σ.M(d) (Arrangement), S5, S7, S7d
**ASN**: The ASN has no claim defining what a *document* is. Two sections treat it incompatibly:
- S5 (within/cross-document constructions): *"N + 1 documents d₁, …, d_{N+1} with explicit witnesses dᵢ = [1, 0, 1, 0, i]… The dᵢ are pairwise distinct **by T3 (CanonicalRepresentation)** since they have distinct last components."* — here a document **is** a tumbler (it is compared for distinctness *as a tumbler*, via T3), and `Σ.M` is indexed by these tumblers.
- S7 Uniqueness / S7d: *"S7d separates distinct documents only at the level of events; what it does not by itself supply is the passage from distinct events to distinct addresses — **for two distinct allocation events could, a priori, still emit the same tumbler value.** That passage is exactly GlobalUniqueness."* and S7d: *"Every document is **realized at** a document-level address… distinct documents arise from distinct allocation events."*

**Issue**: The S7 Uniqueness step's invocation of GlobalUniqueness is load-bearing **only if** a document is an abstract entity distinct from its document-level tumbler (so that distinct documents are first separated at the event level, then lifted to distinct tumblers). But S5 (and the `Σ.M(d)` signature it instantiates) identify a document **with** its document-level tumbler, under which "distinct documents" already means "distinct tumblers" and the entire S7d→GlobalUniqueness routing (the subject of the recent reroute commit) is redundant. A downstream consumer of S7's postcondition "`origin(a₁) ≠ origin(a₂)` for distinct allocating documents" cannot verify the Uniqueness step without knowing which reading holds: under document = tumbler the conclusion is immediate and the routing is dead weight; under document = abstract entity the routing is essential and S5's `dᵢ = [1,0,1,0,i]` conflates a document with its realization. The conclusion is sound under either reading, but the proof's structure is only motivated under one of them, and the ASN never fixes which.
**What needs resolving**: State, in a defining claim (or in Strand / Σ.M(d)), what a document is and its relationship to its document-level tumbler. If documents are abstract entities realized at document-level tumblers — the reading S7/S7d require — say so, and reword S5's "documents dᵢ = [1, 0, 1, 0, i]" to present these tumblers as the documents' *realizations/addresses* (the T3 distinctness then witnessing realization-distinctness in that model). If documents simply are their tumblers, then S7d's event-level separation plus S7's GlobalUniqueness lift collapse to triviality and should be simplified to "distinct document tumblers are distinct."

### S7 invokes T4c's level-labeling machinery without citing T4c
**Class**: OBSERVE
**Foundation**: T4c (LevelDetermination, ASN-0034) — the single definitional site for node/user/document/element labels
**ASN**: S7 Well-definedness / Postcondition: *"only now — **the level labels being defined precisely on the T4-valid subset** — does its zero count zeros(origin(a)) = 2 **place origin(a) at the document level in T4's hierarchy**."* S7's Depends lists T0, T3, T4, T4a, T4b, T10a.4 — but not T4c.
**Issue**: "The level labels being defined precisely on the T4-valid subset" and "the document level in T4's hierarchy" are exactly T4c's content (T4 defers the address-kind labels to T4c). S7 references the existence of that labeling scheme rather than merely re-deriving "zeros = 2" inline (as S7b/S7d do for "element-level"/"document-level"). The underlying postcondition content — `zeros(origin(a)) = 2` plus T4-validity — is fully grounded, so the "document level" phrase is decorative gloss and no consumer is actually blocked; but an exported postcondition that names a T4c notion should reach T4c through Depends.
**What needs resolving**: Either add T4c to S7's Depends (the source of the document-level label it places origin(a) at), or replace "places origin(a) at the document level in T4's hierarchy" with the grounded facts already stated (`zeros(origin(a)) = 2 ∧ T4-valid`), dropping the T4c-flavored phrasing.

### Dependency-list prose has drifted into proof transcripts and repeated posit-status meta-essay
**Class**: OBSERVE
**Foundation**: N/A
**ASN**: Two patterns. (1) The Depends entries of S7/S7a/S7d re-derive proof steps in full inside structural slots — e.g. S7's NAT-order entry re-walks the entire `1 < 1` contradiction with all sub-cases (*"its two equal-assignment cases (i = i + 1)… closed by irreflexivity… its order-reversed case… closed by the exactly-one trichotomy clause…"*), and the NAT-card/NAT-closure/NAT-addcompat entries similarly transcribe the cardinality and length-climb derivations. (2) The posited claims repeat near-verbatim posit-status essays — D-CTG, D-MIN, S8a, S8-fin each carry a paragraph of the form *"We posit it rather than derive it; the point of naming it is methodological… a consumer, **a formalization tool included**, must treat it as an implementation obligation…"*.
**Issue**: A Depends entry's job is to say which foundation symbol is consumed and where; re-deriving the proof there is use-site inventory that the precise reader must skip past to learn the actual dependency. The repeated "design posit, not theorem / implementation obligation / a formalization tool included" prose explains *why the axiom is needed* rather than *what it says*, and being duplicated across four posits it is exactly the kind of meta-prose that compounds across cycles. Neither affects soundness, but both degrade the signal of the dependency graph and the axiom statements.
**What needs resolving**: Trim the S7/S7a/S7d Depends entries to use-site annotations (symbol + consuming step), leaving the derivations in the proof body where they already appear; collapse the repeated posit-status/implementation-obligation essay across D-CTG/D-MIN/S8a/S8-fin to a single short clause per claim.
