### S7 narrative cites T0(b) for an infinitude it does not supply; standalone S7 contradicts it
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: T0(b) (UnboundedLength) — postcondition is purely existential: "For every n ∈ ℕ with n ≥ 1, there exists t ∈ T with #t ≥ n." T0 (CarrierSetDefinition) — comprehension axiom places every finite ℕ-sequence of length ≥ 1 in T.
**ASN**: S7 (CoveringExistence). Narrative version (Span-sets section): "Thus every extension lies in [s, reach(s, ℓ)) = ⟦(s, ℓ)⟧, and **by T0(b) there are infinitely many of them**. Hence ⟦σ⟧ is infinite…" Standalone version: "and there are infinitely many of them: writing s.0ⁿ … each s.0ⁿ … **lies in T by T0's comprehension axiom**, while the lengths #s + n are pairwise distinct, making n ↦ s.0ⁿ injective … (The membership of each extension is what is load-bearing here; the existential UnboundedLength claim T0(b) … **is not what we invoke**.)"
**Issue**: The two versions of S7 in the same ASN disagree on the load-bearing dependency. T0(b) only asserts existence of *some* tumbler of length ≥ n; it does not establish that the specific extensions `s.0ⁿ` of this `s` are members of T, nor that they are infinitely many. The standalone version correctly grounds the infinitude in T0's comprehension axiom and explicitly disavows T0(b). The narrative's inference step is therefore ungrounded as written and contradicts the canonical formal contract — a downstream consumer reading the dependency graph cannot tell whether S7 depends on T0(b). The declared depends (ASN-0034) contains both, so the ambiguity is not caught at the metadata level.
**What needs resolving**: Make the narrative S7 consistent with the standalone: ground the infinitude of the trailing-zero extensions in T0's comprehension axiom (each `s.0ⁿ ∈ T`, lengths pairwise distinct) and remove the T0(b) citation, or otherwise reconcile the two versions to a single dependency story.
