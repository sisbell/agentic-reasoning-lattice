**Foundation**: NAT-card (NatFiniteSetCardinality) — the Formal Contract shown carries only the value-clause Axiom (`|S|` is the unique `k` admitting a strictly increasing length-`k` enumeration of `S`) and the upper-bound Axiom; it exports no *Consequence* of the form `|S| = 0 ⟺ S = ∅`.

D-CTG-depth therefore needs its own grounding of the `k = 0` instantiation, on S8a's own stated terms ("not a packaged consequence... derive it here rather than cite it by name"), but instead treats the label "NAT-card's empty-set characterization" as if it were a citable, already-exported fact.
