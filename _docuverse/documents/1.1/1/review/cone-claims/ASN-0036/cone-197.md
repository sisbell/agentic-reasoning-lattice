### "From-1 specialization" of NAT-induction is an undefined, self-contradicting label
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: NAT-induction (NatInduction) — Axiom is stated only in generation-from-`0` form: `(A S : S ⊆ ℕ ∧ 0 ∈ S ∧ (A k ∈ ℕ : k ∈ S : k + 1 ∈ S) : S = ℕ)`.
**ASN**: D-MIN body: *"The recursion is the from-`1` specialization of NAT-induction's ... generation-from-`0` principle ... NAT-induction generates its predicate from the base element `0`, so the formal base of our induction is P(0), and the from-`1` reading is no shortcut around that base but the assertion P(0) ⇒ P(1)"*; repeated in D-MIN's Depends entry for NAT-induction (*"supplies the induction principle, in its from-`1` specialization of the generation-from-`0` principle"*) and in NAT-induction's own Forward References for both D-MIN and D-SEQ.
**Issue**: No "from-1 specialization" of NAT-induction is ever formalized anywhere in the foundation set or in this ASN — the only stated principle is the base-`0` form. The proof that actually follows uses base `P(0)` (vacuous, since `{j : 1≤j≤0}` is empty) and the ordinary step `P(N)⇒P(N+1)`, which *is* the unmodified NAT-induction axiom, not a variant of it. The prose itself concedes this ("the formal base of our induction is P(0), and the from-`1` reading is no shortcut around that base"), which means the label is describing nothing beyond the ordinary first step of induction, yet it is used five times as if it names a distinct grounding mechanism, including inside two *Depends* entries — the formal-contract slots meant to state precisely what is drawn from a foundation claim. A downstream reader or formalization tool citing "NAT-induction's from-1 specialization" as the grounding for D-MIN's (or D-SEQ's) existence proof would look for a base-1 induction lemma that does not exist in the Foundation Statements.
**What needs resolving**: Either (a) remove the "from-1 specialization" framing throughout D-MIN, NAT-induction's forward references, and (presumably) D-SEQ's parallel usage, and describe the citation as direct, unmodified application of NAT-induction with vacuous base `P(0)`, or (b) if a genuinely distinct base-1 principle is intended, state and derive it explicitly (e.g., as a Consequence of NAT-induction) before citing it as a grounding source in a Depends entry.
