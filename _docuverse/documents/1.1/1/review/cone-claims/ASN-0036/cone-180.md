### D-CTG-depth's Preconditions/Depends use bare `M(d)` instead of the canonical `Σ.M(d)`
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: Σ.M(d) (Arrangement)
**ASN**: D-CTG-depth *Preconditions*: "V_1(d) ⊆ dom(M(d))", "every position in dom(M(d)) satisfies S8a", "dom(M(d)) is finite (S8-fin), in the grounded form S8-fin posits: for the document d there exist a bound N ∈ ℕ and a bijection f : {r ∈ ℕ : 1 ≤ r ≤ N} → dom(Σ.M(d))"; D-CTG-depth *Depends*, S8a entry: "applied to u ∈ V_1(d) ⊆ dom(M(d)) it gives u's components their strict positivity"
**Issue**: The Σ.M(d) (Arrangement) claim establishes exactly one symbol for the arrangement domain, `Σ.M(d)`, and every other formal-contract slot in this ASN (S8a's Axiom, D-CTG's Axiom, S8-depth's Axiom, S8-fin's Axiom, AX-1's Axiom) writes it out fully as `dom(Σ.M(d))`. D-CTG's own claim explicitly establishes a suppressed-Σ convention, but only for the derived symbol `V_1(d)` ("the formal statement's shorthand `V_1(d)` carries this suppressed `Σ`") — no such convention is ever established for `M(d)` itself. D-CTG-depth's Preconditions and one Depends bullet nonetheless use bare `M(d)`, even mixing it with the fully-qualified `Σ.M(d)` within the same Preconditions bullet (the S8-fin precondition writes `dom(M(d))` in its lead clause and `dom(Σ.M(d))` two clauses later for the identical object). Since Preconditions and Depends are the structural fields this ASN repeatedly says a formalization tool must be able to parse and ground symbol-by-symbol, an unestablished bare `M(d)` is a symbol with no defining site distinct from `Σ.M(d)`.
**What needs resolving**: Replace the bare `M(d)` occurrences in D-CTG-depth's Preconditions (all three bullets) and in the S8a Depends bullet with `Σ.M(d)`, matching the notation used in every other formal-contract slot in the ASN.

VERDICT: REVISE
