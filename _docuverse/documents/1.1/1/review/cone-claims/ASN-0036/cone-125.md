### ValidInsertionPosition Depends is missing NAT-cancel; proof citation is wrong
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: NAT-cancel (NatAdditionCancellation, ASN-0034)
**ASN**: ValidInsertionPosition Formal Contract — Proof paragraph: "For j ≠ j′ in {0,...,N} the last components 1+j ≠ 1+j′ **(NAT-order, ASN-0034)**"; Postconditions: "The satisfying set contains exactly N+1 pairwise-distinct positions **(NAT-order and T3, ASN-0034)**"; Depends entry for NAT-order: "used to establish 1+j ≠ 1+j′ for j ≠ j′"
**Issue**: The claim "1+j ≠ 1+j′ from j ≠ j′" cannot be established from NAT-order alone. NAT-order posits < as a strict total order and defines ≤ from it, but carries no axiom connecting the order to the addition operation +. The step needs: suppose 1+j = 1+j′; by NAT-cancel's left cancellation (A m,n,p ∈ ℕ : m+n = m+p : n = p) instantiated at m = 1, n = j, p = j′, conclude j = j′ — contradicting j ≠ j′. The contrapositive gives j ≠ j′ ⟹ 1+j ≠ 1+j′. No path through NAT-order alone (irreflexivity, transitivity, trichotomy, ≤-definition) touches the + operator, so the attribution "(NAT-order, ASN-0034)" is incorrect for this step and the Postconditions attribution is incomplete.
**What needs resolving**: Add NAT-cancel (NatAdditionCancellation, ASN-0034) to ValidInsertionPosition's Depends list with an entry describing its use (left cancellation at m = 1 to exclude 1+j = 1+j′ from j ≠ j′). Correct the proof citation from "(NAT-order, ASN-0034)" to include NAT-cancel, and correct the Postconditions citation from "(NAT-order and T3, ASN-0034)" to "(NAT-cancel, NAT-order, and T3, ASN-0034)".
