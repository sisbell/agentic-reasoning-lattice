### Note S7 proof carries two unjustified inferences that the per-claim S7/S7a already repair
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline); GlobalUniqueness; S7d; S7a
**ASN**: Note-narrative S7 proof (under "## Structural attribution").
(a) *Well-definedness:* "By S7b (element-level I-addresses), `zeros(a) = 3`, and by T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline, ASN-0034), `a` is T4-valid".
(b) *Uniqueness across documents:* "By S7d's postcondition, distinct documents have distinct document-level tumblers."
**Issue**:
(a) T10a.4's conclusion is "for every allocator `A` in the tree and every `t ∈ dom(A)`, `t` satisfies T4." To conclude `a` is T4-valid you must discharge the premise `a ∈ dom(A)` for some allocator `A` in the conforming tree. The note's S7 names no allocator and never asserts `a`'s membership; the note's S7a states only that content is "allocated under the tumbler prefix of the document," never `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯`. The premise is missing. The per-claim S7 inserts exactly this ("Before decomposing `a`… By S7a… `a ∈ dom(A_element)`… instantiating [T10a.4] at `A := A_element` and `t := a`"), and the per-claim S7a supplies the membership the note's S7a lacks.
(b) S7d's postcondition — in *both* the note and claim renderings — is "Distinct documents arise from distinct allocation **events**," not distinct tumblers. Two distinct events could a priori emit the same tumbler value; the passage to distinct addresses is GlobalUniqueness, not S7d. The note attributes tumbler-distinctness to "S7d's postcondition," overstating what S7d gives. The per-claim S7 explicitly flags this ("what it does not by itself supply is the passage from distinct events to distinct addresses… That passage is exactly GlobalUniqueness") and routes through GlobalUniqueness; the note omits the bridge entirely.
**What needs resolving**: Port the per-claim S7 reasoning into the note: in Well-definedness, establish `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` (via S7a) before instantiating T10a.4, and add that membership statement to the note's S7a; in Uniqueness, derive document-tumbler distinctness from S7d's event-distinctness via GlobalUniqueness rather than attributing it to S7d's postcondition.
