### S8 Depends omits the OrdinalShift operator it is defined in terms of
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: OrdinalShift (and OrdinalDisplacement), ASN-0034
**ASN**: S8 (CorrespondenceRunPartition). The per-claim *Depends:* lists only `S2, S3, S8a, S8-depth, S8-fin, OrdShiftHom, TS2, TS3, TS4, T1`. But S8's own *Definition* uses the shift operator directly: "a *correspondence run* is a triple (v, a, n) … such that for every k … `shift(v, k) ∈ dom(M(d))`, `M(d)(shift(v, k)) = shift(a, k)`," and `succ(v) = shift(v, 1)`. Meanwhile the Properties Introduced table states S8 is "theorem from … TS4, **OrdinalShift, OrdinalDisplacement** (ASN-0034)."
**Issue**: The `shift` operator appearing in S8's run definition and lockstep identity is grounded by OrdinalShift, which is absent from S8's Depends. OrdShiftHom/TS2/TS3/TS4 are *consumers* of shift, not its defining claim, so they do not supply the operator. The sibling shift-using claims follow the opposite convention: OrdShiftHom and ValidInsertionPosition both cite OrdinalShift directly ("supplies the shift operator and its expansion"). So S8 is the lone shift-using claim that leaves the operator ungrounded in its Depends, and its Depends contradicts the Properties Introduced table by exactly `{OrdinalShift, OrdinalDisplacement}`.
**What needs resolving**: Add OrdinalShift to S8's *Depends:* (supplying the `shift` operator used in the run definition, the lockstep successor, and the displacement identity), and reconcile OrdinalDisplacement between the table and the contract — either cite it in Depends or drop it from the table row, per whether S8 references `δ` directly (it does not appear to; it reaches `δ` only through OrdinalShift).
