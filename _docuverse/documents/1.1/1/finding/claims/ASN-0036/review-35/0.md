### Σ.C (ContentStore) has no Formal Contract block, unlike its sibling Σ.M(d)
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: T0 (CarrierSetDefinition, ASN-0034); Σ.M(d) (Arrangement)
**ASN**: Σ.C (ContentStore) — the entire claim is prose: "The *content store* `Σ.C : T ⇀ Val`: a partial function mapping Istream addresses to content values. `T` is the set of tumblers (ASN-0034); `Val`, its codomain, is an unspecified nonempty set … The domain `dom(Σ.C)` is the set of I-addresses at which content has been stored." There is no `*Formal Contract:*`, no `*Definition:*` slot, and no `*Depends:*`.
**Issue**: Σ.C is the only state-component declaration in the ASN without a Formal Contract. Its exact sibling Σ.M(d) (Arrangement) carries a full `*Formal Contract:*` with a `*Definition:*` (`Σ.M(d) : T ⇀ T`) and a `*Depends:*` citing T0 for `T`; the other definitional claims (Strand, V-sub, subspace) all have Definition slots. Yet Σ.C is cited as a grounding `*Depends:*` entry by at least ten claims (AX-2, S0, S1, S3, S4, S5, S7, S7a, S7b, S8), each of which says "Σ.C (ContentStore) — supplies the content store `Σ.C`, its domain `dom(Σ.C)`, …" or "its value codomain `Val`." Σ.C is in fact the sole grounding site for `Val` (S5's `w ∈ Val` is explicitly charged here). Under the ASN's own strict direct-citation discipline — which grounds even same-ASN symbols at their defining sites and obsessively distinguishes direct from transitive dependence everywhere else — a defining claim that grounds `T` and introduces `Val` but exposes no structured Definition or Depends is an inconsistency a downstream consumer (a formalization tool reading structured slots) hits: it cannot extract Σ.C's type `Σ.C : T ⇀ Val` or its dependence on T0 from any contract slot. Secondarily, Σ.C's prose attributes the arrangement type to S2 ("the arrangement declaration `Σ.M(d) : T ⇀ T` (S2)") when the declaration's defining site is Σ.M(d) (Arrangement); S2 only reads single-valuedness off that type.
**What needs resolving**: Give Σ.C a Formal Contract paralleling Σ.M(d): a `*Definition:*` slot stating `Σ.C : T ⇀ Val` with `dom(Σ.C)`/`Val` named (Val a primitive opaque codomain introduced here), and a `*Depends:*` citing T0 (CarrierSetDefinition, ASN-0034) for the carrier `T` at both domain and codomain-factor positions. Correct the analogy citation to point at Σ.M(d) (Arrangement) as the arrangement-type declaration site rather than S2.
