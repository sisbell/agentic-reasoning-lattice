### S8 narrative proof justifies depth preservation circularly via S8-depth
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: S8-depth (FixedDepthVPositions); OrdShiftHom frame (ultimately TA0)
**ASN**: Body proof of S8, "succ stays within a subspace" step: *"by OrdShiftHom (b) `shift(v, 1)` again satisfies S8a; **by S8-depth it shares `v`'s depth `m`**."* The formal-contract proof of the same claim replaces this with: *"its depth is fixed by OrdShiftHom's frame, `#shift(v, 1) = #v = m` … an unconditional property of shift **that does not presuppose `shift(v, 1) ∈ dom(M(d))`**, so we may invoke it here, before the lockstep link is known to land back in the domain."*
**Issue**: S8-depth is `(A d,u,w : u ∈ dom(M(d)) ∧ w ∈ dom(M(d)) ∧ subspace(u)=subspace(w) : #u=#w)`. To conclude `#shift(v,1) = #v` from it you must instantiate `w := shift(v,1)` and discharge `shift(v,1) ∈ dom(M(d))` — which is exactly the not-yet-established lockstep-link condition at this point in the proof. The body invokes S8-depth unconditionally (it even covers the case where `succ(v)` is *undefined*, i.e. `shift(v,1) ∉ dom(M(d))`, where S8-depth simply does not apply). The conclusion is true, but via shift's frame, not S8-depth. The formal contract recognizes this and routes through the frame; the body still carries the circular justification.
**What needs resolving**: Rewrite the body's depth step to derive `#shift(v,1) = #v` from shift's frame property (OrdShiftHom / TA0), matching the formal-contract proof; do not cite S8-depth there. While reconciling, settle whether S8 depends on S8-depth at all — the only depth-sensitive steps (this one and the injectivity step) are both grounded in shift's frame in the formal contract, so S8-depth's listed role in S8 may be vestigial.
