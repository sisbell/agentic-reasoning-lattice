### Constructed witness tumblers in D-CTG-depth and D-SEQ lack T0 carrier-membership grounding
**Class**: OBSERVE
**Effective class**: REVISE (classifier override)
**Classifier rationale**: With the artifact left as-is — D-CTG-depth and D-SEQ constructing witness `w` and instantiating D-CTG's `v ∈ T` quantifier without listing T0 — the carrier membership of `w` is ungrounded by the declared Depends, so the proof step is not justified by its dependencies and the artifact is incomplete without the fix.
**Foundation**: T0 (CarrierSetDefinition) — comprehension clause placing every finite ℕ-sequence of length ≥ 1 in `T`.
**ASN**: D-CTG-depth: *"For any natural number n > uⱼ₊₁, define w of length m by: wᵢ = uᵢ … wⱼ₊₁ = n … Then w has depth m … Distinct values of n yield distinct tumblers w … by T3."* D-SEQ Step 3 likewise: *"the tuple w = [1, 1, …, 1, k] satisfies subspace(w) = 1, #w = m …"*. Both invoke D-CTG to force `w ∈ V_1(d) ⊆ T`, and both call `w` a tumbler, but neither contract lists T0 — D-CTG-depth Depends `S8-depth, T1, S8a, D-CTG, T0(a), T3, S8-fin`; D-SEQ Depends `S8-depth, S8a, D-CTG-depth, D-MIN, T1, D-CTG, S8-fin`.
**Issue**: To instantiate D-CTG's inner quantifier (which ranges over `v ∈ T`) at the explicitly-constructed `w`, `w ∈ T` must be grounded — that is T0's comprehension. T0(a) (which *is* listed in D-CTG-depth) supplies only unbounded component values, not the carrier membership of the separately-built `w`; T3 supplies distinctness, not membership. The corpus convention is to cite T0 when constructing a tumbler from explicit components (OrdinalDisplacement, TumblerAdd both do); these two claims skip it. The membership is never actually in doubt, so this is completeness, not a soundness break.
**What needs resolving**: Add T0 (CarrierSetDefinition) to the Depends of D-CTG-depth and D-SEQ to ground the carrier membership of the constructed witness `w`, or cite the constructing axiom inline where `w` is introduced.
