### OrdShiftHom precondition mixes formal and informal domain language
**Class**: OBSERVE
**Foundation**: S8a (ArrangementDomainRestriction); OrdShiftHom formal contract
**ASN**: OrdShiftHom, Preconditions: *"`v` is a V-position with `#v = m ≥ 2`; `n ≥ 1`. For part (b), additionally `v` satisfies S8a (`zeros(v) = 0` and `vᵢ ≥ 1` for every `i`)."*
**Issue**: "V-position" in this specification carries a technical reading — element of `dom(Σ.M(d))` for some `d`. Under that reading, S8a holds automatically for any such element (by S8a's domain constraint `dom(Σ.M(d)) ⊆ {t ∈ T : zeros(t) = 0 ∧ #t ≥ 2}`), making the "additionally v satisfies S8a" clause for part (b) redundant. The proof's structure reveals a different intent: part (a) genuinely needs only `v ∈ T` with `#v = m ≥ 2`, and part (b) adds S8a as a real constraint — which only makes sense if "V-position" is being used informally to mean "tumbler with V-position shape" (depth ≥ 2), not "element of the arrangement domain." The two readings give inconsistent interpretations of what the "additionally" contributes.
**What needs resolving**: Replace the informal "V-position" label in the precondition with the explicit structural condition `v ∈ T` with `#v = m ≥ 2`, so that "additionally v satisfies S8a" for part (b) is unambiguously a genuine additional constraint rather than a repetition of what domain membership already implies. Alternatively, annotate that "V-position" is used informally here to mean the structural shape only.
