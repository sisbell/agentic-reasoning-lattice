### S7 narrative well-definedness invokes T10a.4 without its domain-membership premise
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline) — postcondition is "for every allocator `A ∈ 𝒯` and every `t ∈ dom(A)`, `t` is T4-valid"; requires `a ∈ dom(A)` to instantiate.
**ASN**: S7 main-body proof, Well-definedness: *"By S7b (element-level I-addresses), `zeros(a) = 3`, and by T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline, ASN-0034), `a` is T4-valid; hence T4's field-decomposition machinery applies to `a`."*
**Issue**: T10a.4 is keyed on allocator-domain membership, not on zero-count. The cited premises (S7b gives `zeros(a)=3`; T10a.4) do not connect — nothing here places `a` in any allocator's domain, so T10a.4 cannot be instantiated at `a`. The missing premise is S7a's `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯`. This is not a terseness gap: the S7 formal contract explicitly proves the step is non-trivial — *"`zeros(a) = 3` alone fixes the number of separators but says nothing about their adjacency or the boundary components, which are precisely the constraints T4 imposes"* — and routes through S7a to supply the membership. The narrative omits that route entirely, leaving an unjustified leap from `zeros=3` to T4-validity.
**What needs resolving**: The main-body S7 well-definedness step must establish `a ∈ dom(A_element) ⊆ 𝒯` from S7a *before* instantiating T10a.4 at `a`, matching the formal-contract derivation (S7a membership → T10a.4 → T4-validity), with S7b supplying `zeros(a)=3` only afterward to fix the element level.
