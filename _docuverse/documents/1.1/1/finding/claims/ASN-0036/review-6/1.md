### S7 narrative uniqueness misattributes "distinct tumblers" to S7d, skipping GlobalUniqueness
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: S7d (DocumentAllocationDiscipline) — states "distinct documents arise from distinct allocation *events*"; GlobalUniqueness — supplies the event→address injectivity (distinct events ⇒ distinct addresses).
**ASN**: S7 main-body proof, Uniqueness across documents: *"By S7d's postcondition, distinct documents have distinct document-level tumblers. By T3 (CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034), this distinctness is decidable by component-wise comparison. Therefore, for any `a₁, a₂ ∈ dom(Σ.C)` allocated under distinct documents: `origin(a₁) ≠ origin(a₂)`."*
**Issue**: S7d's postcondition is distinctness of *allocation events*, not of *tumblers*. The narrative asserts S7d directly yields distinct document-level tumblers — a conclusion S7d does not state. The passage from distinct events to distinct addresses is exactly GlobalUniqueness, which the narrative never invokes. The S7 formal contract identifies this precise gap: *"The second fact separates distinct documents only at the level of events; what it does not by itself supply is the passage from distinct events to distinct addresses — for two distinct allocation events could, a priori, still emit the same tumbler value. That passage is exactly GlobalUniqueness."* The narrative both skips a load-bearing step and misreads its cited dependency.
**What needs resolving**: The main-body S7 uniqueness step must lift S7d's event-distinctness to tumbler-distinctness via GlobalUniqueness (whose T10a precondition is discharged by S7d's "arising from an allocation event under T10a"), exactly as the formal contract does, rather than attributing tumbler-distinctness to S7d. S7's narrative `Depends`/citation set should then carry GlobalUniqueness in the uniqueness role.
