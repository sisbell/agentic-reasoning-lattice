### zeros's definer T4 (and NAT-card for |∅|=0) is mis-grounded in the positivity→zeros=0 step across three claims
**Class**: REVISE
**Foundation**: T4 (HierarchicalParsing, ASN-0034); NAT-card (NatFiniteSetCardinality, ASN-0034)
**ASN**: The "componentwise positivity ⟹ `zeros(·)=0`" derivation recurs in OrdShiftHom (b), D-CTG-depth, D-SEQ, and ValidInsertionPosition. OrdShiftHom grounds it fully — its Depends cite T4 ("supplies the symbol `zeros` and its zero-count definition ... part (b) unfolds ... back as `zeros(r) = 0`") and NAT-card ("supplies the empty-set cardinality `|∅| = 0` ... for the closing step of part (b)"). The other three diverge: D-CTG-depth's proof writes "(A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ #w : wᵢ > 0), **which is zeros(w) = 0**" but lists T4 only under *Forward References* (justified there solely by the post-proof ordinal commentary, "zero is a field separator") and omits NAT-card; D-SEQ writes "so **zeros(w) = 0**" with neither T4 nor NAT-card in Depends; ValidInsertionPosition writes "so **zeros(v) = 0** with componentwise positivity" and cites NAT-card but not T4.
**Issue**: Each of the three derived claims writes `zeros(·)` load-bearing in its proof/postcondition, so by the ASN's own convention (a symbol's defining claim goes in Depends wherever the symbol is written — followed by S8a, D-CTG, S7b, S7d, OrdShiftHom for exactly this symbol) T4 must be in Depends, not Forward References. "Forward References" signals "named but not depended upon," so a consumer loading only Depends finds `zeros` ungrounded in D-CTG-depth and D-SEQ. The `|∅| = 0` cardinality step that turns "no zero components" into `zeros = 0` likewise needs NAT-card (cited by OrdShiftHom) but is absent in both D-CTG-depth and D-SEQ.
**What needs resolving**: Add T4 to Depends for D-CTG-depth (a distinct entry for the proof's `zeros(w)` usage, kept separate from the existing commentary Forward Reference), D-SEQ, and ValidInsertionPosition; add NAT-card to D-CTG-depth and D-SEQ for the `|∅| = 0` step — bringing the three into line with OrdShiftHom's grounding of the identical derivation.
