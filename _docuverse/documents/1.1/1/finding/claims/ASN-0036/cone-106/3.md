**Class**: OBSERVE
**Effective class**: REVISE (classifier override)
**Classifier rationale**: With the phrase left as "only position strictly between," the artifact asserts something false in T1's unrestricted total order (depth-3 tumblers like [1,5,1] lie between the extremes), so the independence argument is ungrounded without the V_1(d) qualification.
