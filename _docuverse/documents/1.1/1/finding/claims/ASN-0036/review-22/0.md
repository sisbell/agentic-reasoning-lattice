### S7 no-two-zeros-adjacent: four-case walk re-derives a one-line gap fact
**Class**: OBSERVE
**Foundation**: T4 (HierarchicalParsing) field-segment constraint; the separator gap `p₂ − p₁ = #U(a) + 1` is already computed in S7's own zero-count step via NAT-addassoc + NAT-sub left-telescoping.
**ASN**: S7 Well-definedness, the no-adjacent-zeros conjunct: *"each of `i` and `i + 1` independently equals one of the two members `#N(a) + 1` or `X` — a four-case domain, three of whose assignments we must eliminate before the fourth survives"* — followed by per-case irreflexivity / trichotomy eliminations and the final gap contradiction.
**Issue**: By the time this conjunct is discharged, S7 has already pinned the zero-index set to exactly `{p₁, p₂}` with `p₁ < p₂` and `p₂ − p₁ = #U(a) + 1 ≥ 2`. The walk is correct but is a redundant case enumeration of a fact the gap bound gives directly.
**What needs resolving**: Discharge the no-adjacent-zeros conjunct directly from the established separator gap `p₂ − p₁ = #U(a) + 1 ≥ 2` (two consecutive indices differ by `1 < 2`), replacing the four-sub-case enumeration with the order-forced single case.
