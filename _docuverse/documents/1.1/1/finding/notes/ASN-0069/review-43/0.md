### Issue 1: V11 closing equality chain miscites V4 for a V4+V5 step
**ASN-0069, V11 derivation, closing equality chain**:

```
M^k(d^k_new)(v) at post-step-k = M^k(d^{k-1}_new)(v) at post-step-k     [V4 at step k]
                              = M^{k-1}(d^{k-1}_new)(v) at pre-step-k    [V5 across step k]
                              = M^{k-1}(d^{k-1}_new)(v) at post-step-(k-1) [premise at i=k, Stage 2]
                              = M(d_src)(v) at Σ                          [induction hypothesis]
```

The preceding narrative also states: "V4 takes v ∈ V_{s_C}(d^{k-1}_new) (pre-step-k) as input and concludes v ∈ dom(M^k(d^k_new)) at post-step-k with M^k(d^k_new)(v) = M^k(d^{k-1}_new)(v) at post-step-k."

**Problem**: V4's statement is `M'(d_new)(v) = M(d_src)(v)` — M' is post-state, M is pre-state. At step k this reads `M^k(d^k_new)(v) = M^{k-1}(d^{k-1}_new)(v)` (post = pre-of-source-at-step-k), not the cited `M^k(d^k_new)(v) = M^k(d^{k-1}_new)(v)` (post = post-of-source-at-step-k). The cited equality additionally requires V5 (source unchanged across step k: `M^k(d^{k-1}_new) = M^{k-1}(d^{k-1}_new)`). The combination V4+V5 is named V8 elsewhere in the ASN. Line 2 then re-cites V5 to bridge back to pre-state — but the V5 invocation has already been silently consumed by line 1. A reader checking "V4 at step k" against V4's actual statement will find the cited equality does not follow from V4 alone.

**Required**: Either (a) re-cite line 1 as `[V8 at step k]` or `[V4 + V5 at step k]` and remove line 2's V5 invocation as redundant, or (b) collapse lines 1+2 into a single V4 step that lands directly on the pre-state form: `M^k(d^k_new)(v) at post-step-k = M^{k-1}(d^{k-1}_new)(v) at pre-step-k    [V4 at step k]`, eliminating the intermediate `M^k(d^{k-1}_new)(v)` entirely. Update the preceding narrative paragraph in lockstep — the sentence "V4 ... concludes ... M^k(d^k_new)(v) = M^k(d^{k-1}_new)(v) at post-step-k" needs to match whichever resolution is chosen.
