### Issue 1: The version-dominance argument omits documents allocated under a properly-extending sub-account
**ASN-0103, Effect One ("Strict advance over every prior address under A," subsequent case) and CND.monotone**: "With `Document(v)`, the 'v is a version' reading is licensed: reaching `zeros(v) = 2` from the account `A` (`zeros(A) = 1`) requires exactly one `k=2` descent ... since `#v > #A + 2` strictly exceeds the single-descent document length `#A+2`, at least one `k=1` fork must follow, so `v` is genuinely a version."

**Problem**: The inference "`#v > #A+2` ⟹ at least one `k=1` fork must follow" silently assumes the sole `k=2` descent is taken *off `A` itself* (giving the single-descent length `#A+2`). It is not. The restriction paragraph correctly excludes deeper *accounts* `[N,0,U,x,…]` because they have `zeros ≠ 2` — but it never considers a **document allocated under such a deeper account**. Let `A' = [A, x, …]` with `zeros(A') = 1` and `A ≺ A'`. Then `d' = inc(A', 2) = [A', 0, 1]` satisfies `Document(d')` (`zeros = 2`), `A ≼ d'`, and `#d' = #A'+2 > #A+2` — yet `d'` has **no `k=1` fork**; its single `k=2` descent is off `A'`, not `A`. Such a `d'` falls squarely inside the universal "any `Document(v)` entity with `A ≼ v` and `#v > #A+2`," but the argument's conclusion fails for it: the proof tries to derive `v_{#A+1} = 0`, whereas `d'_{#A+1} = x ≠ 0`. Concretely, writing `d = [A, 0, p]`, the two diverge first at position `#A+1` where `d` carries `0` and `d'` carries `x ≥ 1`, so `d < d'` (T1 case (i)) — `d` does **not** dominate `d'`, contradicting CND.monotone's universal "d strictly exceeds every document address ever baptised under A."

Ruling out such `d'` requires "no account properly extends `A`" — equivalently the account-allocator uniform-length property, which this ASN explicitly declares **out of scope** (freshness paragraph: "the out-of-scope account-allocator uniform-length property"). The proof neither invokes it nor derives it; the "at least one `k=1` fork must follow" step is exactly where the unestablished assumption is smuggled in. The same gap propagates to the first-case (`D_A = ∅`) vacuous argument, which likewise concludes every `A`-prefixed `Document(v)` is a version rooted in `D_A`.

**Required**: Reconcile claim and proof. Either (a) narrow CND.monotone to `A_doc(A) = S(A,2)` emissions and their version chains (`A_v(d_i)` for `d_i ∈ D_A`) — and correspondingly restrict the proof's universal quantifier away from "any `Document(v)` with `A ≼ v`," with an explicit note that freshness against sub-account documents is already discharged by length/divergence and so dominance over them is not needed for correctness; or (b) state the "no account properly extends `A`" property as an explicit precondition/assumption and cite it at the "at least one `k=1` fork must follow" step, acknowledging it is supplied by an out-of-scope result. As written, the universal dominance claim is not established.
