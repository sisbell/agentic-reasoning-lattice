### Issue 1: Cross-chain distinctness re-derives a foundation guarantee
**ASN-0103, Effect One (Freshness and distinctness)**: "we must show `d` differs from every document-level address (`zeros = 2`) not on `S(A, 2)`, and this requires first establishing that every such address lies on *some* SiblingStream other than `S(A, 2)` — an exhaustiveness fact that the B7 discharge presupposes but does not itself supply. We derive it."

**Problem**: The entire paragraph — the ActivatedEmission/AllocatorHierarchy taxonomy walk, the per-type B6-validity checks, and the single B7 instantiation, plus the "future baptisms" extension — re-establishes what GlobalUniqueness (ASN-0034, foundation) already delivers in one citation: "No two distinct allocation events ever produce the same address... each address belongs to exactly one allocator's domain." `d`'s allocation event is distinct from every other document's, so `d` differs from every other document address (present or future, same-chain or cross-chain) directly. The ASN even cites B8 for this in CND.monotone, yet performs the manual derivation in prose. This is the anti-bloat target: a long exhaustiveness derivation replaceable by a foundation citation.

**Required**: Replace the cross-chain exhaustiveness derivation with a citation to GlobalUniqueness (ASN-0034). Keep only the same-chain injectivity note (S0) if a sharper local statement is wanted. The "present and future" framing then needs no bespoke machinery.
