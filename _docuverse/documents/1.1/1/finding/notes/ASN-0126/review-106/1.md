### Issue 2: `Nullify_Binary` has no operation contract
**ASN-0126, Retraction as an attributed Binary**: "The *operation set* an app invokes refines ASN-0086's `{Emit_K, Observe_K, Nullify}`, in step with the transition relation, to `{Emit_K, Observe_K, Nullify_Binary}`."
**Problem**: ASN-0086 gives each of its three operations an explicit precondition/effect/frame block; this note's replacement operation gets only an inline `≡`-definition with its guarantees scattered across two long paragraphs. Missing from any single place: the precondition set (P0 `d_retr ∈ dom(Σ.M)`; *R registered Binary* — without which the call has no `→_sh` image, a failure mode ASN-0086's `Nullify` could not have and which is never stated as a precondition); the effect (deposit of `({r}, {(a, δ(1, #a))}, R)` at `a_emit(Σ, d_retr)`); the frame; and the postconditions — in particular that `a ∈ nullified(Σ')` holds iff `a ∈ A_rel^{Σ'}` (a ghost target is *not itself* nullified; only the link addresses under it are, which is exactly the sterilization case), and that single-tuple scope holds iff P-tgt, demoted to "app obligation" but never collected into the operation's spec. An implementer of the app-facing surface currently has to reassemble the contract from prose.
**Required**: A contract block for `Nullify_Binary` with the same discipline as ASN-0086's operations: preconditions, effect, frame, the conditional single-tuple-scope guarantee (iff P-tgt), and the nullified-set effect for non-P-tgt targets.
