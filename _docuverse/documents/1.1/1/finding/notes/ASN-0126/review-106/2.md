### Issue 3: The frontier-landing of raw `K.λ_sh` deposits is asserted, not derived
**ASN-0126, The shape-gated emit**: "The refinement is confined to the guard: `K.λ_sh` adds only preconditions to `K.λ`, so its C/M/L effect, its fresh emission address `a_emit(Σ, d)`, and its C/M/L frame are identical to `K.λ`'s" — relied on by Range sterilization (ii): "No emission homed at `d` can route around the block — every one, of every registered type, lands at the frontier (FrontierUnification)."
**Problem**: ASN-0086's StateTransition contract, as cited, guarantees only that a K.λ-step extends `dom(Σ.L)` "at a fresh key" — freshness, not chain position. The pinning of *every* K.λ deposit (not just `Emit_K`-mediated ones) to `a_emit` is what makes RangeSterilization (ii)'s no-routing-around claim true; if a raw `K.λ_sh` step could deposit at any fresh conforming address, an app could skip past the block and the exhaustion count `k` would fail. The pinning is derivable — pre- and post-state both satisfy L-ContiguousPrefix (B2-transferred), the deposit grows exactly one home's homed-set by one element, and a one-element extension of a contiguous initial segment that remains a contiguous initial segment must add the frontier slot — but this derivation is nowhere in the note; effect-identity simply attributes the pinned address to `K.λ`'s contract.
**Required**: One sentence deriving frontier-landing of every `K.λ`/`K.λ_sh` deposit from L-ContiguousPrefix at the pre- and post-state (or an explicit citation of the contract clause that pins it), placed where effect-identity or FrontierUnification can invoke it.
