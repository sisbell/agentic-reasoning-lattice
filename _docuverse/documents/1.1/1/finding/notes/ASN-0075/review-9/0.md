### Issue 1: Notational typo in S7 formula citation
**ASN-0075, D-ACT (Actionability), "Longer length" sub-case of the T1-consecutiveness argument**:
> "By S7 (ASN-0036), `origin(t) = N.0.U.0.M = d` (the three zeros of `t` are exactly those of `d.0.s_C`, so T4b's element-field projection truncates to give the same document-level prefix as `a`)."

**Problem**: S7 of ASN-0036 defines `origin(a) = N(a).0.U(a).0.D(a)`, using `D` for the document-level field — not `M`. Throughout this ASN and the foundation, `M` is the arrangement function symbol (`Σ.M(d) : T ⇀ T`), never a tumbler-projection. The reader following the citation to S7 finds no `M`-projection and must mentally substitute `D`. The clash with the established meaning of `M` (arrangement function) makes the substitution non-obvious — for a moment a reader can plausibly wonder whether `M` is shorthand for something else introduced earlier.

**Required**: Replace `N.0.U.0.M` with `N.0.U.0.D` so the formula matches S7's definition character-for-character.
