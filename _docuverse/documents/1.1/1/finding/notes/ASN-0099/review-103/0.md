### Issue 1: Claims Introduced table carries proof-method inventory in a structural slot
**ASN-0099, "Claims Introduced" table**: several entries append derivation methods to the statement/status slot:
- F21 — "...composing `image` with ASN-0098's LP12a"
- F22 — "...(via range invariance + PerLinkInvariance)"
- F23 — "...(via wp-composition + LP9 + wp postcondition-monotonicity)"
- F15 — "...(predicates consult only `Σ.L`/query-data and are closed under intersection with `S`)"
- F19 — "...(filtered/scoped instances under F15(c))"
- F10 — "...(filtered and scoped forms inherit the same finiteness + total-order argument)"

**Problem**: The summary table's role is to state each claim and its status. The foundation ASN tables (e.g., the TA5 table in ASN-0034) carry only Label/Statement/Status — never the proof chain. Here the parentheticals are a proof-method inventory ("via X + Y + Z"), exactly the accretion pattern the anti-bloat pass targets: method inventory relocated into a structural slot. A reader scanning the table for *what* F23 claims has to skip past *how* it is proved. The derivation already lives in the F23 body; the table copy adds nothing and will drift as the proofs are revised.

**Required**: Reduce these entries to the claim statement and status, matching the foundation table convention. Drop the "via …"/"composing …" method annotations; the body sections carry the derivations. (F4's parenthetical naming the alternative designs it individuates is claim content, not method, and may stay.)
