### Issue 1: Query 10 step (iv) — K.σ does not establish d_c ∈ E_doc
**ASN-0099, Worked Example, Query 10, Step (iv)**: "K.ρ (ProvenanceRecording, ASN-0047) records (α_c, d_c) ∈ R, satisfying K.ρ's precondition α_c ∈ dom(Σ_3.C) and d_c ∈ Σ_3.E_doc (the latter via K.σ at step i; reading d_c ∈ dom(M) as d_c ∈ E_doc under the ASN-0093 ↔ ASN-0047 vocabulary correspondence)."

**Problem**: K.σ (ASN-0093) operates on substrate state (C, L, M); its effect adds d_c to dom(M) and its frame names L' = L. K.σ does not modify E (and ASN-0093 has no E component). When the worked example operates in ASN-0047's extended state — which it must, to invoke K.ρ, whose precondition requires d ∈ E_doc and whose effect modifies R ⊆ T_elem × E_doc — K.σ alone does not establish d_c ∈ E_doc. The parenthetical "vocabulary correspondence" is asserted but not derived: ASN-0099 supplies no claim lifting d ∈ dom(M) to d ∈ E_doc, the foundation specs do not either, and the two ASNs use materially different state vocabularies (ASN-0093: (C, L, M); ASN-0047: (C, L, E, M, R)). K.ρ's precondition is therefore unsatisfied at step (iv) as written.

The label "Verifying F9★-cor across V ∖ {K.λ}" positions Query 10 as load-bearing verification rather than mere illustration, so the gap is operationally relevant to the worked example's evidentiary role. The same vocabulary issue surfaces (less critically) at K.α step (ii), which also names ASN-0093 K.α even though the worked example operates against the extended state.

**Required**: Either (a) replace K.σ at step (i) with K.δ-IsDocument (K.δ in ASN-0047 case (ii) at k = 2 from an existing account in E), whose effect adds d_c to E_doc and initializes M(d_c) = ∅ simultaneously, with the account precondition discharged from the worked-example setup or noted as assumed; or (b) explicitly fix the state model (ASN-0093 substrate vs. ASN-0047 extended) ASN-0099 operates in, and supply a derivation that bridges K.σ's M-effect to E_doc membership in that model — either by promoting K.σ to the extended state with an explicit E_doc effect, or by appealing to an invariant tying dom(M) to E_doc. The same fix should be applied at K.α step (ii).
