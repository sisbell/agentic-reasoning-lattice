### Issue 1: The "tight" predicate is unachievable for any non-empty endset, making LP19a and LP19 vacuous
**ASN-0098, "Boundary and Width Behaviour"**:
> "tight(e, Σ_e) ≡ coverage(e) ⊆ dom(Σ_e.C) ∪ dom(Σ_e.L)"
> "Equivalently: every I-address denoted by an endset span is allocated as content or as a link at Σ_e."

**Problem**: The right-hand side coverage(e) is infinite for every non-empty endset, while dom(Σ_e.C) ∪ dom(Σ_e.L) is finite (by C-fin and L-fin of ASN-0093). Hence the containment can hold only when coverage(e) = ∅, equivalently e = ∅.

**Justification — coverage is infinite for any non-empty endset.** Take any well-formed span (s, ℓ) in e and consider its zero-extension `s.0 = [s_1, ..., s_{#s}, 0]`.

(a) s.0 ∈ T (finite ℕ-sequence of length #s + 1 ≥ 1, per T0).

(b) s < s.0 by T1 case (ii) (s is a proper prefix of s.0).

(c) s.0 < s ⊕ ℓ: let k = actionPoint(ℓ) ≤ #s. By TumblerAdd, (s ⊕ ℓ)_i = s_i for i < k, (s ⊕ ℓ)_k = s_k + ℓ_k with ℓ_k ≥ 1, while s.0_i = s_i for i ≤ #s. At divergence position k, s.0_k = s_k < s_k + ℓ_k = (s ⊕ ℓ)_k, so s.0 < s ⊕ ℓ by T1 case (i).

Therefore s.0 ∈ span(s, ℓ) ⊆ coverage(e). The same argument places s.0.0, s.0.0.0, ... in coverage(e) (each strictly between s and s ⊕ ℓ), so coverage(e) is infinite.

**Justification — these elements cannot inhabit dom(C) ∪ dom(L).** s.0 has its last component equal to 0, violating T4's `t_{#t} ≠ 0` clause. So s.0 is not T4-valid. By T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline) together with C1c/L1c (allocator conformance, ASN-0093), every element of dom(C) ∪ dom(L) is T4-valid. So s.0 ∉ dom(C) ∪ dom(L).

**Consequences**:
- LP19a applies only to e = ∅: in that case coverage(∅) = ∅ and a_new ∉ ∅ holds trivially. The lemma carries no information about non-empty endsets.
- LP19 applies only to e = ∅: project(∅, d, Σ') = ∅ vacuously, by the projection definition over an empty coverage.

The intended architectural guarantee — "an endset constructed to refer to specific allocated content does not absorb future K.α/K.λ allocations" — is not captured. The narrative prose around LP19 ("the canonical endset construction... produces tight endsets") describes a property of span endpoint placement, but the formal `tight` predicate as written is satisfied only by the empty endset.

**Required**: Reformulate `tight` against an achievable class of endsets. Two viable directions: (a) characterize tightness through span endpoint placement relative to the allocator frontier (e.g., for every (s, ℓ) ∈ e, s ⊕ ℓ ≤ inc(a_last, 0) where a_last is the last K.α/K.λ-emission in the relevant sub-allocator chain at Σ_e), leveraging the T10a sibling-recurrence discipline to bound future allocation locations; or (b) restrict the containment to the T4-valid subset of coverage and redirect LP19a's conclusion through that restricted set (and re-examine whether the restricted coverage is still finite in general — it likely is not, by the same zero-extension argument applied to T4-valid extensions like s.1, s.1.1, etc., when zeros(s) < 3). In either case, LP19a and LP19 must be re-proved against the revised definition.
