### Issue 2: Open Question 5 acknowledges the gap that LP19a/LP19 claim to close
**ASN-0098, "Open Questions"**:
> "What does the system guarantee about a link whose coverage spans I-addresses partially outside dom(Σ.C) at link-creation time — what must remain true if future K.α allocations fall within the previously unallocated portion of the coverage?"

**Problem**: This open question is exactly the case LP19a/LP19 attempt to address. The ASN both claims a formal lemma about boundary insertion and lists the underlying question as open. Per Issue 1 the lemma is vacuous, so the open question accurately captures what is actually unresolved — but the inconsistency between "LP19 formalises this" (in the prose) and "this is an open question" (in the closing section) should be resolved.

**Required**: After repairing the `tight` definition, either remove this open question (if LP19 now addresses it for the tight class), or scope the open question to the non-tight case (endsets whose coverage spans unallocated I-addresses *without* the tight construction discipline).
