### Issue 1: D8 omits ActivatedEmission from its invariant catalogue while claiming exhaustiveness
**ASN-0101, D8 statement**: "the post-state satisfies *every foundation invariant that the pre-state was required to satisfy*. The invariants partition into three groups..."

**ASN-0101, D8 Group (ii)**: "all of M0, S4, S7a, S7b, S7d, C1, C1b, C1c, C2, L0, L1, L1a, L1b, L1c, L3, L12, SD, L-fin, C-fin, NodeLineage ... hold trivially at the post-state because `C' = C`, `L' = L`, `E' = E`, and `dom(M') = dom(M)`."

**Problem**: ASN-0047's `ExtendedReachableStateInvariants` theorem enumerates the per-state invariants that every reachable state must satisfy. That conjunction includes **ActivatedEmission** — `(A e ∈ Σ.E : ¬Node(e) : (E A : Activated(A) ∧ EntityLevel(A) : e ∈ dom(A)))`. D8 catalogues NodeLineage (the adjacent E-predicating invariant in the same list) but never mentions ActivatedEmission, in Group (ii), Group (iii), the prose, or the summary table. Because D8 makes an explicit completeness claim ("every foundation invariant") and proceeds by exhaustive enumeration, the omission is a genuine gap in the enumeration, not a harmless elision. A reader auditing D8 against the ASN-0047 invariant list finds one required invariant unaccounted for.

**Required**: Add ActivatedEmission to the Group (ii) (or Group (iii)) catalogue with its one-line discharge. The discharge is trivial — ActivatedEmission predicates over `Σ.E` and the activation state of entity-level sub-allocators; DEL leaves `E' = E` and performs no entity-allocation event, so both the entity set and the activated-sub-allocator set are unchanged and the predicate transfers pointwise. But the clause must appear for the completeness claim to hold.
