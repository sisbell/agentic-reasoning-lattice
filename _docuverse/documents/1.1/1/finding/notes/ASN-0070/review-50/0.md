### Issue 1: F-subspace's main postcondition relies on S3★-aux but does not cite it
**ASN-0070, F-subspace (Depends)**: "S3★ (GeneralizedReferentialIntegrity, ASN-0047) — `subspace(v) = s_C ⟹ M(d)(v) ∈ dom(C)`, and `subspace(v) = s_L ⟹ M(d)(v) ∈ dom(L)`. L0 (SubspacePartition, ASN-0047) ... Composing the two implications yields the claim."

**Problem**: The postcondition is the equality `subspace(v) = subspace_I(M(d)(v))`. The two cited implications are conditional on `subspace(v) = s_C` and `subspace(v) = s_L` respectively. Composing them establishes the equality only on those two branches; to discharge the equality for an arbitrary `v ∈ dom(M(d))` you must first know `subspace(v) ∈ {s_C, s_L}`. That exhaustiveness is exactly S3★-aux (SubspaceExhaustiveness, ASN-0047) — it is not supplied by S3★ (which is purely conditional) or L0. Without it, `subspace(v) = v₁` is an arbitrary natural and neither implication need fire, so "composing the two implications yields the claim" is incomplete.

Note the inconsistency: the lemma's own *Consequence* derivation correctly invokes S3★-aux in its reverse directions ("by S3★-aux ... `subspace(v) ∈ {s_C, s_L}`"), but the main-postcondition derivation omits it, and S3★-aux is absent from the Depends list. This violates the per-step citation convention the rest of the note (and the foundation) holds to.

**Required**: Add S3★-aux to F-subspace's Depends, and amend the derivation of the main postcondition to make the case split explicit: by S3★-aux `subspace(v) ∈ {s_C, s_L}`; in each case the matching S3★ + L0 implication yields `subspace_I(M(d)(v)) = subspace(v)`.
