### Issue 1: Configuration 2's parenthetical is factually wrong and contradicts its own premise
**ASN-0070, A Worked Example — Second configuration:** "Suppose no depth-`m_a` member of subtree(b) lies in ran(M(d)) (this is the load-bearing precondition; `b ∉ ran(M(d))` alone would not suffice, since deeper-depth members of the coverage could still be reached)."

**Problem**: Two independent defects.

(a) The span is `(b, δ(1, m_a))`, so `coverage = subtree(b)` and, since `b ≼ t` with `#t = #b = m_a` forces `t = b`, the **unique** depth-`m_a` member of `subtree(b)` is `b` itself. The stated precondition "no depth-`m_a` member of subtree(b) lies in ran(M(d))" is therefore *exactly equivalent* to `b ∉ ran(M(d))` — not strictly stronger as the parenthetical claims.

(b) The justification "deeper-depth members of the coverage could still be reached" is excluded by the section's own P-depth premise, which states "The mapping-block I-extents are all depth-`m_a`." If every block I-extent is depth-`m_a`, no block can ever reach a deeper-depth coverage member. The very next sentence relies on this: "By P-depth, each block's intersection with coverage is empty (no depth-`m_a` coverage member is reached)." The parenthetical imagines a case P-depth rules out.

(The grain of truth — that `b ∉ ran` would be insufficient for a *wider* span — concerns *other same-depth* members `b+1, b+2, …`, not deeper-depth members. The caveat names the wrong mechanism and applies it to a width-1 span where it does not arise at all.)

**Required**: Delete the parenthetical, or replace it with the correct statement: for this width-1 span, `b` is the sole depth-`m_a` coverage member, so the precondition is just `b ∉ ran(M(d))`; under P-depth no deeper member is reachable.
