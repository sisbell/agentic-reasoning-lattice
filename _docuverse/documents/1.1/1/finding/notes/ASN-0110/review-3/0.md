### Issue 1: Two incompatible region models — RE-decide demands `I = coverage(Q)`, but the worked example and RE-Vside use explicit finite I-sets
**ASN-0110, "When does an endset touch a region?" (RE-decide) vs. the worked instance vs. RE-Vside**:
- Prose: "it must be *finitely presented*, supplied as a finite span-set `Q ∈ 𝒫_fin(Span)` whose denotation is `I = coverage(Q)` ... every realizability and termination claim leans on this finite presentation."
- Worked instance: "We query the **I-region** `I = {c₂, c₃}`."
- RE-Vside: `retrieveendsets_V(R, d, Σ) = retrieveendsets(image(R, d, Σ), Σ)`.

**Problem**: These are three different, non-interchangeable notions of "region," and the touching test gives different answers under each.

- `coverage(Q)` is always a union of half-open intervals `[s, s⊕ℓ)`, i.e. a union of *subtrees*. For unit spans at the depth-8 siblings of the example, `coverage({(c₂, δ(1,8))}) = [c₂, c₃)` contains `c₂` **and every deeper extension** `c₂.x`, `c₂.0.3`, … — an infinite set. It is **not** the two-element set `{c₂, c₃}`.
- There is no finite span-set `Q` with `coverage(Q) = {c₂, c₃}` (any interval covering `c₂` drags in its subtree). So the worked example's region is an *explicit finite I-set*, not a `coverage(Q)`, contradicting the stated model.
- `image(R, d, Σ) = {Σ.M(d)(v) : v ∈ R ∩ dom(Σ.M(d))}` is likewise a finite explicit set of I-addresses, generally **not** of the form `coverage(Q)`. An endset whose coverage includes a deeper extension `α.5` but not `α` itself touches the subtree-region but not the explicit `{α}` — so the two models disagree on membership of the result.

Consequently RE-decide's decidability/termination argument (stated only for `I = coverage(Q)`, reduced to per-span-pair interval overlap) does **not** cover the V-side input or the worked example's input, so realizability/termination of `retrieveendsets_V` is never actually established, and the worked example silently computes under a model the ASN's own definition forbids.

**Required**: Pick one region model and use it consistently. The natural choice is an arbitrary *finite explicit I-set* `I ⊆ T` (the actual output of `image`, and what the worked example uses): decidability is then trivial — `coverage(e) ∩ I ≠ ∅` reduces to finitely many per-address membership tests `s ≤ α < s⊕ℓ`. If the span-set/`coverage(Q)` model is genuinely intended, then RE-Vside must be reconciled (it cannot in general produce a `coverage(Q)` region) and the worked example redone. Either way, RE-decide and the opening "the region ... must be supplied as a finite span-set `Q`" paragraph must be brought into agreement with RE-Vside and the example.
