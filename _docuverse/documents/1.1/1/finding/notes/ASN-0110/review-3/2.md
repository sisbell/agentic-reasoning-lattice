### Issue 3: Worked-instance coverage values are stated as singletons when they are subtrees
**ASN-0110, worked instance**: "`F₁ = {(c₂, δ(1,8)), (c₄, δ(1,8))}` coverage `{c₂, c₄}`".

**Problem**: `coverage(F₁) = [c₂, c₃) ∪ [c₄, c₅)`, which includes all deeper extensions of `c₂` and `c₄`, not the two singletons. Only the *intersection with the explicit region* reduces to `{c₂}`. Writing `coverage {c₂, c₄}` conflates the (infinite) coverage with its intersection against the query and reinforces the Issue-1 model confusion.

**Required**: State coverage as the subtree intervals (or label the column "addresses of `I` covered"), so the example does not silently assume the explicit-set model while the definitions claim the `coverage(Q)` model.
