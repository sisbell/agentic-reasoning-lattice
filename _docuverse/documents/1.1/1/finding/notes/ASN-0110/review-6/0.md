### Issue 1: V-side reduction relies on `image` being finite, but never establishes it
**ASN-0110, RE-Vside / RE-decide**: "the V-side reduction below (RE-Vside) produces such a finite I-set through `image`" and RE-Vside: "`retrieveendsets_V(R, d, Σ) = retrieveendsets(image(R, d, Σ), Σ)`".

**Problem**: The entire realizability/termination chain (RE-decide) is premised on the region being a *finite* explicit set `I ∈ 𝒫_fin(T)`. RE-Vside feeds `image(R, d, Σ)` into the I-side operation, but `R` is unconstrained — it may be an infinite V-region. The ASN asserts the resulting I-set is finite ("produces such a finite I-set") yet never discharges this. Finiteness does not come from `R`; it comes from `dom(Σ.M(d))` being finite, so that `image(R, d, Σ) ⊆ ran(Σ.M(d))` is finite regardless of `R`. This is the load-bearing fact that lets the V-side query satisfy RE-decide's precondition, and it is stated without justification or citation. RE-decide itself lists "produced by the V-side `image`" as a source of finite `I` but offers no reason it is finite.

**Required**: In RE-Vside (or RE-decide), cite the foundation guarantee that `dom(Σ.M(d))` is finite (S8-fin, ASN-0036; cf. C-fin/S8-fin, ASN-0093) and derive `image(R, d, Σ) ⊆ ran(Σ.M(d))` finite, so that the I-side precondition `image(R, d, Σ) ∈ 𝒫_fin(T)` is met for arbitrary (even infinite) `R`. One line closes the gap; without it the reduction's realizability is asserted, not shown.
