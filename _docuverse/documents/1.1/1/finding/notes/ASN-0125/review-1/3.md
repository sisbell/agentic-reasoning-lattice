### Issue 4: EL13 conflates "per-asserter" with "per-home"
**ASN-0125, EL13 (TemporalErasure)**: "Within one home the opposite holds: the chain enumeration is strictly increasing (T9; ChainEnumerationInjectivity, ASN-0093), so one asserter's claims are totally ordered by their addresses — *per-asserter* 'latest' is well-defined and state-recoverable."
**Problem**: What the chain enumeration orders is the claims homed at one *document* — one `A_L(d)` chain. Asserters are principals, and a principal's ownership domain spans many documents (account-tier prefix and `odom`, ASN-0042 — the very framework EL8(b) invokes to resolve home → owner). Two claims by the same principal homed at two of their documents fall squarely under EL13's own commutation argument: their order is not a function of the state. So per-asserter "latest" is not state-recoverable in general; per-home "latest" is. The conflation recurs in the claims table ("'latest' is per-asserter only") and in EL14(d)'s example reader policy ("follow per-asserter latest").
**Required**: Replace "per-asserter" with "per-home" (per-document-chain) in EL13, the claims-table row, and EL14(d). If a per-asserter notion is wanted, state explicitly that it is well-defined only under the added assumption that the asserter homes all their claims at a single document — or present it as the approximation a reader policy may choose, not as a state-recoverable fact.
