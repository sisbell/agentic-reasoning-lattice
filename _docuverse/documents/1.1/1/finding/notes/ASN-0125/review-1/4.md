### Topic 1: Formal bridge from this ASN's state to ASN-0042's principal/registry model
**Why out of scope**: EL8(b) gestures from `home(addr(e))` to an effective owner "by prefix (ASN-0042)," but ASN-0042's `ω_Σ` is defined over a state with a baptismal registry and principal set that `(C, L, E, M, R)` does not carry. The attribution this ASN actually needs — home computable and decidable from the address alone — is fully discharged by T4b/T6 within its own state; constructing the cross-model identification (link addresses as registry members with effective owners) is integration work for a future ASN, not an error here.
