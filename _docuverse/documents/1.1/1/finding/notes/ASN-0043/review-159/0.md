### Issue 1: Over-justified existence of a third subspace identifier in the L9 witness
**ASN-0043, L9 (TypeGhostPermission), "Witness"**: "Choose a subspace identifier `s_X ∈ ℕ` with `s_X ≥ 1`, `s_X ≠ s_C`, and `s_X ≠ s_L` (such `s_X` exists because ℕ contains more than two values `≥ 1`: NAT-closure's successor closure yields the three distinct naturals `1`, `1 + 1`, `1 + 1 + 1`, all `≥ 1`, distinct by NAT-addcompat's `n < n + 1` together with NAT-order's irreflexivity and transitivity; at least one of the three avoids the two fixed constants `s_C`, `s_L`)."

**Problem**: This is a fully-cited existence proof for the triviality "a third natural number distinct from two constants exists," invoking three foundation axioms (NAT-closure, NAT-addcompat, NAT-order) plus a pigeonhole step. The conclusion is used (a fresh subspace identifier is picked), but the chain of foundation citations does not advance the L9 argument — a reader following the witness construction skips it to reach the load-bearing step (construction of `g`). This is the accreted defensive-justification pattern the anti-bloat classifier flags: a parenthetical that proves something no precise reader doubts, dressed in per-step foundation citations.

**Required**: Replace the parenthetical with a single clause (e.g., "such `s_X` exists since ℕ is unbounded and `s_C`, `s_L` are only two values"), or drop the justification entirely. Reserve the per-step NAT citations for steps where the arithmetic fact is genuinely load-bearing.
