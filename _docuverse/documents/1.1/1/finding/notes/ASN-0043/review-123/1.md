### Issue 2: Defensive scope meta-prose and a redundant restatement accreted around the L11a shared-home rework
**ASN-0043, L11a — LinkUniqueness, *Shared home* sub-case**: "A link address has element field beginning with the link-subspace identifier `s_L` and depth `#E ≥ 2` by L1b; we make no claim that `#E = 2` (L1b admits deeper element fields, and no invariant of this ASN caps them — Gregory's depth-2 placement is implementation evidence, not an abstract bound)." … "which is all GlobalUniqueness's single-system precondition requires; we need not — and do not — claim sibling-of-one-allocator structure for arbitrary `#E`."

**Problem**: Both parentheticals justify the *scope* of the claim ("we make no claim", "we need not — and do not — claim", "implementation evidence, not an abstract bound") rather than advancing the argument. This is reviser drift: the prose reads as residue from relaxing the proof to arbitrary `#E` (commit `cc376fb0d`), defending against a prior over-claim instead of stating positively how the proof handles deeper fields. Separately, the closing paragraph — "In both cases `a₁` and `a₂` arise as distinct allocation events within the one tree 𝒯, discharging GlobalUniqueness's single-system precondition even when the two links are homed in different documents" — restates the conclusion already reached by the *Distinct homes* and *Shared home* sub-cases in different words.

**Required**: Replace the defensive parentheticals with the positive structural statement (the proof traces shared edges and the terminus is a descendant of the shared link-ordinal allocator, whatever the depth); delete the redundant closing restatement.
