**Problem**: The conclusion is drawn directly from S8's partition claim *stated over V-extents* (`dom(M(d))` is the disjoint union of the `V(βⱼ)`), which immediately gives B1 (existence half) and B2 (uniqueness half) by the meaning of "disjoint union." S8 (ASN-0036) already asserts the partition in terms of the runs' V-extents `{shift(vⱼ, k) : 0 ≤ k < nⱼ} = V(βⱼ)`; the only gap is the notational identity `V(βⱼ) = {vⱼ + k} = {shift(vⱼ, k)}` (OrdinalShiftBase, including the `k = 0` case), which is a one-line convention unfolding, not an interval/M-int argument. The interval machinery is dead reasoning that the precise reader must skip past to reach the actual derivation.

**Required**: Either (a) delete the V-extent translation paragraphs and derive B1 ∧ B2 directly from S8's `⊎ⱼ V(βⱼ) = dom(M(d))` via the OrdinalShiftBase notational identity; or (b) if the interval form is genuinely needed downstream, cite where it is consumed.
