### Issue 2: A6 is a single monster paragraph carrying defensive justification of its own package composition
**ASN-0134, A6**: "The two registry *stability* invariants ... are deliberately *not* conjuncts of this package"; "The stack's append-only monotonicity ... are, all three of ASN-0093, deliberately *not* in this package"; and "Nor is ASN-0093's `M1` recoverable from the per-state package: that package's `C2`/`L1a` forbid removing a document that still *hosts* content, but say nothing against removing a *childless* one, so document permanence is genuinely independent."
**Problem**: Two distinct problems. (a) Most of A6's bulk explains *why certain invariants are excluded* from the package rather than stating what canonicity *is* — the "deliberately not" / "must be carried in its own right" passages are package-composition apologetics, the flagged "prose around an axiom explaining why it is needed rather than what it says" pattern. (b) The childless-document justification imagines a case the invariant being defended (M1) already excludes: *no `→_sh` step removes any document*, childless or not, so "removing a childless one" is unreachable. The argument is a metalogical independence claim wearing the costume of a reachable scenario.
**Required**: Reduce A6 to its definitional content — name the per-state package conjuncts and the companion transition clause — and move the logical-independence remarks (P2/R2 as corollaries, M1 not derivable) to at most one sentence, without the imagined childless-document removal. Break the paragraph; a definition this central should not be one unscannable block.
