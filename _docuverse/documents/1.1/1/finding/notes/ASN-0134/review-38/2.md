### Issue 3: Post-proof design-intent essays that self-admittedly add no derivation
**ASN-0134, W0 (and A6, V0)**: "W0 is Nelson's deepest point about permanence; **the proof stands in the claim above, and his reasoning supplies its design-intent motivation (not its derivation)** ..."
**Problem**: The note here confesses the paragraph is non-derivational, yet it sits in the structural slot immediately after the claim, so the precise reader must skip it to reach the next argument. The same essay-after-proof appears at A6 ("A6 is the formal reading of Nelson's 'canonical order is an internal mandate of the system'...") and V0 ("V0 is Nelson's answer made precise for the single-read verdict, and his framing is the right intuition..."). Design-intent grounding is legitimate for this corpus, but it has accreted to roughly one motivational essay per claim, and these are interleaved with the proofs rather than separated from them.
**Required**: Adopt a single convention for design intent — either one trailing sentence per claim, or a dedicated motivation subsection — so the formal chain reads without interruption. The Nelson/Gregory grounding need not be deleted, only kept out of the slot where the next proof step is expected.
