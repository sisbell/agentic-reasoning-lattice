### Issue 3: "A critical section buys only contiguity, not atomicity" is argued in both §1 and §6
**ASN-0134, §1 (post-A5) and §6 (post-W2)**: §1 — "Contiguity does **not** construct atomicity... What a critical section buys, and all it buys, is contiguity." §6 — "Contiguity is all W2 buys: it keeps the run an unbroken interval of `A_{s_C}(d)` — the writer-side half of read-atomicity, and no more (A5)."
**Problem**: The same conclusion — a (d,s_C) critical section secures contiguity but not reader-atomicity — is established at length in §1 (around A5) and re-established at §6 (around W2). The §1 passage already walks through "even a perfectly contiguous content run *passes through* every interior state... a partial batch *structurally*"; §6 repeats the point. One site should carry the argument and the other cite it.
**Required**: Make §6's W2 the carrier of the contiguity-vs-atomicity scoping and reduce §1's post-A5 elaboration to introducing the two modes of non-atomicity, citing §6/W2 for the "contiguity ≠ atomicity" conclusion (or vice versa).
