### Issue 4: Empty-document and empty-region boundaries are unstated, and σ_full carries an implicit nonemptiness precondition
**ASN-0122, "State, Instances, and Spec-Sets"**: "Whole-document comparison is not a separate notion. When `V_{s_C}(d) ≠ ∅`, D-SEQ★ gives … `σ_full = ([s_C, 1, …, 1], δ(n, m))` … and nothing we prove about windows needs a whole-document special case."
**Problem**: `δ(n, m)` requires `n ≥ 1`, so `σ_full` does not exist for an empty document — "whole-document comparison is not a separate notion" silently excludes exactly the case where the document arranges nothing, and the ASN never says what the whole-document operand is then (the empty span-set is the only witness). More broadly, the mandatory boundary cases are nowhere stated: an empty spec-set (`j = 0`), a span-set clipping to the empty region, and one or both regions empty. They all follow from the definitions (`corr = ∅`, X11 partitions the empty relation vacuously, `CANON = ⟨⟩` conforms), but a specification must state its empty cases, not leave the reader to verify that the maximal-chain machinery degenerates correctly.
**Required**: One paragraph: the whole-document operand for an empty document is `(d, ∅)`; empty region(s) yield `corr = ∅` and the canonical report `⟨⟩`; X11's existence/uniqueness holds vacuously.
