### Issue 5: No concrete worked example
**ASN-0122, whole document**: No key postcondition is verified against a specific scenario. The implementation-observations section is qualitative; the only computation with explicit tumblers is the `σ_full` clipping check.
**Problem**: The ASN's hardest machinery — fan-out under repetition (X8(b), X11's tie-break), window clipping of a maximal pair (X4c), and canonical ordering — is never exercised on data. Per the depth standard, postconditions without a concrete verification are a REVISE item, and this operation is precisely where an example would catch off-by-one and ordering errors.
**Required**: One worked example with explicit arrangements, e.g. `M(d₁): [1,1]↦a, [1,2]↦b, [1,3]↦c, [1,4]↦b` and `M(d₂): [1,1]↦b, [1,2]↦c`: compute `corr` (five elements, including the fan-out from the repeated `b`), the maximal pairs (`(d₁,[1,2]; d₂,[1,1]; 2)` plus the two width-1 pairs from `[1,4]`), the canonical order including the tie-break, then verify X4 on a window and X8(c) on disjoint windows of `d₁` (which must surface the `b`–`b` internal sharing).
