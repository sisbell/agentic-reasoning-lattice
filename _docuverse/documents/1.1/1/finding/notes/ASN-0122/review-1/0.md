### Issue 1: X2's reachability construction is not assembled from valid composites
**ASN-0122, "What 'Correspond' Must Mean," X2 (CoincidenceExclusion)**: "let two allocation events deposit the same `v ∈ Val` at `a₁` and `a₂`. … Arrange each by K.μ⁺ (its precondition `a_i ∈ dom(C)` holds) in documents `d₁, d₂`."
**Problem**: The claim is "There are reachable states…", and in the foundation's transition model a state is reachable only via elementary transitions drawn from *valid composites*. A composite that allocates `a_i` (K.α) and installs it into the content-subspace range of `M(d_i)` (K.μ⁺) is valid only if it also records provenance: J1★ demands `(a_i, d_i) ∈ R'` for every range-new content address, and J0 demands the allocation and arrangement co-occur within one composite. The construction as written names K.α and K.μ⁺ only; the sketched sequence violates J1★ and therefore does not witness a reachable state.
**Required**: Package each allocation explicitly as a valid composite K.α + K.μ⁺ + K.ρ (noting `d₁, d₂` pre-exist or are created by K.δ), and conclude reachability from composite validity. One or two sentences suffice; the conclusion of X2 is unaffected.
