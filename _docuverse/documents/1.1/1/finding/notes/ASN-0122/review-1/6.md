### Issue 7: X10(a) cites TS2 for distinctness across shift amounts
**ASN-0122, "The Pair," X10(a)**: "Both feet sets `{u + k : 0 ≤ k < n}` and `{w + k : 0 ≤ k < n}` have cardinality exactly `n`, since shift is injective and strictly increasing (TS2, TS4)."
**Problem**: TS2 is injectivity in the *base* at a fixed amount (`shift(v₁,n) = shift(v₂,n) ⟹ v₁ = v₂`); it does not deliver `k₁ ≠ k₂ ⟹ u + k₁ ≠ u + k₂`. Distinctness across amounts needs strict monotonicity in the amount: for `1 ≤ k₁ < k₂`, `u + k₂ = shift(shift(u, k₁), k₂ − k₁) > shift(u, k₁)` by TS3 then TS4 (or TS5 directly), with the `k₁ = 0` case by TS4 alone. In a series that prides itself on per-step citation discharge, the wrong lemma is a defect.
**Required**: Replace the citation with TS3+TS4 (or TS5, plus TS4 for the `k = 0` base).
