### Issue 6: X6(b)'s composition premises are unstated, and X7's wp formulas are silently one-sided
**ASN-0122, "Stability," X6(b)–(c) and X7(i)–(ii)**: "Steps compose: after `k` steps the composite `φ_k ∘ … ∘ φ₁` is again injective and res-preserving, so the endpoints correspond exactly on the transported material, with a bound independent of `k`"; "wp(K.μ⁻[d₁, n′], (p, q) ∈ corr) ≡ enabled ∧ p surviving ∧ (p, q) ∈ corr"; "corr_{Σ′} = corr_Σ ∩ (Surv × Q)".
**Problem**: (a) The endpoint conclusion of X6(b) needs premises the one-line proof omits: `d⁰`'s restriction must persist from the chain's start to the evaluation state (each step's frame gives this only in the pure-chain scenario), and edits to an intermediate `d^i` *between* its incoming and outgoing steps are absorbed only by composing that edit's X7 position map into `ψ` — which X6 never says. Since (c) advertises that intermediates "may meanwhile be rearranged, contracted to nothing," the reader is invited to interleave edits with steps, and as written (b) does not cover that interleaving. (b) The wp displays in X7(i) and (ii), and the contraction equation `corr_{Σ′} = corr_Σ ∩ (Surv × Q)`, are valid only when the second foot lies off the edited document; the both-feet-on-`d₁` case (self-comparison, the case X8 makes central) requires pulling back or testing survival of *both* feet. The prose note about instantiating `τ` and `υ` from the same map covers X-T, but the displayed formulas carry no such qualifier.
**Required**: State X6(b)'s premises explicitly (endpoint frames persist; intervening intermediate edits enter the composite via their position maps), and either qualify the wp formulas as the one-foot case or display the two-sided forms (`π⁻¹` on both feet; both feet surviving).
