### Topic 2: The serializing authority I4 presupposes
I4 commits concurrent-emit semantics relative to "a serializing authority orders the two calls before either becomes a step," inheriting ASN-0086's sequential interleaved model. Who serializes, under what protocol, and across what node topology is unaddressed.
**Why out of scope**: The four-component state deliberately has no concurrency machinery; I4 correctly reduces the question to call order and answers everything downstream of that reduction. The authority itself is new territory for a concurrency note.
