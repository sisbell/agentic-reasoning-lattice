### Topic 1: Rejection-cause observability
The surface collapses every rejection — gate failure, the `K ≁ R` exclusion, invalid `d` at a `d`-reading branch, P-tgt failure at the wrapper — into the single outcome "no step, no address." Whether a caller can distinguish causes, and through what error channel, is unspecified.
**Why out of scope**: The rejection *semantics* are fully pinned (which calls reject, with what state effect); cause reporting is an operational-surface concern for a successor note, not a gap in this one's contracts.
