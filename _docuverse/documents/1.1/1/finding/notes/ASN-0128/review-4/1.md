### Issue 2: The wp analysis stops at Case 1 — no consolidated contract or weakest precondition for idem=⊤ `Emit_K`
**ASN-0128, Standard registrations, DR**: "Both branches thus deliver single-tuple scope at the call's post-state — by the step's contract on a miss, by the pre-existing tuple on a hit — which yields the displayed wp, derivation now per branch, and completes the wp Case-1 parallelism ASN-0126's review trail left open."
**Problem**: Case 1 (the wrapped retraction) gets the full treatment: uniform preconditions, branch condition, per-branch postconditions, and a derived per-branch wp. Case 2 (an emit landing active) gets nothing analogous at this note's surface, even though this note is precisely what invalidates the inherited analysis: ASN-0086's wp Case 2 and ASN-0126's WP are computed for the raw, always-stepping gated emit, and under `idem = ⊤` the surface operation may take no step. On a hit, the returned address `a'` bears the incumbent's stored `(F', G')`, not the presented `(F, G)`, so the inherited postcondition `(a, F, G) ∈ A_K^{Σ'}` does not even type-check against what the surface returns. The pieces of the surface contract exist but are scattered — hit-returns-active-incumbent (I1), miss inherits the gated contract (I1), the born-nullified caveat (I3), and the load-bearing "for non-R types every deposit lands active" fact buried as the last sentence of I4, a concurrency item. No single statement gives the caller-facing postcondition — e.g., "the returned address bears an active K-tuple whose coverage pair equals `(coverage(F), coverage(G))`" — or its weakest precondition.
**Required**: A DR-style consolidated contract for `Emit_K` under `idem = ⊤`: per-branch postconditions in one place, and the derived surface wp for the returned-address-active postcondition — on a surface-disciplined substrate, for `K ≁ R`, this should reduce to gate-conformance ∧ (hit ∨ `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`), with the C2/C3 vacuity argued from DR rather than left for the reader to assemble. State it and derive it per branch, as DR does for Nullify.
