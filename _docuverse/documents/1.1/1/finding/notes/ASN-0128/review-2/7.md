### Topic 1: Multi-writer serialization machinery
I4 fixes interleaving as the concurrency model and posits a "serializing authority" that orders calls before they become steps. Specifying that authority — ordering protocol, fairness, what concurrent callers observe mid-race — is genuine new machinery.
**Why out of scope**: the note explicitly inherits ASN-0086's sequential relation and draws the boundary correctly; a concurrency model is a successor's territory, not an error here.
