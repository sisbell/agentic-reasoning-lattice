### Issue 6: B2's Effect sentence claims a transitivity that no provided predicate realizes
**ASN-0128, Behaviors (B2)**: "Substrate treats the relation as transitive and exposes walk predicates over the active view's denoted graph."
**Problem**: Nothing cashes out "treats the relation as transitive." `succs` is one-step; `chain`/`tip`/`is_in_chain` are determinate-walk only and stop at the first branch — on a branched graph, a genuinely reachable target is invisible to all four predicates. The behavior is *named* transitive-closure, yet the catalog ships no closure or reachability predicate, and no shipped semantics depends on transitivity. The note's own canonical case — `supersedes` with competing claims — is precisely where the walk stops and reachability questions become unanswerable.
**Required**: Either add the closure predicate the name promises (e.g., `reach(x, y)` over the denoted digraph — finite and decidable by the same L-fin argument the note already makes), or delete the "treats as transitive" sentence and rename the behavior for what it provides (determinate-walk), recording reachability as an explicit deferral.
