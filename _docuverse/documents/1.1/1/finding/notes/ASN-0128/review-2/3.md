### Issue 4: the surface `Emit_K`'s precondition on `d` is branch-dependent and never stated
**ASN-0128, Idem operational semantics (I1, hit branch)**: "The `d` argument is consulted only on a miss."
**Problem**: ASN-0086's `Emit_K` carries precondition `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`, and `a_emit` is total only on `(Σ, d ∈ dom(Σ.M))` — so the miss branch silently requires it, while the quoted sentence says the hit branch ignores `d` entirely. Admission of a call with `d ∉ dom(Σ.M)` then depends on whether a hit exists — a state-dependent precondition the caller cannot evaluate — and the note never says whether such a call that *misses* is rejected (like a gate failure) or simply undefined. The note pins the gate-versus-dedup evaluation order with an explicit observability argument; the `d`-validation order is equally observable and left open.
**Required**: Add `d`'s clause to I1's contract: either `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` is checked alongside the gate (uniform precondition, hits included), or the hit branch admits arbitrary `d` and a miss with `d ∉ dom(Σ.M)` is rejected with no step and no address — state which.
