### Issue 1: DR's unconditional-postcondition claim contradicts the wrapper's own idem hit branch
**ASN-0128, Standard registrations (DR)**: "For `Nullify_Binary` itself, once `P0 ∧ P-reg ∧ P-tgt` admits the call, every postcondition of ASN-0126's contract holds unconditionally — in particular single-tuple scope, which yields the displayed wp"
**Problem**: R ships `idem = ⊤` (S3) and `Nullify_Binary ≡ Emit_R`, so the wrapper inherits I1's de-duplication — S3 says so itself ("re-retracting the same target from the same document dedups to the existing tuple"). On a hit, I1 takes no step: "Σ' = Σ, nothing is deposited, and ASN-0086's emit postconditions are forfeited wholesale." ASN-0126's Nullify_Binary contract includes Effect clauses — fresh emitter `b ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, `b ∈ dom(Σ'.L)`, `home(b) = d_retr`, `A_rel^{Σ'} = A_rel^Σ ∪ {b}` — and every one of them fails on an admitted hit call. Even restricted to the Postconditions block (coverage nullification, target-nullification-iff-residence, single-tuple scope, persistence), those clauses are postconditions of *the step*; on a hit there is no step, and re-establishing them at Σ' = Σ requires a different argument: hit ⇒ an I0-equal active retraction tuple exists with to-coverage `subtree(a)` ⇒ `a ∈ nullified(Σ)`; surface-discipline ⇒ `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` (the existing tuple's target was P-tgt-valid, and subtree coverage determines its root uniquely); single-tuple scope then needs R0a's antichain. None of this appears in DR, and the displayed wp inherits the gap because it is derived from the unconditional-contract sentence.
**Required**: Split DR's conclusion by branch. Miss: ASN-0126's contract verbatim. Hit: no step, Effect clauses forfeited, plus an explicit proof that nullification, single-tuple scope, and persistence already hold at Σ via the pre-existing tuple (surface-discipline for `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)`, R0a for scope). Since this note *is* the operational layer, state the wrapper's full surface contract — preconditions, branch condition, per-branch postconditions — in one place.
