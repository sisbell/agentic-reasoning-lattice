### Issue 2: RP's transfer principle is invoked for path-quantified claims it does not cover
**ASN-0128, The registration record (RP)**: "Hence every ASN-0126 claim whose conclusion reads only C, M, L, registration status (key-side, ρ-preserved), and shapes — the gate, P3–P6, the projection bridge onto ASN-0086, FrontierUnification, RangeSterilization — holds at extended-record states by evaluation at ρ(Σ)"
**Problem**: "Evaluation at ρ(Σ)" is the single-state move (ASN-0126's B1/B2 analog). RangeSterilization is successor-quantified — its clauses constrain post-states Θ and every `→_sh*`-successor Θ' — so it is not a predicate of one state and cannot be obtained by evaluating anything at ρ(Σ) alone. The same shape is load-bearing elsewhere: S3 imports ASN-0126's Nullify_Binary postconditions including persistence ("`nullified(Σ') ⊆ nullified(Θ)` at every `→_sh*`-successor"), and I2 applies RangeSterilization at extended-record states. ASN-0126 itself models the correct practice by separating B2 (single-state) from B3 (path) with an explicit scope restriction; RP compresses both into one phrase. Separately, RP states the step-*lift* direction (ASN-0126 step ⇒ extended-record step) only for P5's instance, but exposing `Nullify_Binary` as a surface operation at extended states needs the general lift for the wrapper's miss-branch step.
**Required**: State RP's transfer clauses explicitly, mirroring ASN-0126's apparatus: (a) single-state conclusions by evaluation at ρ(Σ); (b) path-quantified conclusions via derivation projection — extended `→_sh*` derivations project under ρ by (ii)/(iii), and per-successor conclusions pull back through shared components; (c) the general step lift (preconditions read only ρ-preserved data, effects determined on C/M/L, registry framed). Cite the appropriate clause at each use site (I2, S3, DR).
