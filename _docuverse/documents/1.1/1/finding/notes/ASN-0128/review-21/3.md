### Topic 1: The serialization model behind I4
I4 correctly scopes `→_sh` as a sequential, interleaved relation and posits "a serializing authority orders the two calls before either becomes a step." What that authority is, what atomicity granularity it guarantees (the dedup-consultation-plus-deposit of a single surface call must be one unit, or I1's hit/miss branch condition is evaluated against a stale state), and how it composes across homes are unspecified.
**Why out of scope**: Concurrency semantics are deliberately outside the step relation, inherited from ASN-0086's model; specifying the authority is a topology/runtime concern for a successor, not an error in this note's per-call contracts.
