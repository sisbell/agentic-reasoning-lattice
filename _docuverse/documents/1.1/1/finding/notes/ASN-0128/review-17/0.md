### Issue 1: `age`'s well-definedness is pattern-matched, not derived
**ASN-0128, BH4 (age-staleness), Provides**: "for an active event tuple at `a = chain_d(j)`, `d = home(a)`: `age(a) → ℕ` — `f_d^Σ − 1 − j`"
**Problem**: The definition assumes every active tuple's address *has* the form `chain_d(j)` for its home and a *unique* index `j`. That is L-ContiguousPrefix (ASN-0086) — a single-state C/M/L result that reaches this note's extended-record reachable states only through ASN-0126's B2 and this note's RP-a — and BH4 cites neither; it cites only HomedChain and FrontierUnification, with no transfer clause. The note's own discipline names the crossing at every comparable use (I2: "FrontierUnification, ASN-0126, at extended-record states by RP-a"; DR names B2/RP-b per step). Uniqueness of `j` — needed for `age` to be a function — is also unstated; it follows from the chain's strict T1-ascent under sibling advance, but nothing says so.
**Required**: One clause before the formula: every `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` lies in its home's contiguous initial segment at a unique chain index (L-ContiguousPrefix via B2 and RP-a; uniqueness by strict ascent of `chain_d`), so `age` is total on active tuples and single-valued.
