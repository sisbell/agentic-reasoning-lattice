### Topic 1: The serializing authority behind I4
**Why out of scope**: I4 correctly stipulates that `→_sh` is sequential and that concurrency is resolved by an ordering authority *ahead of* the relation, then derives first-to-commit consequences. A model of that authority — who serializes, what fairness or atomicity it provides, whether two `Emit_K` calls can observe the same pre-state at all — is a genuine layer below/beside this note, not a gap in it. The note draws the boundary cleanly; formalizing the other side is a future ASN.
