### Topic 2: Derivation-relative discipline hypotheses
**Why out of scope**: "Surface-emitted K-history" (I1a) and "surface-disciplined substrate" (DR) are properties of how a state was reached, not predicates of the state — strictly, the induction establishes the conclusion for states reached by *some* disciplined derivation. The note operationalizes both clearly (no raw `K.λ_sh` bypass) and flags the quantifier where it bites ("the quantifier is real"). A future note could reify discipline as a checkable state property (e.g., a shape-level characterization of disciplined `L_R` contents, related to the note's own Open question 3), but the present phrasing follows ASN-0126's layer-reachability precedent and creates no soundness gap.
