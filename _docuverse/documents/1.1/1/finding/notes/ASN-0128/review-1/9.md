### Issue 10: DR's antichain step is applied to an address that is not yet a link address, and the wp claim names no postcondition
**ASN-0128, DR (DisciplineRestoration)**: "link addresses form a prefix-antichain at every reachable state (R0a…), so no later link address lies strictly under `a`; nor can it equal `a` (key freshness)… and the weakest precondition of `Nullify_Binary` at this surface is `P0 ∧ P-reg ∧ P-tgt`."
**Problem**: At the state Σ where C3 is evaluated, the fresh address `f = a_emit(Σ, d)` is *not* in `dom(Σ.L)`, so R0a at Σ says nothing about `f`. The argument goes through only by instantiating R0a at the post-state of the prospective emit — the step fires regardless of C3 (C3 governs active-vs-born-nullified, not whether the step occurs), so the post-state is reachable, both `a` and `f` lie in its link domain, and the antichain there refutes `a ≺ f`. That instantiation is the load-bearing move and is nowhere stated; "no later link address lies strictly under `a`" hides it. (The equality case is fine: `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` in both P-tgt branches — pre-existing, or the deposit's own emitter — versus `f ∉ dom(Σ.L)`.) Separately, "the weakest precondition of `Nullify_Binary`" is asserted without naming the postcondition; wp is a function of the postcondition, and the reader must infer single-tuple scope from "wp Case-1 parallelism."
**Required**: Spell out the post-state instantiation of R0a (or replace it with the state-local argument: every wrapper to-target is in `dom(Σ.L)`, every fresh address is a chain slot `d.0.s_L.m` with `#E = 2`, and a strict prefix of such a slot that satisfies L1/L1b would need `#E ≥ 2`, forcing full length — contradiction). Name the wp's postcondition explicitly.
