### Issue 3: `Map_fin`'s carrier is never defined
**ASN-0129, COD (Codomains)**: "`Codom = {Bool, T, ℘_fin(T), T ∪ {⊥}, Seq_fin(T), Map_fin, ℕ, ℕ ∪ {⊥}}` — Boolean, address, finite address set, optional address, finite address sequence, finite map, natural number, optional natural number."
**Problem**: Every other entry's carrier is fixed; `Map_fin`'s key and value types appear nowhere in the note. They are recoverable only by inference from V-PRIM's lookup signature (`·[K] : Map_fin → T ∪ {⊥}`, K a registered class) and ASN-0128's BH3 — but COD's stated job is to pin the codomain set ("PC0–PC2a compose within `Codom` and introduce no codomain beyond it"), and a codomain whose elements are undefined undercuts that.
**Required**: One line in COD: `Map_fin` = finite partial maps from registered coverage classes to `T`, introduced by `targets_keyed`, eliminated only by V-PRIM's `·[K]`.
