### Issue 2: `targets_keyed` mistyped as a class-indexed template atom
**ASN-0129, V (BH3 family)**: "*BH3 family* (when typed-reverse-lookup is attached): `sources_to : T → ℘_fin(T)`, `target_of : T → T ∪ {⊥}`, and the join `targets_keyed : T → Map_fin`."
**Problem**: `Tpl(record(K))` is defined as the generator of K's atoms, and every other member of every family is K-indexed. But `targets_keyed` is not K's atom: FP states its footprint "is cross-type by construction: it joins `target_of` across *every* BH3-attached Binary type … indexed by no single K," and V-IDX must specially exempt it ("the class-*unindexed* `targets_keyed` … occurring as the same closed atom in every expansion instance, well-formed iff … *some* registered class attaches BH3"). As written, V generates one "copy" of a global atom per BH3-attached class, and a reader of V reasonably concludes it is class-indexed like `sources_to`; the correction arrives two sections later, at V-IDX and FP. The well-formedness condition V-IDX needs ("some class attaches BH3") is currently only recoverable by noticing that the union over K happens to produce it.
**Required**: State the class-unindexed character at the definition site — either hoist `targets_keyed` out of the per-K family into a registry-level admission conditioned on "some registered class attaches BH3," or annotate it inside the BH3 family as a single global atom that every BH3-attached class's family contributes identically.
