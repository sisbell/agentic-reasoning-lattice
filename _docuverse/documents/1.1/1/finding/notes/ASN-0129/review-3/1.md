### Issue 2: FP's per-atom table contradicts PD2's use of it for behavior atoms at the default view
**ASN-0129, FP**: "BH2 and BH3 atoms of K: `L_K` and `L_R` (active-view reads, ASN-0128)" — stated unconditionally; **PD2**: "(A default-view term adds every BH1 type's active slice to its footprint, FP, so its non-interference set shrinks by Φ as well.)"
**Problem**: Under `v = default`, UV rewrites the collection-valued results of the behavior atoms too — `succs`, `sources_to`, the sequence `chain` returns, and `stale` are all in UV's collections bullet — and computing the rewrite evaluates `is_filtered_J` for every BH1 type J, so those atoms' default-view evaluation footprint includes `⋃_{J ∈ Φ}(L_J ∪ L_R)`. FP records this increment only in its row for "core atoms of K at view default"; the BH2/BH3/BH4 rows carry no default-view variant. PD2's blanket parenthetical is the *correct* claim, but it cites FP for support FP's table only provides for core atoms. The note advertises the dynamics classes as "footprint facts"; the table and its consumer must agree.
**Required**: Add the default-view increment to FP uniformly for every collection-returning atom (the verdict-, Boolean-, and `Map_fin`-valued atoms correctly take no increment under UV — say so), or restate the default rule once, atom-uniformly. PD2's citation then matches.
