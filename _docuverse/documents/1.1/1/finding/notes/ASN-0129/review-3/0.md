### Issue 1: "exactly as conjectural as C-reach" misstates the logical relationship — with inverted polarity
**ASN-0129, PC6 (and the commitments bullet, which repeats it)**: "If C-reach holds, feedback computation strictly exceeds PL; against unrestricted base-read computation, the ceiling claim is therefore exactly as conjectural as C-reach, and PC6 does not assert it."
**Problem**: The relationship is one-directional and the polarity inverts. C-reach — which the note *conjectures true* — would *refute* the unrestricted ceiling (reach is feedback-computable, so reach ∉ PL forces unrestricted-class ≠ PL). So under the note's own conjecture, the unrestricted ceiling is conjectured **false**, not "as conjectural"; a reader of the commitments bullet can come away believing the note tentatively endorses the unrestricted ceiling, when it is committed to the opposite. The converse direction also fails: ¬C-reach (reach ∈ PL) would not establish the ceiling, since other feedback-computable predicates may escape PL independently of reach — e.g., "count(L_dom) is even" is computable by a feedback enumeration of dom(Σ.L), while PL's ℕ fragment (comparisons and + against literals, no quantification over ℕ, no recursion) gives no plausible expression of parity. "Exactly" asserts an equivalence the note nowhere argues and that is false in at least one direction.
**Required**: Replace both occurrences with the precise statement: the unrestricted-ceiling claim *entails* ¬C-reach; since the note conjectures C-reach, it implicitly conjectures the unrestricted ceiling fails; PC6 asserts the ceiling for syntax-directed evaluation only.
