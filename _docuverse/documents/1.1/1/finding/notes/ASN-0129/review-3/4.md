### Issue 5: V-IDX's static expansion is undefined when the body's vocabulary is not attached at every registered class
**ASN-0129, V-IDX**: "in `(∀ K ∈ Reg :: Φ(K))` the body applies atom families at the bound class — `M_K`, `is_K`, `L_K` with K a variable — a term former that would otherwise be undefined. It is defined by *static expansion*."
**Problem**: The expansion is well-defined only when every instance `Φ(K₀)` is a PL term, and the available vocabulary is non-uniform across `Reg`: a body using a behavior-family atom (`succs`, `tip`, `age`) or the keyed lookup has no denotation at registered classes lacking the behavior or the shape — the instance simply is not in PL. V-IDX neither restricts the body to the core family nor states the well-formedness condition, and the closing well-typing remark ("signature unification against COD plus registry lookup") does not address class variables. This is a boundary case of the note's own definitional machinery, left unhandled.
**Required**: One clause: a `Reg`-quantified term is well-formed iff each of its finitely many expansion instances is — equivalently, behavior-family atoms under a class variable require the behavior (and shape) attached at every registered class; decidable at construction since the registry is static.
