### Topic 1: completeness of V-PRIM's finite-set fragment
V-PRIM ships membership, set equality, emptiness, and `elems`, but no binary union, intersection, or difference on set-valued terms. Intersection is recoverable as a filter (`{x ∈ S₁ : x ∈ S₂}`), and indexed `⋃` over a filtered domain recovers many unions, but the union of two ghost-containing enumeration results (e.g., two `targets_of` values) has no evident spelling. The note's relativization paragraph already classifies such gaps as deliberate vocabulary omissions, closable by admission.
**Why out of scope**: This is a future admission decision on the vocabulary axis the note itself names (the ℕ-multiplication category), not an error in any stated claim — nothing in the note asserts binary set union is expressible.
