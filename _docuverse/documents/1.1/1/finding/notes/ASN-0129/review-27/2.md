### Topic 1: Finer stability rules for extremum-built terms
PD0 excludes the T1-extrema from both classes wholesale ("the ⊥-at-empty verdict and the extremum's movement under growth make their polarity conditional"). The exclusion is sound but conservative: over a grow-only domain, `max_{T1}(D)` is T1-nondecreasing once the domain is non-empty, so guarded tests of the form "defined and ≥ a" are in fact ⊤-stable. **Why out of scope**: refining the classification toward completeness is exactly the territory Open Question 5 reserves; PD0's syntactic classes are explicitly sound-not-complete, and a sound exclusion is not an error.
