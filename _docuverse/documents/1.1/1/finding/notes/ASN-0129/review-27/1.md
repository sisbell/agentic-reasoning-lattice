**Problem**: This is the forward-reference accretion pattern the review mode names: the same proof obligation is stated in three places in slightly different words, and multiple sections defer to the same downstream location with prose that describes what the target section contains rather than advancing any claim. "C-reach (stated and assessed in full at C-reach)" points a label at itself — the parenthetical is pure reviewer-directed reassurance ("in full") with zero content for the next reader. These pointers compound: a reader following them traverses PC6 → C-emit → OQ6 → back to PC6 and learns the one fact three times.
**Required**: State the age-bearing obligation once, at its home (PC6's parity passage or C-emit, not both), with the other site reduced to a bare cross-reference if any is needed. Strip Open Question 6's "(stated and assessed in full at …)" parentheticals — naming the three conjectures suffices, since each name is its location. Reduce QD-audit's deferral to the minimal statement plus pointer ("the self-emit disjunct has no PL spelling — see C-emit").
