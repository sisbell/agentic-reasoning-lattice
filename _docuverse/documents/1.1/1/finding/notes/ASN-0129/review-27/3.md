### Topic 2: Evaluation cost model
PC5 establishes termination at every reachable state but says nothing about cost — the trace structure (folds over QD-fin domains, `chain`'s state-dependent read count) would support a complexity semantics, which a protocol checker validating triggers at scale will eventually want. **Why out of scope**: PC4/PC5 are the guarantees this note promises; a cost semantics is new machinery over the same evaluation class, a future ASN.
