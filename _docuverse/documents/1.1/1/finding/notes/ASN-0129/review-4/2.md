### Issue 3: PC2a's "set semantics, settled" clauses omit their view qualifiers, and one is false as stated
**ASN-0129, PC2a (AggregationClosure)**: "under `idem = ⊤` on surface-emitted derivations a per-class count is 0 or 1 (I1a, ASN-0128), under `idem = ⊥` the count is the deposit count because the *substrate* holds the duplicates as distinct tuples"

**Problem**: Both clauses are view-dependent and neither names its view, in a paragraph that opens by saying counts are taken "in the selected view." I1a's 0-or-1 bound is a property of the *active* subset; the *audit* slice under `idem = ⊤` can hold arbitrarily many I0-equal tuples — resurrection-after-nullification re-deposits the same pair at a fresh address by design (I2, R6c, ASN-0128) — so "a per-class count is 0 or 1" is false at view `audit`. Symmetrically, "the count is the deposit count" under `idem = ⊥` holds at the *audit* view only; at the *active* view it overcounts whenever a duplicate has been retracted or born nullified (I3): deposit three duplicates, nullify one, and the active class count is 2 against a deposit count of 3. Since this paragraph is presented as *settling* ASN-0128's Open Question 2, its two illustrative clauses must be exactly right.

**Required**: Qualify both clauses: active-view per-class count ≤ 1 under `idem = ⊤` on surface-emitted derivations; audit-view per-class count = deposit count under `idem = ⊥`, with the active count being deposits minus nullified (including born-nullified) members.
