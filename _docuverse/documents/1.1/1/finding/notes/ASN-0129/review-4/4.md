### Topic 1: Tumbler-field projections as PL vocabulary
V-PRIM admits address comparisons (`=`, `≼`, T1) but no field projections — no `home`, `N`/`U`/`D`/`E`. Consequently home-scoped predicates ("the active K-tuples homed at `d`") are not expressible: `d ≼ a` does not pin `home(a) = d`, since document-level prefixes can properly extend one another at equal zero count. **Why out of scope**: the note never claims field access — BH4 uses `home` only atom-internally, behind its own definition — and admitting projections would be a V-PRIM-style vocabulary extension for a successor note, not a defect in this one.
