### Topic 2: Sum-aggregation over state-dependent domains
PC2a deliberately ships `count`, T1-extrema, and `⋃`; there is no Σ-fold (sum of `g(x)` over a domain). Cross-type totals survive via static hand-expansion over the constant `Reg`, but sums over state-dependent domains — e.g. total target multiplicity of a Multi type, `Σ_{x ∈ A_K} |addrs_G(x)|` — are not PL terms. **Why out of scope**: PC2a's suite is a deliberate selection ("aggregation raises no expressiveness beyond the closure"), and a Σ-fold is a new former with its own PC5/PD0 obligations — a conscious extension, like the bounded-fixpoint successor of Open Question 4.
