### Issue 3: I3-V disclaimer wording ambiguity
**ASN-0100, "Effect Three: Shift" → disclaimed I3 lemmas**: "Applied to INSERT, this would exclude the Insertion positions `shift(p, k)` from `dom(M'(d))` — directly contradicting INS.M-insert."
**Problem**: I3-V's quantifier ranges over `v ∈ dom(M(d))` (pre-state positions). The conflict with INS.M-insert occurs precisely at Insertion positions `shift(p, k)` that coincide with pre-state positions — concretely, those with `k ≤ N − p_m`. For example, with V_{s_C}(d) = {[1,1], ..., [1,5]}, p = [1,3], n = 2: I3-V constrains both [1,3] and [1,4] (both pre-state, both Insertion). For the append case (p_m = N+1), no Insertion position coincides with a pre-state position, and I3-V is vacuous. The current wording reads as a blanket exclusion of all Insertion positions, which obscures both (a) why the append case is unaffected and (b) which specific positions create the conflict in the interior/beginning cases.
**Required**: Tighten the disclaimer to identify the precise scope of the conflict, e.g., "for any Insertion position `shift(p, k)` that coincides with a pre-state position (those with k ≤ N − p_m in the non-empty case), I3-V would force `shift(p, k) ∉ dom(M'(d))`, contradicting INS.M-insert; the append and empty cases produce no such coincidences and are unaffected by I3-V's quantifier".
