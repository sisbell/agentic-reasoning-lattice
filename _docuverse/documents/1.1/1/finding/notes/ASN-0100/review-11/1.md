### Issue 2: S4 misclassified as entity-set invariant
**ASN-0100, "Atomicity and Canonical Order"**: "*Entity-set invariants* — P8 (EntityHierarchy), NodeLineage (NodeDescentFromBootstrap), S4 (OriginBasedIdentity), M0 (DocumentTumblerWellFormed; ASN-0093). The entity set E (equivalently dom(M) for documents under ValidComposite★) is unchanged at every intermediate: no K.δ fires, no K.σ fires. Each invariant is a predicate over E (or dom(M)) and so holds at every intermediate by inheritance from the pre-state."
**Problem**: S4 (OriginBasedIdentity, ASN-0036) is a predicate over dom(C), not over E or dom(M). Its preconditions explicitly quantify `a₁, a₂ ∈ dom(Σ.C)`, and its postcondition asserts distinctness of content addresses produced by distinct allocation events. INSERT extends dom(C) by n new K.α firings, so S4 must be discharged against the changed dom(C) at intermediate states — not inherited from the pre-state by frame. The "no K.δ, no K.σ" argument preserves E-predicates and is sound for P8, NodeLineage, M0, but does not discharge S4. The correct preservation argument requires: (i) pre-state pairs remain distinct (by P0 monotonicity), (ii) new a_k are distinct from pre-state addresses (by K.α freshness), and (iii) the n new addresses {a_0, …, a_{n−1}} are pairwise distinct (by ChainEnumerationInjectivity, ASN-0093, since a_{k+1} = inc(a_k, 0) > a_k). The Effect One discussion establishes (ii) and (iii) implicitly, but the connection to S4's preservation is not made explicit, and the ASN's stated reason for "trivial preservation" of S4 is structurally incorrect.
**Required**: Move S4 out of the "Entity-set invariants" group. Either group it as a content-allocation invariant requiring per-step verification (and discharge it explicitly via ChainEnumerationInjectivity + freshness at each K.α intermediate), or rewrite the "trivially preserved" justification so the chain-injectivity reasoning that actually establishes S4 is named rather than the inapplicable "no K.δ fires" reasoning.
