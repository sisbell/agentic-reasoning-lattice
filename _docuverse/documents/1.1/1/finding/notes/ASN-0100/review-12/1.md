### Issue 2: INS.frame.subspace formal claim is one-directional
**ASN-0100, Claims Introduced table, INS.frame.subspace**: "Non-content subspaces of d are unchanged: M'(d) agrees with M(d) on positions with subspace ≠ s_C"
**Problem**: The formal contract's quantifier `(A v : v ∈ dom(M(d)) ∧ subspace(v) ≠ s_C : v ∈ dom(M'(d)) ∧ M'(d)(v) = M(d)(v))` says pre-state non-s_C positions persist with same images, but says nothing about whether new non-s_C positions appear in dom(M'(d)). The verbose justification argues both directions ("step 3's K.μ⁺ adds only content-subspace V-positions"), and INS.inv.cross-subspace separately states `V_{s_L}(d') = V_{s_L}(d)` for the s_L specialization. The two claims together give bidirectionality (combined with S3★-aux's enumeration of subspaces), but INS.frame.subspace alone permits a reading in which new non-s_C positions appear in M'(d). The reading is excluded only by combining claims — the formal frame should stand on its own.
**Required**: Strengthen INS.frame.subspace's formal statement to `{v ∈ dom(M'(d)) : subspace(v) ≠ s_C} = {v ∈ dom(M(d)) : subspace(v) ≠ s_C} ∧ (A v in that set :: M'(d)(v) = M(d)(v))`, or fold it into INS.inv.cross-subspace as a single bidirectional claim. The current redundancy between INS.frame.subspace and INS.inv.cross-subspace also suggests consolidation.
