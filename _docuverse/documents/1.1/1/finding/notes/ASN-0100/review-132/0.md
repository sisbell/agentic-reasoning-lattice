### Issue 1: "transfers verbatim" over-claims inheritance from I3, whose content frame INSERT violates
**ASN-0100, §Effect Three (INS.I3-coincide)**: "Because M'(d) coincides pointwise with the I3 arrangement on Left ∪ Shifted-right, every per-state property I3 establishes of that arrangement transfers verbatim to M'(d) restricted to those two regions."

**Problem**: I3 (PostInsertionShift, ASN-0082) is derived under its own content frame **I3-C** (PostInsertionContentFrame): `dom(C') = dom(C)` — content store *unchanged*. INSERT does the opposite: it grows `dom(C)` by the fresh `a_k`. So I3's content-frame-dependent lemmas do **not** transfer verbatim:
- **I3-S7** (PostInsertionAllocationInvariants) is established in ASN-0082 *"trivially by I3-C (dom(C') = dom(C))"* — a premise INSERT falsifies. It is not a structural property of the arrangement at all; sweeping it into "every per-state property... verbatim" is incorrect.
- **I3-S3** (referential integrity) asserts `image ∈ dom(C')` against I3's *frozen* store. Under INSERT the conclusion survives only via the monotone step `dom(C) ⊆ dom(C')` (P0) — i.e., a weakening, not a verbatim transfer.

The blanket sentence is both imprecise and redundant: §Referential integrity already re-derives S3★ correctly (via pre-state S3★ + P0), and §Post-state V-position well-formedness re-derives S7 per-address — yet §Referential integrity still cites "the inherited I3-S3," double-justifying the same fact through a frame I3 does not possess.

**Required**: Restrict the claimed I3 inheritance to the content-frame-*independent* structural properties (I3-VP/S8a, I3-VD/depth, I3-fin/finiteness, I3-S2/functionality of the restricted map). State referential integrity and allocation invariants only through INSERT's own content-store reasoning (the monotone `dom(C) ⊆ dom(C')` step). Drop the "transfers verbatim" blanket and the redundant I3-S3 inheritance citation so the load-bearing fact reduces to the pointwise coincidence on Left ∪ Shifted-right.
