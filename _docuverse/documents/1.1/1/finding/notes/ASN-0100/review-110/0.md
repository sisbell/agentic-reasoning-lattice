### Issue 1: Duplicate derivation of the d′≠d projection case
**ASN-0100, §Verifying the Invariants / "Cross-document independence" and §Coverage and link discoverability / INS.proj**:

The cross-document projection result is derived twice with the same mechanism. In "Cross-document independence":

> "...every elementary step of the decomposition frames M(d') (M'(d') = M(d')), so LP4 ... applied at each step and composed across the sequence gives project(ℓ, i, d', Σ') = project(ℓ, i, d', Σ) — the d' ≠ d case of INS.proj."

Then INS.proj's d′≠d bullet re-derives it in full:

> "...LP4 ... applied at each step (its hypothesis M_{j+1}(d') = M_j(d') is met by that step's frame) gives ...; composing across the finite step sequence ... yields project(ℓ, i, d', Σ') = project(ℓ, i, d', Σ)."

**Problem**: Two paragraphs in different sections perform the identical LP4-per-step-then-compose derivation, and the first explicitly self-labels as "the d' ≠ d case of INS.proj" — a flagged anti-bloat pattern (same result, two places). The first occurrence's restatement of the per-step framing mechanism is redundant with the canonical proof in INS.proj.
**Required**: In "Cross-document independence," cite INS.proj's d′≠d case for the projection statement rather than reproducing the LP4 mechanism. The surrounding cross-document-frame discussion already establishes `M'(d') = M(d')`; the projection consequence needs only a pointer to the canonical proof.
