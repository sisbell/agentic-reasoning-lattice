### Topic 1: Cuts on V-positions shared through transclusion
**Why out of scope**: The first Open Question concerns cuts on V-positions shared across documents. This is correctly deferred — but note its premise (a V-position shared by multiple documents) sits uneasily with the per-document arrangement model (`M(d) : T ⇀ T`), where V-positions are document-local and only I-addresses are shared. A future ASN addressing it should restate the question in terms of shared I-addresses, not shared V-positions.
