### Issue 1: The state model is stated as ASN-0036/0043's, but the discharged S3★ postcondition requires ASN-0047's link-subspace arrangement
**ASN-0119, "The two streams" and "What is preserved: I-address correspondence"**: The note declares "We work in the state `Σ = (C, M, L)` of the strand and link models," then later relies on link-subspace arrangement entries: "we state it per-subspace rather than as the plain `ran(M'(d)) ⊆ dom(C)` because a document's arrangement carries link-subspace V-positions as well — these are exactly the positions the operation leaves untouched in the frame, and their images are link addresses in `dom(L)`," citing "(ASN-0047, **S3★**)."

**Problem**: These two commitments contradict each other. The named model — ASN-0036 (strand) + ASN-0043 (link) — is governed by ASN-0043's **L14a (NonTranscludability)**: `(A d, v : v ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) : Σ.M(d)(v) ∉ dom(Σ.L))`. In that model the arrangement maps V-positions only to content (S3: `ran(M(d)) ⊆ dom(C)`); there are *no* link-subspace V-positions in `M(d)` to "carry in the frame," and S3 — not S3★ — is the operative referential-integrity invariant. Link-subspace V-positions inhabit `M(d)` only in **ASN-0047**, which introduces `K.μ⁺_L` to populate them and replaces S3 with the per-subspace S3★ (plus CL-OWN, CL-UNIQ). So the note's S3★ discharge — a postcondition it explicitly proves — is grounded in a model it does not declare, and the model it does declare forbids the very entries the proof reasons about. A builder cannot tell whether `M(d)` carries link V-positions; the note answers both yes (S3★ paragraph) and no (named (C,M,L) model).

A secondary symptom of the same drift: the note cites ASN-0036's *unstarred* D-CTG/D-MIN/D-SEQ for contiguity while citing ASN-0047's *starred* S3★ for referential integrity. If the operative model is ASN-0047's, the starred D-CTG★/D-MIN★/D-SEQ★ are the invariants in force (they coincide with the unstarred forms on `s_C`, but the note should say so rather than mixing the two citation families).

**Required**: Commit to one model. Either (a) ground in ASN-0047's extended arrangement — state that `M(d)` carries `s_C` and `s_L` V-positions, with `E` and `R` inert under REARRANGE, and use the starred invariants consistently — keeping S3★; or (b) stay in ASN-0036/0043, in which case L14a holds, there are no link-subspace V-positions, the "link subspace carried in the frame" prose must go, and the referential-integrity obligation is the plain S3 `ran(M'(d)) ⊆ dom(C)`.
