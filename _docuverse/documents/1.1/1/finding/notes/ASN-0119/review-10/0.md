### Issue 1: The S3★ (referential integrity) justification asserts a false per-position fact
**ASN-0119, "What is preserved: I-address correspondence"**: "`π` permutes the text subspace onto itself without disturbing any I-address *value* and leaves the link subspace literally fixed in the frame, so every position retains both its subspace and its image, and each pre-state inclusion carries to the post-state."

**Problem**: "every position retains ... its image" is false for positions inside the affected interval. By the operation's own equations `M'(d)(v) ≠ M(d)(v)` in general — in the worked pivot `M'([s_C,2]) = a₃` while `M([s_C,2]) = a₂`, so position `ord 2` does **not** retain its image. Referential integrity is a load-bearing invariant, and as written its preservation rests on a statement the rest of the note contradicts. The conclusion (S3★ preserved) is true, but the stated reason is wrong.

**Required**: Route the argument through the inverse permutation, which is the ingredient the note already has ("π permutes the text subspace onto itself"). For a text position `v`, `M'(d)(v) = M(d)(π⁻¹(v))` where `π⁻¹(v)` is again a text position; pre-state S3★ gives `M(d)(π⁻¹(v)) ∈ dom(C)`. Link-subspace positions are fixed in the frame, so their images remain in `dom(L)`. What is invariant is the *multiset* of images (P1) and the fact that π maps each subspace onto itself — not the image filed at any individual key.
