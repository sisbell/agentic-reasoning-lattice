### Issue 4: "No per-cursor local condition characterizes coherence" asserted beyond its support
**ASN-0108, W5**: "No per-cursor local condition characterizes coherence; only the whole-pass property does, since clause-1 failures at different cursors can offset one another."
**Problem**: The "since" clause (the cancellation walk) establishes that clause 1 *specifically* is not necessary — failures cancel. It does not establish the universal negative that *no* per-cursor local condition is necessary-and-sufficient. The stronger claim is asserted, not derived (standard 6). The load-bearing content — clause 1 is sufficient but not necessary — is fully supported; the universal "no local condition characterizes" is not.
**Required**: Either supply the meta-argument (any predicate evaluated independently per transition cannot witness cross-cursor cancellation, so coherence — a whole-pass outcome — is not characterizable per-cursor) or soften to "clause 1, the natural per-cursor condition, is not necessary; coherence is a whole-pass property, as the cancellation walk shows."
