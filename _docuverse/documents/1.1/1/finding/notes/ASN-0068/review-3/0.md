### Issue 1: CV-IN's "equivalently" claim conflates actionPoint ≥ 2 with ordinal displacement at depth m
**ASN-0068, CV-IN**: "and `actionPoint(width(σ)) ≥ 2` — equivalently, `width(σ)` is an ordinal displacement at the document's depth (ASN-0034, ASN-0058 C0)."

**Problem**: By OrdinalDisplacement (ASN-0034), an ordinal displacement at depth m is `δ(n, m) = [0, 0, ..., 0, n]` of length m, with actionPoint = m specifically. ASN-0058 C0 establishes the biconditional `actionPoint(ℓ) = m ⇔ ℓ = δ(ℓₘ, m)`. The constraint actionPoint ≥ 2 with level-uniformity #width = m admits widths like `[0, w_2, 0, ..., 0]` (actionPoint = 2 when m > 2), which are NOT ordinal displacements at depth m. The two constraint classes coincide only when m = 2; for m ≥ 3, actionPoint ≥ 2 is strictly weaker than actionPoint = m.

**Additional sub-problem** (same paragraph, justification): "The minimum action point of 2 confines `⟦σ⟧` to the depth-`m` tumblers sharing first component `S`, which is the in-subspace property the restriction is meant to enforce."

The actionPoint ≥ 2 constraint confines reach's first component to S (TumblerAdd's prefix-copy region at positions i < actionPoint, extended throughout `[start, reach)` by T5 ContiguousSubtrees), so `⟦σ⟧` is confined to the first-component-S region. But `⟦σ⟧` is NOT confined to depth-m tumblers. Counterexample: with m=3, start=[1,1,5], width=[0,1,0], reach=[1,2,0]; the tumbler [1,1,5,7] at depth 4 satisfies start ≼ [1,1,5,7] (T1 case (ii), prefix) and [1,1,5,7] < reach (T1 case (i) at position 2: 1 < 2), so [1,1,5,7] ∈ ⟦σ⟧. The depth-m projection arises from intersecting ⟦σ⟧ with dom(M(d)) per S8-depth (ASN-0036), not from the action-point constraint.

**Concrete semantic consequence**: With width = [0, w_2, 0, ..., 0] (actionPoint = 2, w_2 ≥ 1), reach_2 = 1 + w_2 ≥ 2 dominates every depth-m V-position [S, 1, ..., 1, k'] under T1 case (i) at position 2. So `⟦σ⟧ ∩ V_S(d)` captures every V-position from start_m onward through V_S(d)'s entire extent — not a bounded contiguous range. By contrast, width = δ(n, m) makes `⟦σ⟧ ∩ V_S(d)` exactly n consecutive positions starting at start. The two constraints produce materially different restriction semantics.

**Required**: Either (a) strengthen CV-IN to require actionPoint(width(σ)) = m — equivalently, width(σ) = δ(n_σ, m_σ) for some n_σ ≥ 1 per OrdinalDisplacement (ASN-0034) — making the "equivalently" claim correct, the depth-m confinement claim accurate, and producing bounded ordinal-displacement restriction semantics consistent with ASN-0058 C0; or (b) keep actionPoint ≥ 2 but remove the "equivalently" claim and replace the depth-m confinement claim with "actionPoint ≥ 2 confines ⟦σ⟧ to the first-component-S region; depth-m projection follows from intersection with dom(M(d)) per S8-depth." Option (a) appears to align with the prose's intent and ASN-0058 C0 framing; option (b) is faithful to the constraint as written.
