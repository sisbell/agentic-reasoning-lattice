### Issue 1: D-NONMONO's K.μ~ subcase asserts "rise or fall" without the derivation its siblings receive
**ASN-0127, D-NONMONO (DiscoveryNonMonotonicity), K.μ~ bullet**: "the witnessing bijection can carry a position with otherwise-unshared image across the `W` boundary, so the image moves (F-IMG-SWING) while `Σ.L` is held fixed (F-PRES/F-INERT); `findlinks_disc` may therefore rise or fall, with no link created or retracted."

**Problem**: The K.μ⁺ and K.μ⁻ subcases each get an explicit four-link inclusion chain whose decisive step is F-IMONO applied to comparable image sets (`image(Σ) ⊆ image(Σ')` under extension, `image(Σ') ⊆ image(Σ)` under contraction). The K.μ~ subcase cannot reuse that argument: F-IMG-SWING produces image sets that are generally *incomparable* (the injective witness moves `{a} ↦ {b}`), so F-IMONO does not apply, and the bullet supplies nothing in its place. The inferential leap "the image moves ⟹ `findlinks_disc` may rise or fall" is exactly the step that is unestablished. It is not even obviously true: F-MATCH is an existential over slots, so an in-region I-address that leaves under the reorder may still be reached through *another* slot of the same link or through another retained in-region address — the moved image member need not move any link. Constructing an actual `findlinks_disc` change therefore requires links whose region-local coverage is not redundantly matched, and the note neither proves the general claim nor exhibits such a witness. (The worked illustration, which does compose Phase 1 → Phase 2 concretely for K.α, K.μ⁻, and K.μ⁺, has no reorder scenario at all.)

**Required**: Either (a) supply a concrete reorder witness lifting F-IMG-SWING through Phase 2 — e.g. `v_1↦a_1, v_2↦a_2` with `L_1 = ({a_1}, {a_θ1}, Θ)`, `L_2 = ({a_2}, {a_θ2}, Θ)` (region-local from-slots, type/to-slots outside the region), `R = {v_1}`: the swap reorder sends `image(R) : {a_1} ↦ {a_2}` and `findlinks_disc : {L_1} ↦ {L_2}`, a genuine swing with no link created — and add it to the worked illustration; or (b) give an explicit argument for "may rise or fall" that does not borrow the siblings' monotonicity, and that accounts for the multi-slot existential (a moved image member changes the link set only when it is the *sole* in-region witness for some link's match).
