### Issue 2: Degenerate cases of `image` and `findlinks` are not stated
**ASN-0127, F-IMG (ImageDefinition) and F-FIND (FindPrimitive)**: `image(R, d, Σ) ≡ {Σ.M(d)(v) : v ∈ R ∩ dom(Σ.M(d))}` and `findlinks(I, Σ) ≡ {a ∈ dom(Σ.L) : matches(a, I, Σ)}`.

**Problem**: The directly analogous foundation primitive — ASN-0098's `project` — pins down its boundary behavior explicitly ("`project(∅, d, Σ) = ∅`"; "`project(e, d, Σ) = ∅` for every `d` with `dom(Σ.M(d)) = ∅`"). ASN-0127's `image` and `findlinks` state no such cases, leaving the empty region, the empty/freshly-registered arrangement (`dom(Σ.M(d)) = ∅`, the K.δ Document post-state), and the empty I-argument unrecorded. These are the boundaries the review standard names as mandatory, and the standard is to nail them, not leave them to the reader to re-derive — especially for a note whose entire subject is "what set does this region cover."

**Required**: State the degenerate cases alongside the definitions: `image(∅, d, Σ) = ∅`; `image(R, d, Σ) = ∅` whenever `R ∩ dom(Σ.M(d)) = ∅` (in particular for a freshly registered `d` with `dom(Σ.M(d)) = ∅`); and `findlinks(∅, Σ) = ∅` (no slot meets `∅`), hence `findlinks_V(R, d, Σ) = ∅` whenever the image is empty — matching the ASN-0098 `project` precedent.
