### Topic 2: Reconciling `findlinks` with ASN-0098's `discoverable_from`
**Why out of scope**: `findlinks(ran(Σ.M(d)), Σ)` coincides with the set of links discoverable from `d` (LP12, ASN-0098), so the discovery query is an `I`-keyed generalization of ASN-0098's per-link predicate. The note flags the composition as open (Q4) rather than re-deriving it. Establishing the bridge is future territory, not a revision — `findlinks` is a legitimately distinct (inverse-index) primitive.
