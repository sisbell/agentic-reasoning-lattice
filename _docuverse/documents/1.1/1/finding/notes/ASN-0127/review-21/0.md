### Issue 1: The worked illustration's `a_θ` incomparability premise is asserted, not discharged
**ASN-0127, Worked illustration, "Coverage of the endset shorthand"**: "`a_θ` is a type address in a distinct subspace (`s_L`), so it diverges from every content address at or before the subspace component and is prefix-incomparable with each — a fortiori `a_θ ∉ {a_1, a_2, a_3}`."
**Problem**: The premise for `a_1, a_2, a_3` is properly discharged (siblings on `A_C(d)` by ChainMembershipForOrigin, incomparable by T10a.2). For `a_θ` the note infers prefix-incomparability from subspace difference alone — but no foundation claim delivers that. T7 and L1d(a) give only *distinctness* (`a ≠ b`) for cross-subspace element-level addresses, which is strictly weaker than `a_θ ⋠ a_i`. The actual argument is multi-step: one must show that `a_θ ≼ a_i` would force all three zeros of `a_i` into the agreement prefix, making `E(a_i)₁` coincide with `E(a_θ)₁` and contradicting `s_C ≠ s_L`. The note neither carries this out nor can cite it (T10a.5 doesn't apply: `a_θ` may be a ghost type, L9, hence on no allocator's chain). Worse, `a_θ`'s home document and length are never pinned, so the claim quantifies over a family of addresses the example never fixes. Every slot-3 computation — Phase 2, the contraction bullet, both swing variants, and the K.λ bullet — consumes `subtree(a_θ) ∩ I = ∅` through this premise.
**Required**: Pin `a_θ` concretely, e.g. `a_θ = [d, 0, s_L, 1]`: each `a_i = [d, 0, s_C, kᵢ]` has the same length `#d + 3` (FirstEmission's `#E = 2` propagated by `inc(·, 0)`, TA5(c)), so incomparability is two cited steps — distinctness by T7, and Prefix's `p ≺ q ⟹ #p < #q` ruling out any prefix relation between distinct equal-length tumblers. Alternatively, stipulate prefix-incomparability explicitly in the setup, or state and prove the general cross-subspace incomparability lemma.
