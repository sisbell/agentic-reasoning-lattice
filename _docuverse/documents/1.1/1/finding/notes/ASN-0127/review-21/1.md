### Issue 2: F-INERT's quantification domain is ambiguous, and one reading falsifies the claim
**ASN-0127, F-INERT**: "For every transition in `V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~} ∖ {K.λ}` and every `I ⊆ T`: `findlinks(I, Σ) = findlinks(I, Σ')`."
**Problem**: `∪` and `∖` carry no agreed precedence. Read as `V_atomic ∪ ({K.μ~} ∖ {K.λ}) = V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~}`, the domain includes K.λ — under which F-INERT is false (F-LAMBDA exists precisely because K.λ moves the result). The intended `(V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~}) ∖ {K.λ}` is recoverable from context, but a formal claim's quantification domain cannot depend on the reader choosing the reading that keeps the claim true.
**Required**: Parenthesize `(V_atomic ∪ {K.μ~}) ∖ {K.λ}`, or reuse F-PRES's explicit enumeration `{K.α, K.δ, K.μ⁺, K.μ⁺_L, K.μ⁻, K.ρ}` plus `K.μ~`.
