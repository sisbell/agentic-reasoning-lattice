### Issue 1: D-CTG/D-MIN/D-SEQ labeling inconsistency between body and worked examples
**ASN-0091, "Per-Invariant Discharges (ASN-0036 Foundation Invariants)"**: "The ASN-0036 invariants load-bearing in the unified state — those unchanged between ASN-0036 and the unified state and not superseded — are: S0, S1, S2, S4, S5, S7a–d, S9, S8a, S8-fin, S8-depth, D-CTG, D-MIN, and D-SEQ."

**Problem**: D-CTG, D-MIN, D-SEQ are superseded by D-CTG★, D-MIN★, D-SEQ★ in the unified state. ASN-0047's ExtendedReachableStateInvariants theorem (per the foundation listing) includes D-CTG★ ∧ D-MIN★ ∧ D-SEQ★ as the per-state invariants but does *not* include the ASN-0036 forms. By the ASN's own stated criterion ("not superseded"), D-CTG/D-MIN/D-SEQ should not appear in this list. The inconsistency is internal: the worked examples (first worked example, 4-cut swap, interior cuts, bijection non-uniqueness) all correctly use D-CTG★/D-MIN★/D-SEQ★ in their admissibility verifications, while the body section uses the non-★ forms. A reader following the body discharge cannot match it to the worked examples without inferring the intended substitution.

**Required**: Replace D-CTG/D-MIN/D-SEQ with D-CTG★/D-MIN★/D-SEQ★ in the per-invariant discharges section, or relocate them to the "Per-Invariant Discharges (ASN-0047 Extended Invariants)" subsection. The arguments (RA-dom + state-independent ordering/length/minimum projections) discharge both versions identically — only the labels change. The body and worked examples should use consistent vocabulary.
