### Issue 4: B3 "occupied" predicate undefined; forbidden case is a forward requirement, not current invariant
**ASN-0040, B3 (Ghost Validity)**: The trivalent table uses "t occupied" as a dimension; the case "t ∉ Σ.B ∧ t occupied" is marked **forbidden**.

**Problem**: The predicate "occupied" is never defined here — content storage is correctly noted as out of scope. But B3 is presented as if it states a current system invariant, when in fact the forbidden case is a precondition that future content operations must enforce. The narrative ("The fourth case is a *requirement on content operations*") admits this, but B3's table presents all four cases as if they have current truth values.

**Required**: Either (a) reframe B3 as a forward requirement on future ASNs ("any operation that populates a position must take t ∈ Σ.B as a precondition") without the trivalent table, or (b) parameterize the table over a future predicate `Occupied(t, σ_content)` with explicit notation that this predicate is defined elsewhere.
