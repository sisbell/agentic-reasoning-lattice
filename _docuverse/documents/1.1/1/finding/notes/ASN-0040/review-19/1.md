### Issue 2: B4 uses undefined temporal vocabulary
**ASN-0040, B4 (Namespace Serialization)**: "`(A β₁, β₂ : ns(β₁) = ns(β₂) : commit(β₁) ≺ read(β₂) ∨ commit(β₂) ≺ read(β₁))` where ≺ denotes temporal precedence."

**Problem**: `≺`, `commit(β)`, `read(β)`, and the notion of a "baptismal act β" are introduced informally and never grounded. B4 is a precondition for every Bop, a key step in the freshness argument ("B4 ensures the state observation is current"), a load-bearing premise in B1 preservation ("By B4, this baptism observes the complete state left by any prior same-namespace baptism — no interleaving has occurred"), and central to B8's same-namespace case. Without formal grounding for the event model, the wp analysis "B4 ensures the state observation is current" is unverifiable.

**Required**: Either (a) define the event model precisely — what is a baptismal act, what are commit and read events, what is the precedence relation — or (b) recast B4 as a transition-level constraint (e.g., "Bop is treated as an atomic state transition; B4 is the assertion that two same-namespace Bop transitions are linearly ordered in any execution"). The latter would let B4 fit cleanly into the transition framework requested in Issue 1.
