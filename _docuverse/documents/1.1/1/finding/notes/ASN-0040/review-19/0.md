### Issue 1: State space and transition system never formally defined
**ASN-0040, throughout**: References to "states Σ, Σ'", "transitions Σ → Σ'", "reachable from Σ", and "transition sequence" pervade the ASN. B0's axiom quantifies "(A Σ, Σ' : Σ → Σ' : Σ.B ⊆ Σ'.B)". B1, B10, and B8 proofs induct on transition sequences.

**Problem**: What is a state? What constitutes an admissible transition? What does "reachable" mean? AllocatedSet (foundation) defines `𝒮` as the state space, `Σ` as the transition vocabulary, `s → s'` as `(s, op(s))` for `op ∈ Σ`. ASN-0040 introduces Σ.B as "a state component" without ever defining the state space Σ or the transition relation. The Σ.B justification claims "the type invariant is preserved across all transitions" without specifying what the transition relation is — meaning every preservation proof induces over an undefined object.

**Required**: At the ASN's start, define the state Σ as a tuple containing Σ.B (and noting future components may be added). Define the transition relation as generated by the operations specified here (currently Bop only). Define reachability from B₀ as the reflexive-transitive closure. Without this framework, every "transition sequence" induction is hand-wavy — one cannot induct over an unspecified set.
