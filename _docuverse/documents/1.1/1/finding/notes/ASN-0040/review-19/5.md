### Issue 6: Relationship between Σ.B and ASN-0034's `allocated(s)` not specified
**ASN-0040, Bop and B0 narrative**: "B0 is the state-level reading of T8 (AllocationPermanence)." T8 is stated in terms of `allocated(s)` from AllocatedSet.

**Problem**: AllocatedSet defines `allocated(s) = ⋃ { domₛ(A) : activated(A, s) }`. ASN-0040 introduces Σ.B as "the set of baptized tumblers". The conceptual relationship — are they the same set viewed from different angles, or genuinely distinct components? — is left implicit. If Σ.B = allocated(s) under a suitable identification of namespaces (p, d) with allocators, then B0 should be derivable from T8 rather than stipulated. If they're distinct, the distinction needs articulation.

**Required**: State the relationship explicitly. If Σ.B = allocated(s), justify B0 as a corollary of T8 (and explain what additional content B0 carries — perhaps that B0 quantifies over all transitions in the registry framework, while T8 is stated for the algebraic transition vocabulary). If Σ.B ≠ allocated(s), explain what each captures and why both are needed.
