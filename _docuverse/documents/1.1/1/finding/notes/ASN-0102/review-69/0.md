### Issue 1: PC3 derives the target subspace from the wrong quantity
**ASN-0102, "Precondition", PC3**: "by PC1 every source span is content-subspace-resident, so its `s_C` V-positions map via S3★ to addresses in `dom(Σ.C)` (C1), and L0 ... fixes `subspace_I(·) = s_C` on every member of `dom(Σ.C)`. This pins `subspace(v) = s_C` for the inserted positions."

**Problem**: The chain establishes facts about `subspace_I` — the *element-field subspace identifier of the I-address* — and then concludes a fact about `subspace(v)` — the *V-position subspace projector* (`subspace(v) = v₁`). These are distinct projectors over distinct objects (one over content addresses in `dom(C)`, the other over V-position tumblers in `dom(M(d))`). That the copied *I-addresses* carry `subspace_I = s_C` says nothing about which *V-position subspace* the target placement occupies; the offered derivation is a non-sequitur. The conclusion happens to be true, but the actual forcing runs the other way: S3★ at the *target* requires that any image in `dom(C)` sit at an `s_C` V-position (placing a content address at an `s_L` position would violate S3★'s `subspace(v) = s_L ⟹ M(d)(v) ∈ dom(L)` clause, since `dom(C) ∩ dom(L) = ∅`). The note cites S3★ but binds it to the source mapping rather than to the target placement obligation.

**Required**: Re-derive PC3's `subspace(v) = s_C` from S3★ applied to the inserted (target) mappings — content images force `s_C` V-positions — and drop the `subspace_I`-based argument, which conflates the two projectors. The `subspace_I = s_C` fact about copied addresses belongs to the S3★ image-routing obligation (the `wp` computation), not to fixing the target V-position subspace.
