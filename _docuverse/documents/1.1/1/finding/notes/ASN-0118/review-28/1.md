**ASN-0118, operation section (CP3c, CP6, CP12)**: CP3c's paragraph: "so each text V-position carries exactly one binding and `d`'s per-state invariants — S2 functionality among them — are dischargeable from the postconditions alone, not only through the exhibited composite (CP6's domain-equality conjunct does the same for the non-text subspaces)." **Problem**: This is why-the-clause-is-needed prose, not content of the clauses — and it is repeated in three places. This is the accretion pattern the anti-bloat classifier names: prose around a postcondition explaining why it exists rather than what it says, duplicated across sections.
**Required**: Keep all four clauses as stated. State the closure rationale once — a single sentence where CP3c is introduced suffices — and remove the echoes at CP6 ("the non-text instance of CP3c's closure principle") and CP12 ("without them a transition could…"; "They play … the closure role CP3c plays").
