### Issue 1: StoreDisjointness (SD) is proved twice — statically in its definition and again operationally in the inductive matrix
**ASN-0093, SD definition** vs **Discharge of stated invariants, SD matrix row**:

The SD definition gives a complete *pointwise/static* proof: "T7 gives, for every `a ∈ dom(C)` and every `ℓ ∈ dom(L)`, `a ≠ ℓ` — which is exactly `dom(C) ∩ dom(L) = ∅`." Its premises are L0, SC-NEQ, StoreT4Validity, and T7 — all of which hold at every reachable state (L0 and StoreT4Validity are maintained by the simultaneous induction; SC-NEQ and T7 are axioms/foundation). Therefore SD is a static consequence: once L0 and StoreT4Validity hold at a state, SD holds at that state automatically, with no transition-indexed argument required.

The matrix nonetheless carries a separate SD row that re-discharges it through a second, independent route — "the only new obligation is `a ∉ dom(L)`, given by FirstEmissionFreshness / SubsequentEmissionFreshness, cross-subspace clause."

**Problem**: This is two proofs of the same fact ("two paragraphs say the same thing in different words"). The freshness lemmas are genuinely needed to discharge the *operation's* freshness precondition (`a ∉ dom(C) ∪ dom(L)`), but re-using their cross-subspace clauses to establish the *invariant* SD is redundant — SD follows pointwise from invariants already established at `Σ'`. Unlike C1/C1b/L1 etc. (whose subsequent-emit cases genuinely need the IH), SD requires no operational preservation argument; it is the one invariant in the table that is purely static.

**Required**: Replace the freshness-based SD matrix discharge with a one-line pointer: "SD follows pointwise at `Σ'` from L0 and StoreT4Validity at `Σ'` via T7 (see SD definition)." Keep the freshness lemmas where they discharge the operation preconditions, but do not route SD through them.
