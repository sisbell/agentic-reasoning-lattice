### Issue 1: SD's per-transition matrix rows re-derive the static T7 argument the invariant statement already gives in full
**ASN-0093, Discharge matrix (SD row, K.α and K.λ cells)** vs **Link store invariants, SD (StoreDisjointness)**: The SD invariant statement already contains a complete, self-contained derivation — "every content address has `E(·)₁ = s_C` and every link address has `E(·)₁ = s_L` (L0), and `s_C ≠ s_L` (SC-NEQ), so T7 gives … `a ≠ ℓ` — which is exactly `dom(C) ∩ dom(L) = ∅`." The matrix K.α cell then restates the same chain: "the new content key `a` has `E(a)₁ = s_C` … every `ℓ ∈ dom(L)` has `E(ℓ)₁ = s_L`; SC-NEQ + T7 give `a ∉ dom(L)` …", and K.λ repeats it under the content↔link swap.

**Problem**: SD is marked `INV (derived)` and the K.α cell itself concedes it is "a standing consequence of L0/C1/L1/StoreT4Validity at Σ'." A standing consequence of conjuncts that are themselves invariants needs no separate inductive step beyond "its premises hold at Σ' by their own rows." Re-running the T7 argument inside the matrix cells is the same reasoning stated a second time, in a structural slot, that the reader must skip past to confirm nothing new is being asserted.

**Required**: Reduce the SD K.α/K.λ cells to "standing consequence of the L0/C1/L1/StoreT4Validity rows at Σ'" and let the SD invariant statement carry the one T7 derivation; do not re-exhibit it per transition.
