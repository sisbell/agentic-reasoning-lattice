### Issue 1: S5's proof inflates the claim and forward-references the entire downstream invariant set
**ASN-0036, Sharing (S5)**: The claim states "S0–S3 do not entail any finite bound on sharing multiplicity," yet the proof opens by defining a *"genuine strand state"* as one satisfying "S2 and S3; ... S7a ... and S7b ...; ... S7d ...; the domain-restriction axiom ...; and ... S8-fin, S8-depth, and the text-subspace contiguity constraints D-CTG and D-MIN," then verifies each.

**Problem**: Two compounding defects.
- **Scope mismatch.** To show "S0–S3 do not entail a bound," the witness need only satisfy S0–S3 with unbounded multiplicity. Requiring it to satisfy S7a/S7b/S7d/S8-fin/S8-depth/D-CTG/D-MIN proves a *different* (stronger) proposition than the one stated. The headline names S0–S3; the proof and contract quietly substitute the full always-on invariant set.
- **Forward references.** S5 sits in the *Sharing* section, but S7a, S7b, S7d, S8-fin, S8-depth, D-CTG, and D-MIN are all introduced in *later* sections. The "genuine strand state" paragraph is precisely a use-site inventory that re-enumerates every downstream invariant before any of them has been stated — the forward-reference accretion pattern this note is tasked to surface. The *Depends* list ("S0, S1, S2, S3, S7a, S7b, S7d, S8-fin, S8-depth, D-CTG, D-MIN") encodes the same backward-pointing dependency in the contract.

**Required**: Pick one. Either (a) restate the claim and contract so the headline matches what is proved ("there exists a genuine strand state with unbounded multiplicity"), and relocate S5 after the S7/S8/D-* claims it consumes; or (b) keep S5's stated scope (S0–S3) and trim the proof and *Depends* to the S0–S3 witness alone, dropping the inventory. As written, the claim, the proof, and the document order are inconsistent with one another.
