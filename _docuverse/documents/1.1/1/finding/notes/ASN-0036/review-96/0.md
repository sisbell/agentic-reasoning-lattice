### Issue 1: Incomplete NAT-addcompat citation in positivity chains
**ASN-0036, S8 auxiliary lemma, Conclusion (ii)**: "combined with k ≥ 1, NAT-addcompat's left compatibility gives aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + k ≥ 1 + 1 ≥ 1 > 0."

**Problem**: To derive `aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + k ≥ 2` from `aⱼ_{#aⱼ} ≥ 1` and `k ≥ 1`, the chain requires both directions of NAT-addcompat plus NAT-order transitivity:
- Left compatibility at `(m, p, n) = (aⱼ_{#aⱼ}, 1, k)`: from `1 ≤ k`, obtain `aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + 1 ≤ aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + k`.
- Right compatibility at `(m, p, n) = (1, 1, aⱼ_{#aⱼ})`: from `1 ≤ aⱼ_{#aⱼ}`, obtain `1 + 1 ≤ aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + 1`.
- NAT-order transitivity to chain these into `1 + 1 ≤ aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + k`.

Citing only "left compatibility" misses the right-compatibility step.

The same pattern appears in OrdAddS8a's proof, paragraph beginning "At i = k: rₖ = vₖ + wₖ ≥ 1 + 1 = 2 (since vₖ ≥ 1 by S8a and wₖ ≥ 1 as the action-point component)" — both vₖ ≥ 1 and wₖ ≥ 1 must be lifted through addition's monotonicity, requiring both NAT-addcompat directions.

**Required**: Either explicitly cite both directions of NAT-addcompat (left and right order compatibility) together with NAT-order transitivity, or use a more general phrasing such as "by NAT-addcompat (left and right order compatibility) and NAT-order transitivity, the chain `1 + 1 ≤ aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + 1 ≤ aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + k` gives `aⱼ_{#aⱼ} + k ≥ 1 + 1`."
