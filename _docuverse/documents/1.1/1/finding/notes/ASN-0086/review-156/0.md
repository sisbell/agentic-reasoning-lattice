### Issue 1: R0's formal statement omits the post-state conformance its proof establishes and downstream lemmas rely on
**ASN-0086, R0 — TupleAddressFreshness**: the formal statement's existential conclusion is only

`… (E Σ' reached by one →-step from Σ, a : a ∉ dom(Σ.L) :: a ∈ dom(Σ'.L) ∧ Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K))`

**Problem**: The R0 proof devotes its longest section ("L-invariant preservation across the K.λ-step") to establishing that Σ' satisfies the full state-local L/S-invariant catalog at the fresh key. This work is genuinely load-bearing here — over R0's *state-local-conforming* domain (which admits NestedLinkWitness states), one cannot simply inherit conformance from K.λ's substrate-conformance contract, so the proof re-derives it conjunct-by-conjunct (including the branch-sensitive L1b/L1c arguments). Yet the *statement* exposes none of this. Consumers then cite the unstated postcondition:

- R5, Step 3: "R0 therefore produces a fresh emitter `a' ∉ dom(Σ.L)` and **conforming post-state Σ'** with `Σ'.L(a') = (∅, G_self, K)`."
- R5, Step 4: "R0 applied at home `d` yields a fresh emitter address `a''` with **conforming post-state Σ''** …"

"Conforming post-state" is not in R0's formal contract. A reader checking R5 against R0's stated postconditions finds the cited property absent — the proof proves more than the contract claims, and R5 consumes the surplus.

**Required**: Add the established conformance to R0's formal conclusion — e.g. `… ∧ Σ' is state-local-conforming` (equivalently `∧ Σ' satisfies the state-local L- and S-invariant catalog`) — and reflect it in the Properties table row for R0. The proof already discharges it; the contract and its consumers (R5 Steps 3–4) should reference a stated postcondition rather than a proof-body fact.
