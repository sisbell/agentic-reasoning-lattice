### Issue 1: The cross-owner identity clause's "three constraints suffice" claim is false — V9's Severance theorem provably needs O5's maximality
**ASN-0123, "The Operation" (identity clause, cross-owner branch):** "v := a fresh document identity with Document(v) ∧ v ∉ E ∧ pfx(π) ≼ v, drawn from the forker's own document-creation namespace; those mechanics are out of scope here, **and the three constraints suffice for every claim below**."

**Problem:** V9(a) (Severance, `¬(d_src ≼ v)`) is proved using O5's maximality clause — "O5's maximality at the allocating transition forces #pfx(π) ≤ #pfx(π')" — which V9 obtains from "let v be the identity π' allocates (so O5 gives pfx(π')≼v **and the maximality condition**)." That maximality, `(A π'' ∈ Π : pfx(π'') ≼ v ⟹ #pfx(π'') ≤ #pfx(π'))`, is neither among the three listed constraints nor derivable from them.

Concrete counterexample to sufficiency: take `v = d_src·k` (a version-stream member of the source), fresh, in the configuration where the forker's prefix strictly precedes the source owner's, `pfx(π') ≺ pfx(π) ≼ d_src` (so π' covers d_src but π owns it — `ω(d_src) = π ≠ π'`). Then `Document(v)` ✓, `v ∉ E` ✓, and `pfx(π') ≼ d_src ≺ v` gives `pfx(π') ≼ v` ✓ — all three constraints hold — yet `d_src ≺ v`, so Severance *fails*. What actually excludes this identity is exactly O5(ii): π covers v with a strictly longer prefix than π', so π' may not allocate v. The three structural constraints permit the severance-violating identity; only the allocation-by-the-forker fact (via O5) forbids it. So "the three constraints suffice for every claim below" is false, and the proof of one of the ASN's headline results ("a theorem, not a design choice") rests on a fourth, unlisted hypothesis.

**Required:** Make `allocated_by(π', v)` — whence O5 supplies *both* `pfx(π') ≼ v` and its maximality — an explicit operative hypothesis of the cross-owner clause, or list the maximality as a fourth constraint; and drop or correct the sentence claiming three constraints suffice. V-WF's cross-owner discharge ("draws from the forker's account document sub-allocator") already establishes allocation-by-the-forker, so the operation itself is fine — the fix is to stop the contract prose from under-counting what V9 consumes, since a reader reconstructing V9 from "the three constraints" alone cannot complete the proof.
