# Xanadu

The xanadu lattice: a hypertext-protocol formalization, originally
authored under (node, user) = (1.1, 1) in this docuverse. Notes,
claims, agents, and supporting documents authored for this lattice
live under `_docuverse/documents/1.1/1/`.

Membership in this lattice is expressed as a `lattice` substrate link
from a document to this lattice doc. Configuration that scopes to the
whole lattice (label_prefix, default_campaign) lives in this doc's
frontmatter and is read by `lib/lattice/config.py`.
