The framework binds ASN-0086's Emit_K (the relational-layer operation), not the K.λ substrate primitive — adding preconditions to a layer operation is precisely the framework's scope, and ASN-0086's substrate axioms remain unchanged. Per Nelson (Literary Machines), the state assertion ('is this nullified?') is set-semantic (single answer regardless of call multiplicity) and the event log ('when was this nullified, and by whom?') is multiset/audit-semantic; the framework implements both — NullifyActiveSubsetCompatibility preserves the set-semantic state assertion across both clause-(iii) and clause-(ii) branches, and the attributed-retraction migration discipline (M1–M5) recovers per-event multiplicity at the audit slice. Documenting the relational-layer change at its actual binding site (ASN-0094's Nullify Compatibility section) is appropriate; ASN-0086's Nullify postcondition wording itself is a relational-layer specification of Emit_R and inherits the framework's gate stack when consumed by a shape-conforming layer, with no separate substrate revision required.
