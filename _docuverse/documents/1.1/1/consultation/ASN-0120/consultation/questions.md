# Sub-Questions — MAKELINK Operation

**Inquiry:** What happens when a new link is created connecting endsets across content? What is allocated as the link's identity, what is recorded about the endsets it ties together, and where does the link itself reside, what relationship must the link's home document bear to the endsets it touches and to the discoverability of the new link by content-region search, what does creating a link with multiple endsets — a from, a to, and a type or third endset — reveal about directionality, typing, and the difference between a connection and a relation, and what invariants must the operation preserve about the link's permanence, the immutability of its recorded endsets, the link's residence-independence from the content it connects, and the system's ability to discover the link from any of the regions its endsets reference?

1. [theory] When a new link is created, what identity must the system allocate to it, and must that identity be permanent and never reused even after the link's home document changes?
2. [theory] What must the system record about each endset a link ties together, and must those recorded endsets remain immutable for the life of the link?
3. [theory] In which document does a newly created link reside, and must a link always have a home document just as ordinary content does?
4. [theory] What relationship must a link's home document bear to the documents whose content regions its endsets reference — may a link live anywhere, independent of what it connects?
5. [theory] Once a link is created, must it be discoverable by search from every content region that any of its endsets references, regardless of where the link itself resides?
6. [theory] When a link is created with a from endset and a to endset, what does the ordering of those endsets guarantee about the link's directionality?
7. [theory] What does the presence of a third endset — a type or relation marker — reveal about the difference between a bare connection and a typed relation between documents?
8. [theory] Must the system guarantee that a link, once created, persists permanently and remains available even if the content at its endsets is later superseded by new versions?
9. [theory] What invariant must hold so that a link's residence is fully independent of the content it connects — that connecting two documents never forces the link to live inside either of them?
10. [theory] May a single link tie together endsets drawn from more than one document at once, and what must the system guarantee about each endset being faithfully recorded as a distinct region of content?
11. [evidence] When CREATELINK allocates a link's identity via `findnextlinkvsa`, does the returned V-position come from a per-document link counter (`max(existing_link_v_positions)+1`), and is the link orgl's I-address allocated as `docISA.0.2.N` independently per home document?
12. [evidence] Does CREATELINK store all three endsets (source, target, type) as I-address spans in the link orgl, converting endset V-spans to I-spans at creation time so the recorded endpoints reference content by identity rather than V-position?
13. [evidence] When an endset's V-span maps to non-contiguous I-addresses, does CREATELINK automatically split it into multiple sporgl entries, and does this splitting happen for all three endsets uniformly?
14. [evidence] Does CREATELINK index the endset I-spans in the spanfilade via `insertendsetsinspanf`, and does it write a separate spanfilade entry for each of the three endsets (source, target, type)?
15. [evidence] Does the link orgl get created in the granfilade while a V→I POOM entry for the link is placed in the home document's link subspace (`2.x`), making the link reside in both granfilade and the home document's POOM?
16. [evidence] Must the home document of a new link have any I-address overlap with the content its endsets reference, or can a link be created whose home document shares no content identity with either its source or target endset?
17. [evidence] Since CREATELINK uses `insertpm`+`makegappm` and places links at document end via `findnextlinkvsa`, does the link-creation shift ever actually move existing POOM entries, or is the shift always a no-op because nothing exists beyond the insertion point?
18. [evidence] Does the type endset (third endset) get stored structurally identical to source and target endsets as an I-address span, with the link type itself registered at `1.0.2.x` in the global type namespace, distinguishing a typed relation from an untyped from→to connection?
19. [evidence] Once created, can a link's recorded endset I-addresses ever be mutated by any subsequent operation, or are the endsets permanently frozen at the I-addresses captured at creation time (independent of later V-position shifts of the referenced content)?
20. [evidence] Is a newly created link immediately discoverable by content-region search from ANY document whose POOM shares I-addresses with any of its endsets — including documents other than the home document — confirming that link residence is decoupled from discoverability scope?
