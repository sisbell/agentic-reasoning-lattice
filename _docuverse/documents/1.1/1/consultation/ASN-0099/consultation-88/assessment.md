## Issue 1: "disjoint I-runs" mischaracterizes the image decomposition
Reason: The fix is internal — the ASN already relies on shared I-addresses (F6 transclusion transparency, the worked example's `d_b` transcluding `α₂, α₃`), so V-positions sharing one I-address is established by the ASN's own content.
