**Date:** 2026-05-30 17:31

## Issue 1: EXT-VAC characterizes the empty-right-exterior regime as an open range that R-PRE(iv) has already collapsed to a single point
Reason: The fix is derivable from the ASN's own content. R-PRE(iv) and D-SEQ (V_S(d) = {[S,k] : 1 ≤ k ≤ N}) suffice: any ord(c_{n−1}) > N+1 admits the witness [S, N+1] in [c₀, c_{n−1}) but outside V_S(d), violating R-PRE(iv). No design intent or implementation evidence is required to pin the admissible value to exactly N+1.
