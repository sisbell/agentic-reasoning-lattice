## Question 9 [theory]

> In Nelson's tumbler design, what entity does a tumbler of form [N, 0, U, 0, D, k] (a document with appended component but no new zero separator) represent — a version, a sub-document, or something else?

A tumbler of form [N, 0, U, 0, D, k] — where k extends the Document field without crossing a new zero separator — represents a **version or sub-document** of D. Nelson treats these as the same notational construct.

From 4/29: "The Document field of the tumbler may be continually subdivided, with new subfields in the tumbler indicating daughter documents and versions."

And critically: "In a sense the version, or subdocument number is only an accidental extension of the document number, and strictly implies no specific relationship of derivation." (4/29)

So the system does **not** distinguish "version" from "sub-document" at the tumbler level — both are descendants in the Document subspace via the forking/baptism mechanism (4/17, 4/20). The third zero separator would be required to enter the Element subspace (bytes/links); without it, k remains inside the document tree.
