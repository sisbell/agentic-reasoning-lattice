# Sub-Questions — SHOWRELATIONOF2VERSIONS Operation (compare two spec-sets)

**Inquiry:** What happens when the system is asked to identify which spans of two spec-sets correspond? What is returned as a correspondence and what makes two spans correspond, what relationship must the returned correspondence list bear to the two spec-sets asked about — including the ordering of pairs, the offsets within each pair, and whether self-correspondence (a spec-set compared with itself) is a trivial identity case or a non-trivial diagonal, what does comparing spec-sets that are not full document extents reveal about correspondence that comparing whole documents would not, and what invariants must the operation maintain about the basis of correspondence (shared content identity, not textual equivalence), completeness across transitive transclusion chains, and stability under arrangement edits to either compared document?

1. [theory] When asked which spans of two documents correspond, what exactly must the system return — a list of paired spans, and what does each pair assert about the two documents?
2. [theory] What makes two spans correspond — must they share the same underlying content identity, or is textual equivalence between independently authored passages ever sufficient?
3. [theory] What ordering must the returned list of corresponding pairs follow, and is that ordering defined by position in the first document, the second, or both?
4. [theory] Within a single corresponding pair, what must the offsets and extents of each span express about where the shared content sits in each document?
5. [theory] When a document is compared with itself, must the result be the trivial whole-extent identity, or must the system also report internal correspondences where the same content appears at multiple places in that one document?
6. [theory] When the comparison is restricted to portions of documents rather than their full extents, what must the system guarantee about correspondences that cross or only partially overlap the requested boundaries?
7. [theory] If content traveled from one document to the other through a chain of intermediate documents, must the correspondence still be reported, and does the answer depend on the length of that chain?
8. [theory] Must the reported correspondence be complete — guaranteed to include every span of shared content between the two compared regions, with nothing omitted and nothing spurious added?
9. [theory] If either document is rearranged after content is shared between them, what must remain stable about the correspondence the system reports before and after that rearrangement?
10. [theory] Is correspondence required to be symmetric — must comparing document A with document B yield exactly the mirror of comparing B with A?
11. [evidence] When SHOWRELATIONOF2VERSIONS finds two POOM entries in the compared documents whose I-spans overlap, what exactly does it return for each correspondence — a pair of VSpecs with document IDs, V-start positions, and widths, or some other structure?
12. [evidence] In correspond.c, is the correspondence test pure I-address interval intersection between the two documents' POOM entries, or does it ever consult the granfilade bytes — meaning two spans with identical text but distinct I-addresses can never correspond?
13. [evidence] When a shared I-span overlaps only partially with the queried specset boundaries, does SHOWRELATIONOF2VERSIONS clip the returned correspondence pair to the specset boundary, and are the V-offsets within each returned pair guaranteed to denote the same I-width on both sides?
14. [evidence] What determines the ordering of returned correspondence pairs — V-address order in the first specset, V-address order in the second, or I-address order from the traversal of the underlying enfilades?
15. [evidence] If document A contains the same I-addresses at two different V-positions (self-transclusion) and document B contains them once, does SHOWRELATIONOF2VERSIONS return two correspondence pairs (one per V-occurrence in A), and via what mechanism — does incontextlistnd enumerate all V-positions per shared I-span?
16. [evidence] When a specset is compared against itself (same document, same span), does correspond.c return the trivial identity pair covering the whole span, one pair per POOM bottom crum, or also the off-diagonal pairs arising from within-document shared I-addresses?
17. [evidence] When the compared specsets cover only sub-ranges of each document rather than full extents, is the correspondence computation restricted before the POOM walk (via retrieverestricted-style bounded traversal) or computed over full documents and filtered afterward?
18. [evidence] If document A's shared content has been rearranged so that a single contiguous I-span in B maps to multiple non-contiguous V-positions in A, does the operation fragment the correspondence into multiple pairs, and is the fragmentation count equal to the number of POOM bottom crums covering that I-span?
19. [evidence] Does SHOWRELATIONOF2VERSIONS read only the two documents' POOMs, or does it consult the spanfilade — and therefore can stale DOCISPAN entries for deleted content ever cause a correspondence to be reported that no current POOM entry supports?
20. [evidence] The KB records that compare_versions crashes when either document contains link orgls in its POOM (EC-COMPARE-CRASH) — what specific assumption in correspond.c breaks on link-subspace entries: the I-address structure of link orgls, the V-width/I-width exponent encoding, or the subspace boundary in the traversal cuts?
