## Issue 1: Contradictory characterization of the faceted link
Reason: The two passages clash on whether a faceted link is a single N-ary link or a chain of ternary links glued by link-to-link references — both readings trace to Nelson (the n-set call at LM 4/79 vs. the CONS-cell quote), so resolving which is the model's normative commitment requires Nelson's design intent.
Nelson question: Did the design intend a faceted link relating more than three roles to be a single higher-arity link, or to be composed from a chain of three-endset links via link-to-link references — and is one of these the primary mechanism with the other merely permitted?
