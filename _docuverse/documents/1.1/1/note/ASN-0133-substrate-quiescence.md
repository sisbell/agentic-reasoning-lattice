# ASN-0133: Substrate Quiescence

*The terminal state, recognizable from inside the predicate language; termination as a conditional theorem with every hypothesis named.*

ASN-0129 supplies the language a coordination system's conditions are written in, and the dynamics classes (PD0–PD2, ST/SF) that say how a condition's truth moves. ASN-0130 makes the conditions substrate artifacts. This note closes the arc: it defines what it is for such a system to be *done* — quiescence — and proves what can and cannot be guaranteed about reaching it. The shape of the answer is fixed by a fact about forward-chaining systems generally: unconditional termination is not provable; every honest treatment names conditions (extinction discipline, bounded work, fair scheduling) under which termination follows. The note's discipline is to put each hypothesis where it belongs — what the substrate guarantees unconditionally (recognizability, absorption), what a rule author can make checkable at registration (extinction, via the spelling classes), and what remains assumption (fairness, bounded work) stated as named hypotheses rather than smuggled.

**Depends:** ASN-0086 (Typed Relations on Address Sets), ASN-0126 (Substrate Shape Framework), ASN-0128 (Substrate Type Operational Semantics), ASN-0129 (Substrate Predicate Composition), ASN-0130 (Predicate Definitions as Substrate Content).

## What this note commits

- **The rule model** (RG): a minimal trigger/emission unit — a domain in QD, a Boolean PL trigger, an emission contract over the operation surface — with the action body deliberately opaque: the contract constrains outputs, never algorithms. A registry is a finite set of rules; a rule's trigger may be a registered definition address (ASN-0130), making registries themselves substrate-described.
- **Recognizability and absorption, unconditional** (Q0, Q1): quiescence — no trigger true on any domain element — is a PL term: pure, decidable at every reachable state by any observer, and a fixed point of firing once reached.
- **Extinction discipline** (X-DEF, Q2–Q4) with the class-theoretic upgrade (Q-EXT): a rule whose trigger is an **SF spelling** (ASN-0129) and whose fires falsify it gets *at-most-once firing per argument* as a theorem — the falsification is permanent by the class, not by analysis of other rules. This is the Marker pattern's termination half, and the certificate that makes it registration-checkable is exactly the SF certificate ASN-0130's Open Question 4 anticipates.
- **Conditional termination** (Q5, Q5a, Q6): real fires are bounded under extinction discipline plus the bounded-work hypothesis (H-W) — with the SF case (Q5a) needing only domain-growth bounds — and quiescence is reached in finitely many steps under the fairness hypothesis (H-FAIR). Both hypotheses are named, stated, and not discharged here: H-W is provably *meta-level* (its statement quantifies over reachability, which PL deliberately cannot express), and H-FAIR is a scheduler property this corpus has chosen not to axiomatize beyond the statement.
- **Scope parameterization** (Q7–Q9): everything above relativizes to a scope — any Boolean PL predicate — with scope quiescence itself in PL, recognizability and absorption inherited per-scope, and anti-monotone nesting (outer quiescence implies inner, never conversely).
- **The falsifier accounting** (Q-FLIP): what can re-arm a trigger is not "retraction" but exactly ASN-0129's falsifier inventory — retraction, BH1-type emissions under the default view, BH4's home-traffic footprint — read off FP and PD1/PD2. Termination analyses that assume "no retraction ⟹ triggers flip at most once" are unsound against the shipped view machinery, and this note replaces that folklore with the class-checked accounting.

## The rule model

**RG (Rule, Registry).** A *rule* is a triple `ρ = (D_ρ, T_ρ, Post_ρ)`: a domain expression `D_ρ ∈ QD`, a Boolean trigger `T_ρ : D_ρ → Bool` in PL, and an *emission contract* `Post_ρ` — a PL-expressible predicate over (argument, state, emitted call set) constraining what any fire of ρ must emit, the calls drawn from the operation surface `{Emit_K, Nullify_Binary}` (ASN-0128; `Observe_K` reads, and reads are not emissions). A *registry* `R` is a finite set of rules. A *fire* of `(ρ, x)` at Σ with `x ∈ [D_ρ]_Σ` is: if `T_ρ(x, Σ) = ⊥`, a no-op (`Σ' = Σ`); otherwise the application of some emission set satisfying `Post_ρ(x, Σ, ·)` — a finite sequence of `→_sh` steps through the surface (the gated relation, ASN-0126, over extended-record states by R-TR, ASN-0128), `Σ →_sh* Σ'`. **(H-FIN, a named hypothesis on the fire itself.)** That the sequence is finite is inherited, not assumed away: each surface call (`Emit_K`, `Nullify_Binary`) is a single bounded `→_sh` step (ASN-0128), so a fire terminates exactly when the contract it discharges admits a *finite* emission set — H-FIN is the contract-level demand that it does, separate from the *registry*-level termination (Q5/Q6) of the fire *sequence*. The body that *chooses* the emissions is deliberately outside the model: two implementations producing the same emissions are indistinguishable, so every property below is a property of contracts and substrate state, never of algorithms — and because a contract may admit many emission sets, every termination claim below is read **universally over the admissible choices**: a nondeterministic body terminates iff *every* `Post_ρ`-satisfying fire sequence it can produce does. A trigger may be given as a registered definition address (`pdef`, ASN-0130) evaluated by reference (PR3) — the registry is then itself substrate content, its triggers linkable, versioned, and certified like any definition. Because evaluation-by-reference keys on *ever-registration*, not active registration (PR3), such a trigger stays evaluable — and quiescence (Q0) stays recognizable — even after its definition is de-registered: the content persists, so the verdict never becomes undecidable for want of a withdrawn endorsement. A trigger that itself *references* a since-de-registered definition is the dangling-live-reference case ASN-0130's Open Question 3 leaves open; its evaluation still computes (PR3 reads content, not registration), but it crosses a revoked endorsement, and what that should mean is settled there, not here. A registry built from inline PL triggers depends on ASN-0130 not at all — its triggers are PL terms evaluated directly (PC4/PC5), and every property below rests on ASN-0129 and the operation surface alone. The pdef-trigger option is the only place ASN-0130 enters, and there it enters with ASN-0130's own scoping: evaluation-by-reference carries its guarantees on registration-disciplined derivations (PR-DISC, ASN-0130) — the regime in which the audit-slice spelling of "ever-registered" and the standing registration coincide. A registry whose triggers are pdef addresses therefore inherits PR-DISC as a standing hypothesis, discharged the same way ASN-0130 discharges it (every `pdef` deposit enters through `register_pred`); a registry of inline triggers carries no such hypothesis. Recognizability and absorption (Q0, Q1) hold unconditionally in both cases — PR-DISC conditions only the link between a pdef-trigger's address and the definition it is taken to name, never the decidability of the trigger's PL verdict.

**Q0 (Recognizability).** Define `quiescent_R(Σ) ≡ (∀ ρ ∈ R :: (∀ x ∈ [D_ρ]_Σ :: ¬T_ρ(x, Σ)))`. Then `quiescent_R ∈ PL`, and its value at every reachable Σ is decidable in finite time by any observer from Σ and the registry alone. *Proof.* R is finite; each inner quantification is PC1 over a QD domain, finite at every reachable state (QD-fin, ASN-0129); negation is PC0. Purity (PC4) makes the verdict observer-uniform — no agent reports, no consensus, no decision history; termination of the evaluation is PC5. ∎

**Q1 (Absorption).** A quiescent state is a fixed point of firing: at any Σ with `quiescent_R(Σ)`, every fire of every `(ρ, x)` is a no-op, so `Σ' = Σ` — and the system may halt on first detection, no further monitoring required. *Proof.* Immediate from RG's no-op clause and Q0's definition. ∎ Recognizability and absorption are *unconditional* — they hold for undisciplined registries, unfair schedulers, and divergent systems alike. They say nothing about whether quiescence is reachable; that is the rest of the note.

## Extinction discipline

Reaching quiescence requires that firing *consumes* the condition that fired. Without a contract to that effect, a rule can fire forever on the same argument.

**X-DEF (ExtinctionDisciplined).** Rule ρ is *extinction-disciplined* iff for every reachable Σ and `x ∈ [D_ρ]_Σ` with `T_ρ(x, Σ) = ⊤`, every fire of `(ρ, x)` yields Σ' with `T_ρ(x, Σ') = ⊥`. A registry is extinction-disciplined iff each rule is.

**Q2 (ContractOnOutputs).** Extinction discipline constrains emissions, not bodies: it is decided by `T_ρ` evaluations at the pre- and post-states — both public PL facts — so any two bodies with the same outputs are equivalent under it, and nondeterministic bodies are admissible whenever *all* their permitted outputs flip the trigger.

**Q3 (StaticCheckability).** If `Post_ρ` is *strong enough* — every emission set satisfying it at a trigger-true `(x, Σ)` produces a post-state falsifying `T_ρ(x, ·)` — then ρ's extinction discipline is verifiable at registration time, without executing any body: the implication ranges over PL-typed objects and the surface's emission forms. Sufficient, not necessary: a trivially weak contract (`Post_ρ ≡ ⊤`) certifies nothing, and a rule may be disciplined for reasons its contract is too weak to express. Failure-to-verify is not violation.

**Q4 (Locality).** Extinction discipline is per-rule: its definition mentions no other rule. Registries compose pointwise — and precisely because the property is local, it cannot alone be sufficient for termination: locally disciplined rules can re-arm *each other* without bound (Q5's hypothesis is doing real work).

**Q-EXT (ExtinctionByClass).** If `T_ρ` is an **SF spelling** (⊥-stable, PD0, ASN-0129), extinction discipline strengthens to *at-most-once firing per argument along any derivation*: the disciplined fire makes `T_ρ(x, ·)` false, and SF makes false permanent — no later step of any rule, on any target, re-arms it. The count of real fires of ρ is then bounded by the total growth of `[D_ρ]` alone, with no analysis of other rules' emissions. *Proof.* Immediate composition of X-DEF with PD0's ⊥-stability. ∎ This is the design rule the Marker pattern instantiates: spell the trigger as "no completion marker recorded" (`¬∃` over a grow-only audit slice — ASN-0086's typed-relation machinery, SF by the quantifier rule), and fire by emitting the marker. The check is registration-time and spelling-level — exactly the SF certificate ASN-0130's Open Question 4 anticipates; with it, "every rule in this registry is at-most-once" becomes a structural lint.

**Q-FLIP (FalsifierAccounting).** For triggers *not* in SF, what can re-arm them is the falsifier inventory ASN-0129's FP enumerates, read off with PD1/PD2: a retraction shrinking an active slice the trigger reads; a BH1-type emission moving a default-view result; a BH4-footprint change from any deposit in a watched home. The folklore rule "no retraction ⟹ each trigger flips at most once" is therefore unsound against the shipped view machinery — a registry whose triggers read default views or age atoms re-arms without any retraction. Trigger-flip counting must be done per spelling class, and the conservative design stance falls out: *triggers in SF, termination conditions in ST*, both certifiable.

## Conditional termination

**H-FAIR (FairnessHypothesis).** A fire sequence `σ = (Σ₀, fire₁, Σ₁, …)` is *fair* iff every `(ρ, x)` trigger-true at some Σ_k is eventually either fired or removed from its domain (`x ∉ [D_ρ]_{Σ_m}`, m > k — domains can shrink under retraction, and the scheduler owes nothing to absent arguments). Fairness is a scheduler property; this corpus states it as a hypothesis and deliberately ships no scheduler note — any discipline satisfying the statement (round-robin, queue-fair, priority with aging) discharges it.

**W, H-W (BoundedWork).** `W(σ)` is the set of (rule, argument, index) triples at which a trigger is true along σ; the registry has *bounded work from Σ₀* (H-W) iff `|W(σ)| < ∞` for every σ from Σ₀. H-W is a registry-level hypothesis, and *provably meta-level*: its statement quantifies over the states reachable by fire sequences, and reachability is a fixed-point notion PL deliberately cannot express (PC6a ships no fixpoint operator; the closure admits only finite quantification). The substrate can therefore neither evaluate H-W at a state nor monitor it as a predicate — a runtime defense against its violation is necessarily bookkeeping (fire counts, thresholds), not evaluation. Sufficient conditions are structural: an all-SF registry with bounded domain growth (Q5a); a *stratified* registry — `stratum : R → ℕ` with no rule's emissions re-arming a strictly lower stratum — with per-stratum bounds; acyclicity of the emission/re-arm graph. None is necessary.

**Q5 (RealFiresAreBounded).** Under extinction discipline and H-W, every fire sequence from Σ₀ contains at most `|W(σ)|` real (non-no-op) fires. *Proof.* Each real fire at step k+1 witnesses `(ρ, x, k) ∈ W(σ)`; extinction discipline makes distinct real fires witness distinct triples (a second fire on the same `(ρ, x)` requires an intervening re-arm, which re-indexes the witnessed triple). Injection into a finite set bounds the count. ∎

**Q5a (ExtinctionBound).** For an all-SF registry (every trigger an SF spelling), H-W reduces to bounded domain growth: real fires number at most `Σ_ρ |⋃_k [D_ρ]_{Σ_k}|` — each argument fires each rule at most once (Q-EXT), so the only unbounded-work route is unbounded *new* arguments. The W machinery, the re-arm analysis, and the stratification heuristic are all unnecessary in this case; this is the quantitative payoff of the SF design rule.

**Q6 (TerminationUnderFairness).** Under Q5's hypotheses and H-FAIR, the system reaches a quiescent state in finitely many steps. *Proof.* After Q5's bound is exhausted every fire is a no-op and the state is constant (Q1's argument applies pointwise). If the constant state were not quiescent, some `(ρ, x)` would be trigger-true forever after; H-FAIR forces it to be fired (a real fire — contradiction with the bound) or domain-removed (contradiction with constancy). ∎ The theorem is exactly as conditional as it looks: drop extinction discipline and a rule can spin on one argument; drop H-W and disciplined rules can re-arm each other forever; drop H-FAIR and a trigger-true argument can be starved eternally with the system never settling. Each hypothesis has its own failure mode, and none is derivable from the others.

## Scope parameterization

**SC (Scope), Q7–Q9.** A *scope* is any Boolean PL predicate `S` over addresses; `quiescent_{R,S}(Σ)` restricts Q0's inner quantification to arguments satisfying S. Then: **Q7** — `quiescent_{R,S} ∈ PL` for every PL scope (PC1's filtered quantification), so scope quiescence inherits Q0's recognizability and observer-uniformity wholesale. **Q8** — at a scope-quiescent state, in-scope fires are no-ops (absorption relativizes); out-of-scope fires are unconstrained, and an out-of-scope emission may re-arm an in-scope trigger — *re-entry* — which is itself detectable per-state by Q7. **Q9** — quiescence is anti-monotone in the scope: `S' ⟹ S` gives `quiescent_S ⟹ quiescent_{S'}`; the converse fails whenever the larger scope holds work no smaller scope sees. Canonical tiers (per-target, per-collection, system-wide) are application vocabulary over this machinery — the substrate admits any PL predicate as a scope and commits to nothing about which scopes matter.

## Worked composition

The two illustrative classes of ASN-0129's worked trace — `cmt` (Binary, idem=⊥, comment from finding to target) and `res` (Binary, idem=⊤, resolution from target to comment) — already form a registry; we verify its termination.

*Rules.* Producer `ρ_P`: domain = targets; trigger `T_P(t) ≡ needs_attention(t) ∧ ¬(∃ c ∈ L_cmt :: t ∈ coverage_G(c))` — `L_cmt` the audit slice (ASN-0086's typed-relation machinery) — fires by emitting one `cmt` targeting t. Resolver `ρ_R`: domain = `L_cmt` (audit slice — grow-only); trigger `T_R(c) ≡ ¬(∃ r ∈ L_res :: addr(c) ∈ coverage_G(r))` — fires by emitting one `res` targeting c.

*Class check.* Both triggers are negated existentials over grow-only audit slices with step-constant bodies — **SF spellings** by PD0's quantifier and Boolean rules. (Spelled over *active* slices instead, neither would be: a retraction of a resolution would re-open the comment — Q-FLIP's first falsifier — and termination would need the full H-W analysis. The audit spelling is the design choice, made checkable.)

*Extinction.* `Post_P` requires an emitted `cmt` covering t; `Post_R` an emitted `res` covering `addr(c)` — each strong enough (Q3): the emission makes the trigger's existential true, hence the trigger false. With SF, Q-EXT gives at-most-once per argument for both rules.

*Bound (Q5a).* Real fires ≤ |targets| + |comments ever|. Comments are emitted only by `ρ_P`, at most one per target — so the registry terminates iff the *target* population is bounded, which is the registry's one honest hypothesis on its environment. The classic failure mode is visible in the accounting: let the resolver's emissions also create new targets (`needs_attention` flipping ⊤ on fresh addresses) and the bound's right side grows with the left — locally disciplined rules, globally divergent registry, exactly the divergence Q4 warns locality cannot exclude. Stratification states the repair: `ρ_P` at stratum 0, `ρ_R` at stratum 1, legal iff resolver emissions never re-arm the producer.

*Quiescence.* `quiescent_R` here is precisely "every target commented, every comment resolved" — two nested SF/ST-classed quantifications, in PL, decidable at every state (Q0), absorbing once reached (Q1), and — for this registry — reached in finitely many steps given bounded targets and any fair scheduler (Q6).

## What this note doesn't cover

- **A scheduler.** H-FAIR is stated, not constructed; scheduling disciplines, their fairness proofs, concurrency reconciliation, and violation policy (log, halt, escalate) are operational machinery this corpus deliberately leaves at the implementation layer.
- **Stochastic rule bodies.** A body that flips its trigger with probability p < 1 satisfies the deterministic contracts only operationally (retry policies, N-consecutive-clean stopping rules) — evaluator-reliability engineering, not substrate semantics.
- **The activation binding.** RG assumes rules exist; what registers them, who may, and how rule registries are themselves governed is the protocol layer above (with ASN-0130's machinery available to it).

## Open questions

1. **The SF certificate.** Q-EXT and Q5a make SF membership the load-bearing registration check for triggers, and ASN-0130 ships only the ST⁺ certificate (`pd_stable`). Should `pd_extinct` (SF certification) ship as a designated class — exactly the catalog-growth ASN-0130's own Open Question 4 raises for certificates beyond ST⁺ — closing the loop where "this registry terminates on bounded input" is a structural lint?

2. **A PL surrogate for H-W.** H-W is meta-level; is there a PL-expressible *necessary* condition — a per-state predicate whose failure certifies unbounded work in progress — strong enough to catch the common divergence patterns (mutual re-arm cycles) at runtime, even though the full hypothesis is inexpressible?

3. **Per-scope versus global work.** Q5/Q6 are stated globally; the per-scope analogs (W restricted to in-scope arguments, termination toward `quiescent_S`) appear routine, but whether a registry can have bounded work at every scope of a tier without global H-W deserves a structural answer — it is the formal version of "every document converges but the corpus doesn't."

4. **Cross-scope oscillation.** Q8's re-entry means an outer-scope fire can repeatedly un-quiesce an inner scope. What registry or scheduling discipline bounds re-entry — and whether bounded re-entry is even the right demand, versus monotone progress *across* entries — is open, and is where a future cascade/re-opening theory would live.

5. **Contract necessity.** Q3 is sufficiency only: must every extinction-disciplined rule admit a strong-enough PL contract? A rule disciplined by reasoning PL cannot express would be sound but unverifiable at registration — the same sound-but-uncertifiable gap PD0's spelling-level classes accept by design.
