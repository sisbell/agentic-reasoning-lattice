# ASN-0129: Substrate Predicate Composition

*From atomic predicates to the substrate's full query language — Boolean composition, quantification, aggregation, and the expressive ceiling.*

ASN-0128 ships the atomic read surface of the typed-relation substrate: per-type default predicates (D1–D3), behavior-unlocked predicates (BH1–BH4), the address-denotation discipline that types their arguments and results (AD/AM), and the three result-set views (audit, active, default). Each atom is specified singly. This note closes the chain: it defines what is expressible *from* the atoms — the algebra of Boolean composition, finite quantification, value composition, and aggregation under which the atomic vocabulary generates the substrate's full predicate language — and fixes the ceiling: relative to the substrate's enumerated read primitives, under the syntax-directed evaluation class PC6 defines, the language is *exactly* that closure (PC6), no richer and no poorer. Composition extends expressiveness within a ceiling fixed by the registry's shapes and behaviors; it never raises the ceiling.

The consumers are protocols: any coordination machinery built over the substrate — triggers ("act when P"), termination conditions ("stop when Q"), gating disciplines — is *written in* this language. PL is thereby the substrate's *extension language*: a builder registers types and composes predicates, and composition is the **only** extension mechanism — which is precisely why the guarantees hold for every predicate any builder will ever write, the substrate never executing foreign read-path code, only evaluating terms of a closed algebra. The properties proved here are what such machinery needs: purity (PC4) and termination (PC5) make predicates safe to evaluate concurrently and guaranteed to halt; the closure ceiling (PC6) makes the language's limits knowable; and the dynamics classification (PD0–PD2) tells a protocol author how a predicate's truth *moves* across transitions — the difference between a sound termination condition and a livelock. This note is the complete predicate foundation; what protocols are built, and under what scheduler, is application-layer territory fenced at the end.

**Depends:** ASN-0034 (Tumbler Algebra), ASN-0043 (Link Ontology), ASN-0086 (Typed Relations on Address Sets), ASN-0126 (Substrate Shape Framework), ASN-0127 (Content-Region Link Query — boundary only: the arrangement-reading query layer PL deliberately excludes), ASN-0128 (Substrate Type Operational Semantics).

## What this note commits

- **The atomic vocabulary, generated.** `V_atom` is derived per registered type from its registration record: a core template family every type receives, plus a behavior family per attached behavior — reconciling ASN-0128's fixed catalog with template generation. Four admissions are this note's own, each fenced where introduced: audit readings for the core family (V-AUD), per-tuple projections and coverage-membership tests (V-TUP), the state-independent primitives, constants, and literals (V-PRIM), and the conservative ⊥-totalization of `age` (V, BH4 family). The vocabulary is static (V-STAT), a theorem of registry invariance rather than a stipulation.
- **Four composition primitives.** Boolean closure (PC0), finite quantification over substrate-derived domains (PC1, with `Reg`-indexed families defined by static expansion, V-IDX; address-valued domains double as `℘_fin(T)`-valued terms, QD-refl), value composition through state-indexed constituents with binder-guarded partiality (PC2), and aggregation — counts, T1-extrema, and finite unions over finite domains (PC2a). Aggregation settles the count question: PL counts *sets of addresses or tuples in a view*, never bags; cross-type composites sum per-type set cardinalities by V-PRIM's admitted ℕ-addition (settling ASN-0128 Open Question 2).
- **Three-view parametricity.** Every composed predicate evaluates against a view in `{audit, active, default}` (ASN-0128's lenses), with one per-codomain rule for the default view (UV) that settles ASN-0128 Open Question 1: filtering rewrites collection-valued enumeration results elementwise — never verdicts, never arguments, never traversal, never membership.
- **Evaluation guarantees.** Purity (PC4) and termination (PC5) for every PL term at every reachable state.
- **The ceiling.** PC6 (ExpressiveClosure): predicates computable by *syntax-directed evaluation* over the substrate's read-primitive base — control trees fixed by term syntax, single-pass folds, iteration only inside the atom set, no evaluation-time feedback — are exactly PL; against unrestricted base-read computation (feedback loops over base reads, which provably decide `reach` on the finite denoted graph), PC6 asserts no ceiling — an unrestricted ceiling would *entail* ¬C-reach, so this note's conjecture of C-reach is implicitly a conjecture that the unrestricted ceiling fails; the ceiling PC6 asserts is for syntax-directed evaluation only. PC6a (FixpointExclusion) is the syntactic half of the traversal fence: PL ships no fixed-point or recursion operator. Whether transitive closure (`reach`) is nonetheless *expressible* by some PL term is conjectured not (C-reach) — a conjecture, deliberately: PL's counting, built-in orders, and walk atoms put the question outside the fixed-depth first-order regime where the standard inexpressibility theorem lives, so ASN-0128's withholding of multi-hop traversal stands as a design stance made precise here, not promoted to a theorem.
- **Predicate dynamics.** A classification of PL terms by behavior across `→_sh` steps — monotone (PD0, an inductive polarity class), non-monotone with the retraction counterexample (PD1), frame-stable (PD2, via per-atom read footprints, FP) — the minimal stability theory a protocol author needs to choose trigger and termination predicates soundly.

## The atomic vocabulary

ASN-0128's registration record `(shape, idem, behaviors)` determines, per registered coverage class K, which read predicates exist. We make the determination a generator.

**V (AtomicVocabulary).** For each registered K, the *template family* `Tpl(record(K))` is:

- *Core family* — present for every K, whatever the record: the membership atom `is_K : T → Bool` (D2, ASN-0128) and the enumeration atoms `members(K, view) : ℘_fin(T)` (D1) and `targets_of(x, view) : T → ℘_fin(T)` (D3). For Unary K, `targets_of` is constantly ∅ (D3); the template still instantiates — degenerately, not absently.
- *BH1 family* (when read-filter is attached): `is_filtered : T → Bool`.
- *BH2 family* (when determinate-walk is attached): `succs : T → ℘_fin(T)`, `chain : T → Seq_fin(T)`, `tip : T → T ∪ {⊥}`, `is_in_chain : T × T → Bool`.
- *BH3 family* (when typed-reverse-lookup is attached): `sources_to : T → ℘_fin(T)`, `target_of : T → T ∪ {⊥}`, and the join `targets_keyed : T → Map_fin`.
- *BH4 family* (when age-staleness is attached): `age : T → ℕ ∪ {⊥}` and `stale : ℕ → ℘_fin(T)`. ASN-0128 types `age` as a partial function into ℕ, defined on active tuples only; PL totalizes the same graph by adjoining the verdict — `age(a) = ⊥` exactly when `a` is not the address of an active K-tuple. The re-typing is conservative (no defined value changes) and load-bearing: an unguarded partial atom would make definedness *state-dependent* — a retraction turning a defined term undefined — poisoning PC4/PC5's universal claims and the dynamics classification with them. That definedness-stability ground carries the totalization alone; with ⊥ a value, PC2's guard discipline applies unchanged. (The test `age(a) ≠ ⊥` (V-PRIM) doubles as a tuple-address activeness test, but composition does not otherwise lack one: no *atom* tests tuple-address activeness — `is_K` tests F-coverage, not tuple addresses — yet `(∃ x ∈ A_K :: addr(x) = a)`, PC1 over `A_K` with V-TUP's `addr` and V-PRIM's address equality, tests exactly that for every registered K, BH4-attached or not.)

The atomic vocabulary is `V_atom = ⋃_{K registered} Tpl(record(K))`, each atom's active- and default-view semantics exactly its ASN-0128 specification. This note's own additions are exactly four, each conservative and each fenced where introduced: the audit readings of the core family (V-AUD), the per-tuple projections (V-TUP), the state-independent primitives (V-PRIM), and `age`'s ⊥-totalization above. Only the first two extend what the vocabulary *reads* — V-PRIM reads no state, and the totalization re-types an existing read without changing any defined value — but all four extend the vocabulary itself, and PC6's leaf enumeration counts vocabulary, so the bookkeeping counts four. The `idem` component contributes no atom: it is emit-side machinery (I1, I6), and its one read-side consequence — at-most-one active tuple per I0-class along surface-emitted derivations (I1a) — is a property of states, not a predicate former. Write operations (`Emit_K`, `Nullify_Binary`, `retract_stale`) are outside `V_atom` by construction: predicates read.

Atoms may be *internally iterative*: `chain` walks with a state-dependent step count, bounded by ASN-0128's BH2 termination argument. Internal iteration lives inside the atom, behind its proven bound; the composition primitives below add none. This division is load-bearing three ways: it is where PC6's evaluation class draws its line (iteration only inside atoms; composition adds folds but no feedback), it is the grammar fact PC6a states, and it is the honest framing of C-reach.

**V-AUD (AuditReadings).** ASN-0128 specifies the core family at `active` (D1–D3) and `default` (BH1's rewrite scope); it gives no audit reading, while PC3 ranges every term over three views — so the audit reading is a gap this note must own as a definition rather than claim to inherit. We define it as the evident lift of D1–D3 from the active slice to the audit slice:

`members(K, audit) = ⋃ { addrs(F) : (a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ }`

`targets_of(x, audit) = ⋃ { addrs(G) : (a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ ∧ x ∈ addrs(F) }`

`is_K(addr)` at audit ≡ `(∃ (a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ :: addr ∈ coverage(F))`

— finite and computable by the same bounds as the active readings (L-fin, AD), and AM-conformant (source arguments matched by denotation, the membership test by coverage). The behavior atoms receive *no* audit reading: BH2's walk "reads the active view, never the audit slice" (ASN-0128), and BH1, BH3, and BH4 are likewise defined against active slices — they are *fixed-view* atoms (PC3), their slice part of their meaning.

**V-TUP (TupleProjections).** QD below admits tuple-valued domains (`A_K`, `L_K`), and a bound tuple needs vocabulary to act on it: without per-tuple reads, a filter over `L_K` has no expressible body, and "a test of stored tuple content" would name a predicate the language does not contain. For a variable `x` bound to a tuple `(a, F, G)` (the slices are triple-restricted, ASN-0086), we admit:

- `addr(x) : T` — the tuple address;
- `addrs_F(x) : ℘_fin(T)` and `addrs_G(x) : ℘_fin(T)` — the denoted sets of the from- and to-endsets (AD's `addrs(·)`);
- `t ∈ coverage_F(x)` and `t ∈ coverage_G(x)` : `Bool` — coverage-*membership* tests, decidable span-by-span via T2 over the finitely many spans (AD). Membership only: `coverage(F)` itself is an infinite address set, outside COD, and never a term value.

These are ASN-0128's two argument regimes localized to one tuple — coverage tests in the membership regime, `addrs` projections in the enumeration regime — so the AD/AM discipline is preserved per tuple, and no new conversion between regimes arises. For a fixed binding of `x`, every V-TUP read is constant across states, the stored value being immutable (L12); PD0 leans on this. V-TUP is also what closes PC6's converse: `Observe_K`'s pattern queries are per-tuple coverage conjunctions, expressible only with these primitives.

**V-PRIM (PrimitiveAdmission).** The *state-independent primitives* are admitted to the vocabulary as degenerate atoms:

- the comparisons on addresses: address equality, the prefix order `≼`, and the total order T1 (ASN-0034) — each Boolean-valued, reading no state; T2 (IntrinsicComparison, ASN-0034) is not a fourth comparison but the computability warrant for these three — the theorem that each is decided from the two operands alone;
- the finite-set operations on `℘_fin(T)`-valued terms: membership (`t ∈ S`), set equality, and the emptiness test (`S = ∅`) — each a finite enumeration of an already-finite value — and `elems : Seq_fin(T) → ℘_fin(T)`, the order-forgetting projection (on `chain`'s output it loses no count — the returned sequence's elements are pairwise distinct, the walk's being so by BH2 and UV's rewrite only deleting — so `count(elems(chain(t)))` is the *returned sequence's* length: the walk's length at term views `audit` and `active`, but at `default` the rewritten sequence's length, strictly shorter whenever a traversed element is filtered, UV);
- the meta-level ℕ operations: the comparisons (`=`, `≤`) and addition (`+`) on ℕ-valued terms — addition is the operation PC2a's cross-type sums are made of, admitted as vocabulary rather than left ambient;
- the map lookup, per registered class: `·[K] : Map_fin → T ∪ {⊥}` on `Map_fin`-valued terms, `K` entering as an index — a named class or a V-IDX-bound class variable, statically expanded — with the result composing through PC2's binder guard like any verdict. The lookup is deliberately redundant where its key exists — for BH3-attached Binary `K`, `targets_keyed(s)[K] = target_of(s, K)` by BH3's join, an absent key reading ⊥ exactly where `target_of` verdicts ⊥ (ASN-0128) — and is admitted so that `Map_fin` has a consumer: without one, the join's result would be a root-position-only value, composable with nothing and comparable with nothing — dead vocabulary in an algebra whose converse direction enumerates its forms exhaustively;
- constants and literals, one closed form per COD entry that needs one: the Boolean constants `⊤` and `⊥`, ℕ literals (the `n` of `under_cap`, the `h` of `stale`), address literals (query data — the `t` of `OPEN(t)`, pattern addresses), and the verdict constant `⊥` of each ⊥-adjoined codomain;
- the definedness tests on the ⊥-adjoined codomains: `def : (T ∪ {⊥}) → Bool` and `def : (ℕ ∪ {⊥}) → Bool`, written `· ≠ ⊥` — the conditions of PC2's binder guard. Equality of a defined value against an address or a numeral is reached *through* the binder (`if f(s) is some y then y = c else …`), whose bound `y` carries the narrowed base type (PC2), so no equality operation on a ⊥-adjoined codomain itself is needed, and none is admitted.

They enter terms exactly as atoms do: in Boolean position, in QD filter bodies (the membership and `≼` tests in the worked compositions), and in PC2 chains. Admitting them *as vocabulary*, rather than leaving them ambient, is load-bearing twice over: PC2's typing quantifies over vocabulary members, so a composition through a comparison needs the comparison to be one; and PC6's converse direction enumerates leaf forms exhaustively, which an unadmitted ambient leaf — a bare `⊤`, an undeclared numeral, an unowned definedness test — would silently break.

**V-STAT (VocabularyStaticity).** `V_atom` is identical at every reachable state. *Proof.* The template families and V-AUD/V-TUP are functions of the registration records alone, the extended record is constant at every `→_sh*`-reachable state (R1, ASN-0128), and V-PRIM reads no state. ∎ Registering a new K extends `V_atom` by `Tpl(record(K))` — at construction only (R-VAL, ASN-0128); no transition changes the vocabulary. The expressive ceiling is thereby pinned to `Σ_init`'s registry, inheriting ASN-0128's registry-evolution fence (its Open Question 7).

**COD (Codomains).** Atom and composite codomains are drawn from

`Codom = {Bool, ℘_fin(T), T ∪ {⊥}, Seq_fin(T), Map_fin, ℕ, ℕ ∪ {⊥}}`

— Boolean, finite address set, optional address, finite address sequence, finite map, natural number, optional natural number. Every entry is realized: `Bool` by the membership atoms, `℘_fin(T)` by the enumeration atoms, `T ∪ {⊥}` by the `tip`/`target_of` verdicts, `Seq_fin(T)` by `chain`, `Map_fin` by `targets_keyed`, `ℕ ∪ {⊥}` by `age`, and bare `ℕ` by PC2a's `count` and V-PRIM's literals. Every entry also has an admitted consumer — `Map_fin`, which would otherwise sit root-position-only, is consumed by V-PRIM's keyed lookup, admitted for exactly that reason. PC0–PC2a compose within `Codom` and introduce no codomain beyond it.

**Two argument regimes, inherited.** Composition respects ASN-0128's AD/AM discipline: membership atoms test coverage (total, decidable), enumeration atoms return denoted addresses and match their arguments per AM (denotation-keyed forward, coverage-keyed reverse). A composite cannot convert between regimes except where ASN-0128's own bridges license it (the D2 bridge; the `targets_under` recipe — which this note exhibits below as a PL term rather than an atom). V-TUP adds no conversion: its coverage tests and `addrs` projections are the same two regimes, localized to a bound tuple.

## Quantification domains

**QD (QuantificationDomains).** The class of *domain expressions* is the least class containing the base expressions

`M_K` (the member set at the term's view: `[M_K]_Σ = members(K, v)` for the view `v` PC3 binds), `A_K` (the active K-slice — view-fixed by name), `L_K` (the audit K-slice — view-fixed by name), `C_dom` (`dom(Σ.C)`), `M_dom` (`dom(Σ.M)`), `L_dom` (`dom(Σ.L)`), `Reg` (the registered coverage classes)

and closed under two formations:

- *Filtering*: if `D ∈ QD` and `P : D × S → Bool` is a Boolean PL predicate, then `{x ∈ D : P(x, ·)} ∈ QD` — a filter body may carry free arguments, making the domain expression *parameterized* (the worked `OPEN(t)` below); evaluation binds the parameters before interpreting.
- *Set-valued terms*: every `℘_fin(T)`-valued PL term, its parameters bound, is a domain expression denoting its own value — `targets_of(x)`, a PC2a union, an `elems(chain(t))`. Without this closure, aggregation would reach the bases but not the algebra's own set-valued results: "x targets at most n addresses" would be inexpressible while `under_cap` counts sources.

**QD-refl (TermReflection).** Every *address-valued* domain expression — one whose denotation is a subset of `T`: `M_K`, `C_dom`, `M_dom`, `L_dom`, a filter over an address-valued domain, and trivially every set-valued term (there reflection is the identity; the reflected `M_K` coincides with the `members` atom) — is itself a `℘_fin(T)`-valued PL term denoting `[D]_Σ`, the typing sound by QD-fin. This is the licensing rule that puts a domain in term position, where V-PRIM's set operations consume it — the worked `quiescent(t) ≡ OPEN(t) = ∅` is its use case. The address-valued restriction is load-bearing: `A_K` and `L_K` are tuple-valued and `Reg` class-valued — outside `℘_fin(T)`, indeed outside COD — so they serve as quantification and fold domains only, never as term values. Reflection adds no evaluation form: interpreting the reflected term is interpreting the domain, the same finite enumeration PC1's reduction performs.

`M_dom` is admitted deliberately: document-residence `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` is the emit surface's own gating clause (I1/I5, ASN-0128), and a gating discipline written in PL must be able to state what the surface checks. `QD` and `PL` are defined by mutual induction; the induction is well-founded on syntactic depth. Each `D ∈ QD` denotes at state Σ (and a binding of its parameters) a set `[D]_Σ` by the evident interpretation (`[Reg]_Σ` is state-independent by R1).

**H-init (InitialStoreFiniteness — hypothesis).** `|dom(Σ_init.C)| < ∞ ∧ |dom(Σ_init.M)| < ∞`. The upstream chain fixes `Σ_init`'s stores as ASN-0086's initial components verbatim and pins `Σ_init.L = ∅` (R-VAL, ASN-0128), but no foundation claim — in ASN-0086, ASN-0126, or ASN-0128 — states a cardinality for the initial content and arrangement components; their finiteness is therefore a named hypothesis of this note, not a citation. The natural reading — empty initial stores — satisfies it trivially, and everything downstream of QD-fin (PC1, PC2a, PC5) inherits it.

**QD-fin (DomainFiniteness).** `[D]_Σ` is finite for every `D ∈ QD` at every reachable Σ. *Proof.* Base cases: `dom(Σ.C)`, `dom(Σ.M)`, and `dom(Σ.L)` are finite by one induction on the reaching derivation — the base is H-init for the content and arrangement stores and `Σ_init.L = ∅` (R-VAL, ASN-0128) for the link store; the step, because each K.α adjoins at most one content address, each K.σ at most one document key, and each K.λ_sh at most one link key (step effects, GatedTransitionRelation, ASN-0126, as extended by R-TR, ASN-0128). (For the link store this re-derives, self-containedly at extended-record states, what L-fin (ASN-0043) asserts at its own reachable states — the induction spares the B2/RP-b transfer chain a bare L-fin citation would owe.) `A_K^Σ ⊆ L_K^Σ` inject into `dom(Σ.L)`, hence are finite; `M_K` is finite by D1's bound and V-AUD's (finitely many tuples by the link-store case just established, finitely many spans per endset, AD); `Reg` is finite by C0 (ASN-0126). Filtering yields subsets; a set-valued term denotes a finite set by its COD typing (each `℘_fin(T)`-valued atom is bounded by AD, and PC2a's `⋃` is a finite union of finite sets). ∎

## The composition primitives

**PC0 (BooleanClosure).** For PL predicates `P, Q : S → Bool`, the pointwise `P ∧ Q`, `P ∨ Q`, `¬P`, `P ⇒ Q`, `P ⇔ Q` are PL predicates of the same signature. Both constituents read the *same* Σ and the same view (PC3). The `S → Bool` functions over fixed Σ form a Boolean algebra under these operations; no further axiomatization is needed.

**PC1 (QuantificationClosure).** For `D ∈ QD` and a PL predicate `P : D × S → Bool`,

`(∀ x ∈ D :: P(x, ·)) : S → Bool`  and  `(∃ x ∈ D :: P(x, ·)) : S → Bool`

are PL predicates: by QD-fin the quantifiers reduce at each Σ to finite conjunctions and disjunctions over `[D]_Σ`, well-defined by PC0. Filtered domains compose freely (a filtered active slice is again in QD), and quantifying over `Reg` expresses cross-type questions no single K's atoms can ask.

**V-IDX (IndexedFamilies).** Quantification over `Reg` binds a *class variable*: in `(∀ K ∈ Reg :: Φ(K))` the body applies atom families at the bound class — `M_K`, `is_K`, `L_K` with `K` a variable — a term former that would otherwise be undefined. It is defined by *static expansion*: `[Reg]_Σ` is the same finite set at every state (R1, C0, via V-STAT), so the quantified term denotes the finite conjunction (disjunction) of the closed instances `Φ(K₀)`, one per registered class — substitution of named classes into template families, identical at every state. *Well-formedness is instance-wise*: a `Reg`-quantified term is well-formed iff each of its finitely many expansion instances `Φ(K₀)` is a PL term. The vocabulary is non-uniform across `Reg`, so the condition bites: a body confined to the core family and the fixed-view slices — present at every K whatever the record — is well-formed unconditionally, while a body applying a behavior-family atom (`succs`, `tip`, `age`, `targets_keyed`, …) at the bound class is well-formed only when the behavior — and with it, by R-C0's compatibility, the shape — is attached at *every* registered class: at a class lacking the family the instance is not in PL, and an expansion with a non-term instance is no term at all. The condition is decidable at construction: the registry is static (V-STAT), so the instance set is fixed with the vocabulary. No new semantic former is introduced; the binder is definitional shorthand, and PC6's tree decomposition sees only the expansion.

**PC2 (ValueComposition).** For a PL term `f : S → C₁` and a *state-indexed* PL term `g : C₁ × S → C₂` with matching types in `Codom`, the composition `s ↦ g(f(s), s) : S → C₂` is a PL term. The outer constituent may itself read state — the `is_retired` of the worked `head_live` below — and both constituents read the *same* Σ and the same view: PC0's provision, extended to value position (the state-independent case `g : C₁ → C₂` is the degenerate instance that ignores its state argument). *Partiality is guarded, never silent*: the ⊥-adjoined codomains `T ∪ {⊥}` and `ℕ ∪ {⊥}` (the `tip`/`target_of` verdicts and `age`'s activeness verdict) compose only through the *binder guard*

`if f(s) is some y then g(y, s) else c_default`

whose condition is V-PRIM's definedness test `def(f(s))`, a PC0 Boolean, and whose binder is the typing rule keyed to that test: in the then-branch, `y` names the defined value at the *narrowed* base type — `T` from `T ∪ {⊥}`, `ℕ` from `ℕ ∪ {⊥}` — so vocabulary typed over the base type (address equality, `≼`, `is_K`, the ℕ comparisons) applies to `y` with no coercion, and no operation on a ⊥-adjoined codomain beyond `def` is ever needed (none is admitted, V-PRIM). Partiality surfaces as a branch in the term, not as an undefined evaluation. ⊥ is a *verdict*, with meaning fixed by the atom that returns it (a branch, a cycle, multiplicity, inactivity); the guard propagates the verdict, it does not erase it.

**PC2a (AggregationClosure).** For `D ∈ QD`:

- `count(D) = |[D]_Σ| : ℕ`, with V-PRIM's ℕ comparisons in Boolean position and V-PRIM's `+` combining counts;
- `max_{T1}(D)` and `min_{T1}(D)` over address-valued domains: the T1-extremum of `[D]_Σ` when non-empty, ⊥ when empty — typed into `T ∪ {⊥}` and composing through PC2's binder guard — well-defined because T1 totally orders tumblers (ASN-0034) and the domain is finite;
- `⋃(D, f) : ℘_fin(T)` for a set-valued PL term `f : D → ℘_fin(T)`: the union `⋃_{x ∈ [D]_Σ} f(x, Σ)` — a finite union of finite sets (the `targets_under` recipe's outer operation).

The folds re-enter the algebra: a `℘_fin(T)`-valued fold result is itself a QD domain expression (QD's set-valued closure), so `count(targets_of(x))` and `count(⋃(D, f))` are well-typed — aggregation reaches every set-valued term, not only the bases. All folds are finite, over QD-fin domains — the same reduction PC1 performs with ∧/∨, performed with counting and comparison — so aggregation raises no expressiveness beyond the closure and needs no new evaluation machinery. Two commitments ride on PC2a. *Set semantics, settled*: `count` counts the elements of a domain interpretation — a set of addresses or tuples in the selected view — never occurrences; under `idem = ⊤` on surface-emitted derivations a per-class count is 0 or 1 (I1a, ASN-0128), under `idem = ⊥` the count is the deposit count because the *substrate* holds the duplicates as distinct tuples; the algebra itself is everywhere set-valued, and a cross-type composite is a sum of per-type counts taken with V-PRIM's admitted `+` (settling ASN-0128 Open Question 2: cross-type totals are PL terms — per-type set cardinalities combined by meta-level arithmetic, never a new counting mode). *Ordinal extrema, scoped*: `max_{T1}` over link addresses recovers emission order only within one home's chain (the ordinal-time doctrine, BH4, ASN-0128); cross-home T1 comparison is well-defined but temporally meaningless, and any "latest" built from it inherits the per-home scope.

**PC3 (ViewParametricity).** Every PL term carries one view `v ∈ {audit, active, default}` (the lenses, ASN-0128), fixed at the top level. The view binds the *view-parameterized* constituents: the core-family atoms — `members`, `targets_of`, `is_K` — and the QD base `M_K` read the slice `v` selects (active and default per ASN-0128, audit per V-AUD; `is_K` under default equals its active reading, membership being never rewritten, UV). The *fixed-view* constituents are not rebound: the named slices `A_K` and `L_K` denote the active and audit K-slices at every term view, and the behavior atoms (BH1–BH4) read the active slices their ASN-0128 definitions name — under `v = default` UV's presentation rewrite applies to their collection-valued results, and under `v = audit` they still read active state, their slice being part of their meaning rather than a parameter. There is no author-selectable per-constituent view *parameter* in PL — but this is a convention about atom-name binding, not an expressiveness fence. Cross-view readings are derivable now, inside a term of any view, through the fixed-view slices: `⋃(L_K, addrs_F)` rebuilds `members(K, audit)` within an active-view term (PC2a over V-TUP — it *is* V-AUD's union), `(∃ x ∈ L_K :: t ∈ coverage_F(x))` is the audit `is_K`, and `{x ∈ L_K : P_active(x)}` — an audit-view domain filtered by an active-view body — is a well-formed QD expression whose semantics is already fixed: every constituent reads the same Σ (PC0), the slices being different projections of it, so no coherence condition arises. What PC3 fixes is which slice the view-parameterized *names* — `members`, `targets_of`, `is_K`, `M_K` — denote in a given term; what is deferred (Open Question 1) is first-class surface syntax (`P[v]`) and its pragmatics, not the semantic capability. The D2 bridge (`is_K(addr) ⟺ (∃ x ∈ members(K, active) :: x ≼ addr)` at F-denoting states) is on this account a *lemma relating atoms across views*, not a mixed-view term former.

**UV (UniformDefaultView).** The default view's semantics, given per codomain under one principle: *filtering is presentation — it rewrites collection-valued results elementwise and rewrites nothing else: never verdicts, never arguments, never traversal, never membership.* Write `filtered(x)` for `(∃ J ∈ Φ, J ≠ K_queried :: is_filtered_J(x))`, BH1's rewrite predicate (ASN-0128). Under `v = default`:

- *Collections* (`℘_fin(T)`, `Seq_fin(T)`): the result drops exactly the elements `x` with `filtered(x)` — `members`, `targets_of`, `succs`, `sources_to`, the sequence `chain` returns, and BH4's `stale` alike. (`stale` is a read; the write surface `retract_stale` is no PL term and consumes the active reading per its ASN-0128 contract, outside UV's scope.)
- *Verdicts and optionals* (`T ∪ {⊥}`, `ℕ ∪ {⊥}`, `Map_fin`): never rewritten. A ⊥ is a verdict with atom-fixed meaning — branch, cycle, multiplicity, inactivity — and a presentation layer must not manufacture one: dropping a filtered `target_of` result to ⊥ would conflate retirement with the multiplicity verdict. So `tip` and `target_of` report the active structure's verdict even when the reported address is filtered from every enumeration, `age` reports its count, and `targets_keyed` — a join of `target_of` verdicts — keeps all its keys and values.
- *Booleans*: never rewritten — `is_K` and `is_filtered` (membership atoms), and `is_in_chain` too, which deserves the explicit word because AD classes it as enumeration-*derived*: it is a verdict about the active walk (`target ∈ chain(addr)` evaluated against the unrewritten walk), not a read of the default-view result sequence, so its default-view value equals its active-view value even when the queried element is retired mid-chain.
- *Arguments and traversal*: never filtered — `chain` walks the active denoted graph; a retired mid-chain element is traversed but not shown in the returned sequence.

ASN-0128 committed the collection rule for `members` and `targets_of` and left the behavior surfaces open (its Open Question 1); UV closes the question by extending the committed rule per codomain rather than inventing a second one, on ASN-0128's own ground: filtering is *presentation*, nullification is *state* — a rewrite that altered traversal, membership, or a verdict would let a presentation-layer mark change query topology, exactly the layer confusion the escape-hatch asymmetry exists to prevent (filtering is undone by selecting `active`; topology changes are undone by nothing).

**PC4 (Purity).** Every PL term is a pure function of `(dom(Σ.C), dom(Σ.M), Σ.L, Σ.registry)`, its arguments, and its view — the registry component constant by R1, and the content and arrangement stores entering through their *domains* alone (Structural reads only, below): no atom or domain expression reads a content value `Σ.C(a)` or an arrangement binding `Σ.M(d)(v)`, so in practice a term is a function of the link store and two domain sets. No memoization, no emission-order dependence, no side effects: atoms evaluate through `Observe_K` and the active-subset machinery (ASN-0086), reading state alone; PC0–PC2a and QD-refl's reflected domains preserve purity by induction. Two evaluators of the same term at the same Σ agree — concurrent evaluation is safe by construction, coordination being entirely an emit-side concern.

**PC5 (TerminationOnFiniteSubstrate).** Every PL term's evaluation halts at every reachable state. *Proof sketch.* Atoms terminate by their ASN-0128 bounds (finite enumerations under L-fin/AD; `chain` by BH2's decreasing bound); V-AUD's readings and V-TUP's tests by the same bounds (L-fin, finitely many spans, T2's bounded comparison); V-PRIM's primitives are finite operations on finite values. PC0/PC2 are finite trees fixed by syntax; PC1/PC2a reduce to finite folds over QD-fin domains; a QD-refl reflected domain evaluates by the domain's own finite interpretation (QD-fin). ∎ With PC4: every PL predicate is decidable at every reachable state.

## The ceiling

**PC6 (ExpressiveClosure).** Define `PL` as the least class containing `V_atom` (V-AUD's readings, V-TUP's projections, and V-PRIM's primitives included) and closed under PC0, PC1 (with V-IDX), PC2, PC2a, and QD-refl's term reflection. A ceiling claim is circular until "evaluable" is defined, so we fix its two parameters explicitly — a base and an evaluation class. The *base* is the substrate's read primitives, enumerable: `Observe_K` (the one read on `Σ.L`), the active-subset machinery derived from it, domain membership against `dom(Σ.C)`/`dom(Σ.M)`/`dom(Σ.L)`, the registry lookup (constant, R1), and the state-independent primitives (V-PRIM). The *evaluation class* is *syntax-directed evaluation*: computations whose control structure is a finite tree fixed by the evaluated expression's syntax and traversed once — each node a base call (internal iteration permitted only here, behind the atom's own termination bound: `chain` is a bounded-iteration combinator whose read count is state-dependent under BH2's measure, not a fixed finite read pattern), a combinator from the admitted vocabulary (PC0's connectives, PC2's binder guard, V-PRIM's operations), or a single-pass fold over an already-computed finite collection (PC1's quantifiers, PC2a's aggregates). Iteration *depth* is fixed by syntax — the nesting of folds — while iteration *width* is bounded by the finite collections visited; what the class excludes is *feedback*: control flow that re-drives base reads from the evaluation's own accumulating output (a repeat-until-stable loop), whose depth no syntax fixes. The restriction is the substrate's stance made formal, not a neutral assumption: the substrate evaluates terms of a closed algebra and never executes foreign read-path code (the extension-language commitment), so syntax-directed evaluation is exactly the computation the substrate performs. *Statement: the predicates computable by syntax-directed evaluation over the base are exactly `PL`.* *Forward*: structural induction — a PL term's control tree is its syntax tree (PC6a), its atoms are base calls or bounded-iteration combinators over them, its formers are the admitted combinators and folds, and evaluation is pure and halting (PC4, PC5). *Converse*: a member of the class is by definition a syntax-fixed tree of base-call leaves, admitted combinators, and single-pass folds — tree shape and node vocabulary are supplied by the class definition, not derived — so what remains to check is the leaves: every base primitive must be PL-expressible, or normalization fails at that leaf. The one non-trivial case is `Observe_K` itself — a call `Observe_K(Σ, F̂, Ĝ, view)` returns its matching set, which is the QD filter

`{x ∈ S_view : (⋀_{t ∈ F̂} t ∈ coverage_F(x)) ∧ (⋀_{t ∈ Ĝ} t ∈ coverage_G(x))}`

with `S_view` the selected slice (`L_K` or `A_K`): the pattern sets are finite query data, so the conjunctions are finite syntax over V-TUP's per-tuple coverage tests. This is why V-TUP must be vocabulary: a pattern query is a *per-tuple conjunction* ("some one active tuple's F covers x and its G covers both y₁ and y₂"), and without per-tuple tests it distinguishes states that every atom denotation conflates — the converse would fail at its first leaf. Of the remaining leaves, domain memberships and V-PRIM primitives are vocabulary by admission. A registry lookup is *not*: no PL atom returns registry data — `shape(K)`, a behavior set, registration status — and its discharge is constant-folding rather than admission: the lookup is state-constant (R1), so a computation containing it is extensionally equal to one with the looked-up value inlined — a V-PRIM constant, a branch taken statically, or a `Reg` expansion (V-IDX) — leaving no registry-reading leaf to normalize. So every syntax-directed evaluation normalizes into the grammar: `V_atom` with V-TUP and V-PRIM enumerates the leaf forms, PC0–PC2a (with QD-refl's reflected domains) the node forms.

*What the relativization costs, stated plainly.* Both restrictions are load-bearing, and they fail differently when dropped. The *vocabulary* restriction is benign: an unadmitted combinator — ℕ-multiplication over two counts, say — is computable from the base with syntax-fixed control, merely not admitted; the gap is a deliberate omission, closable by V-PRIM-style admission without touching the state-reading base. The *control* restriction is the substantive one: drop it — admit terminating procedures that drive base reads from evaluation-time feedback — and the base provably decides `reach(x, y)`, the denoted graph being finite at every reachable state (L-fin, QD-fin): `R := {x}; repeat R := R ∪ ⋃_{y ∈ R} succs(y) until stable` halts and answers from base reads alone. The relationship between that unrestricted ceiling and C-reach is one-directional, and the polarity matters: an unrestricted ceiling claim — feedback computation over the base yields exactly PL — would *entail* ¬C-reach, since the loop above computes `reach` from base reads; contrapositively, if C-reach holds, feedback computation strictly exceeds PL. This note conjectures C-reach, so it implicitly conjectures that the unrestricted ceiling *fails*. Nor does the converse hold: ¬C-reach — `reach ∈ PL` — would not establish the unrestricted ceiling, since other feedback-computable predicates may escape PL independently of `reach` (the parity of `count(L_dom)` is computable by a feedback enumeration of `dom(Σ.L)`, while PL's ℕ fragment — comparisons and `+` against literals, no ℕ-quantification, no recursion — offers no evident expression of it). PC6 asserts the ceiling for syntax-directed evaluation only. The ceiling moves only when the registry does: a new behavior or shape adds atomic forms; no composition does.

**Structural reads only.** What the enumeration *excludes* is as load-bearing as what it includes: no read primitive dereferences a content value `Σ.C(a)` or an arrangement binding `Σ.M(d)(v)`. PL consumes the structural substrate — the link store and registry, domain membership against the three store domains (`dom(Σ.M)` included: document-residence is the emit surface's own gating clause, I1/I5, ASN-0128, and a gating predicate must be able to say what the surface checks — the domain, never the binding), address arithmetic — never the value mappings: a function testing the bytes at an address, or which content sits at a V-position, is outside PL and outside this note's claims, an agent-time operation rather than a substrate predicate. The exclusion is what PC6's converse leans on (with value reads excluded, the leaf forms are finitely enumerable), and it draws a deliberate layer boundary rather than leaving a gap: arrangement-reading queries are ASN-0127's territory — the `image`/`findlinks_V` algebra over `Σ.M`, with its own transition dynamics (E-MONO, D-NONMONO) — a separate query layer that meets PL only at the substrate both read. The typed-relation predicate language asks what the link store asserts; the content-region algebra asks what a document's arrangement reaches; neither subsumes the other, by design.

**PC6a (FixpointExclusion).** PL contains no fixed-point or recursion operator: every former PC0–PC2a admits is non-recursive (QD-refl likewise — a typing rule, not a recursion), so a PL term's composition tree is fixed by its syntax, independent of Σ — atoms' internal iteration (the `chain` walk) is bounded behind its own termination proof and contributes a leaf, not an unrolling. This is a fact about the grammar, and it is all PC6a claims; what the *semantics* can nonetheless express is the separate question C-reach addresses.

**C-reach (ReachInexpressibility — conjecture).** *No PL term computes `reach(x, y)` — transitive closure over the denoted graph — at every reachable state.* Status: **conjecture**, deliberately not a theorem. An earlier framing argued it from the inexpressibility of transitive closure in fixed-quantifier-depth first-order logic over finite structures; that argument is unsound for PL as actually defined, on three counts. (i) The walk atoms traverse unboundedly at fixed syntax: on states whose denoted K-graph has out-degree ≤ 1 throughout, `is_in_chain(x, y)` *is* `reach(x, y)` at arbitrary distance — "chains longer than the term can traverse" is no general principle, and a lower bound must be fought on *branchy* graph families, where the determinate walk halts (BH2) and the walk atoms go blind. (ii) PC2a's counting exceeds plain first-order logic — Ehrenfeucht–Fraïssé arguments for FO do not handle cardinality comparison. (iii) V-PRIM builds total orders (T1, `≼`) into every structure, and locality-based lower bounds for counting logics degrade over ordered structures. A genuine proof must therefore exhibit branchy, cardinality-balanced state families on which every atom denotation, every aggregate, and every order-sensitive composite agree while `reach` differs — an invariance argument in a counting-plus-order regime where nearby questions are open. We conjecture the exclusion holds — nothing in the algebra plausibly reconstructs closure across branches — and we record the proof obligation as Open Question 6 rather than discharge it by citation. What stands without it: PL *ships* no closure operator (PC6a, a grammar fact); ASN-0128's withholding of multi-hop traversal ("both authorities place multi-hop traversal outside the system") remains the design stance it was — made precise here, not promoted to a theorem; an app computes closure by iterating `succs` at agent time, and the substrate cannot be handed the loop. Mutually-recursive predicate definitions ("settled iff every dependency is settled") are likewise not formable in PL (PC6a) and must be unrolled at agent time — a deliberate exclusion, not an oversight, and the first candidate for a successor that consciously raises the ceiling is a bounded least-fixed-point operator (Open Question 4).

## Predicate dynamics

PC4 and PC5 govern one evaluation at one state. A protocol author needs one more thing the language itself cannot say: how a term's truth behaves *across* `→_sh` steps — whether a trigger can be un-triggered, whether a termination condition, once reached, stays reached. PL deliberately has no temporal operators (PC6), so the dynamics live here as a classification of terms by their behavior along transitions, each class grounded in the step effects and frames already proven upstream (the step kinds and their frames: GatedTransitionRelation, ASN-0126, over extended-record states by R-TR, ASN-0128).

**FP (ReadFootprints).** The *footprint* of an atom — the state its evaluation consults — is read off the definitions, and the dynamics classes below are footprint facts:

- core atoms of K at view `audit`: `L_K` (V-AUD);
- core atoms of K at view `active`: `L_K` and `L_R` — the active slice is the audit slice minus `nullified(Σ)`, and `nullified` is computed from the retraction audit slice (ASN-0086);
- `is_filtered` of a BH1 type J: `L_J` and `L_R` (an active-view read, ASN-0128);
- BH2 and BH3 atoms of K: `L_K` and `L_R` (active-view reads, ASN-0128) — except `targets_keyed`, whose footprint is cross-type by construction: it joins `target_of` across *every* BH3-attached Binary type (BH3, ASN-0128), so it reads `⋃ {L_J : J Binary with BH3 attached} ∪ L_R`, indexed by no single K;
- BH4 atoms of K: `L_K`, `L_R`, *and the homed segments of `dom(Σ.L)`* — `age(a) = f_d^Σ − 1 − j` at `d = home(a)` is arithmetic on the home's full chain, and "the chain interleaves every type homed at d" (BH4, ASN-0128): the frontier counts deposits of every type, so the footprint is home-wide, not slice-local;
- *the default-view increment, codomain-uniform*: under `v = default`, every *collection-valued* atom — `℘_fin(T)`- or `Seq_fin(T)`-valued, whatever its family: `members`, `targets_of`, `succs`, `sources_to`, `chain`, `stale` — has as its footprint its active-view footprint plus, for each BH1 type J, the footprint of `is_filtered_J` (`L_J` and `L_R`): UV's rewrite is elementwise, and computing it evaluates `is_filtered_J` at each result element. The verdict-, Boolean-, and `Map_fin`-valued atoms — `is_K`, `is_filtered`, `is_in_chain`, `tip`, `target_of`, `targets_keyed`, `age` — take no increment: UV rewrites none of them (its verdict and Boolean clauses), so each one's default-view footprint equals its active-view footprint;
- the bases: `C_dom` reads `dom(Σ.C)`; `M_dom` reads `dom(Σ.M)`; `L_dom` reads `dom(Σ.L)` whole; `M_K`, `A_K`, `L_K` read the corresponding core footprints; `Reg` reads the constant registry; V-PRIM reads nothing; V-TUP reads the bound tuple's stored value.

A term's footprint is the union over its atoms and domain expressions.

**PD0 (AuditMonotonicity).** The monotone classes, defined inductively with explicit polarity — the polarity discipline is what keeps wrong-way aggregate tests out. Call a domain expression *grow-only* iff it is one of `L_K`, `L_dom`, `C_dom`, `M_dom` — each only ever extended along `→_sh` (the step effects extend exactly one store at a fresh key and frame the rest: GatedTransitionRelation, ASN-0126, by R-TR, ASN-0128; per-type slice growth is R3, ASN-0086, carried across extended-record steps by RP-b) — or `M_K` in an audit-view term (V-AUD's union over the growing `L_K`), or a filter `{x ∈ D : P(x, ·)}` with `D` grow-only and `P(x, ·) ∈ ST` for every binding. Define `ST` (the ⊤-stable forms) and `SF` (the ⊥-stable forms) by mutual induction:

- *Step-constants* — in `ST ∩ SF`: terms reading no state beyond already-bound values — V-PRIM primitives and constants over literals and bound addresses, and V-TUP reads of a bound tuple (the stored value immutable: L12, carried by B2 with RP-b). Once binders fix their arguments, these evaluate identically at every successor state. (Registry constancy, R1, contributes no leaf here: PL admits no registry-reading atom — PC6's converse folds registry-lookup leaves away as constants — so R1 enters the classification only through V-IDX's static expansion of `Reg` and `[Reg]_Σ`'s state-independence.)
- *Boolean nodes*: `∧` and `∨` preserve `ST` and preserve `SF`; `¬` swaps `ST` and `SF`; derived connectives classify through their definitions (`P ⇒ Q ∈ ST` when `P ∈ SF` and `Q ∈ ST`).
- *Quantifiers*: `(∃ x ∈ D :: P) ∈ ST` when `D` is grow-only and `P(x, ·) ∈ ST`; `(∀ x ∈ D :: P) ∈ SF` when `D` is grow-only and `P(x, ·) ∈ SF`.
- *Aggregates*, over grow-only `D`, against ℕ literals: `count(D) ≥ c ∈ ST` and `count(D) ≤ c ∈ SF`. Equality and wrong-polarity bounds are in *neither* class: `count(L_K) ≤ 3` is the canonical non-member of `ST` — true while the slice is small, flipped ⊤→⊥ for good by the fourth K-deposit; it lives in `SF`, where that flip is the licensed direction — and `count(D) = c` lives nowhere (falsified upward from below, it can become true; overshot, never again). T1-extrema are excluded from both classes: the ⊥-at-empty verdict and the extremum's movement under growth make their polarity conditional (the guarded lower bound `if max_{T1}(D) is some m then m ≥ a₀ else ⊥` over grow-only `D` is ⊤-stable by the same witness argument, but we keep the class to the enumerated forms).

*Statement.* Every `ST` term is *⊤-stable*: once true at a reachable Σ, true at every `→_sh*`-successor. Dually, every `SF` term is *⊥-stable*: once false, false forever. *Ground*, by induction on the rules: a grow-only domain's denotation at Σ is contained in its denotation at every successor (bases by the step effects; audit `M_K` because a union over a growing index set grows; filters because membership persists — the element stays in the base by growth, and its `ST` body stays true by the induction hypothesis); no step removes a link address or rewrites a stored value (L12a per step and L12, both carried across extended-record steps by ASN-0126's B2 with RP-b, ASN-0128), so an existential witness persists with its content intact; a universal counterexample never leaves its domain; a count over a growing set never decreases, so satisfied lower bounds persist and violated upper bounds stay violated; the Boolean rules are immediate. ∎ "Has this ever happened" is the monotone question; "has nothing of this kind ever happened" is its anti-monotone dual; neither oscillates — and the polarity typing is the price of saying so inductively.

**PD1 (ActiveNonMonotonicity).** Active-view and default-view terms are stable in neither direction, and the perturbations are different in kind. *Active:* `(∃ x ∈ M_K :: P(x))` at view `active` flips ⊥→⊤ on a K-deposit and ⊤→⊥ on a retraction whose target carries the last witness — `Nullify_Binary` removes the tuple from `A_K^Σ` while `L_K^Σ` keeps it. A fire-until-Q loop whose Q is an active-view term can therefore *un-terminate*: Q true at Σ, false again at a successor, with no monotone measure in the language to forbid the oscillation — the worked composition's closing trace computes exactly this flip sequence (⊥→⊤→⊥ on `quiescent(t)` across Σ₁–Σ₃). *Default:* the same term at view `default` additionally flips on a bare BH1-type emission — `retired` deposited on a witnessing address removes it from every default-view enumeration (UV) with the queried type's own tuples untouched: presentation movement changes trigger truth. A protocol that wants a stable gate over active state must either supply the stability *outside* the language (an operating discipline under which the falsifying steps cannot occur — the move DR makes for C3, ASN-0128) or anchor on PD0's audit class instead.

**PD2 (FrameStability).** Per-step invariance, read off the frames and FP's footprints. A term whose footprint excludes `dom(Σ.C)` is invariant under every K.α step, and one whose footprint excludes `dom(Σ.M)` is invariant under every K.σ step: each step kind frames every component outside its one extended store (frame clauses, ASN-0126), so every atomic query in the term evaluates against identical components — `C_dom` buys content-existence tests at the price of K.α-sensitivity, and `M_dom` buys document-residence at the price of K.σ-sensitivity. Sharper, per type, for terms whose domains are per-type slices (not `L_dom`, whose footprint is the whole link domain): a term reading only *audit* slices of types in S is invariant under every deposit of a type outside S — the deposit grows its own slice and nothing else the term reads. The active-view refinement must respect the *two* cross-type effects the substrate has, plus one cross-type *footprint* a single atom carries. First, retraction: an R-deposit nullifies addresses of any type and can shrink any active slice (the same cross-effect I1a's induction handles, ASN-0128), so the clause requires `[R]` off the depositing side. Second, home-chain arithmetic: BH4's footprint is home-wide (FP) — a deposit of *any* type homed at a document carrying one of K's active tuples advances that home's frontier `f_d`, increments `age` at every K-tuple homed there, and can flip a `stale`-built term with no retraction anywhere ("the chain interleaves every type homed at d", BH4, ASN-0128). Third, the atom-side exception: `targets_keyed`'s footprint is cross-type by construction (FP), so a term containing it reads every BH3-attached Binary type's active slice whatever S names. So the active clause reads: a *BH4-free, `targets_keyed`-free* term reading active slices of types in S is invariant under deposits of types outside S with `[R]` not on the depositing side; a term containing BH4 atoms is additionally perturbed by same-home traffic of every type; and a term containing `targets_keyed`, by deposits of every BH3-attached Binary type. (A default-view term adds, through each collection-valued atom it contains, every BH1 type's active slice to its footprint — FP's codomain-uniform default increment — so its non-interference set shrinks by Φ as well.) PD2 is the protocol author's non-interference tool: a trigger built from audit slices of its own types is untouched by every unrelated fire, exactly; a trigger over active slices is untouched by unrelated fires *except retractions, same-home deposits of any type when BH4 atoms appear, and deposits of any BH3-attached Binary type when `targets_keyed` appears*. Name the exceptions or be surprised by them.

The three classes compose with the algebra in the expected way — PC0's `∧` of two ⊤-stable terms is ⊤-stable, `¬` swaps PD0's classes, PC1 over a grow-only domain preserves ∃-stability and breaks ∀-stability — and the classification is the load-bearing input to any termination argument built over this substrate: a sound "stop when Q" wants Q in PD0's class, or wants the discipline that removes PD1's falsifiers stated as an explicit hypothesis. The termination arguments themselves — convergence of fire-until-stable loops, re-opening rules when upstream state moves — are protocol-layer constructions over these classes, outside this note's scope.

## Worked composition

Fix two illustrative app classes alongside ASN-0128's shipped three: `cmt` — Binary, idem=⊥, a comment from a finding address to a target address — and `res` — Binary, idem=⊤, a resolution from a target back to a comment. (These are illustrative registrations exercising the algebra, not standard registrations.)

*The filtered domain.* The open comments on target `t` — the whole term at view `active` (PC3: the view is written once, at the top level, not per atom):

`OPEN(t) = {c ∈ M_cmt : t ∈ targets_of_cmt(c) ∧ ¬(∃ r ∈ M_res :: c ∈ targets_of_res(r))}`

— a QD filter over the base domain `M_cmt`, whose filter body is PC0 over a V-PRIM membership test, an enumeration atom, and a PC1 existential.

*Quiescence-shaped predicate.* `quiescent(t) ≡ OPEN(t) = ∅` — V-PRIM's emptiness test over the reflected domain (QD-refl licenses the address-valued `OPEN(t)` in term position) — equivalently `count(OPEN(t)) = 0` (PC2a) or `¬(∃ c ∈ OPEN(t) :: ⊤)` (PC1, the body V-PRIM's Boolean constant): one composite, three equivalent spellings, all in PL with signature `T → Bool`. Evaluated against `active`; the same term at view `audit` reads the V-AUD slices and asks "was every comment ever resolved at some point recorded" — a different and rarely wanted question, which is why the view is fixed per term (PC3).

*Aggregation with a cap.* `under_cap(t, n) ≡ count({c ∈ M_cmt : t ∈ targets_of_cmt(c)}) ≤ n` — count and meta-comparison against the literal `n` (PC2a, V-PRIM); the operator pattern for any bounded-cycles discipline. The dual direction is equally available, now that set-valued terms are domains: `out_capped(c, n) ≡ count(targets_of_cmt(c)) ≤ n` — "c targets at most n addresses" — counts an enumeration atom's result directly (QD's set-valued closure).

*Value composition through a verdict.* "t's current head is unretired": with `supersedes` shipped (S2, ASN-0128) and BH2 attached,

`head_live(t) ≡ if tip(t) is some h then ¬is_retired(h) else ⊥-case`

— PC2's binder guard through the `T ∪ {⊥}` codomain: the condition is V-PRIM's definedness test, the binder `h` carries the narrowed type `T`, and the outer constituent `is_retired` — a state-indexed read evaluated at the same Σ and view (PC2) — applies to `h` with no coercion; the ⊥-case branch is the term author's explicit decision (a branch or cycle at `t` is a *verdict*, and PC2 forces the author to handle it rather than inheriting an undefined value).

*A derived recipe as a PL term.* ASN-0128's `targets_under(addr)` — "an app-side composition rather than a fourth shipped predicate" — is exactly

`targets_under(addr) = ⋃ {targets_of(x) : x ∈ {y ∈ M_K : y ≼ addr}}`

(at view `active`) — a PC2a finite union over a QD filter whose body is the T2-decidable prefix test: the algebra is where ASN-0128's recipe lives.

*Cross-type universal.* "every registered class's active slice is non-empty somewhere under d" — `(∀ K ∈ Reg :: (∃ x ∈ M_K :: d ≼ x))` at view `active` — the `Reg`-quantification no single type's atoms express, denoting its static expansion over the registered classes (PC1 with V-IDX). (The body is core-family-only, so V-IDX's instance-wise condition holds at every registry.)

*The trace — the dynamics evaluated at concrete states.* The registrations above make PD1's warning a three-step computation rather than a description. Start from a reachable Σ₀ with one document `d ∈ dom(Σ₀.M)` (one K.σ step from `Σ_init`) and an empty link store (`dom(Σ₀.L) = ∅`: `Σ_init.L = ∅` by R-VAL, and K.σ frames `Σ.L`); fix two distinct query addresses `c₁` (a comment) and `t` (its target). Every emission below is homed at `d`, so the deposits land in order on `d`'s chain — `a₁ = chain_d(0)`, `a₂ = chain_d(1)`, `a₃ = chain_d(2)` (frontier-landing, FrontierUnification, ASN-0126, at extended-record states via RP-a, ASN-0128).

- *Σ₀ →_sh Σ₁* by `Emit_cmt(Σ₀, d, {c₁}, {t})`: idem=⊥, so no dedup runs and the admitted call deposits (I5); the gate passes (cmt registered Binary, `|F| = |G| = 1`); the deposit `(enc{c₁}, enc{t}, cmt)` lands at `a₁`, active (C2: cmt ≁ R; C3: `L_R^{Σ₀} = ∅`). Evaluating at Σ₁, view `active`: `M_cmt = {c₁}` and `targets_of_cmt(c₁) = {t}`, so the body's membership conjunct holds at `c₁`; `A_res^{Σ₁} = ∅` gives `M_res = ∅`, so the inner existential reduces over the empty domain to ⊥ (PC1) and its negation to ⊤. `OPEN(t) = {c₁}`; `quiescent(t) = ⊥`.
- *Σ₁ →_sh Σ₂* by `Emit_res(Σ₁, d, {t}, {c₁})`: idem=⊤, and the dedup check consults `A_res^{Σ₁} = ∅` — a miss (I1), so the deposit `(enc{t}, enc{c₁}, res)` lands at `a₂`, active (`L_R^{Σ₁}` still empty). Evaluating at Σ₂: `M_res = {t}` and `targets_of_res(t) = {c₁}`, so the inner existential finds its witness `r = t` and the negated conjunct fails at `c₁` — the filter retains nothing. `OPEN(t) = ∅`; `quiescent(t) = ⊤`.
- *Σ₂ →_sh Σ₃* by `Nullify_Binary(Σ₂, d, a₂)`: P0 (`d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`), P-reg ([R] registered Binary, S3, ASN-0128), and P-tgt (`a₂ ∈ A_rel^{Σ₂}`) all hold; the retraction triple lands at `a₃`, and `a₂ ∈ nullified(Σ₃)` with single-tuple scope — `{u : a₂ ≼ u} ∩ A_rel^{Σ₃} = {a₂}` — so `a₁` stays active. Evaluating at Σ₃: the res tuple persists in `L_res^{Σ₃}` but is gone from `A_res^{Σ₃}`, so `M_res = ∅` — the inner existential has lost its only witness — while `M_cmt = {c₁}` still. `OPEN(t) = {c₁}`; `quiescent(t) = ⊥`.

The value sequence of `quiescent(t)` along the trace is ⊥, ⊤, ⊥ — PD1's flip sequence computed on the named term: a fire-until-`quiescent(t)` loop that observed Σ₂ has terminated against a condition Σ₃ falsifies, with no link ever leaving the store. The audit companion locks instead: `ever_res ≡ (∃ x ∈ L_res :: ⊤)` is a PD0 `ST` term — an existential over the grow-only `L_res` with step-constant body — and evaluates ⊥ at Σ₁, ⊤ at Σ₂, and ⊤ at Σ₃: the retraction removed nothing from the audit slice, whose witness persists with its value intact (L12a and L12, carried across extended-record steps by B2 with RP-b). One trace, both classes: the active-view gate oscillates; the audit-view record locks.

## What this note doesn't cover

- **Protocol constructions.** Triggers paired with write operations, termination conditions and their convergence arguments, re-opening rules, and scheduler disciplines (fairness, fire atomicity, non-interference) are application-layer machinery *written in* PL and *typed by* PD0–PD2, but not part of the predicate foundation: this note supplies the language, its evaluation guarantees, and its dynamics; what is built from them is the builder's.
- **Range-valued predicate atoms.** Inherited from ASN-0128: the surface is address-denoting; a predicate layer over span extents is deferred with it.
- **Normal forms.** Whether PL-modulo-extensional-equivalence admits a normal form, and an effective procedure deciding extensional equivalence of terms, are open theory (Open question 3); well-typing is decidable already (signature unification against COD plus registry lookup, with `Reg`-quantified terms checked instance-wise per V-IDX).

## Open questions

1. **First-class views.** PC3 fixes one view per top-level term as a naming convention, and cross-view mixing is already derivable through the fixed-view slices — an `audit`-view domain filtered by an `active`-view body is a well-formed QD expression whose same-Σ semantics this note already fixes (PC3). What remains open is surface design, not capability: should the view become a first-class parameter (`P[v]`) with per-constituent binding, and which mixed-view idioms deserve named, checkable syntax rather than slice-level reconstruction?

2. **Existential-witness domains.** QD is base-plus-filter. Domains defined by witness extraction — "the set of x such that some y witnesses P(x, y)" — are expressible only by flattening the witness into PC1's quantifier prefix. Is the flattening always possible within PL, or are there meta-expressible domains PL cannot construct?

3. **Extensional normal form.** Is there an effective procedure deciding whether two PL terms denote the same predicate at every reachable state? Syntactic identity is trivially decidable; extensional equivalence over the reachable-state class is the open half.

4. **The fixpoint successor.** PC6a excludes recursion deliberately. If a forcing case arrives (a protocol whose gating genuinely needs dependency-chain walking rather than agent-time unrolling), the conscious extension is a bounded least-fixed-point operator with an explicit termination measure — raising the ceiling by a named primitive rather than by accident. What measure discipline keeps PC5 for such an operator?

5. **Deciding the dynamics class.** PD0–PD2 classify by syntactic criteria (view, polarity, domains read). Is class membership decidable for arbitrary PL terms — in particular, is there an effective syntactic check that soundly (if incompletely) certifies ⊤-stability for terms mixing views or polarities, so that a protocol checker can validate a termination condition mechanically?

6. **Reach inexpressibility.** What invariance argument — one handling PC2a's counting, V-PRIM's built-in orders, and the walk atoms simultaneously, on branchy cardinality-balanced state families — would prove C-reach, that no PL term computes transitive closure over the denoted graph at every reachable state?
