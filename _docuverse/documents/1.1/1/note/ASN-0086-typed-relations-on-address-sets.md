# ASN-0086: Typed Relations on Address Sets

*Drawing the link model forward into a relational vocabulary*

ASN-0043 establishes the link as a primitive: an addressed, owned, typed connection between spans of content. We now adopt a different vocabulary for the same structure. Where ASN-0043 speaks of *links* and *endsets*, we speak of *tuples* and *typed relations*. The two vocabularies describe one object — a standard-triple link `(F, G, Θ)` at address `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` is a tuple in a typed relation indexed by `Θ` — but predicates compose more cleanly over relations than over endsets, and several substrate-level guarantees become easier to state in this form.

We are looking for what a relation algebra over the link store affords. The answer is seven structural properties on the typed-relation substrate (R0–R6), of which six (R0–R5) are derivable from ASN-0043 and one (R6, ActiveSubsetWellDefinedness) is well-definedness by construction. R6 itself is essentially a definitional check — `A_K^Σ = L_K^Σ \ {(a, F, G) : a ∈ nullified(Σ)}` is computable from `Σ.L` by inspection — and the *conceptual contribution* of this note sits not in R6 but in the *active/audit distinction* that R6 makes available: the fact that two coherent views (`L_K`, the audit trail; `A_K`, the operational currently-in-effect set) co-exist over the same link store, with retractions cleanly mediating between them. The substantive properties carrying that distinction are R6a (RetractionStability — nullification persists across state transitions), R6b (SingleDepthRetraction — the predicate does not recurse), and R6c (RestorationByReemission — once retracted, always out of every future active subset). Their joint content is the substrate's own contribution: a self-referential retraction relation (made possible by R5) computed against a monotone audit trail (R3), yielding a usable active subset that ASN-0043's link model alone does not articulate. Alongside the active/audit distinction, this note makes a second structural commitment: the *sibling-frontier emission discipline*, a hypothesis on the substrate primitive (detailed below) under which the supplementary lemma R0a (FlatLinkDomain) establishes `dom(Σ.L)` as a tumbler-prefix antichain. R0a is Setup-free in its dependence on the foundation ASNs but discipline-conditional on the implementation's emission policy — the substrate primitive in isolation admits emissions at strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses, which the discipline forbids; the udanax-green substrate adopts the discipline as evidence of realizability. The conditionality propagates to Nullify's single-tuple-scope guarantee and to R7's reduction (which is closed only under the relational layer's commitment to the disciplined `Emit_K` primitive). On top of these we define three operations (Emit_K, Observe, Nullify), and an eighth composite claim (R7 = R7a ∧ R7b, NullifyIsEmit) closes the argument that — under R7a (PROVEN, from L12 + L12a + the Frame conditions: no `Σ.L`-affecting transition lies outside class (iii)) and R7b (STIPULATED, the relational layer's model commitment: every relational-layer-initiated class-(iii) step is the sibling-frontier-disciplined `Emit_K`) jointly — all *relational-layer* state change reduces to a single primitive: `Emit_K`. *Scoping the reduction.* R7's headline is therefore irreducibly conditional, with the asymmetric standing of its two sub-claims hoisted into the citation form: R7a is fully derived from the listed invariants, whereas R7b is not derivable from them (the substrate primitive in isolation admits broader class-(iii) deposits that R7b excludes by adoption). The "reduces to a single primitive: `Emit_K`" claim is therefore best read as *the relational layer commits to `Emit_K` as its sole state-affecting primitive*, with the closure of the operation set guaranteed under R7a ∧ R7b; R7's Step 3 discharges R7a and characterizes R7b, and Step 4 composes them into the operational closure. (Document allocation and content emission, the other two primitive transitions in `→`, are inherited from ASN-0036 and are not reductions of `Emit_K`; the scope of the reduction is the link store `Σ.L` and the typed relations indexed over it.)


## The Two Foundational Sets

**Setup hypothesis.** We work in systems satisfying ASN-0043 (and therefore ASN-0036 and ASN-0034). We additionally assume globally `s_C`-resident content:

`(A a ∈ dom(Σ.C) :: subspace_I(a) = s_C)`

Under this hypothesis the disjointness between content and tuple addresses (R4 below) holds substrate-wide as a structural property, not merely within the slice scoped to `s_C` that L14 (DualPrimitive, ASN-0043) supplies in its stated form.

*Maintenance protocol — Setup as external constraint on class-(ii) emission policy.* The Setup hypothesis is not derivable from ASN-0036's class-(ii) emission primitive in isolation: that primitive admits content emissions at any T4-valid `c` with `zeros(c) = 3` (per S0–S3), with no first-element-field constraint on `subspace_I(c)`. The Setup hypothesis therefore stands as an *external constraint on the implementation's class-(ii) emission policy* — parallel in form and standing to the sibling-frontier emission discipline on class-(iii) (named below at *Implementation discipline — sibling-frontier link emission*) — requiring that every content emission `Σ → Σ'` of class (ii) at fresh address `c` satisfies `subspace_I(c) = s_C`. Under that policy, Setup is maintained invariantly: the inductive step extends `dom(Σ.C)` to `dom(Σ'.C) ∪ {c}` with `subspace_I(c) = s_C`, preserving the universal quantifier's body. The policy is *not* enforced by class (ii)'s frame condition (Frame conditions, above), which constrains only `dom(Σ.C)`'s extension at a fresh key, not the subspace identifier of that key; an implementation that emitted content at non-`s_C` subspaces would respect the frame condition and break Setup at the first such step. The Nelson link-model design adopts the policy as a structural commitment — all addressable content lives in the conventional content subspace, with `s_L`-resident content nowhere required by the design — and the udanax-green substrate's content-emission path (V-stream–to-tumbler routing) is consistent with the policy, though the policy is not enforced at the V-position level of the codebase: it is held by construction at the routing layer that maps V-stream positions to addresses. Future work that elevates the policy to a substrate-level guarantee — by tightening ASN-0036's class-(ii) emission primitive to require `subspace_I(c) = s_C` as a precondition — would discharge the Setup hypothesis at every reachable state. Pending such a strengthening, the hypothesis is adopted here as an explicit external constraint, and the `[Setup-required]` tags on R0 (Step 4 L14a-preservation bullet), R4, and R5 below mark the consumption sites.

**Subspace-distinctness hypothesis.** We additionally assume that the content and link subspace identifiers are distinct first-element-field values:

`s_C ≠ s_L`.

This is adopted as an explicit hypothesis of this note, parallel to the `s_C`-resident content hypothesis above. The distinctness is *presupposed* by ASN-0043's L0/L0a partition and is structurally required by it, but ASN-0043 does not state it as one labeled fact — so downstream invocations would otherwise have to re-derive the distinctness from the partition's structural intent at each site of use. ASN-0086 promotes it to a single citable clause and consumes it via that clause. *Motivation for the hypothesis.* L0a (ContentSubspaceScope, ASN-0043) defines the `s_C`-resident content slice as a proper sub-slice of `dom(Σ.C)`, which collapses to all of `dom(Σ.C)` if `s_C = s_L`; L14 (DualPrimitive, ASN-0043) asserts disjointness `dom(Σ.L) ∩ dom(Σ.C)|_{s_C} = ∅`, which collapses to `dom(Σ.L) ∩ dom(Σ.C) = ∅` *unconditionally* (rather than scoped to `s_C`-resident content as ASN-0043 stipulates) if `s_C = s_L`, evacuating L14's scoped form of content. L0 (SubspacePartition, ASN-0043) routes link addresses to `s_L` and L0a routes `s_C`-resident content to `s_C`, both as first-element-field values, and their structural disjointness in L14 follows from `s_C ≠ s_L` combined with T3 (CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034). The partition is therefore vacuous without the distinctness — which makes adopting `s_C ≠ s_L` as a labeled hypothesis the right discharge of ASN-0043's implicit structural commitment. *Consumption sites.* This note consumes the hypothesis *directly* at R0 Step 4 (the L14a and L14 preservation bullets), R4, and Nullify's no-content-address-under-`a` argument, and *indirectly* at R5 (via Stage 1's appeal to R0's emission construction, which inherits the L14a-preservation step's use of the distinctness). Each site cites "the subspace-distinctness axiom (`s_C ≠ s_L`)" or equivalent phrasing as a single labeled source, rather than re-deriving the distinctness from the partition's implicit content. *Future work.* A formal derivation of `s_C ≠ s_L` from the foundation ASNs (e.g., from a strengthened L0/L0a that axiomatizes the distinctness directly) would discharge this hypothesis at every consumption site; pending such a derivation, the hypothesis remains an explicit commitment of ASN-0086.

**Definition — element-field depth.** The element-field depth of a tumbler `t` relative to its prefix `s ≼ t` is `zeros(t) − zeros(s)`. By T4 (HierarchicalParsing, ASN-0034), each zero is a field separator, so the difference counts the new element-field boundaries introduced between `s` and `t`. Step (b) of the lemma below uses this directly: a child-spawn `(d, k')` with `k' ≥ 1` produces a child whose zero count exceeds `zeros(d)` by `k' − 1` (TA5 postcondition (d)), so the element-field depth of the spawn relative to `d` is exactly `k' − 1`.

**Definition — allocator-tree depth.** The *allocator-tree depth* of an allocator A relative to a document `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` is the number of T10a child-spawn pairs `(·, k')` with `k' ∈ {1, 2}` on the L1c witness chain from `d` to A's base address (i.e., to A's first emission). This is *not* the same as element-field depth: a `(·, 1)` child-spawn opens a new allocator without introducing a new zero (TA5 with `k' = 1` gives `zeros` unchanged), so it advances the allocator hierarchy by one level without advancing element-field depth. Concretely, the allocator opened by the single child-spawn `(d, 2)` lives at allocator-tree depth 1 and produces outputs at element-field depth 1 relative to `d`; an allocator additionally opened by `(d.0.s_X, 1)` for `X ∈ {C, L}` lives at allocator-tree depth 2 but still produces outputs at element-field depth 1 relative to `d` (its outputs have one *additional element-field component* — `#E = 2` instead of `#E = 1` — but the same zero count).

**Lemma — SharedDepthOneAllocator.** Under each document address `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`, there exists exactly one allocator at allocator-tree depth 1 below `d` — written `A_d` and enumerating the positions of the first element-field component appended to `d.0.`; its outputs sit at element-field depth 1 relative to `d` — and this allocator is *shared across all subspaces*: its enumeration `d.0.1, d.0.2, d.0.3, …` indexes subspace identifiers, with position `s_C` landing in the content subspace and position `s_L` in the link subspace by L0 (SubspacePartition, ASN-0043). The subspace-specific allocators at allocator-tree depth 2 (the content allocator `A_{d.0.s_C.1}` and the link allocator `A_{d.0.s_L.1}`, rooted respectively at `d.0.s_C.1` and `d.0.s_L.1` — the first emissions of the child-spawns `(d.0.s_C, 1)` and `(d.0.s_L, 1)` — and enumerating sibling streams by T10a.7) are mutually *independent*. Their outputs share `A_d`'s element-field depth (the `(·, 1)` child-spawns preserve `zeros`, so outputs sit at element-field depth 1 relative to `d` in both cases); the depth-2 allocators are distinguished from `A_d` by their additional element-field component (#E = 2 vs #E = 1), not by element-field depth.

*Proof.* In three steps.

*(a) The only T10a-admissible child-spawns from `d` are `(d, 1)` and `(d, 2)`.* Document addresses have `zeros(d) = 2` by S7d (DocumentAllocationDiscipline, ASN-0036). T10a (AllocatorDiscipline, ASN-0034) licenses child-spawn via `inc(·, k')` with `k' > 0`, and TA5 (HierarchicalIncrement, ASN-0034), postcondition (d), gives the resulting child's zero count: `zeros(inc(d, k')) = zeros(d) + (k' - 1) = 2 + (k' - 1)` for `k' ≥ 1`. T4 (HierarchicalParsing, ASN-0034) caps every tumbler's zero count at 3, so admissible child-spawns from `d` require `2 + (k' - 1) ≤ 3`, i.e., `k' ∈ {1, 2}`. Both `(d, 1)` and `(d, 2)` are T10a-admissible at `d`; no further child-spawn pair from `d` exists in T10a's vocabulary.

*(b) Only `(d, 2)` introduces a new zero, and only `(d, 2)` opens an allocator at element-field depth 1.* By TA5 (HierarchicalIncrement, ASN-0034), postcondition (d), `(d, 1)` yields a child with `zeros = 2 + (1 - 1) = 2` — no new zero is introduced, and the resulting tumbler `inc(d, 1)` stays at element-field depth 0 relative to `d` (it extends `d`'s rightmost element-field rather than opening a new one). `(d, 2)` yields a child with `zeros = 2 + (2 - 1) = 3` — exactly one new zero is introduced, placing the spawn one element-field deeper than `d`, i.e., at element-field depth 1. The spawn-parameter-to-depth correspondence is in bijection on `k' ∈ {1, 2}`: `k' = 1` keeps the depth, `k' = 2` deepens by one level; no other `k'` is available at `d`. Therefore `(d, 2)` is the *unique* child-spawn pair from `d` whose admissibility opens an allocator at element-field depth 1.

*(c) T10a's at-most-once child-spawn discipline on `(d, 2)` gives uniqueness.* By T10a (AllocatorDiscipline, ASN-0034) — whose axiom binds each parent to a single `inc(·, k')` with `k' > 0` per child-spawn — each child-spawn pair fires at most once across the entire reachability chain. Applied to `(d, 2)`, this entails that the depth-1 element-field allocator opened by `(d, 2)` exists at most once under `d`. Combined with (b), there is exactly one depth-1 allocator under `d`; we name it `A_d`. A per-subspace depth-1 splitting (e.g., separate `A_d^{s_C}` and `A_d^{s_L}` rooted at distinct depth-1 positions) would require a second child-spawn pair from `d` opening an additional depth-1 allocator, which by (b) does not exist in the spawn family; equivalently, re-firing `(d, 2)` would contradict T10a's at-most-once axiom.

*Subspace-routing and allocator-tree-depth-2 independence.* `A_d`'s enumeration produces successive first-post-`d` element-field values `1, 2, 3, …`, which by L0 (SubspacePartition, ASN-0043) are the subspace identifiers `s_1, s_2, s_3, …` (with `s_C` and `s_L` among them by the substrate's standard convention); subspace-internal allocation therefore lives one level deeper in the allocator hierarchy, at the allocator-tree-depth-2 child-spawns `(d.0.s_C, 1)`, `(d.0.s_L, 1)`, etc. (Their outputs remain at element-field depth 1 relative to `d` — the `(·, 1)` child-spawns preserve `zeros` — and so the "deeper" here is allocator-tree depth, not element-field depth.) Each allocator-tree-depth-2 spawn is admitted by T2 and at-most-once by T10a but the spawn pairs are *mutually distinct* (distinct parent tumblers `d.0.s_C ≠ d.0.s_L` by L0 + the subspace-distinctness axiom `s_C ≠ s_L`, named above), so T10a's at-most-once on each pair imposes no joint constraint — the allocator-tree-depth-2 allocators evolve independently. ∎

*Status.* The shared-depth-1 / independent-depth-2 structure is therefore a *derived lemma* from L0 + T10a + TA5 (HierarchicalIncrement) postcondition (d) + T4 + S7d, not a separate model commitment. The conclusion is unconditional given the foundation ASNs.

This corollary is consumed at R0 Step 2 Case A's L1c chain construction (which walks the shared `A_d` from position 1 through the sibling sweep to position `s_L`, then opens the depth-2 link allocator at `(d.0.s_L, 1)`) and at the worked sketch's concrete L1c chain for `a₁` (which exhibits the same structure with `s_C = 1`, `s_L = 2`). The udanax-green implementation realizes the corollary directly: `findisatoinsertmolecule` (granf2.c:158–181) maintains a single granfilade tree rooted at each document address and dispatches subspace-internal allocation by atomtype parameter — bytes and links share the depth-1 enumeration and differ only in which depth-2 sub-allocator (content versus link) is engaged.

*Reconciliation with Nelson's design.* The link-model design intent occasionally referenced in the literature — that bytes are ordered by V-stream position while links are ordered by permanent arrival — is *not* in tension with the shared-depth-1 corollary: V-stream ordering and arrival ordering are properties of the *depth-2-and-deeper* enumerations within their respective subspaces (`A_{d.0.s_C}` and `A_{d.0.s_L}`), which the corollary keeps independent. The shared `A_d` enumerates only subspace identifiers, not the within-subspace ordering. The implementation evidence (Gregory's `findisatoinsertmolecule`) and the design intent (Nelson's per-subspace ordering principles) agree once the level distinction is drawn.

*Setup dependence at a glance.* Of the R-claims that follow, three — R0 (via the L14a-preservation step in Step 4), R4 (directly), and R5 (via its Stage 1 appeal to R0's emission construction, which inherits R0's L14a-preservation Setup-requirement) — make essential use of the Setup hypothesis as stated. R1, R2, R3, R6, R6a, R6b, R6c, R7, R0a, and R0a's corollaries (R0a-Cor1, R0a-Cor2) are Setup-free: their proofs invoke only ASN-0034, ASN-0036, and ASN-0043 invariants and prior R-claims that are themselves Setup-free. Each R-claim's section header below carries a tag — `[Setup-required]` or `[Setup-free]` — to make this dependence visible at the point of use. R0a additionally carries a *discipline-conditional* qualifier (see below): it is Setup-free in its dependence on the foundation ASNs, but it requires the sibling-frontier emission discipline (named below at "Implementation discipline — sibling-frontier link emission") as an implementation hypothesis. R0a's corollaries R0a-Cor1 and R0a-Cor2 inherit the discipline-conditionality from R0a's sibling-stream invariant; no other R-claim carries the qualifier, and all others are unconditional given their Setup tags. Under L14's native scoped form (without globally `s_C`-resident content), R0, R4, and R5 would hold slice-wise on the `s_C`-resident content slice; the Open Questions section traces the further implications.

**State transition relation.** We write `Σ → Σ'` for the substrate's *dom-extending* one-step transition relation — the transitions whose effect on `(Σ.C, Σ.M, Σ.L)` is to add a fresh key to exactly one of the three stores. The primitive dom-extending transitions are exactly the substrate-level emissions inherited from the underlying ASNs and lifted here: (i) document allocation (ASN-0036, S7a, S7d) extends `dom(Σ.M)` with a new document address; (ii) content emission (ASN-0036, S0–S3) extends `dom(Σ.C)`; (iii) `Emit_K` as defined later in this note (which composes the underlying ASN-0043 link-store extension) extends `dom(Σ.L)` by one address. Every dom-extending transition in `→` is one of (i)–(iii); the substrate exposes no removal, replacement, or in-place mutation transition that touches `(dom(Σ.C), dom(Σ.M), dom(Σ.L))` (consistent with S0, L12, and T8 across the underlying ASNs). The operations defined later in this note (Observe, Nullify) either compose `Emit_K` (Nullify is `Emit_R` with a designated argument shape, per R7) or leave Σ unchanged (Observe). R0 through R7 are claims about `→` so defined; in particular, R0's existential `(E Σ' extending Σ, …)` is discharged by exhibiting an `Emit_K` transition.

*Scoping note — arrangement modifications and the broader transition relation `↦`.* ASN-0036 admits a further class of state-changing transitions: *arrangement modifications* that extend `dom(Σ.M(d))` for an existing `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` (e.g., INSERT and related editing operations on a document's arrangement). These transitions affect `Σ.M`'s value but not `dom(Σ.M)` itself, and so are not dom-extending in the sense of `→` as defined here. We name the *broader transition relation* — the union of dom-extending `→` with these arrangement-modifying transitions — `↦`, with arrangement-modifying steps written explicitly as `Σ ↦ Σ'` when their distinction from `→` matters; every `→`-step is an `↦`-step but not conversely. R0–R7 are stated against `→` (dom-extending only); the lift to `↦` is collected in R6c-Corollary, which extends R6c's persistence-of-nullification conclusion from `⊑` (reflexive-transitive closure of `→`, defined below) to `⊑̂` (reflexive-transitive closure of `↦`, defined alongside `⊑`). The arrangement-modifying steps live in a parallel transition vocabulary whose L-invariant preservation is handled in ASN-0036 (and the editing-operation ASNs that extend it). *Arrangement-modification frame (inherited from ASN-0036).* The substrate-level frame on every arrangement-modifying transition is `(Σ'.C, dom(Σ'.M), Σ'.L) = (Σ.C, dom(Σ.M), Σ.L)` — only `Σ.M`'s pointwise values change (specifically, `Σ.M(d)` for the document `d` being edited); the content store, the document-allocation domain, and the entire link store are held identical as ordered components. Each frame component traces to a specific source: `Σ'.C = Σ.C` follows from S9 (TwoStreamSeparation, ASN-0036), which asserts that arrangement-modifying transitions touch only `Σ.M` and leave the content store untouched; `dom(Σ'.M) = dom(Σ.M)` follows from the definitional restriction that arrangement-modifying transitions operate on an *existing* `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` (extending `dom(Σ.M(d))`, not `dom(Σ.M)` itself — the document address `d` is a precondition input, not an allocation event); `Σ'.L = Σ.L` is *definitional* — part of ASN-0036's specification of the arrangement-modifying transition class, which produces no link emission (its sole effect being on `Σ.M(d)`'s arrangement value). L12 (LinkImmutability, ASN-0043) and L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity, ASN-0043) are *consistency checks* on the definitional frame rather than load-bearing sources for it: the identity `Σ'.L = Σ.L` trivially satisfies both L12's no-modification clause and L12a's monotonicity clause, but neither L-invariant entails the identity — L12a in particular permits domain extension, which the definitional frame separately forecloses. The frame is therefore inherited from ASN-0036's class definition without re-derivation; every reference in this note to "arrangement modifications leave `Σ.L` invariant" cites that definitional inheritance, with L12/L12a recorded only as preserved invariants under the inherited frame. The link-store-only claims R0–R7 are stated and proved against `→` as defined above; they are unaffected by arrangement modifications, because such transitions leave `Σ.L` and `dom(Σ.M)` untouched and therefore preserve every L-invariant trivially. Where the broader transition relation (dom-extending ∪ arrangement-modifying) matters in this note (notably R6a's coverage-preservation argument, R3's monotonicity, and R6c's user-facing extension below — all of which appeal to `Σ.L` preservation), the appeal is to the underlying invariant (L12, L12a) together with the arrangement-modification frame just stated; both hold across both transition vocabularies, and the present scoping is purely an enumeration convention for `→`.

**Frame conditions on the primitive transitions.** The abstract substrate model commits, *definitionally*, to the following frame conditions on each primitive-transition class — these are part of what each class *is* at the `(Σ.C, Σ.M, Σ.L)` level of abstraction, not consequences derivable from the underlying ASNs' invariants:

- (i) *Document allocation:* `dom(Σ'.M) = dom(Σ.M) ∪ {d}` for a fresh document address `d ∉ dom(Σ.M)`; `Σ'.C = Σ.C` and `Σ'.L = Σ.L`.
- (ii) *Content emission:* `dom(Σ'.C) = dom(Σ.C) ∪ {c}` for a fresh content address `c ∉ dom(Σ.C)`; `Σ'.M = Σ.M` and `Σ'.L = Σ.L`.
- (iii) *Link emission (`Emit_K`):* `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {a}` for a fresh link address `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` with `Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K)`; `Σ'.C = Σ.C` and `Σ'.M = Σ.M`.

These commitments are at the substrate-model interface and constrain only the visible values of `Σ.C, Σ.M, Σ.L` after the transition. Concrete implementations may maintain auxiliary backing structures (separate index trees, shared B-tree pages, POOM entries) and may write to several such structures per logical transition without violating any frame condition above; the implementation's `docreatelink` in udanax-green, for instance, writes to the granfilade orgl tree, the home document's POOM, and the spanfilade for each link emission, but the abstract values `Σ.C, Σ.M, Σ.L` move only as (iii) prescribes. In particular, R0's role below is to discharge the existential "there exists a `→` step extending `dom(Σ.L)` by a fresh address satisfying L0/L1/L1a/L1b/L1c/L3" — *not* to derive the frame conditions on `Σ.C` and `Σ.M`, which are part of (iii)'s definition. R0 Step 4 invokes the frame definitionally when setting `Σ'.C := Σ.C` and `Σ'.M := Σ.M`.

*Status — model definition, not derivation.* The three classes (i), (ii), (iii) jointly *define* the dom-extending transition vocabulary of ASN-0086's substrate model. They are not derived from L12, L12a, S0, S9, T8, or any other invariant in the foundation ASNs (which permit broader transition vocabularies — ASN-0034's underlying `→_{0034}` admits allocator-state-only transitions that have no class-(i)/(ii)/(iii) projection, and the udanax-green substrate's `findisatoinsertmolecule` writes to multiple backing structures per logical class-(iii) deposit). ASN-0086 selects (i)/(ii)/(iii) as the abstraction at which the relational layer factors cleanly into `Emit_K`, `Observe_K`, and `Nullify`; alternative coarsenings remain admissible (Appendix A.2 traces the precise projection from `→_{0034}` to `→`, with finer or coarser substrate-level abstractions also conformant under stated conditions). ASN-0086's commitment to (i)/(ii)/(iii) is parallel in standing to its other model-level commitments — the Setup hypothesis (class-(ii) emission policy, *Maintenance protocol* above) and the sibling-frontier discipline (class-(iii) emission policy, named below) — each of which constrains a primitive-transition class beyond what the underlying ASNs entail.

**Lemma — FramePreservation.** *[Setup-free.]* Let `↝ ∈ {→, ↦}` be either of this note's transition relations and `Σ ↝ Σ'` a transition. For any substrate-level predicate `P` whose free variables draw only from substrate components `X` for which the transition's frame asserts `Σ'.X = Σ.X` (equal as functions, hence identical in domain, range, and pointwise value), the propositions `P(Σ)` and `P(Σ')` are the same proposition:

`(A Σ ↝ Σ', P : free(P) ⊆ X ∧ Σ'.X = Σ.X :: P(Σ) ⟺ P(Σ'))`

*Justification.* By definitional identity of inputs: substituting `Σ'.X` for `Σ.X` in `P` leaves the proposition unchanged, since the two are equal as functions / values. Preservation holds by input-substitution, not by re-verification of `P`'s content.

*Specializations used below.* Five frame-cases instantiate the lemma:
- *(a) Class-(iii) frame on `→`.* Class-(iii) steps hold `Σ'.C = Σ.C` and `Σ'.M = Σ.M`. Every predicate whose free variables draw only from `(Σ.C, Σ.M)` is preserved.
- *(b) Class-(i) frame on `→`.* Class-(i) steps hold `Σ'.C = Σ.C` and `Σ'.L = Σ.L`. Every predicate whose free variables draw only from `(Σ.C, Σ.L)` is preserved.
- *(c) Class-(ii) frame on `→`.* Class-(ii) steps hold `Σ'.M = Σ.M` and `Σ'.L = Σ.L`. Every predicate whose free variables draw only from `(Σ.M, Σ.L)` is preserved.
- *(d) Arrangement-modification frame on `↦ \ →`.* Arrangement-modifying steps hold `Σ'.C = Σ.C`, `Σ'.L = Σ.L`, and `dom(Σ'.M) = dom(Σ.M)` (Scoping note above). Every predicate whose free variables draw only from `(Σ.C, Σ.L, dom(Σ.M))` is preserved.
- *(e) Scope-orthogonality to endset content under any frame.* Whenever `P`'s free variables draw from substrate components disjoint from the endset values of `Σ.L` — i.e., `P` is a predicate over `(Σ.C, Σ.M, dom(Σ.L))` together with the tumbler algebra and the `→`-admissibility relation (both state-independent in the relevant sense — T1 and T3 fix the tumbler algebra as a fixed pre-state structure, and `→`-admissibility is witnessed by L1c chains under the foundation ASNs) — a transition that *only* alters endset content (or extends `dom(Σ.L)` with a witnessed-admissible new address whose admissibility R0 separately discharges) leaves `P` unchanged.

**Substrate emission primitive (for `Emit_K`).** The substrate admits, as its primitive emission for the link store, *emit-at-any-L1c-conforming-fresh-address*: for any state `Σ` and any address `a` jointly satisfying (1) `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, (2) L0/L1/L1a/L1b at `a` (i.e., `a.E₁ = s_L`, `zeros(a) = 3`, `home(a) ∈ dom(Σ.M)`, `#E(a) ≥ 2`), and (3) the existence of a T10a-conforming producer chain witnessing `a`'s reachability per L1c on `Σ`, there exists a `→` step `Σ → Σ'` of class (iii) with `Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K)` for any `(F, G, K)` satisfying L3. The L1c chain is required to *exist as a conformance witness* on `Σ`; it is not required to be operationally re-traversed by the emission, and intermediate addresses along the chain are not required to be in `dom(Σ.L)` or `dom(Σ.C)`. *Allocator-state commitment.* The atomic class-(iii) step implicitly discharges T10a's child-spawn admissibility at each intermediate spawn pair on the L1c witness chain in one indivisible action; allocator state has no observable evolution apart from the emissions whose witness chains it supports. See Appendix A.1 for the sparse-allocator interpretation, the udanax-green substrate's canonical realization (`findisatoinsertmolecule`, granf2.c:158–181), the alignment with Nelson's ghost-element design, and the Case A sibling-sweep consequence (no `Σ.C` write induced at intermediate positions).

*Coarsening of ASN-0034's transition relation.* ASN-0086's `→` is a coarsening of ASN-0034's underlying transition relation under which maximal sequences of T1 (sibling-increment) and T2 (child-spawn) ASN-0034-transitions with no exposed effect on `(dom(Σ.C), dom(Σ.M), dom(Σ.L))` collapse to `→`-stutter, and each substrate-level dom-extension projects to one `→`-step. The L1c witness chain traversed by Step 2 is one such collapsed sequence. See Appendix A.2 for the precise projection, the admissibility of alternative (finer or coarser) abstractions, and the udanax-green substrate's bijection with this note's `→`-steps.

*Breadth of the primitive vs. the discipline R0a names.* The primitive is permissive — it admits, for example, `a' = a₁.1` for an existing `a₁ ∈ dom(Σ.L)` (set `a' = a₁.1` with `s_L`, `zeros = 3`, `home = home(a₁)`, `#E ≥ 2` satisfied, and the L1c chain extended from `a₁`'s chain by one child-spawn at `(a₁, 1)` — the only fresh child-spawn pair, with `k' = 1` so no zeros constraint). The primitive then permits the step `Σ → Σ'` with `Σ'.L(a') = (F, G, K)`, after which `a₁ ≼ a'` and `a' ≠ a₁`. R0a's antichain conclusion is therefore *not* a property of the substrate primitive in isolation — the primitive admits emissions that falsify it. R0a holds only under a stricter implementation discipline, which we name and characterize next, and which the substrate primitive's R0 witness already constructs (Step 2's Case A and Case B both choose sibling-frontier addresses, never prefix-extensions). The substrate primitive is what R0 *discharges* (existence of *some* fresh-address emission); the discipline is what R0a *additionally hypothesizes*.

**Implementation discipline — sibling-frontier link emission.** The *sibling-frontier discipline* on `→` requires that every class-(iii) transition (every Emit_K) deposits the fresh link address at an address constructed by R0 Step 2: either the first link sited under a document (Step 2 Case A: `a = d.0.s_L.1`) or the next sibling of an existing link in the document's link-allocator enumeration (Step 2 Case B: `a = incⁱ(b, 0)` for the least `i ≥ 1` with `incⁱ(b, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, where `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` has `home(b) = home(a)`). Equivalently: the discipline never invokes the substrate primitive at an address that is a strict prefix-extension of an existing link address; every emission lands at a sibling-frontier position within the relevant document's link-allocator enumeration. The discipline is a hypothesis on the implementation's choice of which substrate-primitive-permissible address to actually deposit at; it is not entailed by the primitive. R0 Step 2's construction witnesses that the discipline is *realizable* on every state with `dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅` — sibling-frontier addresses always exist — but realizability is not adoption. Concrete adoption requires a binding implementation rule. The udanax-green substrate adopts the discipline directly: the `LINKATOM` branch of `findisatoinsertmolecule` (granf2.c:170–175) deposits every link at `lowerbound + 1` (a sibling of the previous link, via `tumblerincrement(·, 0, 1, ·)`) or at `docaddr` extended by two positions (Case A), and never invokes any increment that would place a link as a child of another link.

**Definition — Extension.** `Σ' extends Σ`, written `Σ ⊑ Σ'`, is the reflexive-transitive closure of `→`:

`Σ ⊑ Σ' ≡ Σ →* Σ'`

By the frame conditions of (i)–(iii) — each primitive transition extends exactly one of `Σ.C`, `Σ.M`, `Σ.L` at a fresh key and leaves the other two components unchanged — `Σ ⊑ Σ'` entails `dom(Σ.C) ⊆ dom(Σ'.C)`, `dom(Σ.M) ⊆ dom(Σ'.M)`, `dom(Σ.L) ⊆ dom(Σ'.L)`, with `Σ'.C|_{dom(Σ.C)} = Σ.C`, `Σ'.M|_{dom(Σ.M)} = Σ.M`, `Σ'.L|_{dom(Σ.L)} = Σ.L`. The phrase "Σ' extending Σ" used throughout this note (and lifted from ASN-0043 invariant restatements such as L9, L11b) is this relation.

**Definition — BroadExtension.** In parallel, `Σ ⊑̂ Σ'` is the reflexive-transitive closure of `↦` (the broader transition relation introduced in the Scoping note above):

`Σ ⊑̂ Σ' ≡ Σ ↦* Σ'`

Every `Σ ⊑ Σ'` entails `Σ ⊑̂ Σ'` (since `→ ⊆ ↦`); `⊑̂` additionally collects sequences that include arrangement-modifying steps, which `⊑` excludes. By the arrangement-modification frame (above) every arrangement-modifying step holds `Σ.C` and `Σ.L` identical and changes only `Σ.M`'s pointwise values (specifically `Σ.M(d)` for the edited document `d`); composed with class-(i)/(ii)/(iii) frames, `Σ ⊑̂ Σ'` entails `dom(Σ.C) ⊆ dom(Σ'.C)`, `dom(Σ.M) ⊆ dom(Σ'.M)`, `dom(Σ.L) ⊆ dom(Σ'.L)`, and `Σ'.L|_{dom(Σ.L)} = Σ.L` (link store extension only). R6c is stated against `⊑`; its corollary lifts the conclusion to `⊑̂`, drawing on the link-component identity that arrangement-modifying steps preserve.

**Definition — AddressUniverse.** The substrate's address universe at state Σ is

`A^Σ = dom(Σ.C) ∪ dom(Σ.L)`

By L14 (DualPrimitive, ASN-0043) and the setup hypothesis, `A^Σ` is the entirety of stored-entity addresses at Σ; no third category exists.

**Definition — Partition.** Define:

`A_doc^Σ = dom(Σ.C)` &nbsp; — content addresses
`A_rel^Σ = dom(Σ.L)` &nbsp; — relation-tuple addresses

We claim `A^Σ = A_doc^Σ ⊔ A_rel^Σ` (disjoint union). The disjointness is R4 below.

*Notation.* All three sets are state-dependent — they grow as the substrate evolves. Where the ambient state is unambiguous, we drop the superscript and write `A`, `A_doc`, `A_rel`.

**Definition — TypeCatalog.** The set of *admissible types* is

`T_admissible = {K ∈ Endset : K ≠ ∅}`

— non-empty endsets, eligible to serve as a link's type endset by L3 (NEndsetStructure, ASN-0043). For each state Σ, the *type catalog at Σ* is the subset actually in use:

`T_cat^Σ = {Θ ∈ T_admissible : (E a ∈ dom(Σ.L) :: |Σ.L(a)| = 3 ∧ Σ.L(a).e₃ = Θ)}`

By L4 (EndsetGenerality, ASN-0043) and L9 (TypeGhostPermission, ASN-0043), `T_admissible` is unconstrained by content existence: type endsets may reference any tumbler addresses, including ghosts. We require only that type-equality is decidable by endset comparison — which it is, by L8 (TypeByAddress).

Type indices in what follows range over `T_admissible`, not `T_cat^Σ`. `T_cat^Σ` is descriptive — the snapshot of which type-endset values literally appear at the type slot in state Σ — but is not constitutive: `L_K^Σ` (below) is well-defined for any `K ∈ T_admissible` and is simply empty when no `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` has a stored type-slot endset coverage-equivalent to `K`. This avoids the bootstrap circularity that would arise if `K ∈ T_cat^Σ` were required as a precondition for introducing a genuinely new type via emission.

*Asymmetry between `T_cat^Σ` and `L_K^Σ`.* `T_cat^Σ` is defined by *literal endset equality* at the type slot (`Σ.L(a).e₃ = Θ`), while `L_K^Σ` (below) is defined by *coverage-equivalence* (`coverage(Σ.L(a).e₃) = coverage(K)`). Two coverage-equivalent but literally-distinct endsets `Θ₁ ≠ Θ₂` with `coverage(Θ₁) = coverage(Θ₂)` would appear as separate elements of `T_cat^Σ` yet collapse into a single coverage-class slice `L_{[Θ₁]}^Σ = L_{[Θ₂]}^Σ`. The asymmetry is deliberate: `T_cat^Σ` records the *literal type-slot values stored at Σ*, while `L_K^Σ` is the *coverage-class slice* over which active-subset and retraction machinery operate. Statements that need the coverage-class semantics use `L_K^Σ`-membership (e.g., `L_R^Σ ≠ ∅`) rather than `T_cat^Σ`-membership; statements about literally-stored type-slot values (e.g., "the system has emitted a tuple whose type-slot endset is literally `R`") use `T_cat^Σ`.

For the rest of this development we restrict attention to standard-triple links — those with `|Σ.L(a)| = 3`. Higher-arity links (L3, NEndsetStructure, ASN-0043) exist in `dom(Σ.L)` but are not members of any `L_K`; they admit an analogous construction with additional slot positions, which we do not pursue here.


## The Typed Relation

**Definition — TypeEquivalence.** Two admissible types are *type-equivalent* iff they cover the same address set:

`K ~ K' ≡ coverage(K) = coverage(K')`

This is L8's (TypeByAddress, ASN-0043) notion of `same_type`, lifted from links to type endsets themselves. The quotient `T_admissible / ~` is the set of *coverage classes*; the equivalence class of `K` is written `[K]`.

**Definition — TypedRelation.** For each `K ∈ T_admissible` and state Σ, the *typed relation of type K at Σ* is

`L_K^Σ = {(a, F, G) : a ∈ dom(Σ.L) ∧ |Σ.L(a)| = 3 ∧ Σ.L(a).e₁ = F ∧ Σ.L(a).e₂ = G ∧ coverage(Σ.L(a).e₃) = coverage(K)}`

Each member is a triple of (tuple-address, from-endset, to-endset). The pair `(F, G)` is the *relational content* of the tuple; `a` is the *tuple address*. Membership at the type slot is by coverage-equivalence, not by literal endset value: a tuple stored with third endset `K'` belongs to `L_K^Σ` whenever `K' ~ K`, so `L_K^Σ = L_{K'}^Σ` whenever `K ~ K'`. The substrate's standard-triple link store at state Σ is therefore the disjoint union over coverage classes:

`L^Σ = ⨆_{[K] ∈ T_admissible / ~} L_K^Σ`

We will show (R1) that this disjoint union is well-defined: each tuple address belongs to exactly one coverage-class slice. Note that `L^Σ` collects only the arity-3 links; higher-arity links in `dom(Σ.L)` are outside its scope, as noted above. Where ambient state is clear we drop the superscript and write `L_K`, `L`.

*Rationale for coverage-equivalence.* L8 (TypeByAddress, ASN-0043) defines link type-equality through coverage of the third endset, not through endset-value identity. Taking `L_K^Σ` to use literal endset equality at the type slot would make `L_K` a strict refinement of L8's equivalence: two tuples whose type endsets cover the same address set via differently structured spans would lie in distinct `L_K`'s here yet be "same type" under L8 — and the active-subset machinery would silently miss retractions whose type endset was coverage-equivalent to `R` but not literally equal. The coverage-equivalence definition aligns `L_K` with L8 and renders all coverage-equivalent type endsets interchangeable for retraction-relation membership and active-subset computation. (This matches the convention of every endset-comparison primitive in the substrate stack, which projects through `coverage(·)` rather than comparing raw endset values.)

**Definition — TupleAddress.** Define `addr : L^Σ → A_rel^Σ` by `addr(a, F, G) = a`.

*Remark — relation to ℘(A) × ℘(A).* A generic mathematical typed relation is a subset of `℘(A) × ℘(A)` — a set of address-pair-pairs distinguished only by content. Our typed relation is richer: each tuple carries an address that participates in the relation's identity. The projection `(a, F, G) ↦ (coverage(F), coverage(G))` recovers the address-pair view, but it loses information that the substrate retains (R0, R1).


## Tuple Identity (R0, R1, R2)

A generic mathematical relation distinguishes its members only by content: two tuples with identical (F, G) are the same tuple. The substrate's relations do not work that way. Each tuple emission allocates a fresh address (R0), the address-to-pair binding is a function (R1), and the binding is permanent (R2).

**R0 — TupleAddressFreshness.** *[Setup-required: the L14a-preservation step in Step 4 uses `ran(Σ.M) ⊆ s_C-resident content`, derived from S3 + Setup.]* For any state Σ with `dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅` and any `(F, G, K) ∈ Endset × Endset × T_admissible`, there exists a state Σ' with Σ → Σ' that emits a tuple with content (F, G) of type K at a fresh address:

`(A Σ : dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅ :: (A F, G ∈ Endset, K ∈ T_admissible :: (E Σ' extending Σ, a : a ∉ dom(Σ.L) :: a ∈ dom(Σ'.L) ∧ Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K))))`

*Proof.* We construct the witness in four steps. Let `LS(d) = {a ∈ T : d ≼ a ∧ T4-valid(a) ∧ zeros(a) = 3 ∧ a.E₁ = s_L ∧ #E(a) ≥ 2}` denote the well-formed link-subspace addresses sited under document `d` (combining the T4 element-level constraint, the L0 first-element-field marker `s_L`, and the L1b depth requirement).

(Step 1 — locate a home document.) By precondition, `dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅`; pick any `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`. By L1a (LinkScopedAllocation, ASN-0043), every link address `a'' ∈ dom(Σ.L)` satisfies `home(a'') = N(a'').0.U(a'').0.D(a'') ∈ dom(Σ.M)` — its document-level prefix is an allocated document. We will exhibit a fresh link address with `home = d`.

(Step 2 — constructively exhibit a reachable fresh address sited under `d`, with its T10a-conforming chain.) We case-split on whether `d` already has any link allocations under `Σ`. Both cases produce a concrete `a ∈ LS(d) \ dom(Σ.L)` together with its T10a-conforming chain from `d` (seed `s = d` is T4-valid by S7d, ASN-0036, which fixes `zeros(d) = 2` for every document tumbler). The construction mirrors the L1c-conformance argument used by L9 (TypeGhostPermission, ASN-0043) in its own emission proof, narrowed here to the link store and ground sequentially in the allocator's actual history at `Σ`.

*Case A — `d` has no prior link allocations under `Σ`* (`{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d} = ∅`). Set `a = d.0.s_L.1`. The L1c chain from `d` to `a` describes a walk through the depth-1 element-field allocator `A_d` (single shared across all subspaces under `d`, per the *SharedDepthOneAllocator* lemma in Setup): (i) `t₁ = inc(d, 2)` → `d.0.1` — at element-field depth 1, at position 1 of the first element-field component (an enumeration index within `A_d`, not yet committed to any subspace label); the underlying spawn pair is `(d, 2)`, admissible because `zeros(d) = 2 ≤ 2`. If `A_d` has not yet emitted any address under `Σ` (e.g., `d` has no content allocations either), this is the spawn event that creates `A_d`; if `A_d` already exists under `Σ` from prior content emissions, the chain navigates to its already-emitted base. L1c asserts the existence of a conforming chain to `a`, not the re-issuance of every spawn that chain traverses — and likewise here, the chain witnesses `a` as a legal allocator output, not as a re-emission. (ii) Sibling sweep `inc(·, 0)` within `A_d`, advancing from `A_d`'s base `d.0.1` (its first emission, enumeration index 1 at element-field depth 1) to `d.0.s_L` (enumeration index `s_L` at the same depth), applied `s_L − 1` times — each step is a `k = 0` sibling advance within `A_d`, unconditionally T4-preserving (TA5a, ASN-0034) and consistent with T10a's at-most-once discipline because sibling enumeration is the allocator's own monotone advance, not a fresh spawn. *T4-validity chain induction.* *Base:* the seed `d.0.1 = inc(d, 2)` is T4-valid — `d` is T4-valid (S7d, ASN-0036) with `zeros(d) = 2`, and the spawn `(d, 2)` is T4-admissible by step (a) of the *SharedDepthOneAllocator* lemma, with `zeros(d.0.1) = 2 + (2 - 1) = 3` (TA5(d), ASN-0034) within the T4 zero-count cap. *Step:* assume `d.0.k` is T4-valid for some `k ∈ {1, …, s_L − 1}`; the sibling step `d.0.(k+1) = inc(d.0.k, 0)` preserves T4-validity unconditionally by TA5a (ASN-0034), so `d.0.(k+1)` is T4-valid. By induction over `k = 1, 2, …, s_L − 1`, every intermediate `d.0.k` in the sweep is T4-valid, and in particular the sweep's endpoint `d.0.s_L` is T4-valid — discharging the T4-validity hypothesis of step (iii)'s child-spawn `(d.0.s_L, 1)` below. *Witness, not material traversal.* The intermediate positions `d.0.1, d.0.2, …, d.0.s_L − 1` need not have been emitted into `dom(Σ.C)` or `dom(Σ.L)` for this sweep to be admissible — the chain *witnesses* `a` as a legal allocator output, not a sequence of substrate-level deposits. The substantive commitment licensing this reading is the sparse-allocator interpretation of the substrate emission primitive (above, *Allocator-state commitment*; see Appendix A.1 for details), under which the atomic class-(iii) step implicitly discharges T10a's child-spawn admissibility at each intermediate spawn pair on the L1c witness chain in one indivisible action, with no per-position deposit required. Without that interpretation, the sweep would have to be paired with deposits at every intermediate position; with it, the sweep is a chain-of-witness, not a chain-of-emissions. The udanax-green `findisatoinsertmolecule` (granf2.c:158–181) realizes this directly — it computes the next-available address by structural query of `dom(Σ.L)` and the granfilade tree, depositing only at the final position. The `s_L`-th element of `A_d`'s enumeration lands in the link subspace by L0 (SubspacePartition, ASN-0043), which identifies link-resident addresses by their first-element-field component being `s_L`; nothing in the construction privileges `A_d`'s base as "the content position" — `A_d` enumerates positions whose subspace identity is fixed by L0 and the substrate's `(s_C, s_L)` convention, not by ordinal precedence within `A_d`. (iii) `inc(d.0.s_L, 1)` → `d.0.s_L.1 = a` — child-spawn `(d.0.s_L, 1)` opening the link allocator at allocator-tree depth 2 (`k' = 1`, with TA5a unconditional for `k' = 1`, giving `zeros(a) = 3` — same element-field depth as `d.0.1`; the `(·, 1)` spawn descends one level in the allocator hierarchy without adding a new zero, per the *allocator-tree depth* definition above); this spawn opens the allocator-tree-depth-2 link-allocator rooted at `a` (written `A_a` when needed for downstream reference — Case B's sibling stream `a, inc(a, 0), inc²(a, 0), …` enumerates `A_a`, not `A_d`). *Home and freshness.* Every step from `t₁` onward operates at length `> #d`, so by chain-prefix-preservation (TA5(b) for `k' ≥ 1` and TA5(c) for `k = 0`) every intermediate tumbler agrees with `d` on positions `1..#d`; therefore `home(a) = d`. Case A's hypothesis `{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d} = ∅` then yields `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` directly: if `a` were in `dom(Σ.L)`, it would belong to that set (since `home(a) = d`), contradicting the hypothesis. The argument needs no premise about prior spawn events at `(d.0.s_L, 1)`; the L1c chain merely witnesses `a`'s validity as a producer output, and freshness is settled by Case A's exclusion at the deposit level. (Equivalently, by L11a (LinkUniqueness, ASN-0043) any `a'' ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `home(a'') = d` carries its own L1c chain anchored at `d`; Case A's hypothesis makes that chain set empty, again giving `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` without inspecting the at-most-once spawn ledger.) The membership conditions for `a ∈ LS(d)` are immediate by construction: `d ≼ a`, `T4-valid(a)` by T10a.4 (T4PreservationUnderDiscipline, ASN-0034), `zeros(a) = 3`, `a.E₁ = s_L`, `#E(a) = 2 ≥ 2`.

*Case B — `d` has prior link allocations under `Σ`* (`{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d} ≠ ∅`). Pick any existing link `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `home(b) = d`. By L1c (LinkAllocatorConformance, ASN-0043) on `Σ`, `b` has a T10a-conforming chain `c_b` from `d`. Consider the sibling stream `b, inc(b, 0), inc²(b, 0), …`, which by T10a.7 (EnumerationInjectivity, ASN-0034) is injective and hence infinite. By L-fin (LinkStoreFiniteness, ASN-0043), `dom(Σ.L)` is finite, so the least `i ≥ 1` with `incⁱ(b, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)` exists; set `a = incⁱ(b, 0)`. Freshness is immediate from the least-`i` choice. The T10a-conforming chain from `d` to `a` is `c_b` extended by `i` sibling advances `inc(·, 0)`, each unconditionally T4-preserving (TA5a is unconditional for `k = 0`). The extension's correctness as a producer chain rests on *sibling-stream uniqueness within an allocator's enumeration*, witnessed by T10a.7 (EnumerationInjectivity, ASN-0034) — which makes the stream `b, inc(b, 0), inc²(b, 0), …` injective, so each `incⁱ(b, 0)` is a distinct enumeration index — together with L12 (LinkImmutability, ASN-0043) — which keeps the prior occupied prefix `b, inc(b, 0), …, incⁱ⁻¹(b, 0)` in `dom(Σ.L)` so the least-`i` selection is well-defined against the allocator's already-emitted addresses. (T10a's at-most-once *child-spawn* discipline binds only `(·, k')` pairs with `k' ∈ {1, 2}`; the sibling pairs `(incʲ(b, 0), 0)` along this extension are not child-spawn events, and the at-most-once axiom does not bind them — sibling-stream uniqueness is supplied by T10a.7 + L12 instead.) By T10a.8 (UniformSiblingZeroCount, ASN-0034), `zeros(a) = zeros(b) = 3`; by chain-prefix-preservation (TA5(c) for `k = 0` preserves all positions but the rightmost, applied `i` times), `home(a) = home(b) = d`, `a.E₁ = b.E₁ = s_L`, and `#E(a) = #E(b) ≥ 2` (by L1b on `Σ` for `b`). The membership conditions for `a ∈ LS(d)` are immediate: `d ≼ a` (since `d ≼ b ≼ a` by transitivity of `≼`), `T4-valid(a)` by T10a.4, and the element-field constraints just discharged.

(Step 3 — collect the chain.) Step 2 has, in each case, exhibited a concrete `a ∈ LS(d) \ dom(Σ.L)` together with its T10a-conforming chain from `d` (seed `s = d`, length `≥ 1`, monotone in length by TA5; final step satisfying its `k' ⟹ zeros` precondition). This discharges L1c for `a`: the producer chain is `c_b` extended by sibling steps (Case B) or the fresh three-stage chain (i)–(iii) above (Case A).

(Step 4 — confirm freshness, exhibit Σ', verify invariants.) The T10a axiom's at-most-once child-spawning constraint, combined with T10a.7 (EnumerationInjectivity, ASN-0034), ensures each allocator's enumeration produces each address at most once. T10a.6 (DomainDisjointness, ASN-0034) ensures distinct allocators have disjoint domains. The chain of Step 3 is therefore the unique chain (up to allocator identity) producing `a`, and `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` by Step 2 — so the address `a` is fresh, and the L0/L1/L1a/L1b/L1c preconditions of the substrate emission primitive (above) are jointly discharged by Steps 1–3. Invoking the primitive: there exists a `→` step of class (iii) with `Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K)` and `Σ'.C = Σ.C`, `Σ'.M = Σ.M` *by definition of class (iii)* (Frame conditions, above) — Step 4's role is not to derive the `Σ.C`/`Σ.M` frame but to verify that the resulting `Σ'.L` extension respects every L-invariant. Each invariant is verified directly:

- `Σ'.L` remains a partial function: extension at a fresh key.
- L3 (NEndsetStructure, ASN-0043): `(F, G, K)` has `|·| = 3 ≥ 3`, with `F, G ∈ Endset` and `K ∈ T_admissible` non-empty, so the slot-3 type-endset constraint holds.
- L0 (SubspacePartition, ASN-0043): `a.E₁ = s_L` by Step 2's choice, so `subspace_I(a) = s_L`.
- L1 (LinkElementLevel, ASN-0043): `zeros(a) = 3` by Step 2's choice.
- L1a (LinkScopedAllocation, ASN-0043): `home(a) = d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` by Step 2 and Step 1.
- L1b (LinkElementFieldDepth, ASN-0043): `#E(a) ≥ 2` by Step 2's choice.
- L1c (LinkAllocatorConformance, ASN-0043): the chain of Step 3.
- L12 (LinkImmutability, ASN-0043): no existing entry was modified.
- L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity, ASN-0043): `dom(Σ.L) ⊆ dom(Σ'.L)` by construction.
- L12b (HomeDocumentPersistence, ASN-0043): by Frame `Σ'.M = Σ.M`, `dom(Σ.M) ⊆ dom(Σ'.M)`, so prior homes `{home(a') : a' ∈ dom(Σ.L)}` ⊆ `dom(Σ'.M)`; the new home `d = home(a) ∈ dom(Σ.M) = dom(Σ'.M)` by Step 1.
- L-fin (LinkStoreFiniteness, ASN-0043): finite plus one is finite.
- L11a (LinkUniqueness, ASN-0043): preserved by Step 2's freshness witness `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` together with the link-store extension being a single atomic deposit at `a` (class-(iii) atomicity, above). *Level of discharge.* L11a operates at the *deposit level* — distinctness as keys in the partial function `Σ.L`, equivalently no two `→`-steps mapping the same address. It is *not* a spawn-event-level uniqueness claim: T10a's at-most-once axiom on child-spawn pairs `(·, k')` constrains how often an allocator can be *opened* (a sparse, allocator-mediated event), whereas L11a constrains how often a given address can be *deposited as a link store key* (a per-emission event). The two levels are related but distinct — spawn-event uniqueness bounds allocator creation, deposit-level uniqueness bounds store population — and L11a is the latter. *Case-uniform discharge.* The deposit-level criterion follows directly from `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` (Step 2, both cases) together with class-(iii) atomicity (`Σ' = Σ` outside the single deposit at `a`): the new entry at `a` is a fresh key, and no prior entry's key is altered, so `a` is a strictly-new domain element. Case A and Case B differ only in *how `a`'s producer chain was constructed* — Case A terminates with a child-spawn `(d.0.s_L, 1)` opening a fresh allocator `A_{d.0.s_L.1}` whose first emission is `a` (with `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` vacuous since the allocator is just opened); Case B terminates with a sibling step `inc(·, 0)` within an already-opened `A_{d.0.s_L.1}` to a least-`i` index not previously emitted (with `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` enforced by the least-`i` choice). Spawn-event reasoning (the at-most-once axiom on `(d.0.s_L, 1)`) does *not* discharge L11a in Case A; it discharges the L1c producer-chain canonicity used elsewhere (the chain prefix through `(d.0.s_L, 1)` is forced, not chosen — invoked in R0a's sibling-stream induction and at the worked example's `a₂` step). L11a in Case A is discharged, like Case B, by `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)` + deposit atomicity. L11a preserved.
- L11b (NonInjectivity, ASN-0043): permits, does not require, value-level coincidence — the new entry is consistent whether or not `(F, G, K)` already appears at another address.
- L14a (NonTranscludability, ASN-0043): asserts `dom(Σ.L) ∩ ran(Σ.M) = ∅` — link addresses do not occur as transclusion targets inside any document's arrangement. L14a is a preservation lemma: it holds at every reachable state. We argue here only that the single-step extension from Σ to Σ' preserves it, inheriting its prior-state form from Σ. *Prior-state inheritance:* `dom(Σ.L) ∩ ran(Σ.M) = ∅` holds at Σ by L14a evaluated at Σ. *Single-step contribution:* `Σ'.M = Σ.M`, so `ran(Σ'.M) = ran(Σ.M)`, and the only new link address in `dom(Σ'.L)` is `a`; the step's content is the disjointness `{a} ∩ ran(Σ.M) = ∅`. We verify the latter: by Step 2, `subspace_I(a) = s_L`. By S3 (ReferentialIntegrity, ASN-0036), every tumbler appearing in `ran(Σ.M)` is a content address, i.e., lies in `dom(Σ.C)`; by the setup hypothesis, every such address is `s_C`-resident. By the subspace-distinctness axiom (`s_C ≠ s_L`), `subspace_I(a) = s_L ≠ s_C` excludes `a` from `ran(Σ.M)`. Combining the prior-state form with the single-step contribution: `dom(Σ'.L) ∩ ran(Σ'.M) = (dom(Σ.L) ∪ {a}) ∩ ran(Σ.M) = (dom(Σ.L) ∩ ran(Σ.M)) ∪ ({a} ∩ ran(Σ.M)) = ∅ ∪ ∅ = ∅`, preserving L14a at Σ'. (Without the setup hypothesis, L14a would still hold but require L14's scoped form: arrangements may target only the `s_C`-resident content slice, and `a ∈ s_L` is outside that slice by L0.)
- L2 (OwnershipEndsetIndependence, ASN-0043): asserts `home(b)` depends only on `b`, not on endset content. Adding the new entry `a ↦ (F, G, K)` defines `home(a) = d` by Step 1; this is a fact about `a`'s tumbler value, not about `(F, G, K)`. For every prior `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)`, `home(b)` is determined by `b` alone and is unchanged by adding a new key. Preserved.

The new entry's `F, G ∈ Endset` are arbitrary by R0's quantification and may reference any address class; no restriction is imposed by depositing a fresh entry. Preserved.
- L5 (EndsetSetSemantics, ASN-0043): treats endsets with set semantics. The new entry's endsets `F`, `G`, `K` are sets by construction; the deposit at a fresh address introduces no per-position ordering or duplication assumption. Preserved.
- L6 (SlotDistinction, ASN-0043): asserts that positional accessors `.e₁, .e₂, .e₃` are well-defined for `|·| = 3`. The new entry has `|·| = 3` (discharged at L3 above), so the accessors apply pointwise. Preserved.

Preserved.

We verify `{a} ∩ dom(Σ.C)|_{s_C} = ∅`: by Step 2, `subspace_I(a) = a.E₁ = s_L`. Every address in `dom(Σ.C)|_{s_C}` has `subspace_I = s_C` by definition of the slice (L0a, ContentSubspaceScope, ASN-0043). By the subspace-distinctness axiom (`s_C ≠ s_L`) and T3 (CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034) which makes tumblers that disagree in any component distinct as values, `a` (with `subspace_I = s_L`) cannot equal any address with `subspace_I = s_C`. R0's Setup-required tag attaches at the L14a-preservation bullet, not here.)

This is a *structural* property of ASN-0036's state signature, not a fact requiring case-by-case enumeration: ASN-0036's transition vocabulary is defined over the state pair `(Σ.C, Σ.M)` and excludes `Σ.L` from its signature entirely, so any S-invariant well-formed in ASN-0036's signature is — by well-formedness — unable to mention `Σ.L`, irrespective of the invariant's particular content. A skeptical reader can therefore confirm the disjointness from `Σ.L` by inspecting ASN-0036's signature alone, without enumerating S0–S9. Preservation then follows from Lemma — FramePreservation, specialization (a) (class-(iii) frame): `Σ'.C = Σ.C` and `Σ'.M = Σ.M` substitute through any predicate whose free variables draw only from `(Σ.C, Σ.M)`, giving `P(Σ') ⟺ P(Σ)` by definitional identity of inputs. The bullets above (S0, S3, S7a, S7d) are particular instances we have discharged in expanded form for the reader's convenience; the remaining ASN-0036 invariants — content-store invariants over `Σ.C`'s reference structure, document-allocation invariants over `dom(Σ.M)` and document tumbler structure, and arrangement-machine invariants over `Σ.M`'s arrangement values — are covered by the same FramePreservation citation without further enumeration. ∎

*Remark on the precondition.* The hypothesis `dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅` is necessary: by L1a (LinkScopedAllocation), every link address lives under some allocated document, so before any document exists, no link can be allocated. Once at least one document is present, R0 supplies a fresh link address; in particular, R0 may be invoked recursively to add links into the same document or into any subsequently emitted one.

**R0a — FlatLinkDomain (conditional on the sibling-frontier discipline).** *[Setup-free, discipline-conditional.]* *Discipline-restricted reachability relation.* Write `Σ_0 →_D* Σ` for "`Σ` is reachable from `Σ_0` via finitely many `→`-transitions each of whose class-(iii) steps respects the sibling-frontier emission discipline (named above at *Implementation discipline — sibling-frontier link emission*)" — i.e., `→_D*` is the reflexive-transitive closure of `→` restricted at class (iii) to disciplined emissions, with class-(i) and class-(ii) steps unconstrained. Under the discipline (every class-(iii) `→`-transition along the reachability chain is `→_D`-admissible), `dom(Σ.L)` is a tumbler-prefix antichain at every reachable state:

`(A Σ : (E Σ_0 : dom(Σ_0.L) = ∅ ∧ Σ_0 →_D* Σ) :: (A a, a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) :: a ≼ a' ⟹ a = a'))`

The discipline-restricted quantifier range — `Σ` is `→_D*`-reachable from a Σ_0 with empty link store — is *not* a stylistic abbreviation but the substantive scope of the claim: the universal does *not* range over arbitrary `→`-reachable states. The substrate primitive in isolation admits class-(iii) emissions falsifying the antichain (see *Breadth of the primitive vs. the discipline R0a names* above), so R0a fails as a substrate-level theorem on `→`-reachability without the `→_D` restriction; it holds as a theorem about `→_D`-reachability only.

R0a is *not* a property of the substrate emission primitive in isolation: as noted under "Breadth of the primitive vs. the discipline R0a names" above, the primitive admits emissions (e.g., at `a₁.1` for existing `a₁`) that would falsify the antichain. R0a holds for any implementation that adopts the discipline and *only* for such implementations; the udanax-green substrate is one example (cited in the Remark below).

*Proof.* Direct induction on the antichain conclusion does not go through: the inductive step that adds a fresh link sharing a home document with an existing link must invoke T10a.2 (NonNestingSiblingPrefixes, ASN-0034) to obtain prefix-incomparability between the two, and T10a.2's hypothesis requires both addresses to lie in the *same* sibling stream — information strictly stronger than antichain (antichain alone does not pin either address to `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` for any `j`). We therefore strengthen the induction invariant to the *sibling-stream invariant* below, prove that strengthened invariant by induction, and derive the stated antichain conclusion as a corollary.

*Sibling-stream invariant.* For every state Σ reachable from Σ_0 with `dom(Σ_0.L) = ∅` via finitely many discipline-respecting `→`-transitions,

`(A Σ : reachable under the discipline :: (A a ∈ dom(Σ.L) :: (E j ≥ 0 :: a = incʲ(home(a).0.s_L.1, 0))))`

i.e., every link address lies in its home document's link-element-field sibling stream, anchored at the Case A base `home(a).0.s_L.1`.

*Antichain conclusion as corollary.* Let Σ be a reachable state and `a, a' ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `a ≼ a'`. Let `d = home(a)` and `d' = home(a')` (L1a at Σ). If `d = d'`, the sibling-stream invariant places both `a` and `a'` at `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` and `incʲ'(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` for some `j, j' ≥ 0` — both are siblings in the depth-2 link allocator rooted at `d.0.s_L.1` (the allocator `A_{d.0.s_L.1}` opened by R0 Step 2 Case A's step-(iii) child-spawn `(d.0.s_L, 1)`; in the worked example this is `A_{a₁}`, whose base is its first emission). By T10a.7 (EnumerationInjectivity, ASN-0034), `A_{d.0.s_L.1}`'s enumeration coincides with the sibling stream `{incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0) : j ≥ 0}` — its `j`-th emission is `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)`, with `j ≠ j'` distinguishing distinct enumeration outputs — so `a` and `a'` are members of the *same* allocator's domain, discharging T10a.2's same-allocator precondition. By T10a.1 (UniformSiblingLength, ASN-0034) they have equal length, by T10a.8 (UniformSiblingZeroCount, ASN-0034) they share `zeros = 3`, and by T10a.2 (NonNestingSiblingPrefixes, ASN-0034) any two distinct siblings of the same allocator are prefix-incomparable; so `a ≼ a' ⟹ a = a'`. If `d ≠ d'`, the Case 2 sub-argument below (zero-count additivity along prefix-extension + L1) directly precludes `a ≼ a'`. Either way, R0a's antichain conclusion holds.

*Induction.* The sibling-stream invariant is proved by induction on `→`-chain length. *Base* (`Σ_0`): `dom(Σ_0.L) = ∅` makes the invariant vacuously true.

*Step*: assume the sibling-stream invariant at Σ; consider Σ → Σ'. By Frame conditions (above), only class (iii) transitions extend `dom(Σ.L)`; classes (i) and (ii) leave `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L)`, preserving the invariant by induction. For class (iii), the discipline hypothesis on this step constrains the fresh address `a` to be constructed by R0 Step 2 (Case A or Case B), not just any substrate-primitive-permissible address. So `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {a}`. By IH, every `a' ∈ dom(Σ.L)` already satisfies the invariant at Σ' (the invariant is purely a relation between `a'` and `home(a') ∈ dom(Σ.M) ⊆ dom(Σ'.M)`, both held by the frame); it suffices to exhibit a witnessing `j` for the fresh `a` itself. Let `d = home(a) ∈ dom(Σ.M)` (Step 1).

*Sub-case A — `a` constructed via Step 2 Case A* (`a = d.0.s_L.1`). Set `j = 0`; then `a = inc⁰(d.0.s_L.1, 0) = d.0.s_L.1`, witnessing the invariant for `a` at Σ' directly.

*Sub-case B — `a` constructed via Step 2 Case B* (`a = incⁱ(b, 0)` for the least `i ≥ 1` with `incⁱ(b, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, where `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` has `home(b) = d`). By IH applied to `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` (with `home(b) = d`), there exists `k ≥ 0` with `b = incᵏ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)`. Composing, `a = incⁱ(b, 0) = incⁱ(incᵏ(d.0.s_L.1, 0), 0) = incⁱ⁺ᵏ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)`. The composition is well-defined and the equality of iterated forms holds: by TA5a (IncrementPreservesT4, ASN-0034), `inc(·, 0)` is unconditionally T4-preserving for `k = 0`, so the seed `d.0.s_L.1` (T4-valid by R0 Step 2 Case A's discharge) admits an unbroken sequence of k=0 increments, with every intermediate `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` for `j ∈ {0, …, i+k}` remaining T4-valid and signature-valid (TA5-SigValid, ASN-0034) as a legal input to the next step. By TA5(c) for `k = 0` (chain-prefix-preservation, ASN-0034), each sibling step preserves all positions but the rightmost, so all `i + k` increments operate within the *same* depth-level enumeration anchored at `d.0.s_L`, and iterated function application `incⁱ ∘ incᵏ = incⁱ⁺ᵏ` is unambiguous on that single sibling-stream. Setting `j = i + k` (with `j ≥ 1 ≥ 0`) witnesses the invariant for `a` at Σ'.

In both sub-cases the sibling-stream invariant extends to Σ', completing the induction.

*Case 2 sub-argument (no cross-home prefix-comparability)* — invoked by the antichain corollary above. Suppose `d ≠ d'` and `a ≼ a'`. Then `a' = a · w` for some suffix `w` (the digits appended to `a` to obtain `a'`). Zero counts add along concatenation: `zeros(a') = zeros(a) + zeros(w)`. By L1 (LinkElementLevel, ASN-0043), `zeros(a) = zeros(a') = 3`, so `zeros(w) = 0` — `w` contains no zero positions. By L1a (LinkScopedAllocation, ASN-0043), `home(·) = N(·).0.U(·).0.D(·)` — the prefix of the link extending through the third element-field `D(·)` and ending *just before* the third zero. Since `a ≼ a'`, the positions `1..#a` of `a'` agree pointwise with all of `a`; the remaining positions `#a + 1 .. #a'` of `a'` are `w`, which contains no zeros. Therefore every zero of `a'` sits at a position `≤ #a`, and the three zeros of `a'` are *exactly* the three zeros of `a`, at the same positions. In particular, `a'`'s third zero sits at the position of `a`'s third zero — call this position `p₃`, with `p₃ ≤ #a`. The `home` prefix has length `p₃ − 1` (the positions up to and including `D(·)`, which immediately precedes the third zero). Since `p₃ − 1 < p₃ ≤ #a`, the prefix of `a'` of length `p₃ − 1` agrees pointwise with the prefix of `a` of length `p₃ − 1` (by `a ≼ a'` applied at positions `1..#a`); equivalently, `N(a') = N(a)`, `U(a') = U(a)`, and `D(a') = D(a)` — the three element-field components delimited by `a'`'s first and third zeros coincide with those of `a` position-by-position. Therefore `home(a') = N(a').0.U(a').0.D(a') = N(a).0.U(a).0.D(a) = home(a) = d`, contradicting `d' ≠ d`. The reverse direction `a' ⊀ a` (under `d ≠ d'`) is covered by the same argument since the antichain corollary's universal quantification ranges symmetrically over the unordered pair `(a, a')`: instantiating at `(a', a)` repeats the derivation with roles swapped, requiring no separate proof. The Case 2 sub-argument consumes no documents-antichain premise and is independent of the link-emission discipline; the discipline-conditionality of R0a is concentrated entirely in the sibling-stream induction above (which is what makes the same-home case go through). ∎

*Failure modes — necessity of the discipline.* The R0a hypothesis is *necessary*, not just sufficient: a single non-disciplinary class-(iii) transition permanently breaks the antichain (by L12 + L12a: no removal transition exists, so the bad pair persists forever), and Nullify's single-tuple-scope fails at every subsequent state. See Appendix B for the full case analysis with a concrete instantiation against the Worked Sketch, and for the operational summary on partial discipline.

*Remark — substrate evidence (discipline held in practice).* The flat-link-domain invariant matches the link-allocation behavior of the udanax-green implementation: the `LINKATOM` branch of `findisatoinsertmolecule` (granf2.c:170–175) deposits every link at `lowerbound + 1` (a sibling of the previous link, via `tumblerincrement(·, 0, 1, ·)`) or at `docaddr` extended by two positions (the first link sited at depth +2 under the document), and never invokes any increment that would place a link as a child of another link. This is evidence that the sibling-frontier discipline is *held* by the concrete implementation — i.e., the discipline-hypothesis of R0a is empirically satisfied for udanax-green-trajectory systems — but it is not a derivation of the discipline from the substrate primitive. The substrate emission primitive's permission to deposit at any L1c-conforming-fresh-address (above) is broader than the discipline: in particular, a hypothetical alternative emission policy that deposited at `a' = a₁.1` (a strict prefix-extension of an existing link) would satisfy the primitive's conditions (1)/(2)/(3) yet would not respect the discipline. R0a's conditional form makes the dependence visible. Future work that elevates the discipline to a substrate-level guarantee — e.g., by tightening Emit_K's specification to require prefix-incomparability with `dom(Σ.L)` as a postcondition, or by tightening the substrate primitive to forbid emissions at descendants of existing link addresses — would strengthen R0a from discipline-conditional to substrate-level. Under such a strengthening, the sibling-frontier hypothesis above becomes redundant and R0a holds unconditionally.

**R0a-Cor1 — ContiguousPrefixUnderDiscipline.** *[Setup-free, discipline-conditional.]* Under R0a's hypothesis, for every reachable state Σ and every `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`, there exists `J_d^Σ ∈ ℤ_{≥-1}` such that

`{a ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a) = d} = {incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0) : 0 ≤ j ≤ J_d^Σ}`

(with `J_d^Σ = -1` denoting the empty set when no link is homed at `d`). The R0a sibling-stream invariant supplies the `⊆` direction (every homed link lies at some `incʲ`); R0a-Cor1 strengthens this with the `⊇` direction — the occupied positions form a contiguous prefix `{0, 1, …, J_d^Σ}` of the stream, with no skipped indices.

*Proof.* By induction on the `→`-chain length, parallel to R0a's induction. *Direction of the strengthening.* R0a's sibling-stream invariant supplies the `⊆` direction — every homed link lies at some `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` — and is inherited unchanged here. The induction below proves the `⊇` direction: that the set of occupied indices forms a contiguous prefix `{0, 1, …, J_d^Σ}` with no skipped indices. The induction is therefore over the *strengthened* (contiguous-prefix) invariant, not over R0a's sibling-stream invariant alone — the IH below carries the contiguous-prefix property, which is what licenses Sub-case B's bound on `k` and its identification of `i = J_{d_new}^Σ - k + 1`.

*Base.* `dom(Σ_0.L) = ∅`, so `J_d^{Σ_0} = -1` for every `d ∈ dom(Σ_0.M)` (the empty set on the left, the vacuous range on the right).

*Step.* Assume the invariant at Σ; consider Σ → Σ'. Classes (i) and (ii) leave `dom(Σ.L)` unchanged (Frame), preserving the invariant. For class (iii) emitting fresh `a` at home `d_new` via R0 Step 2:

*Sub-case A* — `a = d_new.0.s_L.1 = inc⁰(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0)`; Case A's hypothesis `{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d_new} = ∅` gives `J_{d_new}^Σ = -1`. The post-state homed set is `{a} = {inc⁰(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0)} = {incʲ : 0 ≤ j ≤ 0}`; set `J_{d_new}^{Σ'} = 0`.

*Sub-case B* — `a = incⁱ(b, 0)` for `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `home(b) = d_new`, where `i ≥ 1` is the least with `incⁱ(b, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)`. By IH, the existing homed set is `{incʲ(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0) : 0 ≤ j ≤ J_{d_new}^Σ}` with `J_{d_new}^Σ ≥ 0` (Case B's `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` requires the IH'd set to be non-empty). Hence `b = incᵏ(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0)` for some `k ∈ {0, …, J_{d_new}^Σ}`. The IH's exact form gives both: (i) every `incᵏ⁺¹(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0), …, incᴶ_{d_new}^Σ(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0)` is in `dom(Σ.L)`; (ii) `incᴶ_{d_new}^Σ⁺¹(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)` (no index `> J_{d_new}^Σ` lies in the IH'd set). The least `i ≥ 1` with `incⁱ(b, 0) = incᵏ⁺ⁱ(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)` is therefore `i = J_{d_new}^Σ - k + 1`, yielding `a = incᴶ_{d_new}^Σ⁺¹(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0)`. The post-state homed set is `{incʲ(d_new.0.s_L.1, 0) : 0 ≤ j ≤ J_{d_new}^Σ + 1}`; set `J_{d_new}^{Σ'} = J_{d_new}^Σ + 1`.

For every `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` with `d ≠ d_new`, `{a' ∈ dom(Σ'.L) : home(a') = d} = {a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d}` (no new link is homed elsewhere); the invariant holds at Σ' with `J_d^{Σ'} = J_d^Σ`. In both sub-cases the strengthened invariant extends to Σ', completing the induction. ∎

*Use.* R0a-Cor1 licenses the seed-independence argument in `Emit_K`'s Definition (below): under the discipline, Case B's choice of `b` ranges only over the contiguous prefix `{inc⁰, …, incᴶ_d^Σ}`, and the least-`i` selection lands the fresh emission at the *global* next index `J_d^Σ + 1` regardless of which `b` is chosen. The corollary is otherwise dormant — it is consumed exactly at the `Emit_K`-as-function argument.

**R0a-Cor2 — DepthTwoLinkAddresses.** *[Setup-free, discipline-conditional.]* Under R0a's hypothesis, every link address in `dom(Σ.L)` has exactly two element-fields past its home document:

`(A Σ : (E Σ_0 : dom(Σ_0.L) = ∅ ∧ Σ_0 →_D* Σ) :: (A a ∈ dom(Σ.L) :: #E(a) = 2))`

(Here `#E(a)` counts post-home element-fields, matching the convention used in the worked sketch — e.g., `E(a₁) = [2, 1]`, `#E(a₁) = 2` at the concrete instantiation — and the threshold of L1b (LinkElementFieldDepth, ASN-0043), which admits `#E ≥ 2`.)

*Proof.* By R0a's sibling-stream invariant, every `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` lies on the form `a = incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` for `d = home(a)` and some `j ≥ 0`. The base case `j = 0` gives `a = d.0.s_L.1` with post-home element-fields `[s_L, 1]`, so `#E(a) = 2` directly. Each subsequent sibling step `inc(·, 0)` operates within the depth-2 link allocator `A_{d.0.s_L.1}` and, by TA5(c) (chain-prefix-preservation, ASN-0034), preserves every element-field position other than the rightmost — in particular preserving the element-field count. Hence `#E` is constant across the entire sibling stream at value 2. ∎

*Narrowing — design-vs-implementation tension.* L1b (LinkElementFieldDepth, ASN-0043) admits link addresses with `#E ≥ 2`, allowing in principle deeper-sited link addresses (e.g., `#E ≥ 3` via a chain that opens a level-3 link allocator under an existing link, with the depth-3 link sited as a child of an existing depth-2 link). The sibling-frontier discipline strictly narrows this to `#E = 2`. The narrowing is *not separable* from R0a's antichain conclusion: a depth-3 link sited as a child of an existing depth-2 link `a₁` would have `a₁` as a prefix (`a₁ ≼ a_child`), simultaneously breaking the antichain — so any relaxation that admits deeper links must also relax R0a. The narrowing matches the udanax-green implementation directly: `findisatoinsertmolecule`'s `LINKATOM` branch (granf2.c:170–175) emits at `lowerbound + 1` (a depth-2 sibling, via `tumblerincrement(·, 0, 1, ·)`) or at `docaddr` extended by two positions (the Case A base, also depth-2), and never produces a depth-3-or-deeper link address. Nelson's foundational link-model design admits deeper-sited links as a general principle of the recursive address space — sub-links of sub-links being expressible in the tumbler hierarchy — but the udanax-green implementation never exercises that admission. R0a-Cor2 records this tension explicitly: the discipline's `#E = 2` matches the implementation, narrower than what L1b and Nelson's design jointly permit. Implementations that intend to admit deeper link addresses would require a relaxation of the sibling-frontier discipline (see Open Questions below); under such a relaxation, R0a-Cor2's `#E = 2` would be replaced by a depth bound determined by the relaxation, R0a's sibling-stream invariant would have to be reformulated over a tree of allocators rather than a single stream, and Nullify's single-tuple-scope guarantee would need re-derivation under the broader prefix-incomparability checks the tree structure entails.

**R1 — AddressInjectivity.** *[Setup-free.]* The map `addr : L → A_rel` is an injection:

`(A (a, F, G), (a', F', G') ∈ L : a = a' :: F = F' ∧ G = G' ∧ both belong to the same coverage-class slice L_{[K]})`

*Proof.* `Σ.L` is a partial function `T ⇀ Link` (ASN-0043, Definition of LinkStore). Function-ness gives uniqueness of value: if `a = a'`, then `Σ.L(a) = Σ.L(a')`, and that single value determines the triple `(F, G, K'')` stored at `a`. Therefore `F = F'`, `G = G'`, and the third endset `K''` is unique. Since `coverage(·)` is a pure function on endset values, `coverage(K'')` is a single fixed address set, so the coverage class `[K'']` is unique — whence both members of `L` lie in the same `L_{[K'']}`. ∎

**R2 — TupleAddressPermanence.** *[Setup-free.]* Once allocated, a tuple address resolves permanently to the same relational content:

`(A Σ → Σ', a ∈ dom(Σ.L), (F, G, K) = Σ.L(a) :: a ∈ dom(Σ'.L) ∧ Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K))`

*Proof.* Direct from L12 (LinkImmutability, ASN-0043): for every state transition, every existing link address persists with its value unchanged. ∎

*Consequences.* Each bullet is tagged by type — [COROLLARY] for a theorem-level implication of the R-claim and prior R-claims/L-invariants; [POLICY] for a higher-layer convention not entailed by the substrate; [ARCHITECTURE] for a structural design observation about the substrate's shape. The typology is fixed once here and applied uniformly in subsequent Consequences sections (R3, R4, R5, R6c).

(a) *[COROLLARY]* *Distinct emissions are distinguishable even when content matches.* Two agents independently filing tuples with identical `(F, G)` under identical `K` produce distinct addresses (R0 produces a fresh address regardless of value). By L11b (NonInjectivity, ASN-0043), value-level coincidence is permitted; by R1, address-level identity nevertheless distinguishes them. The substrate does not silently merge them.

(b) *[COROLLARY]* *Counting is well-defined.* `|{(a, F, G) ∈ L_K : pattern matches (F, G)}|` is a number, not an equivalence-class size, because the elements counted are distinct addresses (R1).

(c) *[COROLLARY]* *Audit references are stable forever.* An address written into the to-set of any tuple in cycle 1 still resolves to the same emission in cycle N, after the substrate has grown by orders of magnitude (R2). The reference does not need re-validation.

(d) *[POLICY]* *Idempotency on emit is policy, not substrate guarantee.* The substrate accepts duplicate emissions — R0 produces a fresh address regardless of whether identical content already exists. Higher layers wishing at-most-once semantics check `(E (a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ :: F, G match)` before calling Emit. This is a layer above the substrate's primitive.


## Append-Only Slices (R3)

**R3 — TypedSliceMonotonicity.** *[Setup-free.]* Each typed relation grows monotonically:

`(A Σ → Σ', K ∈ T_admissible :: L_K^Σ ⊆ L_K^{Σ'})`

where `L_K^Σ` denotes the typed relation evaluated at state `Σ`.

*Proof.* Let `(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ`. By Definition of `L_K^Σ` (membership at the type slot is by coverage-equivalence, not by literal endset value), `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `Σ.L(a) = (F, G, K'')` for some `K'' ∈ T_admissible` satisfying `coverage(K'') = coverage(K)`. By L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity, ASN-0043), `dom(Σ.L) ⊆ dom(Σ'.L)`; by R2, `Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K'')` — the literal value stored at `a` is preserved exactly. The membership test for `L_K^{Σ'}` is `coverage(Σ'.L(a).e₃) = coverage(K)`, i.e., `coverage(K'') = coverage(K)`, which holds by the choice of `K''`. Therefore `(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^{Σ'}`. ∎

*Consequences.* (Typology per R2's Consequences key.)

(a) *[COROLLARY]* *One-directional audit stability.* "A tuple of type K with this `(F, G)` existed at some point" stays true once true. Histories do not rewrite themselves.

(b) *[COROLLARY]* *Retractions are themselves auditable.* When we introduce the retraction type `R` (R6), `L_R` is one of the typed slices and R3 applies to it as well. Every nullification leaves an entry in `L_R` that persists.

(c) *[COROLLARY]* *Historical replay is well-defined.* `L_K` at past cycle `n` is a prefix of `L_K` at any cycle `m ≥ n`; "what was the substrate at cycle n?" is computable from the current substrate and a cycle-cutoff predicate. No separate snapshot mechanism is required.

(d) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *No information loss.* No compaction, no garbage collection, no archive tier removes tuples from `L_K`. The substrate's reliability for downstream agents — that an emission in cycle 3 is still observable in cycle 30 — is exactly R3.


## Subspace Disjointness (R4)

**R4 — TupleAddressDisjointness.** *[Setup-required: the proof reduces L14's scoped form to substrate-wide disjointness by appealing to globally `s_C`-resident content.]* Under the setup hypothesis, tuple addresses and document-content addresses are disjoint:

`A_doc^Σ ∩ A_rel^Σ = ∅`

*Proof.* By the setup hypothesis, every content address is `s_C`-resident, so the slice `dom(Σ.C)|_{s_C}` (L0a, ContentSubspaceScope, ASN-0043) coincides with `dom(Σ.C)`. L14 (DualPrimitive, ASN-0043) asserts `dom(Σ.L) ∩ dom(Σ.C)|_{s_C} = ∅`. Substituting, `dom(Σ.L) ∩ dom(Σ.C) = ∅`, i.e., `A_doc^Σ ∩ A_rel^Σ = ∅`. ∎

*Remark on the underlying structural mechanism.* L14's derivation in ASN-0043 rests on three facts: link addresses have `subspace_I = s_L` (L0, SubspacePartition), the `s_C`-resident content slice has `subspace_I = s_C` (L0a, ContentSubspaceScope), and the subspace-distinctness axiom (stated above near the Setup hypothesis) fixes `s_C ≠ s_L` as distinct first-element-field subspace identifiers. T3 (CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034) makes tumblers that disagree in any component distinct as values, so addresses with distinct first-element-field components are distinct tumblers. T7 (SubspaceDisjointness, ASN-0034) packages this for element-level addresses, but its applicability requires `zeros(a) = zeros(a') = 3` on both addresses — which L1 (LinkElementLevel, ASN-0043) supplies for link addresses and the analogous ASN-0036 element-level constraint supplies for `s_C`-resident content. R4 invokes only the consolidated L14, deferring this chain to ASN-0043's derivation.

*Remark on L14's scoped form.* L14 supplies disjointness scoped to its hypothesis on which subspace each content address inhabits; here the setup hypothesis is what discharges that scoping globally. Where future work admits `s_L`-resident content, R4 would be replaced by L14 in its native scoped form, and the consequences below would apply slice by slice rather than substrate-wide.

*Consequences.* (Typology per R2's Consequences key.)

(a) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Predicates are typeable.* A predicate like `is_classified(d, K)` has signature `A_doc × T_cat → Bool`; a predicate like `is_active(τ)` (R6) has signature `A_rel → Bool`. No predicate has an ambiguous signature; categorical confusion at the address level is impossible.

(b) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Retraction is well-typed.* Only `A_rel` addresses are valid arguments to Nullify (R6). "Retracting a document" is not directly expressible — the to-set of an `L_R` tuple must contain a tuple address, not a document address. Document removal from active consideration is done via classifier tuples (e.g., `L_retired`) targeting the document; the document's `A_doc` address is never disturbed.

(c) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Lifecycle separation.* Documents have mutable bodies (arrangements `Σ.M` change per ASN-0036); tuples never mutate (R2). The address-level structure permits these to diverge without interference.


## Self-Reference (R5)

**R5 — TupleSelfTargeting.** *[Setup-required: Stage 1 invokes R0's emission construction, whose L14a-preservation step at Step 4 uses the Setup hypothesis; the Setup-requirement of R0 propagates to R5 along that invocation.]* A tuple's from-set or to-set may reference tuple addresses. Specifically, for any state Σ and any `a ∈ A_rel^Σ`, the unit-depth span `(a, δ(1, #a))` is well-formed and may appear in the from-set or to-set of an emitted tuple, with `a` in its coverage. (The Worked Sketch below, Step 1, instantiates R5: the retraction tuple's to-set references the link address `a₁` directly.)

*Justification (positive permission).* We argue in two stages.

(Stage 1 — the construct is permitted.) L4(c) (EndsetGenerality, ASN-0043) explicitly states that endset spans may reference addresses in the link subspace `s_L` — i.e., addresses of other links. L13 (ReflexiveAddressing, ASN-0043) establishes that for any address `b`, the unit-depth span `(b, δ(1, #b))` is well-formed with coverage `{t : b ≼ t}` ⊇ `{b}`. Specializing to `b = a` with `a ∈ A_rel^Σ`, the span `(a, δ(1, #a))` is well-formed by L13 and is span-target-admissible by L4(c). R0's construction discharges invariant-preservation for an emission carrying such a span as an endset component: R0 Step 4 verifies each L-invariant against an arbitrary well-formed triple `(F, G, K)`, and the verification chain does not condition on what addresses appear inside `F` or `G` — so taking `F` or `G` to contain `(a, δ(1, #a))` with `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` is admissible by the same step-by-step check. (The L14a-preservation bullet in R0 Step 4 uses the Setup hypothesis to exclude the *fresh emitter address* from `ran(Σ.M)`; that dependence carries through into R5's appeal to R0's emission, so R5 is Setup-required even though Stage 2's check against L14a is orthogonal to span targets.)

(Stage 2 — no invariant opposes the construct.) An exhaustive check of the ASN-0043 invariants confirms none is in opposition to the construction in Stage 1. We list each and identify why:

- L-fin (LinkStoreFiniteness): bounds `|dom(Σ.L)|`; orthogonal to span targets.
- L0 (SubspacePartition): constrains link-address residence (`s_L`), is silent on endset-target subspace.
- L0a (ContentSubspaceScope): defines the `s_C`-resident content slice; orthogonal to span targets.
- L1 (LinkElementLevel), L1a (LinkScopedAllocation), L1b (LinkElementFieldDepth), L1c (LinkAllocatorConformance): constrain `Σ.L`'s domain — what addresses may be in `dom(Σ.L)` — not what values may appear within their endsets.
- L2 (OwnershipEndsetIndependence): asserts `home(a)` depends only on `a`, not on endset content; adding new endset content (including self-targeting spans) leaves `home(·)` undisturbed.
- L3 (NEndsetStructure): requires `|Σ.L(a)| ≥ 3` and the type endset non-empty; agnostic to span targets within endsets.
- L4 (EndsetGenerality), L4(c): the positive permission used in Stage 1.
- L5 (EndsetSetSemantics): treats endsets as unordered sets; orthogonal to target subspace.
- L6 (SlotDistinction): positional accessor `Σ.L(a).eᵢ`; orthogonal to span targets.
- L7 (DirectionalFlexibility): silent on directional interpretation; orthogonal.
- L8 (TypeByAddress): operates on type slot via address-identity comparison; orthogonal to span targets in from/to slots.
- L9 (TypeGhostPermission): permits non-content type endsets; if anything, broadens what can be referenced.
- L10 (TypeHierarchyByContainment): operates on coverage of type endsets; orthogonal to span targets in from/to slots.
- L11a (LinkUniqueness): distinct allocation events produce distinct addresses; compatible with emissions whose endsets self-target.
- L11b (NonInjectivity): permits value-level coincidence; compatible.
- L12 (LinkImmutability), L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity), L12b (HomeDocumentPersistence): forbid mutation and assert persistence; do not forbid new emissions with self-targeting endsets.
- L13 (ReflexiveAddressing): the constructive lemma used in Stage 1.
- L14 (DualPrimitive): asserts content/link residence disjointness; does not restrict span-target subspace within endsets.
- L14a (NonTranscludability): forbids tumblers occurring in arrangements from being link addresses; orthogonal to span targets within link endsets.

The R-properties already derived (R0–R4) similarly impose no restriction on what addresses an emitted endset may contain.

*ASN-0036 and ASN-0034 invariants — orthogonal by scope.* The Stage 2 enumeration above ranges over ASN-0043's L-invariants. The remaining substrate invariants — ASN-0036's S-invariants and ASN-0034's tumbler-algebra invariants (T-, TA-, T10a-) — are also non-opposing, by scope rather than by case analysis. *ASN-0036 (S-invariants).* Every S-invariant is a predicate over `(Σ.C, Σ.M)` (content store and arrangement machine); none references `Σ.L` or its endset values. Stage 1's appeal to R0's emission construction takes a class-(iii) `→` step; preservation across that step is discharged by Lemma — FramePreservation, specialization (a) — exactly the citation invoked at R0 Step 4's ASN-0036 S-invariants bullet, since the choice of endset content does not enter `(Σ.C, Σ.M)` and the frame holds those components identical. *ASN-0034 (T-, TA-, T10a-).* These are predicates over the tumbler algebra and the allocator history, scoped to address-level structural relations on `T` and `→`-step admissibility; they constrain *which tumblers are addresses and how they are produced*, not which addresses may appear inside endsets. Preservation across Stage 1's class-(iii) step is discharged by Lemma — FramePreservation, specialization (e) (scope-orthogonality to endset content): the address `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` referenced by the span `(a, δ(1, #a))` is already in `dom(Σ.L)` by hypothesis (hence already T10a-conforming), and the span itself is a value in `Endset` whose tumbler arguments are validated by L4(c) + L13; the tumbler-algebra invariants neither extend nor restrict endset values. R0's witness chain for the new emitter discharges any T10a-conformance check the post-state introduces. Therefore the construct is permitted by some invariant (L4(c) + L13) and contradicted by none across the full ASN-0034 / ASN-0036 / ASN-0043 invariant base — it is admissible. ∎

*Modal note.* R5 is a positive admissibility lemma: it asserts that a specific substrate-level construction is exhibitable (Stage 1, via L4(c) + L13 + R0) and is not opposed by any other invariant (Stage 2's exhaustive check). The classification is therefore LEMMA, not META — Stage 1 constructs the witness and Stage 2 verifies non-opposition, both substantive proof steps that R5's content depends on. The modality differs from R0–R4 only in surface phrasing: those state existence directly ("the substrate exhibits the property"); R5 states existence indirectly ("the construction is admissible — i.e., exhibitable and non-opposed"). The construction is exhibited concretely by the worked example in ASN-0043 Step 2, where a meta-link of exactly this form appears, confirming the permission is exercised within ASN-0043's own examples.

*Consequences.* Several constructs that would otherwise require out-of-band machinery collapse into the relational primitive. (Typology per R2's Consequences key.)

(a) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Retraction.* A tuple in a designated relation `L_R` whose to-set contains the address of the tuple being nullified. *Dependency chain — existence of the post-state with the retraction tuple:* R5 admits the unit-depth self-targeting span `(a, δ(1, #a))` as a to-set component (Stage 1's L4(c) + L13 chain establishes the span as well-formed and span-target-admissible at the link subspace); R0 at a caller-supplied home `d_retr ∈ dom(Σ.M)` then emits the retraction triple `(∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))}, R)` by its Step 4 invariant-preservation argument, witnessing a state `Σ'` with the retraction tuple at a fresh address in `dom(Σ'.L)`. Mutation becomes Emit; `L_K` is never modified (R3).

(b) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Resolution.* A tuple in `L_resolution` whose to-set contains a comment-tuple's address. *Dependency chain — existence of the post-state with the resolution tuple:* R5 admits the to-span targeting the comment-tuple's address (a relational address in `A_rel^Σ`, admissible by the same L4(c) + L13 chain as (a)); R0 at a caller-supplied home then emits the resolution triple, witnessing a state with the resolution recorded at a fresh `A_rel` address. Comment lifecycle is uniformly substrate-tracked; "this comment is closed" is an ordinary observation, not a flag stored elsewhere.

(c) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Agent provenance.* A tuple whose from-set contains an agent's address and whose to-set contains the emitted tuple. *Dependency chain — existence of the post-state with the provenance tuple:* R5 admits both the from-set's agent-address span and the to-set's emitted-tuple-address span as `A_rel^Σ`-targeting endset components (each unit-depth span discharges via L4(c) + L13, and R5's Stage 1 argument does not distinguish from-slot vs. to-slot — both inherit the same admissibility); R0 at a caller-supplied home emits the provenance triple, witnessing a state with the attribution recorded. Every emission has an attributable emitter as a substrate fact, with no separate metadata channel.

(d) *[COROLLARY for substrate-derivable half; POLICY for conventionally-derivable half]* *Higher-order predicates.* The substrate's Observe machinery uniformly answers any predicate expressible as a query over `L_K`, but the questions split by what each requires as input. *Substrate-derivable from Σ alone [COROLLARY]:* "Has τ been retracted?" reduces to `τ ∈ nullified(Σ)` (Definition of `nullified`), and "What tuples target τ?" to Observe filtered for tuples whose endset coverage contains τ — both decided directly against `Σ.L`. *Conventionally derivable [POLICY]:* "Who emitted τ?" is *not* answered by Σ.L alone — `Emit_K` writes `(F, G, K)` to a fresh address with no implicit emitter slot (Definition of `Emit_K`), so provenance has no substrate-level meaning. By consequence (c) above, the emitter includes its own address in the from-set of an `L_provenance` tuple targeting τ; once that convention is in force, "who emitted τ?" reduces to Observe over `L_provenance`. Predicates over documents face the same split (e.g., "who authored d?" is conventional in exactly the same way). The substrate's contribution is uniform evaluation machinery; *which* relations exist to evaluate against is a caller obligation upstream.

Without R5, each construct would require its own layer that predicates could not see and that the audit trail (R3) would not preserve. R5 collapses such layers into the relational structure.


## The Active Subset (R6)

R0–R5 are derivable from ASN-0043. R6 itself, stated below as `A_K^Σ`'s well-definedness, is a definitional check — `A_K^Σ = L_K^Σ \ {(a, F, G) : a ∈ nullified(Σ)}` is computable from `Σ.L` by inspection of the definition, with no auxiliary state. The *conceptual* contribution of this note — added here, not present in Nelson's link model — is the *active/audit distinction* that R6 makes available: two coherent views (`L_K`, the audit trail accumulating monotonically per R3; `A_K`, the operational currently-in-effect set obtained by excluding `nullified(Σ)`) co-existing over the same link store. The substantive properties carrying that distinction are R6a (RetractionStability — nullification persists across state transitions), R6b (SingleDepthRetraction — the predicate does not recurse through the retraction relation), and R6c (RestorationByReemission — once retracted, always out of every future active subset). R6 furnishes well-definedness; R6a–R6c jointly establish that the distinction is stable, non-recursive, and persistent under restoration-by-re-emission. The construction is made possible by R5 (a self-referential retraction relation can exist) and R3 (the retraction relation accumulates monotonically, providing the audit trail against which the active subset is computed).

**Definition — RetractionType.** Fix a designated coverage class `[R]` reserved for retraction, represented by any `R ∈ T_admissible` whose coverage selects the conventional retraction address set. The corresponding typed relation `L_R^Σ` is the *retraction relation at state Σ*. By L9 (TypeGhostPermission, ASN-0043), `R` need not refer to anything stored — its coverage is an address set, chosen by convention — and `L_R^Σ` is well-defined as a coverage-class slice regardless of whether any literal representative endset has yet been stored. Before the first retraction emission, `L_R^Σ = ∅`; after the first such emission, `L_R^Σ ≠ ∅`. The definition of `L_R^Σ` does not depend on which case applies, and it is stated in coverage-class terms rather than `T_cat^Σ`-membership terms — `T_cat^Σ` is literal-endset-valued (per the asymmetry noted above), so `T_cat^Σ`-membership is not the right indicator of "has any retraction been emitted yet?" — that question is exactly `L_R^Σ ≠ ∅`. By coverage-equivalence (Definition of TypedRelation), any emission with a type endset `R'` satisfying `coverage(R') = coverage(R)` contributes to `L_R^Σ` and to `nullified(Σ)` — callers are not required to use a canonical span structure for `R`, only its canonical coverage.

**Convention — RetractionDirectionality.** *[Substrate-level convention; consumed by Definition of Nullified.]* For the retraction coverage class `[R]`, the to-set carries the retraction's targets — addresses whose tuples are being withdrawn from the active subset — and the from-set is reserved for *attribution-bearing endset content* (e.g., the retractor's own address under the agent-provenance pattern of R5 Consequence (c)) or is left empty for unattributed retractions. The asymmetry is *not* a substrate-level entailment of L4–L8: L4 (EndsetGenerality) and L5 (EndsetSetSemantics) treat from- and to-slots symmetrically, L6 (SlotDistinction) gives them only positional identity, and L7 (DirectionalFlexibility) explicitly defers directional interpretation to the type. The convention names how the retraction type `R` exercises L7: the from-/to-slot asymmetry is *contributed by the retraction type's intended use*, not derived from the substrate's link model. Symmetric and reverse-direction conventions are equally well-formed at the L-invariant level (the substrate would not reject `Emit_R(Σ, d, {(a, δ(1, #a))}, ∅)` — a from-set retraction — as malformed); ASN-0086 adopts the to-set-as-target convention to match Nelson's link-model design intent for retraction and the udanax-green retraction-tuple shape.

**Definition — Nullified.** *[Inherits Convention — RetractionDirectionality.]* The set of *nullified* tuple addresses at state `Σ` is

`nullified(Σ) = {a ∈ A_rel^Σ : (E (b, F', G') ∈ L_R^Σ :: a ∈ coverage(G'))}`

The existential checks `coverage(G')` only — the to-set's coverage — and does not inspect `coverage(F')`. This is the Convention exercised at the substrate-level predicate: retraction targets are in `G'` by Convention; an `Emit_R` call whose to-span coverage misses `a` does not nullify `a`, regardless of what its from-set covers. A reformulation under the reverse convention would replace `G'` with `F'` here; a symmetric convention would replace it with `coverage(F') ∪ coverage(G')`. Both alternatives are well-formed but adopt a different directional reading of the retraction type; the present Definition is bound to the to-set convention. By R5, `coverage(G')` may include `A_rel^Σ` addresses, so `nullified(Σ)` is well-defined as a subset of `A_rel^Σ`.

*Operational scope of the `A_rel^Σ` filter.* The `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` filter scopes `nullified(Σ)` to relational addresses, while `coverage(G')` is a subset of `T` (not of `A_rel^Σ`) and may also contain content addresses (`A_doc^Σ`), ghost tumblers (addresses without stored representatives, admitted by L9), or higher-arity link addresses (where `|Σ.L(a)| > 3`). Retractions whose `coverage(G')` lies entirely outside `A_rel^Σ ∩ {a : |Σ.L(a)| = 3}` — e.g., a tuple emitted via `Emit_R(Σ, d', ∅, {(c, δ(1, #c))})` for `c ∈ A_doc^Σ`, or a tuple whose to-span covers only a higher-arity link — are *well-formed* `Emit_R` calls (`Emit_R`'s precondition imposes no `A_rel^Σ` constraint on the to-set's coverage) but *operationally inert* for the active-subset machinery defined here: they contribute their `(b, F', G')` triple to `L_R^Σ` (preserved by R3) yet contribute nothing to `nullified(Σ) ∩ {a : |Σ.L(a)| = 3}`, and hence leave every `A_K^Σ` (defined only over standard-triple links) unchanged. This asymmetry is structural — between *syntactic admissibility* (`Emit_R` permits any to-span) and *operational effect* (active-subset machinery is scoped to standard-triple `L_K`'s) — and is handled in two complementary ways: `Nullify`'s precondition P1 (`a ∈ A_rel^Σ`) confines that *named operation* to the operationally effective regime; other `Emit_R` calls with crafted spans remain syntactically permitted but operationally a no-op for `A_K`. The higher-arity case is flagged in the Open Questions section (proposing `A_K^{(n)}` machinery as a possible extension); the content-targeting case faces the same asymmetry but has no `A_K`-side counterpart at the relational layer at all — content's operational presence is governed by arrangements (`Σ.M`, ASN-0036), not by relational retraction.

**Definition — ActiveSubset.** For each `K ∈ T_admissible`, the *active subset of type K at state Σ* is

`A_K^Σ = {(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ : a ∉ nullified(Σ)}`

**R6 — ActiveSubsetWellDefinedness.** *[Setup-free.]* For every state `Σ` and every `K ∈ T_admissible`, `A_K^Σ` is well-defined and computable from `Σ.L` alone, with no auxiliary state.

*Proof.* `nullified(Σ)` is determined entirely by `L_R^Σ`, which by Definition of TypedRelation is determined by `Σ.L`. `A_K^Σ` is a set-difference between `L_K^Σ` and tuples whose addresses lie in `nullified(Σ)`; both inputs are determined by `Σ.L`. No flag, no version field, no separate snapshot is consulted. ∎

**R6a — RetractionStability.** *[Setup-free.]* Once a tuple's address is nullified, it stays nullified across all future state transitions:

`(A Σ → Σ', a ∈ A_rel^Σ : a ∈ nullified(Σ) :: a ∈ nullified(Σ'))`

*Proof.* Recall that `coverage : Endset → ℘(T)` is a pure function on endset values, fixed by the substrate model (ASN-0043, Definition of coverage): given an endset value `E`, `coverage(E)` is determined entirely by `E` and the tumbler-order relation `≼`, which itself is state-independent (T1, ASN-0034). The codomain is `℘(T)` — the full tumbler space — not the state-dependent address universe `A^Σ`; coverage may include addresses outside `dom(Σ.C) ∪ dom(Σ.L)` (L9, TypeGhostPermission, ASN-0043). In particular, `coverage(E)` does not depend on the state Σ in which `E` is evaluated.

Suppose `a ∈ nullified(Σ)`. By Definition of `nullified(Σ)`, this entails `a ∈ A_rel^Σ = dom(Σ.L)`, and there exist `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` and `(b, F', G') ∈ L_R^Σ` with `a ∈ coverage(G')`. By the coverage-equivalence membership criterion of `L_R^Σ`, the literal value stored at `b` in Σ is `Σ.L(b) = (F', G', R'')` for some `R'' ∈ T_admissible` with `coverage(R'') = coverage(R)` — the third entry need not equal `R` literally; only its coverage must. We exhibit the same witness at Σ': by L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity, ASN-0043) applied to `a ∈ A_rel^Σ`, `a ∈ dom(Σ.L) ⊆ dom(Σ'.L) = A_rel^{Σ'}`, discharging the `a ∈ A_rel^{Σ'}` predicate required by Definition of `nullified(Σ')`. By R3 (applied to the type slice indexed by `R`), `L_R^Σ ⊆ L_R^{Σ'}`, so `(b, F', G') ∈ L_R^{Σ'}`. By R2, `b ∈ dom(Σ'.L)` with `Σ'.L(b) = (F', G', R'')` — the literal stored value is preserved exactly, so in particular the from- and to-endsets `F'` and `G'` are preserved; the proof requires only this preservation of `G'`, not any literal equality at the type slot. Since `coverage` is a pure function on endset values, `coverage(G')` is a single fixed set, and `a ∈ coverage(G')` is a state-independent proposition once `G'` has been fixed. Therefore `a ∈ nullified(Σ')`. ∎

**R6b — SingleDepthRetraction.** *[Setup-free.]* The retraction predicate is *single-depth*: `nullified(Σ)` checks only whether some tuple in `L_R` directly targets `a`, regardless of whether that retracting tuple is itself nullified.

*Justification.* We derive R6b mechanically from the Definition of `nullified(Σ)` in three steps. *Step 1 — quantifier-range identification.* By the Definition, `nullified(Σ) = {a ∈ A_rel^Σ : (E (b, F', G') ∈ L_R^Σ :: a ∈ coverage(G'))}`. The existential's domain is `L_R^Σ` — the audit slice of `R`-typed tuples — not `A_R^Σ = L_R^Σ \ {(b, F', G') : b ∈ nullified(Σ)}` (the *active* subset of `R`-typed tuples, with nullified retractors excluded). *Step 2 — witness sufficiency.* The membership predicate `a ∈ nullified(Σ)` is therefore witnessed by *any* `(b, F', G') ∈ L_R^Σ` with `a ∈ coverage(G')`; the witness `b`'s own active-subset status — whether `b ∈ A_R^Σ` or `b ∈ nullified(Σ)` — is not consulted. *Step 3 — non-recursion.* Consequently, deciding `a ∈ nullified(Σ)` requires only one level of existential check against `L_R^Σ`, with no further evaluation of `nullified(·)` on the witness `b`; the predicate does not recurse through the retraction relation. This is the single-depth claim.

This is a property of *logical depth* within a single state, distinct from R6a's property of *temporal persistence* across state transitions. R6a says "nullification persists as Σ evolves" — given by R3 + R2 + coverage purity. R6b says "the predicate does not iterate through the retraction relation" — given by the choice of `L_R^Σ` (not `A_R^Σ`) at the quantifier in the Definition. The two properties are independent: even within a *single* fixed state Σ at which no transition has yet occurred, R6b already determines `nullified(Σ)`'s value, while R6a is vacuous at a single state.

To see the distinction concretely, consider two emissions at the same state. (1) Emit `(b, F', G_a, R)` with `a ∈ coverage(G_a)` — call this *direct retraction of a*. After this emission at state `Σ_1`, `(b, F', G_a) ∈ L_R^{Σ_1}`, so `a ∈ nullified(Σ_1)`, and also `b ∈ A_R^{Σ_1}` (b is not yet retracted). (2) Emit `(c, F'', G_b, R)` with `b ∈ coverage(G_b)` — *second-order retraction targeting b*. After this emission at state `Σ_2`, `(c, F'', G_b) ∈ L_R^{Σ_2}`, so `b ∈ nullified(Σ_2)`, and now `b ∉ A_R^{Σ_2}`. Question: is `a ∈ nullified(Σ_2)`? By Definition of `nullified(Σ_2)`, the existential at `a` is witnessed by `(b, F', G_a) ∈ L_R^{Σ_2}` — which holds by R3 applied to step (1)'s tuple. The existential succeeds *whether or not* `b ∈ A_R^{Σ_2}`; only `b ∈ L_R^{Σ_2}` is checked. So `a ∈ nullified(Σ_2)` regardless of `b`'s active-subset status. This is R6b. R6a is the separate claim that `a` having been in `nullified(Σ_1)` guarantees `a ∈ nullified(Σ_2)` independently of step (2); both properties hold here, but R6b would still answer the question even if R6a were absent. ∎

**R6c — RestorationByReemission (dom-extending, on `⊑`).** *[Setup-free.]* Once retracted, a tuple stays out of every active subset at any state reachable from Σ through dom-extending transitions — i.e., under the `⊑` relation:

`(A Σ, K, (a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ : a ∈ nullified(Σ) : (A Σ' : Σ ⊑ Σ' :: (a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{Σ'}))`

*Scope of this statement.* R6c is the dom-extending half of the persistence guarantee. The relation `Σ ⊑ Σ'` is the reflexive-transitive closure of the dom-extending `→`, so the formal quantifier above ranges over `→`-reachability only; ASN-0036's arrangement-modifying transitions live in a parallel transition vocabulary outside `→` (Scoping note above). The user-facing reading "once retracted, always out of every future active subset" is the *broad* sense — persistence across both dom-extending and arrangement-modifying successors — and is the content of R6c-Corollary below, which lifts the conclusion from `⊑` to `⊑̂`. The split between R6c (this statement) and R6c-Corollary (the lift) tracks the two scopes precisely: R6c's `⊑`-statement is the substantive proof step, and R6c-Corollary derives the broader `⊑̂`-statement by an additional pointwise-preservation step on arrangement-modifying transitions.

*Proof.* We first show `a ∈ nullified(Σ')` by induction on the length `n` of the `→`-chain `Σ = Σ_0 → Σ_1 → … → Σ_n = Σ'` witnessing `Σ ⊑ Σ'` (Definition of Extension). *Base* (`n = 0`): `Σ' = Σ` and `a ∈ nullified(Σ)` by hypothesis. *Step* (`n = k+1`): assume `a ∈ nullified(Σ_k)`. Apply R6a to the single step `Σ_k → Σ_{k+1}` to obtain `a ∈ nullified(Σ_{k+1}) = nullified(Σ')`. By R3 (TypedSliceMonotonicity) applied along the inductive chain `Σ = Σ_0 → Σ_1 → … → Σ_n = Σ'`, `(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ ⊆ L_K^{Σ'}` — the tuple persists in the audit slice across every `→`-step. By Definition of `A_K`, since `(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^{Σ'}` and `a ∈ nullified(Σ')`, `(a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{Σ'}`. ∎

**R6c-Corollary — RestorationByReemission on the broader transition relation.** *[Setup-free.]* R6c lifts unchanged from `⊑` to `⊑̂` (broader transition relation `↦` and its reflexive-transitive closure `⊑̂` defined in the State transition relation and BroadExtension sections above):

`(A Σ, K, (a, F, G) ∈ L_K^Σ : a ∈ nullified(Σ) : (A Σ'' : Σ ⊑̂ Σ'' :: (a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{Σ''}))`

*Proof.* We show the stronger pointwise statement that arrangement-modifying transitions preserve `A_K` exactly; the corollary then follows by induction on the `↦`-chain composing arrangement-modifying and dom-extending steps in any order. *Step 1:* `L_R^Σ` is the coverage-class slice of `Σ.L` indexed by `R` (Definition of TypedRelation), and so depends only on `Σ.L`. *Step 2:* `nullified(Σ) = {a ∈ A_rel^Σ : (E (b, F', G') ∈ L_R^Σ :: a ∈ coverage(G'))}` (Definition of `nullified`); its inputs are `A_rel^Σ = dom(Σ.L)`, `L_R^Σ`, and the state-independent pure function `coverage` (R6a's proof) — so `nullified(Σ)` depends only on `Σ.L`. *Step 3:* `A_K^Σ = L_K^Σ \ {(a, F, G) : a ∈ nullified(Σ)}` (Definition of ActiveSubset); both `L_K^Σ` (Definition of TypedRelation) and `nullified(Σ)` (Step 2) depend only on `Σ.L`, so `A_K^Σ` does too. *Step 4:* by the arrangement-modification frame stated in the Scoping note above (inherited from ASN-0036), every arrangement-modifying transition leaves `Σ.L` identical — `Σ_arr.L = Σ.L` — and changes only `Σ.M`'s pointwise values. Substituting `Σ_arr.L = Σ.L` through Steps 1–3 yields `L_R^{Σ_arr} = L_R^Σ`, `nullified(Σ_arr) = nullified(Σ)`, and `A_K^{Σ_arr} = A_K^Σ`. The post-arrangement-modification active subset is therefore pointwise identical to the pre-modification one. Now induct on the `↦`-chain `Σ = Σ_0 ↦ Σ_1 ↦ … ↦ Σ_n = Σ''`: each `Σ_k ↦ Σ_{k+1}` is either a dom-extending `→` step (covered by R6c on `⊑`) or an arrangement-modifying step (pointwise preserves `A_K` by Steps 1–4 just discharged); in either case `(a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{Σ_{k+1}}` follows from `(a, F, G) ∉ A_K^{Σ_k}`. ∎

To "restore" content, emit a fresh tuple with the desired value (R0). The new tuple receives a fresh address; the retracted tuple keeps its address (R2) and stays out of `A_K` (R6a).

*Consequences.* (Typology per R2's Consequences key.)

(a) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Operational vs. historical views.* `A_K` is the operational view ("what is currently in effect"); `L_K` is the audit view ("what has ever existed"). Both are computed from `Σ.L` by the same observation machinery, differing only in whether `nullified(Σ)` is excluded. Operational and historical queries use the same observation primitive but specify different views.

(b) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Mutation as set-difference.* `A_K^Σ = L_K^Σ \ {(a, F, G) : a ∈ nullified(Σ)}`. Computed live; no flag, no cache, no version field anywhere in the architecture.

(c) *[ARCHITECTURE]* *Quiescence is operational, not historical.* "Every public predicate over `A_K` holds" is the convergence condition. It does not require historical agreement; it requires the current substrate to satisfy every public check.

(d) *[COROLLARY, stipulation-conditional]* *All visible state-transforming relational-layer operations reduce to `Emit_K` — anticipating R7 below.* *[The reduction has a definitional half; see R7b below.]* This consequence is the substantive content of R7 (NullifyIsEmit), stated here in R6's vocabulary because the active subset already places Nullify within the relational-layer operation set. *Up front:* the reduction's closure is *not* purely derived — it rests on a definitional commitment of the relational layer to the sibling-frontier-disciplined `Emit_K` primitive (the substrate emission primitive in isolation admits broader class-(iii) deposits, including strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses, which the reduction excludes). R7's headline decomposes the closure into R7a (PROVEN: no `Σ.L`-affecting transition exists outside class (iii), from L12 + L12a + the Frame conditions) and R7b (STIPULATED: every relational-layer-initiated class-(iii) step is an `Emit_K` call — the relational layer's commitment to the sibling-frontier-disciplined subset of the substrate primitive); we restate the R7a/R7b split here at first use so that the consequence is not mistaken for a purely derived structural fact. With that scope made explicit: file a comment, close it, retract a citation, retire a document (by classifier tuple), revive it — each is one or two `Emit_K` calls (varying `K`). The substrate's response (`A_K` shifts, predicates flip) is uniform across the lot. The qualifier "state-transforming" is essential: Observe is also a relational-layer operation but leaves `Σ` unchanged (it has no `→` step to reduce), and is therefore not in the scope of the reduction. Document allocation and content emission remain separate primitive transitions in `→`; the reduction here is scoped to *state-transforming* operations of the relational layer, not to the relational layer as a whole and not to the substrate as a whole. This consequence inherits R7b: it holds *for the relational layer as committed to the disciplined `Emit_K` primitive* (i.e., to the sibling-frontier-disciplined subset of the substrate emission primitive), not for arbitrary class-(iii) emissions the substrate would admit if invoked outside the relational-layer commitment.

(e) *[COROLLARY]* *`A_K` is not monotone; `L_K` is.* R3 (TypedSliceMonotonicity) makes the audit slice monotone — `Σ ⊑̂ Σ' ⟹ L_K^Σ ⊆ L_K^{Σ'}` — but the same is *not* true of the active subset: a single retraction emission strictly shrinks `A_K` at every type whose tuple address it covers (witnessed by R6c's set-difference: `(a, F, G) ∈ A_K^Σ ∩ (L_K^{Σ'} \ A_K^{Σ'})` for the retracted tuple), and a subsequent re-emission of the same `(F, G)` strictly grows `A_K` again at a *different* address (R0's fresh-address guarantee). The Worked Sketch exhibits both directions concretely: `A_K^{Σ_0} = {(a₁, F₁, G₁)}` shrinks to `A_K^{Σ_1} = ∅` under Step 1's Nullify, and grows to `A_K^{Σ_2} = {(a₂, F₁, G₁)}` under Step 2's re-emission. Neither `⊆` nor `⊇` holds in general between `A_K^Σ` and `A_K^{Σ'}` for `Σ ⊑̂ Σ'`; the active subset is therefore *not* a monotone function of `Σ` under either inclusion direction, while the audit slice `L_K` is monotone under `⊆`. Predicates and observation views over `A_K` must accommodate non-monotone evolution as a substrate-level fact, not assume monotonicity inherited from `L_K`'s audit semantics.


## Three Operations

The six properties yield three operations that suffice to span all visible substrate change.

**Definition — Emit_K.** Emit is a state-transforming operation with signature

`Emit_K : Σ × dom(Σ.M) × Endset × Endset → Σ' × A_rel^{Σ'}`

*Postcondition (address-construction discipline).* The returned address `a` is constructed by R0 Step 2 — *Case A* `a = d.0.s_L.1` when `home(d)` is unused (i.e., `{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d} = ∅`); *Case B* `a = incⁱ(b, 0)` for the least fresh `i ≥ 1` (where `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` has `home(b) = d`) otherwise. This postcondition is part of the signature: `Emit_K` is the sibling-frontier-disciplined subset of the substrate emission primitive, not the primitive in full. (The substrate primitive admits a strictly broader set of class-(iii) addresses — including strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses — which `Emit_K` excludes by construction.)

*Scope of `Emit_K`'s contract — substrate-primitive bypass is outside it.* Callers that bypass the discipline by invoking the substrate emission primitive directly at a prefix-extension of an existing link address have *not* invoked `Emit_K` and are not covered by R0a or its corollaries (R0a-Cor1, R0a-Cor2): the resulting transition is a class-(iii) `→`-step at the substrate level — L12, L12a, and the Frame conditions admit it — but it lies outside `Emit_K`'s contract, since `Emit_K`'s postcondition binds the address to R0 Step 2's sibling-frontier construction and a prefix-extension address fails that postcondition. Such a bypass invocation is admitted by R7a (which is categorical over any-layer class-(iii) `→`-steps) but is excluded by R7b (which scopes the relational layer to `Emit_K` invocations only); from the substrate's perspective the deposit is a well-formed class-(iii) emission, but from the relational-layer-as-committed perspective the deposit is not a relational-layer operation and the discipline-dependent guarantees of R0a / Nullify / `A_K`-stability that flow from R7b's commitment no longer apply at the post-state. The failure modes of such bypasses are catalogued in Appendix B.

The defining precondition is `K ∈ T_admissible`. Given input state `Σ`, a *caller-supplied home document* `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`, and finite endsets `F, G ∈ Endset`, `Emit_K(Σ, d, F, G)` deposits a fresh tuple under `d` at an address constructed by *R0 Step 2's sibling-frontier construction* applied at that `d`: emit at the Case A address (`a = d.0.s_L.1` when `{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d} = ∅`) or the Case B address (`a = incⁱ(b, 0)` for the least `i ≥ 1` with `incⁱ(b, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, where `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` has `home(b) = d`). The returned `(Σ', a)` satisfies `a ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, `a ∈ dom(Σ'.L)`, `home(a) = d`, and `Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K)`. All other components of Σ are held in frame: `Σ'.C = Σ.C` and `Σ'.M = Σ.M`. By R2, the binding `Σ'.L(a) = (F, G, K)` is permanent across all subsequent transitions. (The precondition `dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅` of R0 is enforced by the signature itself: a parameter `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` cannot be supplied unless the document-allocation domain is non-empty.)

*Case B's seed-independence — `Emit_K` is a function of `(Σ, d, F, G)`, not a relation.* Case B's selection of `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `home(b) = d` is non-deterministic in form — many such `b` may exist when `d` has accumulated multiple link allocations under `Σ` — but the resulting fresh address `a` is *independent of which `b` is chosen*. Under R0a's sibling-stream invariant (above), every `a' ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `home(a') = d` lies on the single sibling stream `d.0.s_L.1, inc(d.0.s_L.1, 0), inc²(d.0.s_L.1, 0), …` anchored at the Case A base. Choosing any `b ∈ dom(Σ.L)` with `home(b) = d` gives `b = incᵏ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` for some `k ≥ 0` (R0a's invariant), and Case B's least-`i` selection yields `a = incⁱ(b, 0) = incⁱ⁺ᵏ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)`. Let `j = i + k`. By the definition of the least-`i` choice — the smallest `i ≥ 1` with `incⁱ(b, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)` — the resulting `j` is exactly the least `j ≥ k + 1` with `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)`; combined with R0a-Cor1 (ContiguousPrefixUnderDiscipline) applied at Σ — which gives `{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : home(a') = d} = {incʲ'(d.0.s_L.1, 0) : 0 ≤ j' ≤ J_d^Σ}` for a contiguous-prefix bound `J_d^Σ ≥ 0`, with `k ≤ J_d^Σ` (since `b = incᵏ(d.0.s_L.1, 0) ∈ dom(Σ.L)` forces `k` into Cor1's prefix range), and therefore every `incʲ'(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` for `j' ∈ {0, …, J_d^Σ}` — in particular for `j' ∈ {0, …, k}` — is in `dom(Σ.L)`, while `incᴶ_d^Σ⁺¹(d.0.s_L.1, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)` — this `j` is in fact the least `j ≥ 0` (equivalently the least `j ≥ 1`, since `j ≥ k + 1 ≥ 1`) with `incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0) ∉ dom(Σ.L)`, namely `j = J_d^Σ + 1`. So `a = incʲ(d.0.s_L.1, 0)` is fixed by `Σ` and `d` alone — the next-frontier index `j` is determined by `dom(Σ.L)`'s sibling-stream extent under `d`, with no dependence on which prior link `b` the caller (or implementation) happens to pick as the seed. The Case B definition's apparent choice of `b` is therefore a presentation device for the construction, not a genuine non-determinism in the operation's output; `Emit_K` returns a unique `(Σ', a)` for each input `(Σ, d, F, G)` with `dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅` and `K ∈ T_admissible`, and is a function in the standard sense.

*Why `d` is a caller parameter, not a substrate-internal choice.* Two independent considerations fix the home document as an explicit input rather than a substrate-chosen one. *Design-level (the link model).* In Nelson's link design, every link is *owned by* a specific document — its home — and the home-document choice is a relational fact about what document the link belongs to, settled at emission time by the caller's intent. The substrate is not free to assign homes; assignment is a caller obligation. *Implementation-level (the udanax-green code path).* The `MAKELINK` / `docreatelink` path takes the home document address as an explicit input parameter (the function's `docaddr` argument), routes allocation through that document's link enumerator (granfilade orgl tree) and POOM, and never selects the home internally. Promoting `d` into the signature aligns the abstract operation with both the design intent and the concrete code-path interface; an earlier "pick any `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)`" wording at this level would have understated the determinacy of the abstraction by treating the home as substrate-internal. R0's existential quantification over `d` (`dom(Σ.M) ≠ ∅ ⟹ (E d ∈ dom(Σ.M) :: …)`) is preserved as a *foundational existence claim* — the substrate admits *some* emission whenever a home document exists — but `Emit_K`'s operational definition selects a specific `d` per call.

The address-returning convention in the rest of this note — `Emit_K(d, F, G) → A_rel` — is a metonym: the state is the ambient one, `d` is the caller-supplied home document, and `Σ'` is the post-emission state in which the returned address resides.

*Why the construction is bound into the definition.* The substrate emission primitive (above) admits a strictly broader set of addresses than R0 Step 2 — including strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses, which would falsify R0a's antichain (see "Breadth of the primitive vs. the discipline R0a names" above). If `Emit_K` were defined as "any primitive-permissible emission," a single non-disciplinary call would break R0a, and Nullify's single-tuple-scope argument — which relies on R0a's reachable-state antichain holding at the post-state — would fail. Binding `Emit_K` to R0 Step 2's construction makes every `Emit_K`-induced trace respect the sibling-frontier discipline by definition: R0a holds unconditionally on traces whose class-(iii) transitions are all `Emit_K` calls (the discipline hypothesis of R0a is discharged at every such step), and Nullify's single-tuple-scope argument goes through without further discipline assertions. The broader substrate primitive remains documented above as the abstract emission interface; this note's `Emit_K` operation selects the sibling-frontier-disciplined subset of that primitive.

*A_K^{Σ'} membership of the fresh emission — conditional on retraction discipline.* The Definition above guarantees `(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^{Σ'}` for the fresh emission (coverage-equivalence membership applied to the literal stored triple) but is silent on `(a, F, G) ∈ A_K^{Σ'}`, which turns on whether `a ∈ nullified(Σ')`. Two regimes are operationally relevant. (i) *Nullify-only discipline.* If every `L_R^Σ` tuple is the result of a `Nullify` call (below) — i.e., every retraction has a unit-depth to-span of the form `{(b, δ(1, #b))}` for some target `b ∈ A_rel^Σ` — then each retraction's coverage `{t : b ≼ t}` intersected with `A_rel^{Σ'}` reduces to `{b}` by R0a's antichain on `dom(Σ'.L)`. The fresh `a` produced by Emit_K is, by R0 Step 2's sibling-frontier construction and R0a's reachable-state antichain at Σ', prefix-incomparable with every `b ∈ A_rel^Σ` (the discipline keeps `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {a}` an antichain, so no existing link is a prefix of `a`). Therefore `a` is not in `coverage(G')` for any `(_, _, G') ∈ L_R^{Σ'}`, hence `a ∉ nullified(Σ')`, and `(a, F, G) ∈ A_K^{Σ'}`. (ii) *Crafted-span retractions admitted.* The substrate primitive does not preclude callers from emitting retractions with broader-coverage to-spans — e.g., a subtree-broad retraction `Emit_R(Σ, d_retr, ∅, {(d, δ(1, #d))})` whose coverage is `{t : d ≼ t}`, which intersected with `A_rel^Σ` covers every link sited under `d` (and propagates to every link subsequently emitted under `d`, since R3 preserves the retraction tuple). If any `L_R^Σ` tuple has such a crafted span, a fresh `a` emitted under a covered home falls within `coverage(L_R^Σ)` immediately upon emission: `a ∈ nullified(Σ')` at the very step that allocates `a`, and `(a, F, G) ∈ L_K^{Σ'} \ A_K^{Σ'}` — the audit slice records the emission, but the active subset excludes it from emission onward. The Nullify definition below scopes that operation to the unit-depth-span form (regime (i)); whether the wider crafted-span form (regime (ii)) is admitted is a discipline-level property of caller retraction practice, not an Emit_K guarantee. A_K membership of a fresh emission is therefore conditional on which regime is in force.

**Definition — Observe_K.** For `K ∈ T_admissible`, a pattern `(F̂, Ĝ) ∈ ℘_fin(A) × ℘_fin(A)`, and a view selector, Observe is a pure read with signature

`Observe_K : Σ × ℘_fin(A) × ℘_fin(A) × View → ℘_fin(L_K^Σ)`

where `View ∈ {hist, oper}` selects between `L_K^Σ` (audit) and `A_K^Σ` (operational). It returns

`{(a, F, G) ∈ view : F̂ ⊆ coverage(F) ∧ Ĝ ⊆ coverage(G)}`

with `view = L_K^Σ` if `View = hist` and `view = A_K^Σ` if `View = oper`. Observe leaves Σ unchanged.

*Rationale for the match relation.* `F̂ ⊆ coverage(F)` is the *minimal* substrate-level match relation, in two senses. First, every substrate computes `coverage(·)` (ASN-0043, Definition of coverage) as the canonical endset-to-address projection, and subset-containment of finite address sets is universally available — no auxiliary primitive is required. Second, the relation answers the canonical substrate-level question: "does this tuple's from-endset cover every address in the requested pattern?" This is exactly the question for which subset-on-coverage is the affirmative answer; richer queries (span-prefix containment, regex over a content projection, type-equality at the address level via L8) post-compose with Observe rather than parameterizing it. Observe is therefore fixed as the substrate-level primitive that returns "tuples whose endsets cover the requested address sets"; layered query languages obtain other relations by filtering Observe's output.

**Definition — Nullify.** *[Discipline-conditional for single-tuple scope.]* Nullify has four preconditions: (P0) `d_retr ∈ dom(Σ.M)` — the caller-supplied home document for the retraction tuple itself; (P1) `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` — the target tuple's address; (P2) `|Σ.L(a)| = 3` (i.e., `a` is the address of a standard-triple link); and (P3) `a` has no strict prefix-extension in `dom(Σ.L)`:

`(A a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : a ≼ a' :: a' = a)`

(Equivalently, no other link address in `dom(Σ.L)` is a tumbler-prefix-extension of `a`.)

*Discipline assumption.* Nullify's *single-tuple-scope* behavior (below) — the property that exactly one tuple address, namely `a` itself, is contributed to `nullified(Σ')` — is conditional on the sibling-frontier emission discipline of R0a (see "Implementation discipline — sibling-frontier link emission" above). The discipline ensures that the fresh emitter address `b` produced by Nullify's internal `Emit_R` step is prefix-incomparable with `a` at the post-state, which together with P3 at the prior state yields the single-tuple-scope conclusion. The substrate emission primitive *in isolation* does not entail this: it admits emissions at strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses, which would falsify the conclusion. The detailed decomposition of how P3 and the discipline interact appears in *"Remark on the role of P3"* below; the table entry for Nullify in "Properties Introduced" also flags P3's *automaticity* under the discipline. (The Open Questions section enumerates two routes to lift this conditionality: tightening the substrate emission primitive to forbid prefix-extension emissions, or tightening `Emit_K`'s post-condition to require prefix-incomparability with `dom(Σ.L)` — either would discharge P3 at every state and remove the discipline-conditional tag here.)

Under these preconditions and the discipline assumption, Nullify is the composition

`Nullify(Σ, d_retr, a) ≡ Emit_R(Σ, d_retr, ∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))})`

That is, emit a tuple into the retraction relation with empty from-set and a unit-depth to-span targeting `a`, with the retraction itself homed at the caller-supplied `d_retr ∈ dom(Σ.M)`. *Dependency chain — existence of the post-state Σ' with the retraction tuple:* R5 admits the unit-depth self-targeting span `(a, δ(1, #a))` as a to-set component (Stage 1's L4(c) + L13 chain, applied at `a ∈ A_rel^Σ`); R0 at `d_retr` then emits the retraction triple `(∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))}, R)` by its Step 4 invariant-preservation argument, depositing a fresh emitter address `b` with `Σ'.L(b) = (∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))}, R)` — this is the Emit_R call's substrate witness. By PrefixSpanCoverage (ASN-0043), `coverage({(a, δ(1, #a))}) = {t : a ≼ t}`, which contains `a`. Let `(Σ', _) = Nullify(Σ, d_retr, a)`. By Definition of `nullified`, `a ∈ nullified(Σ')`. By R6a, `a` remains nullified thereafter.

*Why `d_retr` is a caller parameter, not `home(a)`.* The substrate places no constraint on which `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` houses a retraction tuple: `nullified(Σ)` is computed from `L_R^Σ`'s endset coverage, which is independent of the retraction tuple's own home. An earlier formulation specialized `d_retr := home(a)` for well-definedness — `home(a)` is determined by `a`'s tumbler structure and persists in `dom(Σ.M)` by L1a + L12b — but that choice is a *convention*, not a substrate requirement: any `d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` satisfies Emit_R's home precondition, and the resulting `nullified(Σ')` is the same regardless of which `d` is picked. Two reasons make explicit caller-supply the right interface. *Design-level (Nelson's link model).* Retractions are themselves authored objects: in Nelson's design, every link — including a retraction — is owned by the document of its creator, not by the document of the link it retracts. Defaulting to `home(a)` would home the retraction at the *target's* document, contradicting authorship-based home assignment. *Symmetry with `Emit_K`.* `Emit_K`'s signature already exposes the home document as an explicit parameter for the same design and implementation reasons (above); Nullify, being `Emit_R` with a designated to-span shape (R7), inherits the same interface obligation. The `home(a)` shorthand remains available as a caller-side default for the case where retractor and target genuinely share a document — e.g., a document retracting its own internal classification — but is no longer baked into the operation's signature.

*Single-tuple scope, from P3 + R0 Step 2 + R0a. [Discipline-conditional.]* The to-span's coverage `{t : a ≼ t}` is in principle the entire prefix-subtree of `a` within `T`; restricted to `A_rel^Σ = dom(Σ.L)`, however, P3 gives that the only link address with `a` as a prefix is `a` itself: by P3, `{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : a ≼ a'} = {a}`. Since the class-(iii) `→` step taken by `Emit_R` only adds the fresh emitter address `b` (which by R0 satisfies `b ∉ dom(Σ.L)` and which is not `a` by R0 Step 2's construction yielding `b ≠ a` and by R0a's reachable-state antichain property at Σ' giving `a ⊀ b`), we have `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {b}` with `b` prefix-incomparable with `a`, so `{a' ∈ dom(Σ'.L) : a ≼ a'} = {a}` as well. Consequently `{t : a ≼ t} ∩ A_rel^{Σ'} = {a}`, and Nullify's `→` step contributes exactly `a` to `nullified(Σ')` — never a sub-tree of `A_rel`.

*Why no content address lies under `a`.* The unrestricted coverage `{t : a ≼ t}` ranges over all of `T`, so one must also account for tumbler positions in `A_doc^{Σ'}`. Under Setup, every `c ∈ A_doc^{Σ'}` is `s_C`-resident, hence has first element-field `c.E₁ = subspace_I(c) = s_C`; the link address `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` has `a.E₁ = subspace_I(a) = s_L` (L0, SubspacePartition, ASN-0043). By T3 (CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034), tumblers that disagree at any element-field position are distinct, and tumbler-prefix-extension preserves the first element-field exactly (extension appends digits past `a`'s end without rewriting any prior position). So `a ≼ c` would force `s_L = a.E₁ = c.E₁ = s_C`, contradicting the subspace-distinctness axiom (`s_C ≠ s_L`). Therefore `{t : a ≼ t} ∩ A_doc^{Σ'} = ∅`, and the *unrestricted* intersection `{t : a ≼ t} ∩ A^{Σ'} = {a}` as well — Nullify's single-tuple scope is not merely a filtering artifact of `nullified(Σ')`'s `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` predicate but a structural consequence of subspace-distinctness on the substrate's address universe.

*Remark on the role of P3.* P3 and the sibling-frontier emission discipline of R0a play complementary roles in the single-tuple-scope argument, and conflating them would misstate what P3 alone does. *P3's role* is to constrain `dom(Σ.L)` at the prior state: it makes the substrate-level precondition "no link address in `dom(Σ.L)` is a strict prefix-extension of `a`" well-defined as a property of Σ and ensures the prior-state part of the argument (`{a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) : a ≼ a'} = {a}`) goes through. *P3 does not discharge the conclusion on its own*: the substrate emission primitive (above) permits, in principle, a class-(iii) step whose fresh emitter address `b` is a strict prefix-extension of `a` (`a ≺ b`), and such a step would extend `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {b}` to contain a strict prefix-extension of `a` — breaking single-tuple-scope at Σ' without contradicting P3 at Σ. The conclusion therefore requires, in addition to P3, that (a) the class-(iii) step taken by `Emit_R` uses R0 Step 2's construction (which yields `b ≠ a` directly), and (b) R0a's reachable-state antichain property holds at Σ' (giving `a ⊀ b`). Both ingredients are supplied by the sibling-frontier emission discipline of R0a — which holds for systems reachable from `dom(Σ_0.L) = ∅` via the link-emission protocol R0 commits to — and so under that discipline the conclusion goes through. Under that same discipline P3 is *itself* automatic at every state for every `a ∈ dom(Σ.L)`: the antichain property gives `(A a, a' ∈ dom(Σ.L) :: a ≼ a' ⟹ a = a')`, of which P3 is the specialization to a fixed `a`. P3's separate listing makes the precondition statable and the prior-state argument well-defined for any system in which P3 holds at `a` — including ones for which the sibling-frontier discipline is asserted only as an interface contract on Emit_K, or for which a future revision admits broader Emit_K-spec choices (Open Questions, below) — but without the emission discipline supplying the post-state antichain, P3 alone is insufficient to discharge single-tuple-scope.

*Crafted-span retractions.* Nothing in `Emit_R`'s definition prevents a caller from emitting a retraction with a broader-coverage to-span — e.g., `Emit_R(Σ, d', ∅, {(d, δ(1, #d))})` (for some home `d' ∈ dom(Σ.M)` of the retraction tuple itself and a document address `d` referenced in the to-span), whose coverage is `{t : d ≼ t}`, intersected with `A_rel^Σ` is *every link sited under `d`*. This is a deliberate subtree-broad retraction, not Nullify in the sense of this definition. The substrate permits it; this note's `Nullify` covers only the single-tuple form, which is the typical case for retracting individual relational assertions. Higher-layer policies that wish to authorize or forbid broader retractions can do so by predicates over the emitted `L_R` tuples (e.g., requiring the to-span's `(seed, δ)` to be a unit-depth span at a known tuple address).

The arity-3 restriction matches this note's scope. `A_K^Σ` is defined only over standard-triple links (Definition of `L_K^Σ`), so the active-subset effect of Nullify is meaningful only on arity-3 addresses. Nullifying a higher-arity address (`|Σ.L(a)| > 3`) would be a well-formed Emit_R, and would deposit `a` into `nullified(Σ')`, but no `A_K^{Σ'}` would feel the effect under the present definitions; extending the active-subset machinery to multi-arity relations `A_K^{(n),Σ}` is left to the open question on higher-arity links.

**R7 — NullifyIsEmit.** *[Setup-free, stipulation-conditional.]* R7 has two sub-claims of asymmetric standing — one proven, one stipulated — composed at Step 4 to yield the operational closure result. We hoist the decomposition into the headline so that the asymmetry is visible at the point of citation, not buried in mid-paragraph prose.

**R7a — NoExtraClassAffectsL (PROVEN, from L12 + L12a + Frame conditions).** No `Σ.L`-affecting transition exists outside class (iii) — i.e., for any state-affecting transition `Σ ↝ Σ'` with `Σ.L ≠ Σ'.L`, that transition is a class-(iii) `→`-step at the substrate level. Equivalently: no relational-layer (or any-layer) operation can affect `Σ.L` outside class (iii). This rules out the negative space categorically.

**R7b — RelationalLayerCommitsToEmitK (STIPULATED, model commitment, not derived).** Every relational-layer-initiated class-(iii) `→`-step is an `Emit_K` call. The relational layer commits to the sibling-frontier-disciplined subset of the substrate emission primitive as its sole state-affecting primitive. This is a *definitional* commitment — it specifies *which* substrate-primitive deposits the relational layer issues via — and is not extractable from L12, L12a, or the Frame conditions; those invariants permit broader class-(iii) deposits (strict prefix-extensions, in particular, which the substrate primitive admits) that R7b excludes by adoption.

**R7 (the composite reduction).** Under R7a and R7b jointly, the relational layer admits exactly two operational primitives — `Emit_K` (state-affecting, class-(iii) `→`-step) and `Observe_K` (state-preserving, no `→`-step) — and every relational-layer state change reduces to a single `Emit_K` call. Nullify is not a third primitive but a definitional alias for `Emit_R(Σ, d_retr, ∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))})`. R7a rules out the negative space (no operation can escape class (iii) and still touch `Σ.L`); R7b fixes the positive content (within class (iii), only `Emit_K` is invoked); Step 4 below composes the two to yield the operational closure.

*Proof.* We argue in four steps. Steps 1–2 are preparatory: Step 1 enumerates the relational-layer operations introduced by this note, and Step 2 unfolds `Nullify` to `Emit_R`. Step 3 then discharges R7a (the proven half) and characterizes R7b (the stipulated half) explicitly. Step 4 composes R7a and R7b to yield the operational closure.

(Step 1 — enumerate the relational-layer operations.) This note defines three named relational-layer operations (above): `Emit_K` (Definition of Emit_K), `Observe_K` (Definition of Observe_K), and `Nullify` (Definition of Nullify). `Emit_K`'s signature is `Σ × dom(Σ.M) × Endset × Endset → Σ' × A_rel^{Σ'}` with the post-state satisfying `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {a}` for the freshly allocated `a`; by the Frame conditions on class (iii) (above), `Emit_K` takes a class-(iii) `→`-step at the substrate level. `Observe_K`'s signature is `Σ × ℘_fin(A) × ℘_fin(A) × View → ℘_fin(L_K^Σ)`, and its Definition explicitly states "Observe leaves Σ unchanged" — it takes no `→`-step. `Nullify`'s signature is `Σ × dom(Σ.M) × A_rel^Σ → Σ' × A_rel^{Σ'}` with the post-state arising from an internal emission step. These three exhaust the relational-layer operations introduced by this note; the substrate-level transitions of classes (i) and (ii) — document allocation and content emission — sit below the relational layer (ASN-0036) and are out of scope for this lemma's reduction claim.

(Step 2 — unfold `Nullify` to `Emit_R`.) By Definition of Nullify, for any `Σ`, `d_retr ∈ dom(Σ.M)`, and `a ∈ A_rel^Σ` satisfying P0–P3,

`Nullify(Σ, d_retr, a) ≡ Emit_R(Σ, d_retr, ∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))})`

This is `Emit_K` instantiated at three specific argument positions: the type slot `K` is fixed at `R` (the designated retraction coverage class), the from-set `F` is fixed at the empty endset `∅`, and the to-set `G` is fixed at the unit-depth span set `{(a, δ(1, #a))}`. The home-document slot `d_retr` and the input state `Σ` remain free parameters of `Nullify`'s signature. No further operational machinery is invoked: the `→`-step taken by `Nullify` is exactly the class-(iii) `→`-step of its internal `Emit_R` call, with the same fresh-address allocation (R0 Step 2's sibling-frontier construction at `d_retr`) and the same post-state `Σ'.L` extension. `Nullify` is therefore a *definitional shorthand* for an `Emit_R` call with three argument positions fixed; it is not an operation with its own substrate-level `→`-step distinct from `Emit_K`'s.

(Step 3 — discharge R7a (proven) and characterize R7b (stipulated).) We discharge the two sub-claims separately, in that order.

*Discharging R7a (PROVEN, from L12 + L12a + Frame conditions).* We argue that no `Σ.L`-affecting transition exists outside class (iii). Suppose, for contradiction, that some operation `Op` takes a transition `Σ ↝ Σ'` with `Σ.L ≠ Σ'.L` and `Op` is not a class-(iii) `→`-step. Two cases. *Case (a): `Op` modifies an existing entry,* i.e., `(E a ∈ dom(Σ.L) ∩ dom(Σ'.L) :: Σ'.L(a) ≠ Σ.L(a))`. This contradicts L12 (LinkImmutability, ASN-0043), which asserts that for every state transition (every `→`-step in the substrate's transition relation, of which class (iii) is the link-affecting class), every existing link address persists with its value unchanged: `(A Σ → Σ', a ∈ dom(Σ.L) :: Σ'.L(a) = Σ.L(a))`. *Case (b): `Op` removes an entry or extends the domain without going through class (iii),* i.e., either `dom(Σ'.L) ⊊ dom(Σ.L)` or `dom(Σ'.L) ⊋ dom(Σ.L)` via a transition not of class (iii). The removal sub-case contradicts L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity, ASN-0043), which asserts `dom(Σ.L) ⊆ dom(Σ'.L)` across every state transition. The extension-without-class-(iii) sub-case contradicts the Frame conditions on `→` (above), which fix class (iii) as the *only* transition class that extends `dom(Σ.L)` — classes (i) and (ii) hold `Σ'.L = Σ.L` by their respective frames, and there is no class (iv). So in both sub-cases of Case (b), the hypothesis that `Op` produces a `Σ.L`-affecting transition outside class (iii) is rejected by ASN-0043 invariants (L12, L12a) and ASN-0086's Frame conditions on `→`. R7a is therefore discharged: any `Σ.L`-affecting transition is a class-(iii) `→`-step. The discharge is categorical — it rules out non-class-(iii) `Σ.L`-affecting transitions for any operation, not just relational-layer ones.

*Characterizing R7b (STIPULATED, model commitment).* R7b restricts which class-(iii) emissions the relational layer issues: every relational-layer-initiated class-(iii) step is the `Emit_K` defined above (which fixes the construction at R0 Step 2's sibling-frontier address). The stipulation is *definitional* — it specifies *which* substrate-primitive deposits the relational layer commits to emitting via — and is *not* extractable from L12, L12a, or the Frame conditions; those invariants permit broader class-(iii) deposits (strict prefix-extensions, in particular, which the substrate primitive admits) that R7b excludes by adoption. Under R7b, the relational-layer state-affecting operations are exactly `{Emit_K}` ∪ {definitional shorthands for `Emit_K` invocations}; no additional relational-layer operation exists *given the commitment*, even though additional class-(iii) emission policies remain admissible at the substrate level. R7b is not proved here — it is the relational layer's operational charter, recorded for citability.

*Scoping note.* R7a is categorical (ranges over any-layer operations); R7b is scoped to relational-layer-initiated transitions. The substrate emission primitive (above) in isolation admits broader class-(iii) deposits — strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses, for instance — which a non-relational-layer caller might in principle invoke. R7b commits the relational layer to the sibling-frontier-disciplined `Emit_K` subset of the substrate primitive; class-(iii) steps initiated by other layers, or by direct substrate access, are outside the scope of R7b's commitment and are governed instead by the discipline-conditionality stated at R0a (FlatLinkDomain), the substrate emission primitive definition, and Nullify's *Remark on the role of P3*.

(Step 4 — compose R7a and R7b to yield the reduction.) Combining Steps 1–3: the relational-layer operations are `{Emit_K, Observe_K, Nullify}` (Step 1); `Nullify` reduces to `Emit_R`, a special case of `Emit_K` (Step 2); by R7a (Step 3, proven), any `Σ.L`-affecting transition is a class-(iii) `→`-step; by R7b (Step 3, stipulated), every relational-layer-initiated class-(iii) step is an `Emit_K` call. Composing R7a with R7b: any `Σ.L`-affecting transition initiated by the relational layer is a class-(iii) `→`-step (R7a) and is an `Emit_K` call (R7b), so the relational-layer operation set reduces to exactly two distinct primitives: `Emit_K` (state-affecting, taking a class-(iii) `→`-step) and `Observe_K` (state-preserving, taking no `→`-step). `Nullify` is not a third primitive but a definitional alias for `Emit_R(Σ, d_retr, ∅, {(a, δ(1, #a))})`. Every state change at the relational layer is therefore expressible as a single `Emit_K` call with appropriate `(K, F, G)` arguments; "update" and "mutate" do not exist as substrate-level primitives, and the user-facing "retraction" operation collapses into the same primitive as ordinary emission. The composition is essential: R7a alone would leave open *which* class-(iii) emissions the relational layer issues (the substrate primitive admits broader ones); R7b alone would leave open whether *non-class-(iii)* operations might affect `Σ.L` (the relational layer cannot rule that out by stipulation). The closure is the join of both. ∎


## Worked Sketch

We illustrate the structure of a retraction cycle in the relational vocabulary, building on the ASN-0043 worked example.

*Setup.* Let `K ∈ T_admissible` be any content-classifying type with `K ∈ T_cat^{Σ_0}`. Suppose at state `Σ_0` the substrate contains:

`L_K^{Σ_0} = {(a₁, F₁, G₁)}` &nbsp; — one classification tuple
`L_R^{Σ_0} = ∅` &nbsp; — no retractions yet

By Definition, `A_K^{Σ_0} = L_K^{Σ_0} = {(a₁, F₁, G₁)}` and `nullified(Σ_0) = ∅`. Let `d = home(a₁) ∈ dom(Σ_0.M)`, so `a₁ ∈ LS(d)` (recall `LS(d) = {a ∈ T : d ≼ a ∧ T4-valid(a) ∧ zeros(a) = 3 ∧ a.E₁ = s_L ∧ #E(a) ≥ 2}` from R0's proof). - `(a₂, F₁, G₁)`: same coverage check as above, passes.

*Sub-step 5.2 — arrangement modification at Σ_4 ↦ Σ_5.* R6c-Corollary lifts the persistence from `⊑` to `⊑̂` — the closure of the broader transition relation including ASN-0036's arrangement-modifying transitions. *This sub-step is structurally abstract by necessity, in contrast to the concrete tumbler-level instantiation of Steps 1–4.* The asymmetry is intrinsic to ASN-0086's scope, not a stylistic choice: Steps 1–4 are concrete because every variable they touch — `dom(Σ.C)`, `dom(Σ.M)`, `dom(Σ.L)`, the endset values stored in `Σ.L`, the tumbler positions of `a₁`, `b₁`, `a₂`, `b₂`, `a₃` — is populated by this note's own emission machinery (R0 / `Emit_K` / `Emit_R`), and each tumbler value is fixed by the Setup's concrete numerical choices. The bridge requires no rewriting of this sub-step's logic; only the schematic `Σ_4 ↦ Σ_5` is replaced by the concrete instance. Take `Σ_4 ↦ Σ_5` to be *any* arrangement-modifying transition admitted at Σ_4 (existence is assumed by the editing-operation ASNs that extend ASN-0036; the specific transition's identity is orthogonal to what is being demonstrated here). By the arrangement-modification frame (inherited from ASN-0036 and stated in the *Scoping note — arrangement modifications* of the main body), `(Σ_5.C, dom(Σ_5.M), Σ_5.L) = (Σ_4.C, dom(Σ_4.M), Σ_4.L)` — only `Σ.M(d)`'s pointwise arrangement value changes (in whatever way the chosen transition prescribes); the link store, the content store, and the document-allocation domain are held identical. Verification of `(a₁, F₁, G₁) ∉ A_K^{Σ_5}`: by `a₁ ∈ nullified(Σ_5) = nullified(Σ_4) ⊇ {a₁}`. ✓

The composite trace `Σ_0 → Σ_1 → Σ_2 → Σ_3 → Σ_4 ↦ Σ_5` mixes four dom-extending steps with one arrangement-modifying step, and `(a₁, F₁, G₁) ∉ A_K^{Σ_n}` holds at every `n ≥ 1`. *Step 6 (concrete) — exercise R6b: Nullify the retraction `b₁` (single-depth-retraction observation).* R6b's content is that `nullified` is single-depth: even if a retraction tuple `b₁` is itself nullified by a second-order retraction, the tuples `b₁` was retracting (namely `a₁`) remain in `nullified` — the predicate does not iterate. *Precondition checks for `Nullify(Σ_5, d, b₁)`.*

- Must Emit be atomic with respect to concurrent Observe, and if so, what is the consistency model under which `A_K` transitions are observed?
- What guarantees does the substrate provide about the cardinality bound of `nullified(Σ)` relative to `dom(Σ.L)` — is unbounded retraction permitted, or must some structural ratio hold?
- Should the sibling-frontier discipline on which R0a is conditional be elevated to a substrate-level guarantee — e.g., by tightening Emit_K's specification to require prefix-incomparability with `dom(Σ.L)` as a postcondition, or by tightening the substrate emission primitive to forbid emissions at strict prefix-extensions of existing link addresses? Either tightening would make R0a unconditional and would discharge Nullify's P3 precondition (above) automatically; the design tradeoff is between substrate primitiveness and the structural guarantees the primitive can deliver without auxiliary implementation contracts.
- Should the sibling-frontier discipline be relaxed to admit deeper-sited link addresses (`#E ≥ 3`), aligning with Nelson's foundational design intent — in which the address space admits depth-N link addresses as a general principle of recursive sub-link allocation? R0a-Cor2 records the present narrowing: under the discipline, every link address sits at `#E = 2` strictly, matching the udanax-green implementation (`findisatoinsertmolecule` never produces depth-3 link addresses) but narrower than L1b's `#E ≥ 2` admission. A relaxed discipline would have to (a) redefine R0a's sibling-stream invariant over a tree of allocators (depth-2 base links spawning depth-3 sub-allocators, and so on) rather than a single sibling stream, (b) re-derive R0a's antichain conclusion within each sub-stream rather than substrate-wide, and (c) re-derive Nullify's single-tuple-scope guarantee under the broader prefix-incomparability checks the tree structure entails — a depth-2 link `a₁` would no longer be guaranteed prefix-incomparable with its depth-3 children, so P3 at `a₁` would no longer suffice to confine `nullified(Σ')` to `{a₁}` even within the disciplined regime. The design tradeoff is between operational tractability (single sibling stream, uniform depth, simple antichain) and design expressiveness (depth-N sub-links as the recursive address space natively admits).
- Under L14's native scoped form — i.e., dropping the globally `s_C`-resident-content Setup hypothesis stated at the head of the note — what is the precise slice-wise statement of R0, R4, and R5 on the `s_C`-resident content slice, and what auxiliary preservation argument is required to lift each across the slice boundary? Concretely: (a) R0's Step 4 L14a-preservation step is currently discharged against `dom(Σ.C)` as a whole via Setup; the slice-wise reformulation would have to discharge it against `dom(Σ.C) ∩ {a : subspace_I(a) = s_C}` and establish that emissions outside the slice cannot break L14a within it. (b) R4's `A_doc^Σ ∩ A_rel^Σ = ∅` would need to be restated as a disjointness between `A_rel^Σ` and the `s_C`-resident sub-universe `A_doc^{Σ, s_C}`, with non-`s_C`-resident content addresses occupying a separate sub-universe whose disjointness from `A_rel^Σ` is a separate question (and would require an additional subspace-distinctness obligation per such alternative subspace). (c) R5's Stage-1 appeal to R0's emission construction inherits R0's slice-wise reformulation; whether the to-set admissibility argument depends on the slice restriction at any point is a question for the reformulated proof. The interaction with R0a is also worth tracing: R0a is Setup-free, so its slice-wise statement is identical to its global statement, but R0's slice-wise reformulation may change which addresses are reachable as `a' = home(a').0.s_L.1` bases in the antichain corollary.

## Appendix A — Substrate-level commentary on `→`

### A.1 — Sparse-allocator interpretation of the substrate emission primitive

At the allocator-state level (ASN-0034's T10a child-spawn admissibility, which constrains each parent–child step along the chain to a single `inc(·, k')` with `k' > 0`), the substrate primitive's atomic class-(iii) step implicitly discharges T10a's child-spawn admissibility at each intermediate spawn pair on the L1c witness chain, in one indivisible action. The substrate exposes no separate allocator-activation transition that must precede the emission: allocator state has no observable evolution apart from the emissions whose witness chains it supports, and no auxiliary per-allocator activation record is consulted or pre-positioned by a prior `→` step. The udanax-green link-emission path is the canonical implementation — `findisatoinsertmolecule` and the sibling-frontier increment `tumblerincrement(·, 0, 1, ·)` (granf2.c:170–175) choose the next-available address by structural query of the existing `dom(Σ.L)` alone, with no per-allocator activation record consulted or maintained. This decouples address-conformance verification from substrate-level deposit and matches both Nelson's ghost-element design — "the address population of tumbler-space is also an abstraction, since things may be addressed even though nothing is there to represent them in storage" [LM 4/23] — and the sparse-allocator behavior of the udanax-green link-emission path, which deposits at the next-available address without materializing any intermediate position. R0 in the main body uses this primitive: its Step 2 constructively exhibits the conformance witness for the chosen `a`, and Step 4 invokes the primitive to discharge the existential by depositing `(F, G, K)` at `a` in one substrate step. In particular, Case A's sibling sweep `inc(·, 0)` through positions `1..s_L−1` of the depth-1 element-field allocator `A_d` is a *path traced by the conformance witness*, not a sequence of substrate-level deposits at those intermediate positions; no `Σ.C` write is induced.

### A.2 — Coarsening of ASN-0034's transition relation

ASN-0086's `→` is the equivalence-class projection of ASN-0034's `→_{0034}` under the equivalence "compose all maximal sequences of T1 (sibling-increment) and T2 (child-spawn) ASN-0034-transitions that have no exposed effect on the substrate-level abstract values `(dom(Σ.C), dom(Σ.M), dom(Σ.L))`". Equivalently, an ASN-0034 sequence collapses to a single ASN-0086-`→` step iff exactly one of `dom(Σ.C)`, `dom(Σ.M)`, `dom(Σ.L)` extends by exactly one fresh key across the sequence and the other two are held identical; sequences with no such effect (pure allocator activation, e.g., a child-spawn that prepositions an allocator without depositing) collapse into the *same* equivalence class as the surrounding state, becoming `→`-stutter rather than a distinct `→`-step. The L1c witness chain traversed by R0 Step 2 — `(d, 2)` followed by `s_L − 1` sibling sweeps followed by `(d.0.s_L, 1)` — is one such collapsed sequence: its only exposed effect on `(dom(Σ.C), dom(Σ.M), dom(Σ.L))` is the single fresh `dom(Σ.L)` extension at `a`, and ASN-0086 projects it to one `→`-step.

*This coarsening is one admissible choice, not the unique substrate-level abstraction over ASN-0034.* A coarser abstraction — e.g., one that also fuses adjacent class-(i)/(ii)/(iii) emissions whenever a higher layer treats their composition as atomic — would also be conformant in the sense of preserving every R-claim in this note (the R-claims quantify over `→`-states, and any coarsening that respects the frame conditions of (i)/(ii)/(iii) inherits the claims). A *finer* abstraction — e.g., one that exposes per-allocator activation transitions distinct from class (iii)'s deposit — would require additional invariant-preservation arguments at the newly visible intermediate states and is not within the scope of this note's R-claims as stated (R0's existence claim, in particular, would need a finer-grained witness construction that exhibits an intermediate allocator-activation step before the deposit step). The choice taken here — collapse all allocator-state-only sequences into stutter, and treat each `(dom(Σ.C) ∪ dom(Σ.M) ∪ dom(Σ.L))`-extending op as one `→`-step — is the abstraction at which `Emit_K`, `Observe`, and `Nullify` factor cleanly as relational-layer primitives. Implementations that expose intermediate allocator-state transitions remain conforming to ASN-0086 provided that their externally-observable `(dom(Σ.C), dom(Σ.M), dom(Σ.L))`-extending steps are in bijection with this note's `→`-steps; the udanax-green substrate is one such implementation (its `findisatoinsertmolecule` performs allocator state-update and link-store deposit within a single externally atomic action, satisfying the bijection trivially).


## Appendix B — Failure modes: necessity of the sibling-frontier discipline

The R0a hypothesis is *necessary*, not just sufficient: a single non-disciplinary class-(iii) transition permanently breaks the antichain, and any operation that relies on R0a (notably Nullify) loses its substrate guarantee from that point onward. We state the failure modes explicitly.

*(a) One non-disciplinary class-(iii) transition is sufficient to break the antichain permanently.* Suppose at some reachable Σ the prior `→`-chain has respected the discipline (so `dom(Σ.L)` is an antichain by R0a applied to the prefix). Suppose Σ → Σ' is a class-(iii) transition that invokes the substrate emission primitive at a strict prefix-extension of some `a₁ ∈ dom(Σ.L)` — concretely, at `a' = a₁.1` (admissible by the substrate primitive per "Breadth of the primitive vs. the discipline R0a names" in the main body, which records the L1c witness chain — extension of `a₁`'s chain by one child-spawn `(a₁, 1)`, `k' = 1` having no zeros constraint — and verifies L0/L1/L1a/L1b at `a'`). *Concrete instantiation, building on the Worked Sketch.* With the Worked Sketch's values (`s_L = 2`, `d = 1.0.1.0.1`, `a₁ = 1.0.1.0.1.0.2.1`), the non-disciplinary deposit lands at `a' = a₁.1 = 1.0.1.0.1.0.2.1.1`. The same substrate-primitive checks instantiate concretely: `a' ∉ dom(Σ.L)` (fresh — the worked sketch's `dom(Σ.L)` contains `a₁` only, and `a' ≠ a₁` by their length difference 8 vs. 9); `a'.E₁ = 2 = s_L`; `zeros(a') = 3` at positions 2, 4, 6 (appending the non-zero digit `1` adds no new zero); `home(a') = 1.0.1.0.1 = d ∈ dom(Σ.M)` (the prefix ending at `a'`'s second zero); `#E(a') = 4 ≥ 2` (element fields `1, 1, 1, 2.1.1`). The substrate primitive accepts this deposit; the discipline forbids it (R0 Step 2 Case B at seed `a₁` would yield `incⁱ(a₁, 0)` — a sibling, not a child — never the prefix-extension `a₁.1`). Then `dom(Σ'.L) = dom(Σ.L) ∪ {a'} = {a₁, a'} = {1.0.1.0.1.0.2.1, 1.0.1.0.1.0.2.1.1}`, which contains the pair `(a₁, a')` with `a₁ ≼ a'` (`a'` extends `a₁` by one digit) and `a₁ ≠ a'`; the antichain conclusion fails at Σ'. By L12 (LinkImmutability) and L12a (LinkStoreMonotonicity), `{a₁, a'} ⊆ dom(Σ'.L) ⊆ dom(Σ''.L)` for every Σ'' reachable from Σ' via any further `→`-chain — the substrate exposes no removal transition (Frame conditions on `→` in the main body), so the bad pair persists in `dom(Σ''.L)` forever. The antichain failure is therefore *permanent*: no subsequent disciplinary behavior can repair it. Consequently the sibling-stream invariant fails at Σ' (the new `a' = a₁.1` cannot be expressed as `incʲ(home(a').0.s_L.1, 0)` for any `j`, since the sibling stream at depth 2 enumerates extensions of `home(a').0.s_L`, not extensions of `a₁`), and the discipline hypothesis of R0a is *never again* satisfied along any `→`-chain extending Σ'.

*(b) Nullify's single-tuple-scope fails immediately at any post-Σ state where the discipline is broken, regardless of which `a` is being nullified.* Let Σ' be a state reached by an `→`-chain with at least one non-disciplinary class-(iii) step (so by (a), some pair `a₁ ≼ a'` with `a₁ ≠ a'` sits in `dom(Σ'.L)`). Consider `Nullify(Σ', d_retr, a)` for any `a ∈ A_rel^{Σ'}`. Two failure cases arise. *Case (b.i) — nullifying the broken party at `a₁`:* if `a = a₁`, P3 fails at Σ' directly — `{a'' ∈ dom(Σ'.L) : a₁ ≼ a''} ⊇ {a₁, a'}` with `a' ≠ a₁` — so Nullify's precondition is unmet at the call site. *Case (b.ii) — nullifying an unrelated `a` ≠ a₁`:* P3 may hold for `a` at Σ' (if `a` is prefix-incomparable with every other link), so the call is admissible; but Nullify's internal `Emit_R` step invokes R0 Step 2 Case B with seed `b ∈ dom(Σ'.L)` of matching home. *If* the seed chosen is `b = a₁` and Case B's least-`i` selection yields `incⁱ(a₁, 0)` that happens to coincide with no existing link, the freshness witness goes through; but in general the broken pair `{a₁, a'}` perturbs the sibling-stream invariant for *all* links homed at `home(a₁)`, and R0a's post-state antichain conclusion (which the single-tuple-scope argument in the main body relies on) is no longer available at Σ'. The argument that the fresh emitter address `b` is prefix-incomparable with `a` no longer follows from R0a, so single-tuple-scope is not guaranteed even when P3 holds at the call's target. In short, the discipline failure at any point along the chain compromises Nullify's substrate guarantee at every subsequent state, regardless of whether the call's target is the directly broken party.

*Operational summary.* Partial discipline (early-disciplined, late-non-disciplined) gives no graceful degradation: R0a's antichain and Nullify's single-tuple-scope are *both* guaranteed everywhere on disciplinary chains, and *neither* is guaranteed at any state reached by a chain containing one or more non-disciplinary steps. Implementations whose substrate primitive does not enforce the discipline must therefore carry an external invariant — typically at the `Emit_K` interface, as udanax-green does via `findisatoinsertmolecule`'s sibling-frontier behavior — to keep R0a and Nullify usable.
