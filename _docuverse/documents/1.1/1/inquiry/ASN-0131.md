question: What happens when the system is asked, of a given region of
  content, which link endsets touch it? What is returned and in what
  unit — endsets as sets of spans rather than link identities — and
  what relationship must each returned endset bear to the region asked
  about and to the link it belongs to? How does an operation that
  surfaces anchoring structure without naming the anchored links
  relate to the content-region link query foundation's distinction
  between a link's existence and its discoverability through
  arrangements, and which side of that distinction does this operation
  answer for? What must hold for endsets whose links reach the region
  through transcluded content rather than through the documents the
  links reside in, and what invariants must the operation maintain
  about completeness, soundness, and the stability of its answer as
  documents are edited and links are retracted?
