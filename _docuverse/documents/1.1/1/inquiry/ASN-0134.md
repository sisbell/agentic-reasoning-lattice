out_of_scope: scheduler construction and fairness discipline (who fires
  when — deliberately the implementer's/protocol layer, named as a
  hypothesis in ASN-0133, not specified here), agent activation and rule

  and the BEBE back-end protocol (Nelson left BEBE undefined in LM 87.1),
  performance/throughput of any particular concurrency control, the
  concrete lock or transaction implementation, predicate evaluation cost
  bounds (ASN-0129's deferred territory)
question: The substrate's transition relation is sequential — atomic
  steps in a single total order (ASN-0047), with the operation surface
  Emit_K / Nullify_Binary / Observe_K defined step-by-step (ASN-0128).
  But the coordination layers built on it assume many agents act at once —
  predicates are evaluated by any observer (ASN-0129), quiescence must be
  recognizable while writers may still be active (ASN-0133 Q0), a
  definition's content run must be allocated contiguously even as other
  agents allocate (ASN-0130), and the runner that produced this corpus
  runs many workers against one store. What consistency does the
  substrate actually guarantee when concurrent agents touch the operation
  surface? What is the unit of atomicity — a single →_sh step (the gated
  transition relation, ASN-0126), a typed emission, or a whole
  fire/batch — and what does an Observe_K see
  relative to an Emit_K or Nullify_Binary that is in flight — only
  committed state, or can it witness a partial effect? How do
  independently-proposed operations become positions in the one
  sequential order — is there a linearization point, is ordering
  per-home-independent or globally serialized, and may two agents
  allocating in the same home or writing the same registry interleave
  their effects? Which of the substrate's invariants — dense contiguous
  allocation chains (ASN-0093), unique addresses, append-only
  monotonicity, the audit/active-slice coincidence (ASN-0086) — are
  preserved by the model itself versus only by the serialization, and
  what is the minimal contract an implementation must honor so that a
  reader never observes a torn or duplicated effect and a writer never
  collides with a concurrent writer's allocation? What does a quiescence
  verdict (ASN-0133 Q0) mean if it is read while a write is in flight —
  is the verdict taken at a consistent snapshot, and what relationship
  must hold between the observer's read and the writers' linearization
  for the verdict to be sound rather than a race?
depends: [47, 86, 93, 126, 128]
