  directionality, typing, and the difference between a connection and
  a relation, and what invariants must the operation preserve about
  the link's permanence, the immutability of its recorded endsets, the
  link's residence-independence from the content it connects, and the
  system's ability to discover the link from any of the regions its
  endsets reference?
