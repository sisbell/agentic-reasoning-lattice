  and inter-server protocol (BEBE)
question: What happens when the system is asked which links match a
  description given as four sets — a home-set bounding where the links
  reside, a from-set bounding what their first endset references, a
  to-set bounding what their second endset references, and a third-set
  bounding their type or connector endset? What is returned and how
  must each returned link relate to all four criteria together, what
  relationship must the result bear to the link store as it stands at
  the moment of inquiry and to the union of all documents' arrangements
  that could surface the same links, what does combining four
  independent slot criteria (rather than a single content-region
  filter) reveal about typed and directional link navigation and about
  the difference between a connection's residence and its endpoints,
  and what invariants must the operation maintain about completeness,
  soundness, stability under content edits and link retraction, and
  the cross-document reach of the discovery?
