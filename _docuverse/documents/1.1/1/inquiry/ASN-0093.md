# Inquiry: Allocation Substrate

ASN-0047 (Transition Model) gives the full state model `Σ = (C, L, E, M, R)` with seven
transition kinds and the J-family coupling constraints. Downstream ASNs that operate on
the link store (e.g., ASN-0086 typed relations) inherit more machinery than they need —
the entity set, the provenance relation, the arrangement-mutation primitives — none of
which the typed-relation algebra references.

This inquiry asks for the *allocation-substrate* layer: the three address-allocation
primitives (K.σ for documents into `dom(M)`, K.α for content into `dom(C)`, K.λ for
links into `dom(L)`) and the structural invariants on `(C, L, M)` they preserve.
Downstream ASNs that only need allocation semantics can depend on this substrate
directly; downstream ASNs that need arrangement mutation, entity stratification, or
provenance recording continue to depend on ASN-0047 (which itself depends on this
substrate).
