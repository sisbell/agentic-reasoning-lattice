- `⟦·⟧` — span-membership bracket operator; `⟦σ⟧` denotes the set of T1 addresses x ∈ T satisfying the half-open bound `start(σ) ≤ x < reach(σ)`
- `start` — function mapping a span σ to its least included T1 address (the closed endpoint of ⟦σ⟧)
- `reach` — function mapping a span σ to its least excluded T1 address (the open endpoint of ⟦σ⟧)
- `≤` — non-strict order on T1 addresses, abbreviating T1's strict order via `a ≤ b ≡ a < b ∨ a = b`; the Convexity proof composes ≤-chains into the bound `start(σ) ≤ q < reach(σ)` by case analysis on this abbreviation, not by a posited ≤-transitivity
- `<` — strict order on T1 addresses, exported by T1 with its transitivity postcondition (c); appears in the half-open interval definition (`x < reach(σ)`) and is the relation to which the proof's case analysis reduces every non-strict step
