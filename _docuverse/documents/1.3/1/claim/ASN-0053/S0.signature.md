- `⟦·⟧` — span-membership bracket operator; `⟦σ⟧` denotes the half-open interval of T1 addresses satisfying `start(σ) ≤ x < reach(σ)`
- `start` — function mapping a span σ to its least included T1 address (the closed endpoint of ⟦σ⟧)
- `reach` — function mapping a span σ to its least excluded T1 address (the open endpoint of ⟦σ⟧)
- `≤` — total non-strict order on T1 addresses; posited as an Axiom and used to derive betweenness transitivity in the Convexity proof
- `<` — strict order on T1 addresses; appears in the half-open interval definition (`x < reach(σ)`) and in the Axiom's transitivity chain
