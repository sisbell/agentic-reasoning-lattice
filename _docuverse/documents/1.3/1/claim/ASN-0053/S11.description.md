Proves that when a level-uniform span β is contained within a level-uniform span α, the set difference ⟦α⟧ \ ⟦β⟧ can always be expressed using at most two spans — a left remainder before β and a right remainder after it. The bound of two is tight: when neither boundary of β coincides with a boundary of α, convexity of spans (S0) makes it impossible to cover the disconnected left and right remainders with a single span.
