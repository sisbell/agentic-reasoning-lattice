Defines the canonical form for a span-set: its spans must be strictly sorted by start position and strictly separated (no two spans adjacent or overlapping), so no further merging is possible. Strict separation is the key constraint — adjacent spans fail normalization because they could still be joined, and overlapping spans must be merged before the form is valid. The normalized span-set is the unique minimal representation in which every span is maximally extended and no pair can be combined.
