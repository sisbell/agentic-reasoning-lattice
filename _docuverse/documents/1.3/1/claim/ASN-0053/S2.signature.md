- `⟦·,·⟧` — the denotation map; `⟦s, ℓ⟧` is the half-open set of positions `{ p : s ≤ p < s ⊕ ℓ }` denoted by the well-formed span `(s, ℓ)`
- `⊕` — position-advancement operator; `s ⊕ ℓ` is the end offset obtained by advancing start position `s` by length `ℓ`
- `actionPoint(·)` — the natural-number action point of a length; `actionPoint(ℓ) ≤ #s` (a comparison in ℕ) is the fit-within-stream precondition every well-formed span carries
- `Pos(·)` — strict-positivity predicate on lengths; `Pos(ℓ)` (i.e. `0 < ℓ`) is the well-formedness precondition that the span has positive length
- `#` — stream-size function; `#s` is the upper bound that the action point `actionPoint(ℓ)` must not exceed in the fit-within-stream precondition `actionPoint(ℓ) ≤ #s`
- `≤` — non-strict ordering; on positions in the denotation comprehension (`s ≤ p`), and on natural numbers in the fit-within-stream condition (`actionPoint(ℓ) ≤ #s`)
- `<` — strict ordering on positions; appears in the denotation comprehension (`p < s ⊕ ℓ`) and in TA-strict's strict-advancement postcondition `s ⊕ ℓ > s` (equivalently `s < s ⊕ ℓ`), the single step that places `s` in the interval
- `∅` — the empty set of positions; this claim establishes it has no preimage under the denotation map
