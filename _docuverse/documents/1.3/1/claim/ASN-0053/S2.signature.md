- `⟦·,·⟧` — the denotation map; `⟦s, ℓ⟧` is the half-open set of positions `{ p : s ≤ p < s ⊕ ℓ }` denoted by the well-formed span `(s, ℓ)`
- `⊕` — position-advancement operator; `s ⊕ ℓ` is the end offset obtained by advancing start position `s` by length `ℓ`
- `#` — stream-size function; `#s` is the upper bound that the end offset `k` must not exceed in the fit-within-stream precondition `k ≤ #s`
- `≤` — non-strict ordering on positions; appears in the denotation comprehension (`s ≤ p`) and the fit-within-stream condition (`k ≤ #s`)
- `<` — strict ordering on positions; appears in the denotation comprehension (`p < s ⊕ ℓ`) and in the strict-positivity axiom TA-strict (`ℓ > 0`, i.e. `0 < ℓ`)
- `∅` — the empty set of positions; this claim establishes it has no preimage under the denotation map
