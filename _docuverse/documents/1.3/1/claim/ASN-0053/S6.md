**S6 (LevelConstraint).** Two tumblers t₁ and t₂ are *level-compatible*, written level_compat(t₁, t₂), when they have the same length:

  level_compat(t₁, t₂)  ≡  #t₁ = #t₂

A span σ = (s, ℓ) is *level-uniform* when level_compat(s, ℓ), i.e., #s = #ℓ. For a level-uniform span, reach(σ) = s ⊕ ℓ inherits its length from the width: #reach(σ) = #(s ⊕ ℓ) = #ℓ by TumblerAdd's result-length identity #(a ⊕ w) = #w, and #ℓ = #s by level-uniformity — so start, width, and reach all share one tumbler length.

Gregory confirms the implementation enforces this: the split operation requires the cut and the width to share a tumbler length and aborts when this invariant is violated (Q14, Q15).

*Formal Contract:*

- *Postconditions:* For a level-uniform span σ = (s, ℓ) — one with level_compat(s, ℓ), i.e. #s = #ℓ — start, width, and reach inhabit a single tumbler length: #start(σ) = #width(σ) = #reach(σ) = #s. The reach equality, #reach(σ) = #(s ⊕ ℓ) = #ℓ, is read directly off TumblerAdd's result-length identity; level-uniformity (#ℓ = #s) then closes the chain to #s.
- *Depends:*
  - TumblerAdd (TumblerAdd, ASN-0034) — supplies the result-length identity #(a ⊕ w) = #w, an exported postcondition of ⊕. Instantiated at the start s and width ℓ of a well-formed level-uniform span — where Pos(ℓ) and actionPoint(ℓ) ≤ #s hold, so s ⊕ ℓ is defined — it gives #reach(σ) = #(s ⊕ ℓ) = #ℓ; the level-uniform hypothesis #ℓ = #s then closes #reach(σ) = #s. This is the sole source of the addition result-length: the in-scope foundations supply only the subtraction length (TumblerSub: #(a ⊖ w) = L) and the round-trip identity (D1: a ⊕ (b ⊖ a) = b), neither of which yields #(s ⊕ ℓ) = #ℓ for a general width ℓ.
