Proves that splitting a level-uniform span at any interior point yields a left and right sub-span that together cover the original exactly, with no overlap and no gap. The split point becomes simultaneously the reach of the left part and the start of the right part, and both sub-spans are themselves well-formed level-uniform spans at the same tumbler depth. The partition is unique and forced by the total order on tumblers: every address in the original span belongs to exactly one of the two parts.
