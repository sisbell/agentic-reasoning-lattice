- `⟦·⟧` — denotation function mapping a span sequence to the union of its constituent half-open intervals; defined as `⟦⟨γ₁,…,γₖ⟩⟧ = ⋃ₗ ⟦γₗ⟧` and `⟦γₗ⟧ = [start(γₗ), reach(γₗ))`
- `start` — function returning the start position of a span; primitive identifying parameter used throughout N1, N2, and all case analyses
- `reach` — function returning the exclusive upper-bound position of a span; defined as `reach(γ) = start(γ) ⊕ width(γ)`
- `width` — function returning the width of a span; the primitive identifying parameter alongside start, from which reach is derived
- `⊕` — position monoid addition; used to express reach in terms of start and width, and axiomatized by left-cancellation TA-LC
- `<` — strict order on positions; governs the normalization conditions N1 (strictly increasing starts) and N2 (strict gap between reach and next start)
- `≡` — span-equivalence relation on sequences; holds iff the two sequences have equal denotations under `⟦·⟧`
