Proves that for a level-uniform span, subtracting the start address from the reach recovers the original width exactly. The proof discharges the preconditions of DisplacementUnique (D2) to confirm that the round-trip s ⊕ ℓ followed by ⊖ s returns ℓ — a guarantee that depends critically on start and reach having equal address length, as the worked counterexample shows: when the start is longer than the width, the recovered displacement gains a spurious trailing zero and the round-trip fails.
