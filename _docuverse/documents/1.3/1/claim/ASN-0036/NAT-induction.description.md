Posits Peano induction over ℕ in set form: the only subset of ℕ containing 0 and closed under the successor n ↦ n+1 is ℕ itself. A foundation-level posit that adds the generation-from-0 principle to ASN-0034's NAT-* group, whose well-ordering, discreteness, and ordered cancellative addition axioms do not entail it — well-ordering alone admits models carrying elements unreachable from 0 by finite succession — and on which the predecessor-existence and segment-length induction arguments that follow in ASN-0036 both depend.
