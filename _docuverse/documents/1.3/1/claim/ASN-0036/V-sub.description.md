Defines the subspace projection V_S(d) as the subset of active V-positions of a document whose subspace identifier equals S, partitioning the arrangement domain into disjoint per-subspace slices. The text-subspace specialization V_1(d) — the slice at S = 1 — is the object that the arrangement-contiguity invariants D-CTG, D-CTG-depth, D-MIN, and D-SEQ constrain, and this claim is what gives that symbol its formal meaning.
