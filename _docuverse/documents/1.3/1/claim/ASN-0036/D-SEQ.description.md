Proves that the text-subspace positions of any non-empty document form a complete initial segment of the form {[1, 1, …, 1, k] : 1 ≤ k ≤ n} for some n ≥ 1, where all leading components are 1 and only the final ordinal varies. This establishes that document content is addressed by a single sequential counter starting at 1, formally grounding Nelson's "addresses 1 through n" in the structural constraints on valid V-positions.
