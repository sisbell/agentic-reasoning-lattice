Proves that ordinal shift is subspace-preserving and well-formedness-preserving: shifting a V-position forward by n steps leaves its first component (the subspace identifier) unchanged, and if the original position satisfies S8a (all components positive, depth at least 2), so does the shifted result. Both parts follow from the component-wise structure of tumbler addition, which copies all components before the action point and only increments the final component.
