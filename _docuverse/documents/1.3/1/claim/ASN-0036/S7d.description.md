Posits that every document occupies a distinct two-zero tumbler produced by a dedicated allocation event, inheriting GlobalUniqueness from the allocator discipline so that no two documents can share an address. The zeros-equals-two constraint is not incidental: it is the structural marker that separates document-level identity from content (zeros = 3) and from the span root (zeros = 1), making document tumblers a recognizable, non-overlapping tier in the address hierarchy.
