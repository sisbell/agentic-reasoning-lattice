Proves that the active V-positions of any document uniquely partition into finitely many maximal correspondence runs — contiguous blocks in which V-positions and their content images advance in lockstep under ordinal displacement, admitting no forward or backward extension while preserving that lockstep property. The partition is unique because the induced lockstep-successor relation is injective and acyclic on a finite domain, so every V-position belongs to exactly one maximal run.
