**S8-depth (FixedDepthVPositions).** Within a given subspace `s` of document `d`, all V-positions share the same tumbler depth:

`(A d ∈ T :: (A u, w : u ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) ∧ w ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) ∧ subspace(u) = subspace(w) : #u = #w))`

We adopt S8-depth as a *design constraint* on the arrangement — a per-subspace posit, asserted by fiat — not a uniformity read off the evidence in full. For the *text* subspace the posit is evidentially grounded: Gregory's evidence shows V-addresses there consistently take the form `s.x`, two tumbler digits with `s` the subspace identifier and `x` the ordinal, so every active text position sits at depth 2. The quantifier ranges over *all* subspaces, and there it outruns that evidence — for link, annotation, or any other subspace we have no comparable address corpus, so their depth-per-subspace uniformity is asserted by design, not observed. To ground the non-text portion one would need, per subspace, an analogous survey of its V-address forms exhibiting a fixed component count (the link subspace being the first such corpus to record); until then S8-depth binds those subspaces by the posit alone. What the rest of this ASN consumes from that non-text scope is the posit's *truth*, never its *grounding*. D-CTG-depth, D-MIN, and D-SEQ constrain only `V_1(d)`, the text-subspace projection, so they do not reach the non-text scope at all. S8 does reach it: its chain decomposition writes `m = #v` and cites S8-depth for the subspace-wide common depth shared by every active position in `v`'s subspace, and `dom(Σ.M(d))` there is not restricted to the text subspace — so S8 genuinely uses this quantifier across all subspaces, not the text subspace alone. What no S8 step relies on is the *evidentiary grounding* of that depth for link or annotation positions, only its posited truth; and the per-position equality `#shift(v, 1) = #v` the decomposition needs link-by-link it draws from `shift`'s frame (OrdShiftHom's frame, ultimately TA0), not from this quantifier. So no soundness gap opens here *while the posit is granted by design* — but the grounding gap for non-text subspaces is real, and we record it lest a future ASN read S8's subspace-wide framing as license to apply S8-depth to link positions as though that scope were already grounded in evidence. Any correct implementation must still satisfy the constraint as stated.

S8-depth allows us to define "consecutive V-positions" precisely. Within a subspace, consecutive positions differ only at the ordinal (last) component: a position `v` is followed by `shift(v, 1)` (equivalently `v ⊕ δ(1, #v)` per OrdinalShift, ASN-0034), the next ordinal at the same depth.

### Shift preservation for V-positions

For the successor `shift(v, 1)` to be itself a V-position of the same subspace — so that "consecutive V-positions" is a well-formed notion — ordinal shift must leave a V-position's subspace identifier and its S8a well-formedness intact. It does, and we do not re-derive it here: OrdShiftHom (OrdinalShiftPreservation) establishes both halves — part (a), `subspace(shift(v, n)) = subspace(v)`, and part (b), `shift(v, n)` satisfies S8a whenever `v` does. We invoke OrdShiftHom wherever a shifted V-position must be shown to remain in its subspace and well-formed.

*Formal Contract:*

- *Axiom:* For every reachable state `Σ` and every document `d`, within each subspace the active V-positions share one tumbler depth — any two active keys of `dom(Σ.M(d))` carrying the same subspace identifier sit at the same depth:
  `(A d ∈ T :: (A u, w : u ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) ∧ w ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) ∧ subspace(u) = subspace(w) : #u = #w))`
  — the outer universal binding `d` over the tumbler carrier `T`, exactly the document-level tumblers to which Σ.M's declaration scopes its assignment `d ↦ Σ.M(d)`, so that for every admitted `d` the arrangement `Σ.M(d)` — and with it the domain `dom(Σ.M(d))` over which `u` and `w` then range and which the antecedent `u ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) ∧ w ∈ dom(Σ.M(d))` names — is a defined object, with no `d ∉ T` let in for which `Σ.M(d)` would be undefined and that domain ill-formed; `u` and `w` ranging over the active V-positions `dom(Σ.M(d))`, the range guard `subspace(u) = subspace(w)` selecting the pairs that share a subspace identifier, and the term `#u = #w` comparing their tumbler depths — holding at every state reachable from the base state `Σ₀` by the transition relation. The equality binds *per subspace*, not globally: `dom(Σ.M(d))` may mix subspaces at different depths, and only the pairs the guard `subspace(u) = subspace(w)` admits are constrained. This is a protocol design posit on the class of permitted arrangements, accepted within this ASN without proof; it constrains which arrangements an implementation may produce, admitting no reachable arrangement whose active keys differ in depth within a single subspace. It is not derived from any other claim — in particular not from the content-stream invariants S0, S1, S2, S3, which govern the evolution of `C` alone, nor from the transition axioms AX-1 (which fixes only the empty base arrangement) and AX-2 (which constrains the targets of newly established V-mappings, never the depth of the keys); none of these fixes the depth of a V-position. S8 consumes it as the subspace-wide common depth `m = #v` shared by every active position in `v`'s subspace — so a consumer, a formalization tool included, must treat it as an implementation obligation on permitted arrangements rather than as a theorem to discharge.

- *Depends:*
  - Σ.M(d) (Arrangement) — supplies the arrangement `Σ.M(d)` and its domain `dom(Σ.M(d))`, the set over which the formal statement quantifies `u` and `w`; the active-key object whose per-subspace depth uniformity this posit asserts. It is likewise this claim's declaration — `Σ.M` assigns an arrangement to each document `d ∈ T` — that scopes the outer universal `(A d ∈ T :: ...)`: restricting `d` to precisely the carrier on which `Σ.M` is defined is what keeps `Σ.M(d)`, and with it the antecedent's `dom(Σ.M(d))`, a defined object throughout the quantifier, admitting no `d ∉ T` for which `Σ.M(d)` would be undefined and that codomain ill-formed — the same `d ∈ T` restriction S8-fin's `(A d ∈ T :: ...)` carries for the identical reason. S8a separately restricts this same domain, but the domain symbol itself is grounded here at its defining claim, not at S8a, as V-sub grounds the `dom(Σ.M(d))` it ranges over
  - subspace (VPositionSubspaceIdentifier) — supplies the projection `subspace(·) = (·)₁` whose equality `subspace(u) = subspace(w)` is the range restriction in S8-depth's formal statement, selecting the pairs of V-positions that share a subspace identifier
  - T0 (CarrierSetDefinition, ASN-0034) — supplies the length operator `#· : T → ℕ` whose values are compared in the formal statement's term `#u = #w`, the per-subspace common tumbler depth this posit asserts; T0 is the defining source of `#` throughout the ASN, cited here exactly as S8a cites it for the `#t` appearing in its axiom. T0 also furnishes the carrier `T` over which the outer universal ranges its document index `d ∈ T` — the *restriction* of `d` to `T` read from Σ.M(d)'s declaration above, the carrier `T` it is restricted to grounded here, exactly as S8-fin grounds the `T` of its own `(A d ∈ T :: ...)` against this same defining claim

OrdinalShift and OrdShiftHom are *not* dependencies of the formal posit: neither `shift` nor `δ` nor the shift-preservation result appears in `#u = #w`. They are cited in the commentary above — OrdinalShift for the `shift(v, 1) = v ⊕ δ(1, #v)` that defines consecutive V-positions, OrdShiftHom for the shift-preservation that keeps the successor in its subspace — and remain commentary citations there, not entries in this Depends list. S8a is excluded by the very same test, and it is worth saying why it does not earn a place the test would otherwise seem to grant it. S8a is a parallel, independent posit about the same domain: its restriction `dom(Σ.M(d)) ⊆ {t ∈ T : zeros(t) = 0 ∧ #t ≥ 2}` constrains the keys, but it contributes no symbol to `#u = #w`. The domain `dom(Σ.M(d))` over which `u` and `w` range is grounded at its defining claim Σ.M(d), and the length operator `#` at T0, so S8-depth can be stated and posited with S8a entirely out of scope — to list it among the dependencies would assert a topological order the logic does not have, as though S8a had to be established before S8-depth could even be written down, when in truth the two posits stand side by side. What S8a does supply is semantic, not symbolic: the assurance that every key whose depth we compare is a genuine well-formed V-position of depth at least 2. That co-ordination between the two posits is recorded in the shift-preservation commentary above, where ordinal shift is observed to leave a V-position's S8a well-formedness intact — a commentary citation, not an entry in this Depends list.
