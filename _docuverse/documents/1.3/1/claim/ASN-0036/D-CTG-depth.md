**D-CTG-depth (SharedPrefixReduction).** For depth m ≥ 3, all positions in a non-empty V_1(d) — the text-subspace projection `{v ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) : subspace(v) = 1}` defined in V-sub (SubspaceProjection) — share components 2 through m − 1. Contiguity reduces to contiguity of the last component alone — structurally identical to the depth 2 case.

*Proof.* Let V_1(d) be non-empty with common depth `m` (S8-depth) and `m ≥ 3` — a scope restriction ensuring the interior index range `2 ≤ i ≤ m − 1` is non-empty, so the shared-prefix claim has content; the `m = 2` case, where that range is empty and the claim is vacuous, is handled separately in D-SEQ's `m = 2` case. Suppose for contradiction that V_1(d) contains two positions u and x with u < x (both depth m) that disagree somewhere in the interior range: the set `{i : 2 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 ∧ uᵢ ≠ xᵢ}` of interior components at which they differ is then a non-empty subset of ℕ, so by NAT-wellorder (NatWellOrdering, ASN-0034) — the least-element principle for ℕ — it has a least element. Let j be that first point of disagreement, with 2 ≤ j ≤ m − 1 (the bound holds because j belongs to the interior set). Then uᵢ = xᵢ for all i < j — for interior i by the minimality of j, and at component 1 by the shared subspace value u₁ = x₁ = 1 — and uⱼ < xⱼ (the inequality follows from u < x by T1(i), since j is the first disagreeing component and j ≤ min(m, m)).

We construct infinitely many intermediates. For any natural number n > uⱼ₊₁, define w of length m by:

- wᵢ = uᵢ for 1 ≤ i ≤ j (agreeing with u on the first j components),
- wⱼ₊₁ = n,
- wᵢ = 1 for j + 2 ≤ i ≤ m (an empty range when j = m − 1, in which case wⱼ₊₁ = w_m is already the last component; otherwise this clause fills components j + 2 through m).

Every component of w lies in ℕ — wᵢ = uᵢ ∈ ℕ for 1 ≤ i ≤ j since u ∈ T, wⱼ₊₁ = n ∈ ℕ, and wᵢ = 1 ∈ ℕ for j + 2 ≤ i ≤ m — and w has length m ≥ 3 ≥ 1, so by T0's comprehension clause (CarrierSetDefinition, ASN-0034), instantiated at length m and this component map, w ∈ T: the construction yields a genuine tumbler, which is what later lets us instantiate D-CTG's inner v ∈ T quantifier at the constructed w. Then w has depth m (it has m components by construction), and subspace(w) = w₁ = u₁ = 1 (since j ≥ 2, the first component is copied from u). We verify u < w < x:

- **w > u**: w agrees with u on components 1 through j. At component j + 1, wⱼ₊₁ = n > uⱼ₊₁. Since j + 1 ≤ m = min(m, m), by T1(i), w > u.
- **w < x**: w agrees with x on components 1 through j − 1 (since u and x agree on these components by the definition of j). At component j, wⱼ = uⱼ < xⱼ. Since j ≤ m − 1 ≤ min(m, m), by T1(i), w < x.

We also verify that w satisfies S8a — necessary because D-CTG ranges over V_1(d) ⊆ dom(M(d)), and every position in dom(M(d)) satisfies S8a. By construction, every component of w is at least 1: wᵢ = uᵢ ≥ 1 for i ≤ j by S8a applied to u; wⱼ₊₁ = n > uⱼ₊₁ ≥ 1 (again by S8a on u); and wᵢ = 1 for j + 2 ≤ i ≤ m. Hence zeros(w) = 0 and `(A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ #w : wᵢ > 0)`. Combined with #w = m ≥ 3 ≥ 2, w satisfies S8a — so the candidate w qualifies for D-CTG's consequent.

Since u < w < x, subspace(w) = 1, #w = m = #u, and w satisfies S8a, D-CTG requires w ∈ V_1(d). We now exhibit infinitely many admissible values of n, each read off T0(a)'s postcondition. That postcondition delivers not a bare natural but a tumbler: instantiated at a supplied tumbler t, a component index i, and a bound M ∈ ℕ, T0(a) (UnboundedComponentValues, ASN-0034) furnishes a tumbler t′ ∈ T with #t′ = #t that agrees with t at every component k ≠ i and has t′ᵢ > M. We instantiate it at t = u and i = j + 1 — placing the large value at exactly the component our witness w varies — and read off the natural n := t′ⱼ₊₁ ∈ ℕ (a component of a tumbler, hence in the carrier ℕ by T0), which satisfies n > M. Iterating the bound: starting from M₀ = uⱼ₊₁, this instantiation supplies a tumbler whose (j + 1)-th component n₁ > M₀; setting M₁ = n₁ and re-instantiating at t = u, i = j + 1, M = M₁ supplies a further tumbler whose (j + 1)-th component n₂ > M₁ ≥ n₁; continuing, we obtain a strictly increasing sequence n₁ < n₂ < n₃ < … of natural numbers, all exceeding uⱼ₊₁. The sequence is infinite and pairwise distinct. Distinct values of n yield distinct tumblers w (they differ at component j + 1, so by T3, CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034, they are unequal). This produces infinitely many distinct positions in V_1(d), contradicting S8-fin (dom(M(d)) is finite).

Therefore no two positions in V_1(d) can disagree at any component j with 2 ≤ j ≤ m − 1: every pair agrees on components 2 through m − 1. This is the first postcondition.

It remains to convert this shared prefix into the promised reduction — that D-CTG's multi-component betweenness guard is decided by component m alone. Since subspace(p) = p₁ = 1 for every p ∈ V_1(d), the agreement just established extends downward to component 1: any two positions of V_1(d) agree on their whole prefix, components 1 through m − 1. Fix u, x ∈ V_1(d) with u < x, and let v ∈ T be any intermediate admitted by D-CTG's guard — one with #v = #u = m and u < v < x. We show v is itself forced to share that prefix, so that its only remaining freedom lies at component m.

Suppose otherwise — that v differs from u somewhere in components 1 through m − 1; the set `{i : 1 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 ∧ vᵢ ≠ uᵢ}` of such components is then a non-empty subset of ℕ. Component 1 is not among them: D-CTG's guard fixes subspace(v) = v₁ = 1, and u ∈ V_1(d) gives subspace(u) = u₁ = 1, so v₁ = u₁ and hence 1 ∉ `{i : 1 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 ∧ vᵢ ≠ uᵢ}`. The set is therefore a subset of {2, …, m − 1}, so the least element NAT-wellorder (NatWellOrdering, ASN-0034) again furnishes satisfies j ≥ 2. Let j be that least component, 2 ≤ j ≤ m − 1; then v agrees with u on components 1 through j − 1 (by the minimality of j, component 1 already secured by v₁ = u₁ = 1) and vⱼ ≠ uⱼ. Because #v = #u = m, the prefix clause T1(ii) — which requires one tumbler to be a proper, hence strictly shorter, prefix of the other — cannot order u and v; clause T1(i) orders them at their first differing component, which is exactly j. The hypothesis u < v therefore yields uⱼ < vⱼ. Now u and x agree across components 1 through m − 1, so xⱼ = uⱼ and hence xⱼ < vⱼ; and v agrees with x on components 1 through j − 1, since it agrees with u there and u agrees with x. Component j is thus the first point of disagreement between x and v as well, and T1(i) — reading off xⱼ < vⱼ with j ≤ m − 1 ≤ min(m, m) — witnesses x < v. But the guard supplies v < x, and T1's trichotomy forbids x < v and v < x at once. The supposition is untenable: v agrees with u and x on every component 1 through m − 1.

With v, u, and x sharing components 1 through m − 1, component m is the only site at which any two of them can differ, so T1(i) reads each surviving comparison off that component: u < v reduces to uₘ < vₘ, and v < x to vₘ < xₘ. Two results now stand together — every guard-admitted intermediate shares the prefix (the paragraph above), and for a prefix-sharing v the betweenness reads off component m alone (the line just derived). Packaging them characterises the intermediates exactly: for u, x ∈ V_1(d) with u < x and any v ∈ T with #v = m, subspace(v) = 1, zeros(v) = 0,

`u < v < x  ≡  ( (A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 : vᵢ = uᵢ)  ∧  uₘ < vₘ < xₘ )`.

The forward direction is the work just done — a guard-admitted v inherits the prefix, whereupon T1(i) collapses u < v < x to uₘ < vₘ < xₘ; the converse is immediate, since a v carrying that prefix agrees with both u and x through component m − 1, so T1(i) lifts uₘ < vₘ < xₘ straight back to u < v < x. The prefix conjunct cannot be dropped: a v differing from u before component m can satisfy uₘ < vₘ < xₘ yet violate u < v < x, so it is the conjunction, not the bare component-m comparison, that is equivalent to betweenness. This equivalence is the second postcondition and the reduction D-SEQ consumes: D-CTG's multi-component betweenness guard u < v < x collapses, modulo the shared prefix, to the single ordinal betweenness uₘ < vₘ < xₘ. Contiguity of V_1(d) is determined by component m alone — structurally identical to the depth-2 case, where m − 1 = 1 and the shared prefix is the lone subspace component v₁ = 1. ∎

Nelson's statement specifies not just contiguity but also the starting ordinal: "addresses 1 through 100," not "42 through 141." All ordinal numbering in the tumbler system starts at 1: the first child is always .1 (LM 4/20), link positions within a document begin at 1 (LM 4/31), and position 0 is structurally unavailable since zero serves as a field separator (T4, ASN-0034). V-positions follow the same convention.

*Formal Contract:*

- *Preconditions:*
  - V_1(d) ≠ ∅ (non-empty).
  - All positions in V_1(d) share a common depth m (S8-depth), with m ≥ 3 — a scope restriction, not a derived bound: at m ≥ 3 the interior index range 2 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 is non-empty, so the shared-prefix claim has content, whereas the m = 2 case, where that range is empty and the claim is vacuous, is handled separately in D-SEQ's m = 2 case.
  - Every position p ∈ V_1(d) has subspace(p) = p₁ = 1.
  - V_1(d) ⊆ dom(M(d)), and every position in dom(M(d)) satisfies S8a: `#p ≥ 2 ∧ (A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ #p : pᵢ > 0)`.
  - V_1(d) is contiguous in the position order (D-CTG): if u, x ∈ V_1(d), u < w < x, subspace(w) = 1, #w = #u, and w satisfies S8a, then w ∈ V_1(d).
  - dom(M(d)) is finite (S8-fin).
  - Component values are unbounded (T0(a), ASN-0034): instantiated at a tumbler t, a component index i, and a bound M ∈ ℕ, T0(a) supplies a tumbler t′ ∈ T with #t′ = #t agreeing with t at every component k ≠ i and with t′ᵢ > M; the i-th component is then a natural n = t′ᵢ > M. The proof instantiates this at t = u and i = j + 1, extracting n = t′ⱼ₊₁.

- *Postconditions:*
  - `(A u, x : u ∈ V_1(d) ∧ x ∈ V_1(d) : (A i : 2 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 : uᵢ = xᵢ))` — every pair of positions in V_1(d) agrees on components 2 through m − 1.
  - `(A u, x : u ∈ V_1(d) ∧ x ∈ V_1(d) ∧ u < x : (A v : v ∈ T ∧ #v = m ∧ subspace(v) = 1 ∧ zeros(v) = 0 : u < v < x ≡ ( (A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 : vᵢ = uᵢ) ∧ uₘ < vₘ < xₘ )))` — every position v admitted by D-CTG's guard strictly between two V-positions u, x agrees with them on the common prefix (components 1 through m − 1), and the betweenness u < v < x is then decided by the single ordinal comparison uₘ < vₘ < xₘ at the last component. This is the citable form of the reduction D-SEQ consumes. The shared-prefix conjunct is load-bearing: dropping it makes the equivalence false, since a v differing from u before component m can satisfy uₘ < vₘ < xₘ while violating u < v < x — it is the conjunction, not the bare component-m betweenness, that is equivalent to the guard.

- *Definition:* For positions u, x ∈ V_1(d) (u < x, both depth m) whose first disagreement is at component j with 2 ≤ j ≤ m − 1, and for any n > uⱼ₊₁, the intermediate witness w of depth m is constructed by: wᵢ = uᵢ for 1 ≤ i ≤ j; wⱼ₊₁ = n; wᵢ = 1 for j + 2 ≤ i ≤ m (an empty clause when j = m − 1).

- *Depends:*
  - V-sub (SubspaceProjection) — supplies the definition of the text-subspace projection `V_1(d) = {v ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) : subspace(v) = 1}` the proof reasons about; the preconditions `V_1(d) ≠ ∅` and `V_1(d) ⊆ dom(M(d))`, and the membership tests `u, x ∈ V_1(d)` and `w ∈ V_1(d)`, are all stated against this set, each membership unfolding to `· ∈ dom(Σ.M(d)) ∧ subspace(·) = 1`
  - S8-depth (FixedDepthVPositions) — supplies the shared depth `m` for all positions in V_1(d), consumed as the proof's starting invariant that all elements have a common depth before the contradiction is constructed
  - T1 (LexicographicOrder, ASN-0034) — supplies the component-comparison clause (i) and the trichotomy of the lexicographic order; the clause is invoked in the shared-prefix construction to derive u < w and w < x from the first differing component, and again in the reduction step where — together with trichotomy, which forbids x < v and v < x at once — it forces every guard-admitted intermediate v to share components 1 through m − 1 with u and x, collapsing the betweenness u < v < x to the single comparison uₘ < vₘ < xₘ
  - NAT-wellorder (NatWellOrdering, ASN-0034) — supplies the least-element principle for ℕ (every non-empty subset of ℕ has a least element), invoked twice to name a least point of disagreement: in the contradiction setup, applied to the interior components `{i : 2 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 ∧ uᵢ ≠ xᵢ}` where u and x differ, it furnishes the first disagreement j; in the reduction step, applied to `{i : 1 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 ∧ vᵢ ≠ uᵢ}`, it furnishes the least component j at which a guard-admitted intermediate v departs from u — each use extracts the minimum of a non-empty subset of ℕ, the same construction TumblerAdd (ASN-0034) makes in its divergence sub-case
  - S8a (ArrangementDomainRestriction) — supplies the well-formedness predicate (`#p ≥ 2 ∧ (A i : 1 ≤ i ≤ #p : pᵢ > 0)`) that the constructed intermediate w must satisfy before D-CTG can require w ∈ V_1(d)
  - D-CTG (VContiguity) — supplies the contiguity axiom applied to force w ∈ V_1(d) from u < w < x with matching subspace, depth, and S8a well-formedness; the claim's contradiction rests on producing infinitely many such w
  - T0 (CarrierSetDefinition, ASN-0034) — supplies the comprehension clause that grounds the carrier membership w ∈ T of the explicitly-constructed witness w (length m, every component ℕ-valued), the prerequisite for instantiating D-CTG's inner v ∈ T quantifier at w
  - T0(a) (UnboundedComponentValues, ASN-0034) — instantiated at the tumbler u and component index i = j + 1 with successive bounds M, supplies a tumbler t′ ∈ T agreeing with u off component j + 1 and with t′ⱼ₊₁ > M; the extracted natural n = t′ⱼ₊₁ drives the strictly increasing sequence n₁ < n₂ < … of admissible intermediates that contradicts S8-fin
  - T3 (CanonicalRepresentation, ASN-0034) — supplies tumbler equality as component-wise identity; used to conclude that distinct values of n yield distinct depth-m positions w (differing at component j + 1)
  - S8-fin (FiniteArrangement) — supplies finiteness of dom(M(d)); the proof is by contradiction, and the infinite sequence of distinct positions in V_1(d) contradicts this finiteness
  - subspace (VPositionSubspaceIdentifier) — supplies the projection `subspace(p) = p₁` unfolded twice: in the witness construction's step `subspace(w) = w₁ = u₁ = 1`, where the value subspace(w) = 1 is what the constructed witness w must satisfy to meet D-CTG's `subspace(v) = 1` guard; and in the reduction step, where `subspace(v) = v₁ = 1` (from the guard) and `subspace(u) = u₁ = 1` (from u ∈ V_1(d)) together exclude component 1 from the disagreement set `{i : 1 ≤ i ≤ m − 1 ∧ vᵢ ≠ uᵢ}`, forcing the least disagreement j ≥ 2
