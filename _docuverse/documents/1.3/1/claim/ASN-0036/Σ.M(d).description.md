Defines the arrangement of a document as a partial function from V-position tumblers to I-address tumblers, encoding which virtual positions are active and what content each references. The domain restriction (S8a) confines keys to zero-free tumblers of depth at least two, ensuring every active position carries a subspace identifier and a within-subspace ordinal. Together with the invariant content store, the arrangement forms the strand — the two-component object whose separation of mutable mapping from immutable content is the structural premise of the entire ASN.
