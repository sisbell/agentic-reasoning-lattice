Defines the arrangement Σ.M(d) as the indirection layer that separates where content appears in a document (V-positions) from what content exists (I-addresses): editing modifies the arrangement while the content store remains invariant, making non-destructive editing structurally possible rather than a policy choice. The formal contract pins the domain to zero-free tumblers of depth at least two, naming this restriction S8a, and pairs the arrangement with the content store as the two-component object called the strand whose invariants the rest of the ASN derives.
