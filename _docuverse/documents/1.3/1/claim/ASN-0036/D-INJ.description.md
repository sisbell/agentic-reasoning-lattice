Proves that injectivity preserves count: for any injection from {1,…,P} into {1,…,n}, the image has cardinality exactly P — with no condition that P ≤ n. The proof proceeds by induction on P, constructing the required strictly increasing enumeration by deleting the index of the least image value, applying the hypothesis to the shorter image, and prepending the least value. Combined with NAT-card's upper bound on images inside {1,…,n}, the exact count yields finite pigeonhole — no injection exists from a strictly longer initial segment into a shorter one — the consequence D-CTG-depth exploits at P := N+1, n := N to close its finiteness contradiction.
