**S0 (ContentImmutability).** For every state transition `Σ → Σ'`:

`[a ∈ dom(Σ.C) ⟹ a ∈ dom(Σ'.C) ∧ Σ'.C(a) = Σ.C(a)]`

Once content is stored at address `a`, both the address and its value are fixed for all future states. This is the central invariant of the two-stream architecture.

*Formal Contract:*
- *Axiom:* For every state transition `Σ → Σ'`, `[a ∈ dom(Σ.C) ⟹ a ∈ dom(Σ'.C) ∧ Σ'.C(a) = Σ.C(a)]` — once content is stored at an address, both that address's membership in `dom(Σ.C)` and its stored value are fixed across every successor state. This is the root posit of the two-stream architecture: it is accepted within this ASN without proof, it supplies the precondition S1 invokes, and it grounds the dependence of S3 and S5. It holds by the design of the content stream, not by derivation from other claims.

- *Depends:*
  - Σ.C (ContentStore) — supplies the content store `Σ.C` and its domain `dom(Σ.C)`, the stored-address component over which this axiom is stated: the antecedent `a ∈ dom(Σ.C)`, the consequent membership `a ∈ dom(Σ'.C)`, and the value equality `Σ'.C(a) = Σ.C(a)` all read against it. The axiom is not *derived* from ContentStore — it remains a root design posit on how `Σ.C` evolves across a transition — but the state symbol `Σ.C` it constrains is grounded here at its defining claim, exactly as V-sub and S7 ground the state objects they range over
