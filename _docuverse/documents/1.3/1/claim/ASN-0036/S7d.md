**S7d (DocumentAllocationDiscipline).** Every document is realized as a distinct node of the global allocator tree `𝒯` (ASN-0034) at document level — its address being the document-level tumbler `N.0.U.0.D` (`zeros = 2`) carried as that node's base, established by exactly one child-spawning event of T10a's allocator discipline. *Distinct documents are distinct such nodes*, and since T10a's at-most-once constraint forbids a parent from spawning two children on a single pair `(t, k')`, distinct documents arise from distinct allocation events.

We make precise the bridge on which S7's Uniqueness step turns. *Document* is a notion of this ASN's model; *allocator node* a notion of ASN-0034's T10a. S7d fuses the two: a document is not merely *named* by some tumbler that happens to sit at document level — it *is* the document-level node T10a spawns for it, and its address `N.0.U.0.D` is that node's base. This is the document-level counterpart of S7a, which reads each element address back to an element allocator spawned *under* a document's own allocator; S7d fixes that document allocator as the document's very identity. Like S7a it is not derived within this ASN — it records how the model's documents are laid into T10a's tree — but, once posited, it makes the event-distinctness S7 needs immediate.

That immediacy is the whole point of naming the identification, so we trace it. Each document, established (by the posit) through a child-spawning event, is a non-root node, and T10a's tree construction assigns every non-root node exactly one spawning triple `(parent, spawnPt, spawnParam)`; so reading a document to its allocation event `ev` is a total, single-valued operation. It is moreover injective. Were `ev(d₁) = ev(d₂) = (P, t, k')`, both documents' nodes would be children of the one parent `P` spawned by the very pair `(t, k')`, and T10a's at-most-once constraint — each `(t, k')` pair yields at most one child-spawning event — admits at most one such child, collapsing the two nodes and, by the identification, the two documents `d₁ = d₂`. (Documents whose nodes hang under *different* parents are separated already, their events differing in the parent component; the at-most-once constraint is needed only for the common-parent case.) Contrapositively, distinct documents have distinct allocation events — exactly the second fact S7 feeds to GlobalUniqueness. We mark the boundary of the claim: S7d separates documents at the level of *events*, not yet of *addresses* — two distinct events could, for all S7d says, still emit one tumbler value — and lifting event-distinctness to address-distinctness is GlobalUniqueness's step (ASN-0034), not S7d's; S7d's office is precisely to hand GlobalUniqueness two distinct events to discriminate.

*Formal Contract:*
- *Axiom:* Every document is a distinct node of the allocator tree `𝒯` (ASN-0034) at document level, its address the document-level tumbler `N.0.U.0.D` (`zeros = 2`, by T4's zero count) carried as that node's base and established by exactly one child-spawning event of T10a's discipline. Hence the map `ev` taking each document to its spawning triple `(parent, spawnPt, spawnParam)` is total and single-valued — every non-root node carries exactly one triple (T10a's tree construction) — and injective: were `ev(d₁) = ev(d₂) = (P, t, k')`, T10a's at-most-once constraint (each `(t, k')` pair yields at most one child) would coincide the two children, hence the two documents `d₁ = d₂`. Therefore **distinct documents arise from distinct allocation events**. The identification of the model's documents with T10a's document-level allocator nodes is a design posit (the document-level counterpart of S7a), not derived within this ASN; given it, the event-distinctness conclusion follows directly from T10a's at-most-once constraint, as derived in the prose above. S7d yields event-distinctness only — lifting it to address-distinctness is GlobalUniqueness's step, consumed by S7's Uniqueness argument.

- *Depends:*
  - T4 (HierarchicalParsing, ASN-0034) — sole definition site of the zero count `zeros(t) = |{i : 1 ≤ i ≤ #t ∧ tᵢ = 0}|`, the function S7d's statement invokes in the parenthetical `zeros = 2` that characterises each document's address as a document-level tumbler (the `N.0.U.0.D` prefix, exactly two field separators present)
  - T10a (AllocatorDiscipline, ASN-0034) — supplies the allocator tree `𝒯` and its child-spawning rule, by which each document is identified with a document-level node carrying exactly one spawning triple `(parent, spawnPt, spawnParam)`; and supplies the at-most-once constraint — each `(t, k')` pair yields at most one child-spawning event — whose contrapositive, applied to two documents sharing a parent, makes the document→allocation-event map injective and so grounds the conclusion that distinct documents arise from distinct allocation events, the event-level separation S7's Uniqueness step feeds to GlobalUniqueness
