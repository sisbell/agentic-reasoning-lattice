# Claim Decompose

Claim-decompose is a producer agent whose primary substrate effect is granting per-claim identity from a confirmed note: emitting `claim` classifiers, label/name sidecars, and `provenance.derivation` per derived claim. Brings the note's content into the substrate as first-class per-claim citizens.

## Triggers

- Operator-invoked, after the source note is confirmed and the operator decides it's ready for derivation.
