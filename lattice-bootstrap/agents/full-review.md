# Full Review

Full-review is a producer agent whose primary substrate effect is a new `review.content` covering an entire ASN's derived claims. Its findings drive downstream tightening of claims across the ASN.

## Triggers

- The ASN is quiescent (no open revises) but not yet confirmed.
- Every derived claim has a Formal Contract.
- No other agent is holding any of the ASN's claims for review.
