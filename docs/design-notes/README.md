# Design Notes

Notes on the system's architecture and operation that aren't patterns. Patterns document observed emergent behavior with a specific solution/forces/structure form (see [patterns/](../patterns/)). Design notes document deliberate architectural choices, aggregate observations about how the system behaves, or cross-cutting principles that don't fit the pattern template.

## Notes

- [Domain Language Emergence](domain-language-emergence.md) — how vocabulary produced by [prose coinage](../patterns/prose-coinage.md) is structured into layers (theory-channel / invented / formalization-level) and narrowed as it moves through the stages.
- [Self-Healing](self-healing.md) — map of where the system could detect and respond to disequilibrium patterns from its own operation, organized by area (cycle health, dependency structure, sprawl, structural integrity, stage transitions). The load-bearing criterion is mechanical-signal availability: LLM-level evaluation is itself subject to [Self-Report Laundering](self-healing.md#observation-layer-limitation) and cannot serve as a self-healing detector.
- [Question Generator Context](question-generator-context.md) — why theory-channel and evidence-channel question generators receive different amounts of corpus context, and how to configure this for a new domain. Downstream of [channel asymmetry](../patterns/channel-asymmetry.md).
- [Campaigns](campaigns.md) — multi-pairing lattices. Campaign = (theory channel, evidence channel, bridge vocab, target). First-class artifact in `lattices/<lattice>/campaigns/<name>/`. ASNs bind via manifest or inherit lattice default.
- [Audit by Content](audit-by-content.md) — design rule for the evaluation layer: evaluators read artifacts, not summaries. Addresses [Self-Report Laundering](self-healing.md#observation-layer-limitation). Enforced in prompts and context configuration at every evaluation site (cone-review, full-review, meta-audit).
- [Claim Document Contract](claim-document-contract.md) — the output contract for claim derivation. What must be true when decomposition finishes (transition-checkable) and what must remain true across every revise (steady-state). Structural invariants enforced by a validator; semantic invariants enforced by review. First instance of the transition-contract resolution for [Uncontracted Representation Change](../equilibrium/uncontracted-representation-change.md).
- [Validate Before Review — Design](validate-before-review.md) — the design decisions behind running a mechanical structural-invariant validator before every review cycle. Why two passes instead of one, why mechanical validation instead of LLM, why per-invariant fix recipes, why the validator runs at every scope of [claim refinement](../protocols/maturation/note-to-claim.md).
- [Production Drive](production-drive.md) — the LLM behavioral force that pushes toward action. Off-ramp categories (OBSERVE in claim refinement review, OUT_OF_SCOPE in discovery) channel this force so observations don't all become mandatory revisions. Calibrated by the REVISE-first discrimination test and the [Voice Principle](../principles/voice.md).
- [Stochastic Quiescence](stochastic-quiescence.md) — how the protocol handles agents whose progress-discipline holds probabilistically rather than deterministically. Substrate quiescence is structural and stable; reviewer quiescence is statistical. Operational response: N-consecutive-CONVERGED gate (n=2 for note-scope, n=1 for per-property claim-scope) at the trigger layer. Closes the [Quiescence](../protocols/substrate/quiescence.md) spec's Open Question on probabilistic progress-discipline without modifying the substrate.
- [Stigmergic Coordination](stigmergic-coordination.md) — how the protocol stack handles agents with overlapping write resources. Two pheromone polarities: attractive (`comment.revise`) calls refiners in to a finding; repellent (`holding`) keeps other agents out while a fire is in flight. Replaces biological time-decay with explicit closure-as-emission for auditability; advisory-lock semantics via `agent.scope.<type>` classifiers and the `is_held` predicate.
- [Substrate Migration Trajectory](substrate-migration-trajectory.md) — how the substrate evolves from filesystem-backed scaffolding to a native link graph. The architectural commitment that guides current design decisions to anticipate the transition; what changes and what stays the same.
