1. **[DEFECT] "Design commitments → Forced," subspace-partition bullet (and its cross-reference at the end of "Conventional"): the claim that the digit values `s_C=1, s_L=2` are "forced by the inc-algebra, not conventional" is false, and the derivation is unsound.** The note pins `s_C=1 ∧ s_L=2` as the *SubspaceConventionAxiom*, explicitly grounded in Green's source (`xanadu.h:144–146`, `granf2.c:162`, `do2.c:94`) — i.e., the values are a convention, not an algebraic necessity. The digest's derivation ("`k₁=2` necessarily introduces digit 1, so `b_C(d)=inc(d,2)=[d.0.1]` and `s_C=1`") has a gap: `k₁=2` forces only that the *first step* lands on `[d.0.1]`; it does not force the content *anchor* — hence `s_C` — to be that tumbler. A conforming chain may open content one sibling higher: `b_C(d)=inc(inc(d,2),0)=[d.0.2]`, chain `(d,[d.0.1],[d.0.2],[d.0.2.1])`, which still satisfies C1c (`k₁=2`, every `#tᵢ>#d`) and L0 (`E(a)₁=2=s_C`) yet yields `s_C=2`. So the digits are not forced; what the algebra actually forces is only their *distinctness* (`s_C≠s_L`, which is what T7 needs for SD). Note the digest contradicts itself: it lists "swap the ordering" as a permitted conventional move, but swapping makes `s_C=2` — so the value can't also be "forced." Fix: keep the values under "Forced" in the digest's *downstream-cannot-violate* sense (it's a hard substrate commitment, like the SequentialTransitionAxiom listed beside it), but characterize the *specific numerals* as Green-grounded convention realized by the anchor construction `b_C=inc(d,2), b_L=inc(b_C,0)`; state that the inc-algebra forces distinctness (load-bearing for SD), not `1` and `2`. Delete "not conventional."

2. **[SHARPENING] "Forced," isolation-TCB bullet / "What must be built → document registry": surface that documents may be *nested*, and that this is exactly where `zeros(d)=2` earns its keep.** The worked example registers `d'=[1,0,2,0,5,3]` under `d=[1,0,2,0,5]` (so `d≺d'`), and Cross-document disjointness proves isolation for the `d₁≺d₂` case *precisely because* `zeros(d₂)=2` forces position `#d₁+1` to be nonzero, diverging from the anchor's separator `0`. Two takeaways: (a) the registry must **not** reject a `d'` that extends an existing document — K.σ's precondition permits it, and a builder might wrongly add a prefix-incomparability check; (b) `zeros(d)=2` is isolation-critical *specifically* in the nested case (when documents are already prefix-incomparable, zeros plays no role) — which makes the digest's TCB point land harder.

3. **[SHARPENING] "Implementation approaches → replay safety": the "re-mint and self-check rather than trust the record" advice applies to `K.α`/`K.λ` only, not `K.σ`.** Content/link addresses are deterministically re-derivable (the chain discipline), so replay can recompute and verify them against the journal; but `K.σ`'s `d` is *caller-supplied and validated*, so it must be recorded as truth and re-applied, not re-derived. Carve this out so a builder doesn't try to re-mint document addresses on replay.
