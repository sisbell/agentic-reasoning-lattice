Strong digest overall — accurate on the forced/conventional split, faithful to the recovery-equation and frontier subtleties, well-grounded in the evidence, and at the right altitude (Green function names are used as references to the proven implementation, not as specifications). The most valuable improvements are below, most-important first.

## Revision list

**1. "What must be built" → Link writer: "Record `home-address ↦ (from, to, type)`" is inaccurate.** The link store keys on the link's *own* freshly-minted address `a` (home-*scoped*: `home(a)=d`, but `a ≠ d`), not on the home document `d`. A document homes a *growing set* of links — the note's entire contracted-home / subsequent-emission machinery exists precisely because of this — so keying the store by `d` would clobber. Fix: "Record `link-address ↦ (from,to,type)`, where the link address is the home-scoped address just minted (`Σ'.L = Σ.L ∪ {a ↦ (e₁,e₂,e₃)}`)."

**2. "What must be built" lists "A discovery capability" as a co-equal build item, diluting the note's sharpest commitment.** The note is emphatic that discovery requires *nothing of MAKELINK beyond writing the endsets* — it is "the standing meaning of having content-identity endsets in `Σ.L`," not an indexing step the operation performs (the "Note what discharges ML9" passage). The first six bullets are MAKELINK-internal; discovery is not. The approaches section recovers this ("index maintenance lives in the discovery subsystem"), but at the place a builder reads *what to build*, the flat list invites exactly the "remember to index" over-engineering the note warns against. Fix: separate the six MAKELINK components from discovery, and mark discovery as a read-side capability the recorded endsets *enable for free* — reinforcing ML9 rather than blurring it.

**3. Endset-resolution: "an ordered map suffices [for the write side]" risks a builder duplicating the single shared arrangement.** The digest correctly identifies `Σ.M(d)` as one shared structure owned by the document subsystem, and correctly notes MAKELINK needs only V→I. But "ordered map suffices" describes MAKELINK's *demand*, not a recommendation for the shared structure: FOLLOWLINK needs I→V over the *same* `Σ.M(d)`, so the bidirectional representation is effectively settled by the read side and MAKELINK just consumes its V→I direction. As phrased, a reader could stand up a write-side V→I map *beside* the read-side I→V structure — duplicating authoritative state, the Lampson anti-pattern the rest of the digest is careful to avoid. Fix: state plainly that MAKELINK imposes no representation of its own and inherits whatever bidirectional structure the read side already requires; "ordered map" is the floor of its *requirement*, not a structure to build.

**4. Identity-allocation parenthetical "(append-at-extent / scan for the previous link)" mis-attributes Green's mechanism and softly blurs a distinction the note guards.** "Append-at-extent" (`vspanreach`) is Green's *seat* mechanism (V-position, arrangement-keyed) — exactly what the digest correctly cites later under home-seating. Green's *identity* I-address is store/granfilade-keyed: scan the highest existing homed link I-address, increment (Q11). Lumping the seat mechanism into the identity paragraph muddies the address-selector (store-keyed) vs. seat-selector (arrangement-keyed) split — the very split the note shows *diverges* at a contracted home and warns against collapsing. Fix: cite only "scan the previous homed link's I-address + increment" for identity; reserve "append-at-extent" for seating. (Minor accuracy nit, but it touches a load-bearing distinction the digest otherwise gets right.)

**5. Speculative target-repo filenames (`links.jsonl`, `paths.json`) add risk without design value.** The recommendations they decorate — append-only link journal, single-WAL-record atomicity, per-home counter as a recomputable hint — are sound and self-supporting. Even flagged, the filename guesses invite a builder to trust files that may not exist or may not carry the inferred semantics. Drop them, or demote to "(whatever append-only store the repo provides)" without naming.

**6. (Minor) "confirms decomposition is unobservable to the coverage-determined views" overstates the inference.** Green's deterministic-but-non-canonical fragmentation is *consistent with* decomposition being invisible to the coverage-determined observables; it doesn't *confirm* it (the note establishes that from L8/LP12/LP21 being functions of coverage). Reword to "consistent with."
