This is false on two counts. `A_new = {shift(a,0),…,shift(a,n−1)}`, so the block's terminus is `shift(a, n−1)` — and that terminus *is* the frontier, not beyond it. The note states this explicitly ("the block's I-terminus `shift(a, n−1)`") and separately identifies `shift(a, n)` as "the address immediately following the allocated run … beyond the frontier" — i.e., the *would-be merge target*, not the terminus. Rewrite: a forward merge would require the shifted-suffix head `M(d)(q_J)` to be I-adjacent to the terminus `shift(a, n−1)`, i.e., to equal `shift(a, n)`; that address lies just beyond the frontier and is not in the store, while the suffix head is — so they cannot coincide, and the right neighbor need not be checked. The conclusion survives, but the digest ships a false identity for the terminus.
