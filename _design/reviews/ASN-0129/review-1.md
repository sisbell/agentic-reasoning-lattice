Below is the revision list, most-important first. The digest is strong and unusually disciplined about grounding its Green claims in the evidence channel — the defects are concentrated in a few accuracy/completeness points, not in unsound approaches.

**1. Design commitments ("expressive ceiling") and "How it fits" — the reach/self-emit exclusions are stated as settled facts when the note marks them conjectures.** The digest lists "transitive closure, self-emit, and mutually-recursive definitions are deliberately *outside*," and says reachability/self-emit are "handed out to agent-time computation … not built into the substrate." The word "conjecture" appears nowhere in the digest, yet the note splits these sharply: mutual recursion and the *absence of a closure operator* are a grammar fact (PC6a, proven), but reach- and self-emit-*inexpressibility* are **conjectures** (C-reach, C-emit) the note "deliberately [does] not promote to theorems," with OQ6 and a third conjecture (PC6's parity candidate) devoted to the missing invariance proofs. This is the note's signature epistemic move and the digest flattens it. Fix: separate the proven (PC6 ceiling = PL is a theorem; PC6a ships no closure operator) from the conjectured ("no PL term computes reach/self-emit"). The *design stances the builder acts on* — reach unrolled at agent time, self-emit checked on the emitting surface — are settled regardless and need only the grammar fact, so the build is unaffected; but a reader must not treat inexpressibility as proven. (Note explicitly that this does *not* weaken "safety comes from inexpressibility": that framing rests on the proven PC4/PC5/PC6a/structural-reads, not on C-reach/C-emit — say so, or the two get conflated.)

**2. "What must be built → view machinery" and "Implementation approaches → Views" — the view machinery omits the fixed-view vs view-parameterized split, which the evaluator must get right.** The digest reduces views to "three lenses, active computed as audit-minus-nullified, default-view rewrite elementwise." But PC3/V-AUD commit that only `members`/`targets_of`/`is_K`/`M_K` rebind with the term view; `A_K`/`L_K` are fixed (active/audit by *name*, at every term view), and **the behavior atoms always read the active slice and have no audit reading** — at term-view = `audit`, `succs`/`tip`/`age`/… still read active state. A builder told only "apply the lens" will pass the term view to every atom and compute wrong values (and try to read a non-existent audit reading for behavior atoms). Add the view-parameterized-vs-fixed-view distinction to the view-machinery component and the evaluator. Also surface the note's corollary (PC3): cross-view readings are reconstructible *within* a term through the fixed-view slices (`⋃(L_K, addrs_F)` rebuilds audit `members` inside an active term), so per-atom view parameters are an OQ1 deferral, not a missing capability — the builder should not invent one.

**3. "Make the common case fast" + dynamics certifier — the headline footprint-dispatch optimization is sound only if FP's cross-type footprints are honored; the digest under-flags this.** The digest elevates footprint-indexed re-evaluation to "the single biggest evaluation-cost win" and says the footprint is "union over atoms, per FP" — technically correct, but it omits the precise warning the note ends PD2 with ("Name the exceptions or be surprised by them"). A naive per-type-slice footprint *under-approximates* for exactly the terms that break the optimization: BH4 atoms read home-wide (any same-home deposit of any type perturbs `age`/`stale`), `targets_keyed` reads every BH3-attached Binary slice, active-view terms are perturbed by `[R]`-retractions of any type, and a default-view collection term carries every BH1 slice (FP's default-view increment). Miss any of these and the dispatcher skips a trigger that did change → stale termination gate. Make the cross-type footprint cases an explicit soundness precondition where the optimization is recommended, not just a citation in the certifier section.

**4. "Guarantees to uphold → expressive ceiling" — "forfeits … termination" mis-states what a feedback breach costs and contradicts the note's own OQ4.** The digest says adding feedback "forfeits the ceiling, termination, and the dynamics theory." The unconditional casualties are the ceiling (PC6 is asserted for syntax-directed evaluation only) and the dynamics theory (PD0–PD2 classify *syntactic* terms). Termination is *not* an automatic casualty: PC6's own `reach` loop "halts and answers from base reads alone" over the finite substrate, and OQ4 is precisely the project of adding a *bounded* fixpoint operator with an explicit measure that *keeps* PC5. Reword: a feedback breach forfeits the ceiling and the dynamics theory and *risks* non-termination absent a measure — the measure being exactly what OQ4's bounded-fixpoint successor would supply (which the digest already references under "Decisions for the builder," so this also fixes an internal tension).

**5. "Implementation approaches → The read base (Recovery)" — the `is_doc`/`dom(Σ.C)` inference conflates two distinct Green facts.** The digest writes that the append-only "address exists ⇒ content exists" invariant "is exactly why `is_doc` is membership-only and `dom(Σ.C)` has no read." Those have *different* groundings. `dom(Σ.C)`-no-read follows from the no-content-existence-check evidence (Green never queries the permascroll on the read/gating path — Q1). But `is_doc`-membership-*only* (no enumeration) follows from the *separate* point-lookup / no-document-census evidence (granfilade `findorgl` is a point lookup; no read enumerates document keys — Q1, document-enumeration). The digest does cite the point-lookup fact in the next clause, so the information is present; just retarget the "this is exactly why" so the content invariant grounds the content-domain exclusion and the point-lookup/no-census grounds the membership-only residence. (Both are well-supported by the evidence — only the attribution is crossed.)

**6. "Implementation approaches → The read base" — the `links.jsonl` repo claim is presented as established but is grounded in none of the review materials.** "this repo's own substrate (an append-only `links.jsonl` journal with a small registry, recovered by replay)" is a specific source-level claim not in the note, the evidence, or documented Green structure. The append-only-journal recommendation already stands on the note's grow-only property (PD0) plus Green's append-only granfilade (evidence) — so the repo reference is decorative. Either cite where the repo substrate is described or soften it to an as-suggested-by framing so the recommendation doesn't appear to derive force from an unverifiable claim.

**7. Smaller sharpenings.**
- *Term representation / type checker:* surface QD-refl's reflection restriction (only *address-valued* domains reflect to terms; tuple-valued `A_K`/`L_K` are domains-only, `Reg` is quantification-plus-`count(Reg)`-only) and `Reg`'s prohibition on filtering/folding/class-equality. These are what the checker enforces; stating them prevents a builder from generalizing reflection.
- *Design commitment "atomic vocabulary":* "a pure function of the registration records (shape, idempotency, attached behaviors)" lists idempotency among the determinants, but the note is explicit that **`idem` contributes no read atom** (it is emit-side; its sole read-side consequence, I1a's at-most-one-active, is a state property, not a predicate former). The generator branches on shape and behaviors only — say so (the "What must be built" component description already correctly omits idem).
- *Design commitment "Conventional, not forced":* calling UV's rewrite "conventional" understates the note, which argues its collections-only *shape* is forced by the layer-confusion-avoidance principle (a rewrite touching verdicts/membership/traversal would let a presentation mark change query topology). The *per-codomain choice* is this note's, but its shape is principled, not free.
- *Misc:* "pure, total functions of state (PC4)" — totality is PC5 + the guard discipline, not PC4 (purity); where result memoization is discussed, note that OQ3 leaves extensional equivalence undecided, so a memo cache can key only on *syntactic* identity + state-version, not semantic equality; and `im`'s persistent maps/sets pins a specific crate — keep it at "persistent/immutable structures with structural sharing."

**Genuinely solid, leave alone:** the forced-vs-conventional reading of AM-keying and BH3-opt-in as *naming* conventions ("the atom, never the question"), backed precisely by the Green symmetric-index evidence; the span-arithmetic-inside-the-leaf approach (note + evidence both confirm `tumbleradd`-then-`tumblercmp` and home read from the stored record); the append-only-journal-plus-indexes-as-hints model (faithful to grow-only and to cache/hint-not-duplicate); the immutable-snapshot concurrency story from PC4; and the sound-but-incomplete spelling-level certifier. No proposed approach violates a note commitment.
