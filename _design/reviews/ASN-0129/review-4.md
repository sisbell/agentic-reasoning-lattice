## Revision list

**1. [DEFECT] — "What must be built / A representation … & A type checker": the domain grammar drops one of QD's two closure formations.** The digest enumerates domain forms as bases, filters, and "reflected domains (address-valued domains only)" (QD-refl, the domain→term direction), and the type-checker bullet states only that direction. It omits QD's *second* closure formation — **every `℘_fin(T)`-valued PL term is itself a domain expression** (the term→domain direction; WT's rule "a `℘_fin(T)`-valued term is a domain at `dom(T)`"). This is load-bearing, and the note says so in as many words: "Without this closure, aggregation would reach the bases but not the algebra's own set-valued results: 'x targets at most n addresses' would be inexpressible." A builder who implements only the listed forms builds a language that cannot type `count(targets_of(x))` or `out_capped` (count/quantify need a *domain*; an atom's set result or a fold result is only a *term* without this rule). Add the formation to the term/domain representation and the matching type-checker rule. ("Mutually recursive QD/PL" gestures at it, but the explicit "what to build" list contradicts the gesture and is what a builder will follow.)

**2. [DEFECT] — "Design commitments / The expressive ceiling": the conjectures are called "weaker" than the grammar fact; they are *stronger*.** The digest: "the grammar ships no fixpoint … (PC6a, **proven**). That *no PL term computes* transitive closure or the self-emit test is a different and **weaker** claim — a **conjecture**." This inverts the logic. PC6a (no explicit fixpoint former / no direct spelling) is the *weaker, easy* claim; C-reach/C-emit (no term computes reach/self-emit by **any** encoding) are *stronger* — C-reach would *imply* the grammar-fact conclusion (a fixpoint former would compute reach) and additionally rule out clever counting/order encodings, which is precisely why they are left unproven while the trivial grammar fact is discharged. The note is explicit: "What stands without it: PL *ships* no closure operator (PC6a, a grammar fact)" — i.e., the grammar fact is the weaker residual. This is also in tension with the digest's own (correct) "How it fits" framing ("rests on the grammar fact alone, independent of whether they are PL-inexpressible"). Keep "different"; change "weaker" to "stronger" and say why (it implies the grammar fact and rules out indirect encodings besides).

**3. [SHARPENING] — "Design commitments / Conventional, not forced": the default-view forced/conventional split is self-contradictory.** It lists "the *per-codomain specifics* of the default-view rewrite" as conventional, then immediately says "its collections-*only* shape is forced." But the per-codomain behavior (collections rewritten; verdicts/Booleans/traversal/membership not) *is* the collections-only shape — it is derived from the layer-boundary principle the digest itself cites as forcing it. Little here is genuinely conventional. Tighten to: the rewrite's content is forced by the collections-only/layer-boundary principle (UV "extends ASN-0128's committed rule rather than inventing a second"); what is at most conventional is inherited from 0128 (that a presentation-filtering default view exists at all), not chosen here.

**4. [SHARPENING] — "Guarantees / The expressive ceiling": present granularity as an extension axis, not only a prohibition.** The digest gives a principled extension path for the vocabulary axis (V-PRIM-style admission) and the control axis (OQ4's bounded-fixpoint-with-measure), but frames granularity purely as "do not expose span arithmetic." The note offers a principled granularity extension too — *paired admission* ("granting the operation to V-PRIM and the base together so both sides of the equality move at once"). Add it, so all three axes are calibrated consistently with the note's "calibrate extensions by axis" theme.

**5. [SHARPENING] — "What must be built / An evaluator": aggregates are single-pass, not short-circuiting.** "folds to short-circuiting quantifier/aggregate passes" overstates: only the quantifiers (∃ at first witness, ∀ at first counterexample) short-circuit; `count` and `⋃` are single-pass-must-visit-all. Reword to "short-circuiting for quantifiers, full single-pass for count/union-fold."

Genuinely solid, leave alone: the **footprint-indexed re-evaluation** approach (correctly makes the FP cross-type cases — BH4 home-wide, `targets_keyed` over every BH3 Binary slice, active-view `[R]`-sensitivity, default-view BH1 increment — a *soundness* precondition, not decoration); the **V-TUP two-regimes asymmetry** (projections irreducible, coverage tests derivable from `addrs`+`≼`, admitted only to keep span arithmetic in the leaf); and **append-only-journal + indexes-as-hints + MVCC snapshot**, all correctly grounded in the Green evidence (intersection matching, symmetric reverse index with no per-type opt-in, point-lookup store, append-only occupancy invariant) without drifting into uncheckable source-level claims.
