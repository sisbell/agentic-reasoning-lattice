Skeptical read complete. The digest is, overall, strong and unusually well-grounded — it reads green's evidence *through* its misleading synthesis prose (correctly mapping green's RETRIEVEENDSETS to the note's `RE_clip` touching-spans reading, not the adopted whole-endset one), catches the 3-band/arity completeness trap, separates the monotone index from the addressability filter cleanly, and stays at design altitude throughout. The Lampson hint/journal-replay framing is apt and correctly applied. But it ships two factually wrong statements, one of which contradicts the digest itself.

## Revision list

**[DEFECT] — "Implementation approaches → Addressability as a separate live-set filter": "only retraction can remove a pair" is false and self-contradictory.** Content-subspace contraction (`K.μ⁻`), reorder (`K.μ~`), and shift-delete all remove pairs by shrinking or swinging the image — the digest's *own* caching section lists them as movers and invokes RE-CWP's "RE is monotone-decreasing under contraction." The note is explicit that retraction is only the removal channel *via the addressable population*, "whereas a content-subspace `K.μ⁻` removes through the image instead." Fix: scope it — "among link-store (`K.λ`) changes only retraction removes a pair (via the addressable population); image-side movers (content contraction, reorder, shift-delete) remove pairs through the image — see caching." As written a builder doing incremental cache maintenance could under-handle removal.

**[DEFECT] — "Implementation approaches → Caching by region cover": "the note proves no arrangement restriction recovers it" misreads the RE-UDIST-∩ result.** The note proves no *injectivity-style* restriction recovers `⊇`, and states the obstruction is "injectivity-proof, **not** restriction-proof" — a degenerate arrangement too impoverished to admit two disjoint regions with distinct nonempty images *does* force `⊇` (vacuously). The builder takeaway (never compose by intersection) survives, but the stated result is wrong. Fix: "no *injectivity-style* restriction recovers it (injectivity-proof, not restriction-proof; only a degenerately impoverished arrangement forces `⊇`, and vacuously) — so a real cache must never compose by intersection." Optionally cite OQ4 (the weakest structurally-restricted *sufficient* condition).

**[SHARPENING] — "Guarantees → Present-tense correctness": scope the green comparison to selection.** "gate the answer ... exactly as green's POOM gates input *and output*" is imprecise for the content-identity primitive: it gates only *selection* (which endsets touch, via the current image); the returned endset *values* are permanent and arrangement-independent (RE-IDENT). Green's *output*-gating (silently dropping unarranged I-addresses) is the *rendered* mode (OQ3) — a builder who output-gates the content-identity answer would violate RE-IDENT. Restrict the comparison to input/selection-gating.

**[SHARPENING] — "Design commitments": disambiguate the retraction-disjointness attribution.** Saying the "retraction from-set/to-set disjointness and the net-removal-only result ... hold because the image is content" risks contradicting the (correct) "content-disjoint *unconditionally*" stated later. Precisely: the from-set (∅) and unit-depth to-set are content-disjoint *unconditionally* (RE-NCD); what rests on `I ⊆ dom(Σ.C)` is their *non-touching of the region*; and net-removal-only *additionally* requires the Θ-discipline (`coverage(Θ) ∩ dom(Σ.C) = ∅`, OQ6), not just a content image.

**[SHARPENING] — "Stability/Decisions": flag the conservative-lift as an assumption.** The digest treats ASN-0082 shift-based insert/delete as established image-only movers but doesn't mark that this rests on the note's *conservative-lift modelling assumption* (shift-based insert/delete touch no store but `Σ.M(d)`). Since the digest flags its other standing assumptions (Θ-discipline; "every retraction is a Nullify"), flag this one too — and note the builder must ensure their insert/delete primitives actually frame `Σ.L/Σ.E/Σ.R` for RE's stability under them to hold.

**[SHARPENING] — "Design commitments → dedup": "provisional" overstates it.** Dedup is the *committed* behavior (baked into RE-DEF as a set; RE-UNIT is "introduced," not "provisional"); OQ2 *questions* whether collapsing is faithful but does not leave it uncommitted the way OQ1 leaves RE-WHOLE ("provisional"). Distinguish "committed-but-OQ2-questioned" from "explicitly provisional."

**[SHARPENING] — "Implementation approaches → scan (Option A)": state RE-FIN's computability precondition.** Finiteness is unconditional, but *computability* needs `W` finitely presented (region membership decidable, e.g. `W` given as finitely many spans). Always met in practice (green takes the region as a specset of spans), but it is the note's stated hypothesis and deserves a clause.

Genuinely solid and needing no change: the "one index, two readers" decomposition; the second-fetch caveat for whole-endset surfacing atop a span index; the 3-band arity completeness trap; the touching-spans-vs-whole-endset OQ1 reading of green (and the "verify against a discontiguous endset" caution); the content-identity-vs-rendered separation; the incremental-refcount insight; and the Θ unit-depth content-disjoint-subspace seating recommendation (which is correctly *sharper* than the formal claim's "element-level subspace").
