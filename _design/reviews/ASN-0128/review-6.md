## Revision list

**1. [DEFECT] — "coverage class" is used with two incompatible meanings, making a load-bearing guarantee false-as-written.** The note *defines* "coverage class" as the registry key — the type itself (the `~`-class of a representative endset) — and the digest uses it correctly that way in *What must be built* ("coverage-class keys pairwise distinct"). But in *Guarantees to uphold*, *Concurrency and recovery*, and *Decisions for the builder* the digest reuses "coverage class"/"coverage-class" to mean the **I0-class** (the coverage-pair equivalence *within* a type). Under the note's vocabulary, the guarantee heading "**At most one active tuple per coverage class (idempotent types)**" reads as "at most one active tuple per *type*," which is plainly false — an idempotent type holds many active tuples with distinct coverage pairs (the digest's own example, `fanout` idem=⊤ Multi, can hold `(a_src1,…)` and `(a_src2,…)` simultaneously). The note's actual guarantee (I1a) is "at most one per **I0-class**." The intent is recoverable from the parentheticals ("same I0-class → same shard"), but the statement is false as written and internally inconsistent with the digest's own correct usage. **Fix:** rename the dedup-equivalence concept to "I0-class" (or "coverage-pair class") everywhere it denotes coverage-pair equivalence — the guarantee heading, "ordered per coverage class," "coverage-class-partitioned," "same-class emits," and "partition it by coverage-class … never by home" — and reserve "coverage class" for the type/registry key. This also sharpens the partition instruction: partition the idem-⊤ check-and-deposit by **I0-class** (same coverage-pair-within-a-type → same shard), never by home.

**2. [SHARPENING] — "which three types ship" is mis-split on the forced/conventional axis.** *Design commitments → Conventional, not forced* lists "which three types ship (a substrate parameter)." That under-applies the same forced/conventional split the digest handles well for the slot direction. The note *forces* that the three lifecycle/supersession/retraction universals ship pre-registered (this is its resolution of ASN-0126 OQ4, which left open whether *anything* ships) and fixes their registrations (S1–S3, shapes/idem/behaviors); only their **representative endsets / reserved type-addresses** are the substrate parameter (the `s_C`/`s_L` analogy the digest cites points at addresses, not roles). Reword to: *that* the three universals ship and *with these registrations* is forced; *which addresses* they occupy is the parameter — mirroring "*that* you must fix a direction is forced; *which* you pick is convention."

**3. [SHARPENING] — two builder-relevant deferrals are omitted.** The digest names most open questions but skips (a) **audit-view chain walking** (OQ6 — BH2 walks the active view only, so a nullified mid-chain tuple breaks the chain; whether an audit-view `chain()`/`tip()` variant is a substrate obligation or app-side reconstruction is open) and (b) **multi-app registry composition** (OQ8 — the registry builder is described as taking a single declaration set; how several apps' declarations merge and how an app-vs-substrate key collision resolves is unspecified). One line each would close the picture for a builder who will hit both.
