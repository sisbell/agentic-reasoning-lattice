It's a strong, well-grounded digest — the "Forced" commitments are accurate, the absolute-vs-implicit V-position sub-choice with the μ-term-as-trap is a genuinely valuable and correct insight, and the by-construction-vs-active-enforcement guarantee taxonomy is clean. The defects below are concentrated in one paragraph (the depth-2 discussion) plus a few sharpenings.

## Revision list

**1. Design commitments → Conventional, "Fixing m_1 = 2": the bucket label misclassifies an imposed scope boundary as a soft framing choice.** The header reads "Conventional — natural framing, not forced," but the note *imposes* depth 2 as an explicit scope restriction ("documents with m_1 > 2 are outside the scope of this ASN"), and CS4 makes depth-2 cuts mandatory — a 0084-conformant builder *must* assume it, and the singleton-tumbler/prefix-sum apparatus the digest recommends depends on it. That is qualitatively different from the *other* conventional item ("represent M(d) as the run partition"), which a builder genuinely can swap. Filing both under "not forced" flattens the distinction between *what you may freely deviate from* and *what you may rely on but is a hard boundary*; a skimming builder is told to under-weight depth 2. Fix: give m_1 = 2 a distinct status — *forced as a scope assumption you may rely on; the foundation permits deeper, so extending past 0084 is what revisits it.* The prose already says exactly this; make the classification match it.

**2. Same item, grounding: "text content is fixed at depth 2 by `findisatoinsertmolecule`" cites the wrong axis.** Per the evidence, `findisatoinsertmolecule` fixes the *I-address* (granfilade ISA) of a text atom — placing it at a hardcoded offset of 2 *below the document ISA*, yielding a depth-7 address like `1.1.0.1.0.1.1`. That is the content-address axis, not the V-position depth the note's `m_1 = 2` is about. The note's `m_1 = 2` means *text V-positions are depth-2 tumblers* `[1, k]`; the evidence that grounds *that* is the POOM V-space layout (text at `V = 1.x`; `setlinkvsas`/`findnextlinkvsa` establish depth-2 V-addresses `1.1`/`2.1`/`3.1`). Re-cite to the V-space-layout evidence and drop the `findisatoinsertmolecule` attribution (or keep it only if explicitly relabeled as the *separate* I-address confirmation).

**3. Implementation approaches → Canonicalization: the run-count bound `|Δ| ≤ n` is offered as usable but its load-bearing step is asserted, not shown.** The digest says this is "enough to size storage against" and that it answers the note's open question on run-count increase — but the "no cascade" claim (new merges occur *only* at the ≤ n region seams) carries the whole bound and is stated in one clause. Since a builder is invited to provision on it, make it explicit: (a) region interiors were canonical pre-state and a *uniform* per-region translation preserves non-adjacency, so no interior pair newly merges; (b) a seam merge's product inherits the outer endpoints of its two inputs, which were already region-boundary positions, so it creates no new *non-seam* adjacency — hence `#seam-merges ≤ #seams = n`. Either spell this out or downgrade the bound to a sketched conjecture; as written it reads more proven than shown.

**4. Implementation approaches → arrangement map / Decisions for the builder: "implicit positions" and "subspace storage" are presented as independent knobs but are coupled.** An implicit (prefix-sum) position axis is *per-subspace* — the clean `1..N` ordinal range that lets a "position" be a prefix sum exists only within one subspace; a single unified rope cannot carry implicit positions cleanly across the `1.x`/`2.x` boundary. Surface the coupling: the recommended implicit-position model pairs naturally with per-subspace sequences, and that same pairing is what *delivers* subspace-confinement-by-construction. These two recommendations reinforce each other rather than being orthogonal; saying so strengthens both.

**5. (Minor) How it fits → reverse-index point is correct and valuable; soften one phrase.** The insight that a content-*membership* index (find-documents/DOCISPAN) is invariant under rearrange because `ran(M') = ran(M)`, while only a V-position-recording index needs π-driven update, is sound. "Retroactively justifies Green's commented-out index update" states a design inference as a verdict on Green's code; "is consistent with / explains why Green could leave it out" is more honest, and keep the V-position caveat you already have.

**6. (No change needed) Altitude — naming `im::Vector`/RRB-vector** brushes code level but is hedged as one swappable instantiation and matches the project's decided stack; the abstract contract ("a persistent ordered sequence with O(log n) split/concat and structural sharing") is correctly the headline.
