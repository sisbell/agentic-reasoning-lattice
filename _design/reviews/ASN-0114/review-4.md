I reviewed the digest against ASN-0114, its formal claims, and the Q11–Q20 evidence. It is a strong, well-grounded digest — the domain-guard/F7 engineering and the resolution de-bundling argument are excellent. One factual error blocks convergence.

## Revision list

**1. [DEFECT] — "Guarantees to uphold," F6 / *what the result discloses*: "ghost (L9)" is wrongly listed among addresses that "name no document."** The digest writes "node-level (`zeros=0`), user-level (`zeros=1`), ghost (L9), and non-T4-valid interior tumblers … name no document." That is false and misreads the note. Whether a covered address discloses a home document is *purely structural* — it discloses one iff it is T4-valid and document-bearing (`zeros(t) ≥ 2`, with `N`/`U`/`D` defined). Ghost-ness is *content-absence* (L9 / F8 territory) and is orthogonal: a ghost target sitting at a document-bearing address still discloses its home document, because the field projections read off the *address*, not the content. The note cites L9/ghost only as justification for *arbitrary targeting* ("L4 … may reference any addresses, and L9 permits ghost targets, **so** a covered address may sit at node level …") — its "names no document" set is exactly the three structural cases. **Fix:** drop "ghost (L9)" from the enumeration, leaving `{zeros=0, zeros=1, non-T4-valid interior}`; optionally add one clause that content-absence (ghost) is an F8 matter, irrelevant to disclosure, and must not be conflated with structural non-document-bearing-ness. (This is the spot the note flags as needing precise statement, so shipping a wrong category here matters even though the "document-bearing slice only" takeaway survives.)

**2. [SHARPENING] — "Implementation approaches → Link lookup": qualify "safely lock-free under a concurrent appending writer."** Monotonicity of the *logical* address→link map does not by itself make a *physical* read safe without synchronization — a mutable structure mid-resize can tear even when no entry is overwritten (the exact failure mode this project hit on append-only `paths.json`). Lock-free reads require the store to *publish* entries atomically via an immutable/persistent representation; that property is inherited from ASN-0093's representation choice (which the link's immutability invites), not a logical consequence of "monotone." Either attribute the lock-freedom to the store's representation, or add the atomic-publication qualifier — the digest itself defers the store's shape to ASN-0093, so it shouldn't assert a shape-dependent safety property as if monotonicity forced it.

**3. [SHARPENING] — "How it fits" dependency list: add ASN-0047.** F4 (PureRead) ranges over all five components `(Σ.C, Σ.L, Σ.E, Σ.M, Σ.R)` — the state model of ASN-0047. The digest enumerates the five components in the F4 commitment but omits ASN-0047 from the leans-on list; add it so the builder knows where the frame's component inventory is defined.

**Solid, leave as-is:** the domain-guard/F7 section (lower-bound-at-boundary vs upper-bound-post-lookup; derive emptiness in the core from the endset, never from a downstream null; copy RETRIEVEENDSETS' empty-handling rather than the followlink path's), the resolution de-bundling argument and its Q11/Q15/Q19 grounding (correctly separated from the F7 empty-*recorded* bug), the forced/conventional split, and the verbatim-emission reasoning (F1-by-construction, F2-follows-from-F1, Q13 placed correctly at the representation level with the raw-I-span copy chain flagged as the *exceptional* path). All accurate and actionable.
