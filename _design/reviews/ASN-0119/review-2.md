Below is the revision list, ordered most-important first. Real accuracy/grounding defects lead; genuine sharpenings follow. I flag the few places the digest is genuinely strong at the end rather than inventing changes there.

---

**1. "Atomic commit and durable recovery" + Decision 2 — the journal-intent rationale misreads the note's state model and rests on a false tradeoff.** The note's `Σ = (C, L, E, M, R)` treats the arrangement `M(d)` as *authoritative mutable state* — "the record of how content is currently ordered" — and OQ5 says explicitly that REARRANGE "records only the new V→I mapping" (the operation log is *not* in the permanent record; that is *why* the prior order is "no longer expressed"). So calling the arrangement "a hint" and saying intent-journaling "matches the Istream philosophy (… order is a derived view)" inverts the note: the Istream is *content* (`C`); order is recorded state, not derived from operations. Separately, "journaling the effect … throws history away / loses history" is false for an *append-only* effect log, which retains every prior map and recovers prior arrangements *more* directly than replay. Keep the recommendation if you wish, but re-ground it: event-sourcing the arrangement is a *design choice* that makes the operation-log authoritative (with real costs — replay from a base arrangement plus the content store, plus checkpointing), and its genuine edge over effect-journaling is *small, self-validating entries*, not history. Bring the approaches paragraph in line with your own (more honest) Decision-2 caveat that the spec-level OQ5 guarantee remains open.

**2. Decision 7 (Concurrency) and the two "(RA8b)" tags — RA8b is misapplied.** RA8b is about realizing *one* transposition as two sub-moves and exhibiting an intermediate `≠` both endpoints; it says nothing about two *distinct* rearranges. So "what is settled is that their intermediate states always differ (RA8b)" in the concurrency decision is the wrong claim about the wrong scenario — drop it. What the note actually settles is **RA8a** (a *fixed* `π`'s final arrangement is realization-independent); whether two *distinct* rearranges commute is exactly OQ2 and is open — and that alone justifies "pick a serialization story." Likewise, the no-observable-intermediate property you (correctly) get from the atomic root swap is the **atomicity of the imported primitive**, not RA8b — RA8a/RA8b are the formal treatment of the *rejected* move-then-move realization. Re-cite: atomicity (no intermediate) + RA8a (same final state) for the root-swap argument; OQ2 (open) + RA8a for concurrency.

**3. Cut/region resolver and the "Cut boundary-hood" guarantee — "cuts on run boundaries / reject non-boundary cuts" conflates two granularities.** In the model a cut is a *position* boundary (a depth-2 `s_C` coordinate between two active positions), always valid within the active run; it is *not* required to fall on a *run-decomposition* boundary. The implementation *creates* the boundary by splitting the run at the cut (exactly Green's `makecutsnd`, Q16) — there is no "non-boundary cut" to reject. Reframe boundary-hood as "split runs at cut points"; the only rejections are genuine R-PRE failures (interval out of active range, non-strict ascent, run shorter than the minimum interval). As written it could push a builder toward aligning cuts to run structure or rejecting otherwise-legal cuts.

**4. "The arrangement store" — drop the Rust/`im` commitment (altitude).** The note is language-agnostic; "an `im`-style persistent vector" and "from-scratch Rust implementation" silently narrow to one language and name a specific crate. Say "a structurally-shared (persistent) ordered sequence / ordered map" and leave the language to the builder. The rest of this section is at the right altitude.

**5. Design commitment "a tiling, not per-region displacement arithmetic" overstates — restore the "uniform/naive" qualifier.** The forced thing is reaching the *target arrangement* (which tiles); correct per-region offsets *derived from the tiling* realize it fine — as your own approaches section says (`β: −(w_α+w_μ)`, `μ: w_β−w_α`, `α: w_β+w_μ`), and as ASN-0084's postconditions themselves are displacement equations. What the note forbids is the *uniform* offset (Green's `cut2−cut0` for α). Restate as "the post-state is the specific target arrangement given by the postconditions; a *uniform* per-region offset does not in general realize it," so the commitment and the approaches section agree.

**6. Surface that the permutation is cut-determined, not content-inferred — the value-degenerate case (completeness).** The note makes a point of it: when `M(d)` is non-injective (an interval all mapping to one I-address — legal shared content, S5), the equation `M'∘π = M` has many solutions, the identity among them, and REARRANGE is legal with `π ≠ id` yet `M'(d) = M(d)`. The builder takeaways your digest implies but never states: (a) accept the operation on cut geometry alone — do not gate on "did content actually move"; (b) compute `π` from the cuts, never by diffing before/after content. Your tile-by-placement already does (b) correctly; one line naming the reason would close the loop and reinforce your (good) value-blind-canonicalization argument.

**7. Distinguish the link *subspace* of `M(d)` from the link *store* `L` (completeness, minor).** Frame discipline and "link survival" lean on RA6 (store `L` frozen), but the note also relies on **R-NS** pinning the document's *own* link-anchor positions (the `s_L` slice of `M(d)`) pointwise — a different freeze from the store. For a builder who keeps text and link anchors in one arrangement, the obligation is "operate only on the text interval; leave `s_L` entries fixed." One clause keeps the two freezes from blurring.

**Genuinely solid — leave alone:** the *frame-by-unreachability* argument (the right Lampson move, well-grounded in Q20 — a stronger and cheaper guarantee than post-hoc frame checks); the explicit *content-dedup foreclosure* (runs coalesce on I-address contiguity, never value equality, justified by S4 OriginBasedIdentity and the value-blindness of R-PRE — exactly the soundness trap to avoid, handled with rationale rather than a rule); the *link-footprint resolution* design (I-keyed index, query-time projection through the live arrangement, fragmentation treated as correct, discoverability answered without touching V via LP12); and the grounding discipline throughout — every Green source-level claim carries a Q-citation, with no fabricated function-level behavior.
