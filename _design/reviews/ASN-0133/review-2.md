## Revision list

1. **[DEFECT] Scope feature — the digest omits Q9 and its sole precondition.** The note makes *S-monotonicity* "the one standing constraint" on scope-body declarations (S occurring only positively) and devotes a full lemma, Q9, to the consequence: quiescence is anti-monotone in scope — `S' ⟹ S` gives `quiescent_S ⟹ quiescent_{S'}` (global done ⟹ every scoped done; converse fails). The digest builds a scoped quiescence detector, lists scope bodies as choices, and even quotes the note's "the relation is the rule's to declare" framing implicitly — but states neither the constraint nor the guarantee. A builder declaring a *custom* non-monotone body (which the note explicitly enables) silently inverts the nesting, and the useful global⟹scope inference is unavailable. **Do:** add Q9 to "Guarantees to uphold," state the S-monotonicity constraint where scope bodies are introduced ("What must be built"), and add "choice of scope body + S-monotonicity" to "Decisions for the builder."

2. **[SHARPENING] "Domain enumeration": "The domain source is the grow-only audit-slice log" is imprecise.** The grow-only log is the *storage* base; a *domain* may be an active-slice view (non-grow-only) — the note's worked-example producer domain `{t ∈ M_tgt : is_attn(t)}` is read at the active view and is explicitly *not* grow-only. Taken literally the sentence is in tension with the digest's own grow-only/non-grow-only distinction in the scheduler section. The evaluation section already establishes "active = audit-minus-nullified, layered filters over the audit base," so the fix is one clause: enumerate a domain by scanning the grow-only log of its type *and applying the view filter* (active domains shrink under nullification).

3. **[SHARPENING] Guarantee "Recognizability … always decidable by construction (Q0)" is unconditional only for inline triggers.** For pdef triggers Q0's PL-membership is *conditional on PR-DISC* (expand must terminate) — the digest states this in the trigger-carrier decision but drops the caveat from the guarantee. Carry it into the guarantee line so the boundary isn't lost.

4. **[SHARPENING] "usually the primary query" (scoped quiescence) is not note-grounded.** The note says the substrate "commits to nothing about which scopes matter" and treats tiers as "application vocabulary." Soften to "often the operationally relevant query" or mark it application-dependent rather than implying the note ranks scopes.

5. **[SHARPENING] The "verified Green-side" grounding for retraction should be bounded.** What the evidence verifies is the append-only *content/index* base (write-only granfilade/spanfilade, stale entries persist). The active-view-by-subtraction (`A_K = L_K ∖ nullified`, retraction as appended record) is the *note's* model and a design choice — Green's active document view is a *mutable* POOM (DELETE removes V→I mappings), not a subtraction-from-log. The digest captures *nondeterministic* bodies (Q2) but not the stochastic sub-case; add one line to the upward-hand-off so the omission is acknowledged rather than silent.
