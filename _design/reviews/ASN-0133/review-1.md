## Revision list

**1. [DEFECT] — Design commitment 4 ("Where a trigger reads decides whether it terminates"): two false statements.** (a) "A trigger over the grow-only history (audit slice) is ⊥-stable (SF)" over-generalizes — SF requires the *negated-existential* spelling; a positive existential over the same audit slice is ⊤-stable (ST), not SF. Your own "registration-time certification" section states this correctly ("an SF spelling — a negated existential over a grow-only audit slice"). (b) "giving at-most-once firing per argument (Q-EXT)" attributes at-most-once to SF-stability *alone* — but Q-EXT requires SF **plus extinction discipline**, and the note's Q5a exists precisely to kill this misconception ("SF alone does not bound the count — an SF trigger paired with a contract that emits something *other* than its own falsifier… is a real fire at *every* step"). This also contradicts your own "Guarantees" entry ("for SF-spelling + extinction-disciplined rules (Q-EXT)"). Fix: "A *negated-existential* trigger over the grow-only audit slice is ⊥-stable (SF) — once extinguished it stays false; combined with **extinction discipline** (the rule emits its own falsifier) this gives at-most-once firing per argument (Q-EXT). SF alone does not."

**2. [DEFECT] — Missing: scoped quiescence (Q7) and scope re-entry (Q8).** "What must be built" lists only a global `quiescent_R` detector and "Guarantees" lists only global recognizability, but the note makes scoped quiescence `quiescent_{R,S}` a first-class, independently-decidable verdict (Q7 — same machinery + scope filter). For a coordination system "is *this* document/region done?" is usually the primary query, so scoped detection is a real build target and Q7 a real guarantee. Worse, the digest omits Q8's **scope re-entry** hazard entirely: an *out-of-scope* fire — the registry's own activity outside scope S, not the external environment — can re-arm an in-scope trigger and silently un-quiesce a scope you just reported "done" (detectable per-state by Q7). The per-target scoping body appears under "domain enumeration," but the recognizability guarantee, the scoped detector as a component, and the re-entry hazard are absent. Add all three.
