## Revision list

**1. Implementation approaches → arrangement: surface the contiguity *foreclosure* the implicit run-list bakes in.** The digest recommends the implicit-position run-list as the primary representation and correctly notes that contiguity for non-text subspaces is "Green-observed, not note-mandated" — but it stops one step short. The consequence it leaves implicit: *choosing implicit positions commits you to contiguity in **every** subspace*, which forecloses any sparse/keyed subspace that S8a would otherwise permit (S8a constrains V-positions only to zero-free depth-≥2 tumblers; only D-CTG forces gaplessness, and only for text). State this as a conscious tradeoff — implicit positions buy free splicing and structural D-CTG/D-MIN/D-SEQ, but a subspace that must ever be sparse needs explicit V-keys there. This is the most actionable sharpening because it bears on the headline representation choice.

**2. Sharing inverse / Decisions: tighten the spanfilade grounding to match its scope.** "maintain an inverted index `I-address → {(d,v)}` — this is the spanfilade's role in Green" is stated definitely here but hedged ("spanfilade analog," "spanfilade-style") elsewhere. The evidence (Q5) shows the spanfilade indexing *link endpoints* by fixed key (FROM/TO/THREE), not the general transclusion-backlink `{(d,v)}`. The reverse-reference role is widely-documented Green structure, so this isn't fabrication — but the `{(d,v)}` granularity *generalizes* the documented endpoint-indexing role. Use the hedged form consistently, or say explicitly that you are generalizing the spanfilade's endpoint role to all references.

**3. Design commitments (deferred) + Decisions → depth `m`: scope it to per-(document, subspace).** "chosen once per subspace" overstates what S8-depth commits to. Its quantifier ranges over a single document (`u, w ∈ dom(Σ.M(d))` for one `d`), so distinct documents may carry distinct depths for the *same* subspace; the note's `m_s` notation is shorthand inside a fixed document, not a global per-subspace constant. Tighten to "per-(document, subspace)" so a builder doesn't read in a global depth commitment the note doesn't make. (Low practical impact — depth 2 everywhere satisfies it — but it's the faithful reading, and the digest's own per-document run-list already implies it.)

**4. Design commitments (forced) → S5: keep the distinction, cut the elaboration.** The "Nelson-mandated, formally cappable" analysis is correct and is exactly the forced-vs-conventional call worth making (S5 is a non-entailment result; the no-cap *requirement* comes from Nelson, not the formal core). But it is over-elaborated relative to its payload — the actionable core ("build no cap, require no injectivity") is buried under the cappability argument. Lead with the upshot; compress the consistency reasoning to a single clause.

**5. Content store → index: disambiguate "rebuilt by replay on startup and after a miss."** For a *complete* in-memory map over an append-only store, a lookup miss is a definitive "not stored," not a rebuild trigger — "after a miss" only makes sense for a *bounded cache* with journal-scan fallback. Say which you intend; the two have different memory/latency profiles, and the rest of the section reads as if the index is complete.
