Skeptical review of the Design Digest against ASN-0036.

The digest is strong overall — the two-stream split, S0/S1 append-only-never-reclaim, S4 origin-not-value identity (and the correct warning not to key the store by value), S7 attribution-as-pure-projection, S5 unbounded sharing, and the run-list/position-map tradeoff are all read accurately and pitched at the right altitude. The udanax-green mapping at the coarse level (content store ≈ granfilade, arrangement ≈ POOM/enfilade, reverse index ≈ spanfilade) is correct. But there are real defects a reviser must fix.

**REVISE.**

1. **Implementation approaches → Insertion-position oracle: the characterization is wrong.** It says the valid positions are "just `min` and `max+1` of the text-subspace ordinal block. O(1)." ValidInsertionPosition holds for `v = min(V_1(d))` *or* `shift(min, j)` for **every** `j ∈ {1..N}` — i.e. the entire set `{[1,1], [1,2], …, [1,N+1]}`, `N+1` positions, including every currently occupied position (insertion-at-occupied is explicitly in scope per the note's open question). "min and max+1" describes only two positions and would mislead a builder into rejecting valid mid-document inserts. Fix: state that the valid set is the whole contiguous range `[1,1]..[1,N+1]`; tracking first/last run gives O(1) *bounds*, but the predicate admits all N+1 interior positions.

2. **"References must always resolve … No dangling references in any observable state" overstates the note.** The note leaves this open (Open Question 3: "must [S3] hold at every observable state, or only at quiescent states between operations?"). The digest asserts the every-observable-state reading as a locked commitment and even hard-codes a mechanism for it. Fix: present S3 as an invariant on well-formed/quiescent states, flag the every-state-vs-quiescent question as an open builder decision (it already appears as such in the note), and keep the journal-ordering mechanism as one *option*, not the entailed contract.

3. **Grounding: the symbol-level Green claims need verification or qualification.** The coarse enfilade/granfilade/POOM/spanfilade mapping is fine, but the digest leans hard on very specific assertions — `MAXBCINLOAF=1`, `levelpull` stubbed, `recombine` height floors / "at most one pair per pass", 30% probabilistic compaction, `isanextensionnd`, `acceptablevsa` returning `TRUE`, `INV-ENFILADE-CONFLUENCE` — none of which trace to ASN-0036. Two problems: (a) these must be confirmed against the actual Green-evidence source, because the digest's central arrangement recommendation ("do not make canonical run-count an invariant") rests entirely on them; if any are invented, the recommendation loses its warrant. (b) The `MAXBCINLOAF=1` reasoning is likely *misapplied*: the granfilade is a tree-structured enfilade, so "flat singleton list … did not try to compress the store" is a shaky inference, and it's used to "validate keeping the store dumb and flat." The keep-the-store-flat conclusion follows directly from S0/S1 anyway — drop the granfilade-is-flat justification or correct it rather than resting the point on a contested reading of crum packing.

4. **Minor — altitude drift to repo-specific code.** `im::HashMap`/`im::OrdMap`, `links.jsonl`, `paths.json` are concrete enough to read as code/substrate naming. Defensible as grounding in this repo's substrate, but tighten if the digest is meant to stay representation-agnostic; at minimum these are recommendations, not entailments of the note.

Fixes 1–3 materially change what a builder would implement or believe; 4 is a polish note.
