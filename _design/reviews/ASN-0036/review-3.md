This is a strong digest: the readings are mostly accurate, the approaches are largely sound and Lampson-aligned, and the evidence is used well (especially the canonicity subsection). My revision list targets the places where a recommendation is fragile, a commitment is dropped, or grounding is inflated.

---

**1. "Content typing" / open-question-1 answer — the recommended "kind carried by the V-subspace" silently depends on an obligation the digest itself says storage won't enforce; the evidence supports a more robust answer.** In *The content store* and *Decisions*, the digest answers "what is `Val`?" with: untyped values, kind recovered from "the referencing V-position's subspace (`1.x` text, `2.x` links)." But under S5 an I-address may be referenced from many `(d,v)`, so "kind from the referencing subspace" is well-defined *only if subspace alignment holds* — and the digest's own *Contiguity and subspace alignment* section establishes (correctly, from the Q2 evidence) that storage never enforces alignment. So the typing answer rests on the one obligation the digest flags as unenforced. Meanwhile the Q2 evidence gives an alignment-*independent* source: the I-address structure itself distinguishes kind (text under `doc.3.x`, link orgl under `doc.2.x`, SS-ADDRESS-SPACE), which is why no value tag is needed. Do: recommend recovering kind from the **I-address's own structure** (intrinsic, alignment-independent), with the V-subspace as a convention that must agree; explicitly note the dependency on subspace alignment if kind is read from the V-side. Also soften "the Green evidence *settles* the engineering answer" — the note poses `Val` as open; the evidence *supports* a recommendation, it doesn't close the question (the *Decisions* section already hedges this correctly; align the two).

**2. Design commitments — S8-depth (fixed depth per subspace) is dropped, but it is load-bearing.** The digest states "depth ≥ 2 is forced (S8a); exact `m` is a one-time convention" but never states S8-depth: *all V-positions within a subspace share one depth*. This is not cosmetic — the run structure's notion of "consecutive position" (shift-by-1 at fixed depth) and the S8 proof's injectivity step both rely on it, and it is an operations-layer preservation obligation (you may not introduce a position of a different depth into an existing subspace). Do: add S8-depth to the deferred-commitments list and to *Guarantees to uphold* as an ops obligation; note it is *automatic* under the recommended implicit run-list (store one depth `m` per subspace; reconstruct positions as length-`m` tumblers) and a *required guard* under explicit absolute keys. This also tightens the "default depth `m`" decision: `m` is chosen once *per subspace* and then locked by S8-depth.

**3. Implementation approaches — strip the spec-authoring-pipeline references; "proven on both sides" inflates an unrelated analogy into evidence.** Three times (*content store*, the concurrency note, *Recovery*) the digest cites this repo's own tooling (`links.jsonl`, `paths.json`, per-worker buffers) as support for the journal/replay shape. That tooling is not the note, the Green evidence, or documented Green structure — it is out-of-scope for the engine being specced (the digest admits as much), so it adds words a builder can't use, and "the shape is **proven on both sides**" / "a **proven-here** analogy" treats it as co-equal with the genuine Green grounding. The underlying designs stand on their own (append-replay from S0+S1 and Green's permascroll; per-writer buffer-then-merge for serializing physical appends; journal+registry recovery). Do: keep the patterns, source them to S0/S1 and Green only, and delete the repo-pipeline citations and the "proven on both sides / proven-here" framing.

**4. "The arrangement — canonicity" — the Green source detail is slightly inaccurate and finer-grained than it needs to be.** "`recombineseq`/`recombinend` carry height floors and **merge at most one pair per pass**" — the Q3 evidence shows the one-pair-per-pass `break` only in `recombineseq`; `recombinend` runs a double `(i,j)` loop with no break (multiple merges per pass). Both do carry height floors, so only the "one pair per pass" half is mis-attributed. The non-canonicity conclusion is unaffected. Do: attribute "one pair per pass" to `recombineseq` specifically, or (better, for altitude) compress to "Green disables its height-reducing consolidation (`levelpull`) and only partially/opportunistically re-merges," which is accurate and keeps the section at design altitude rather than tracking per-routine loop structure.

**5. Decisions / arrangement representation — the recommended implicit-position run-list needs its multi-subspace behavior stated.** The prefix-sum/implicit-V-position scheme is described as a single position sequence, but one document holds multiple subspaces (text `1.x`, links `2.x`) with possibly distinct depths (S8-depth). As written, "V-start = prefix sum of preceding run lengths" is only correct *within a subspace*. Do: state that the implicit scheme is **per-subspace** — each subspace is its own contiguous block whose reconstructed V-positions carry that subspace's identifier and depth — so contiguity (D-CTG/MIN/SEQ) and S8-depth hold by construction inside each subspace and the subspaces stay disjoint (the two-blade-knife boundary the digest already cites).

**6. Minor sharpenings (genuine, lower stakes):**
- *Design commitments header.* "Forced — downstream design **cannot violate** these" is too absolute for the unbounded-sharing bullet, which itself (correctly) notes a cap *would* satisfy S0–S3. Split the bullet's two halves: S2 (functionality) is hard-forced; unbounded sharing is **Nelson-mandated but formally permitted to cap**. The bullet's prose is excellent — only the header's blanket claim needs the caveat.
- *Altitude.* The `im` crate / Rust is named ~3×; say it once. Naming a specific crate is borderline below design altitude even given the project's locked Rust choice.
- *What must be built — run-decomposition.* It reads "produces the maximal partition," which sits oddly beside "do not maintain a canonical merged form." Mark it explicitly as an **on-demand** capability (compute when a query needs run structure or a wire format), not maintained state.
- *Attribution.* S7(c) — `origin` *discriminates* distinct documents (`origin(a₁) ≠ origin(a₂)`) — is worth one line, since it's what makes structural attribution usable for grouping/backlink-by-document queries, not just per-address lookup.
