## Revision list

1. **Design commitments → "Forced", K.λ bullet: scope the change-tracking claim — it's false for discovery.** "The entire change-tracking story for link queries therefore reduces to 'observe link creations'" contradicts the two-regime split stated two bullets later, and the note refutes it directly: D-PRES and the worked *Rise under K.μ⁺* / *Swing under K.μ~* clauses show the discovery result moving with `dom(Σ.L)` frozen. K.λ being the sole mutator of `Σ.L` is correct and forced; the *reduction* is not. Restrict it: change-tracking for the **link store**, and hence for **fixed-I existence** queries, reduces to observing K.λ — but discovery-anchored results additionally change under arrangement edits (`Σ.M`) with no link created.

2. **Design commitments → "Forced", match bullet + Implementation → matcher: "conjunctive composes from the disjunctive primitive" is unsound.** The slot-disjunctive `findlinks(I)` collapses slot identity (`∃ i` against one filter `I`), so the conjunctive Q2 query *cannot* be built from it. The shared substrate is a **per-slot matcher** `slotmatch(a,i,J) ≡ coverage(eᵢ)∩J≠∅`: `findlinks(I) = ⋃ᵢ slotmatch(·,i,I)` (OR over slots) and Q2 `= ⋂_{specified i} slotmatch(·,i,Jᵢ)` (AND over slots) — neither composes from the other; both compose from `slotmatch`. Fix the commitment to say that, and reconcile with the implementation section, which already (correctly) says "build per-slot result sets" but ties them only to the AND side — note that `findlinks` *unions* those same sets. Also, "build the primitive so…" is build-advice, not a forced commitment; keep only "F-MATCH is slot-disjunctive" under Forced and move the rest.

3. **Query planning (and Design commitments): surface *why* the union laws need no disjointness.** The digest carries "no disjointness needed" but drops the load-bearing reason the note makes explicit in the F-VDIST derivation: under content sharing (M13/M14) distinct V-positions can resolve to a *single* I-address, so images of disjoint regions can overlap — which is exactly why a disjointness-restricted union would fail to close, and why the resolver's image must be a deduped *set* and per-run results must be *set*-unioned (not concatenated or counted). Add the many-V→one-I consequence; it's a real resolver/index constraint, not just an algebra footnote.

4. **Recovery and durability: don't pair the granfilade with the permascroll as "source of truth."** The digest is otherwise scrupulous that indexes are rebuildable hints — but "the analog of Green's permascroll/granfilade" lumps an enfilade *index* (granfilade) in with the append-only backing. Map journal ≈ permascroll (truth); put the granfilade on the derived-index/hint side alongside spanfilade and POOM (or drop it from the analogy). As written it muddies the truth/hint line the rest of the digest relies on.

5. **Implementation → region resolver: drop the ungrounded persistence justification.** "the prior version survives for free (needed to evaluate `image` at a past state, and for MVCC)" — the note never requires evaluating `image` at a past *arrangement*; it's always evaluated at the current `Σ`, and the cross-state lemmas relate states without retaining old arrangements. This also contradicts your own builder-decision ("Decide whether you need to evaluate `image` at past states; if yes, persistence is effectively forced"), which correctly treats it as optional. Keep the MVCC/snapshot and cheap-edit justifications (sound); cut or condition the historical-query one.

6. **Implementation → coverage evaluator: "prefix-closed" is the wrong term for `subtree(x)`.** `subtree(x) = {t : x ≼ t}` is the set of *extensions* of `x` (the principal up-set under the prefix order — every address having `x` as a prefix); it is *not* prefix-closed (it omits `x`'s proper prefixes). The builder codes this exact membership test, so fix the description. The order-contiguity / interval claim itself is fine — that contiguity is precisely what the enfilade exploits.

7. **Query planning: the empty-image short-circuit ≠ Green's intersection short-circuit.** The note's short-circuit is empty-*input* (`I = ∅ ⟹ ∅`, F-V/F-FIND); Green's is empty-*conjunct*-in-an-AND, a degenerate-conjunctive optimization belonging to Q2, not to this disjunctive primitive. The "(here on the empty-I case)" parenthetical half-acknowledges this — make it explicit that these are different mechanisms sharing only the "detect empty early" lesson, so a builder doesn't import Green's conjunctive short-circuit into the wrong place.

8. **Recovery → snapshot rationale: "violate phase independence" is the wrong diagnosis.** Phase independence (phase 1 reads `M`, phase 2 reads `L`) holds unconditionally; a torn read doesn't break it. The hazard is a result corresponding to **no single coherent state** — `image` read at one `Σ.M`, matched against a later `Σ.L`. Reframe around snapshot atomicity.

9. **Implementation → region resolver: minor altitude slip.** Drop the bare library token "(`im`)"; "persistent ordered map with structural sharing" already pins the mechanism at the right altitude.

Solid and needing no change: the existence-vs-discovery cache disciplines (monotone-append vs recompute/conservative-invalidate), the D-ABSORB "over-invalidate but never under-invalidate" direction, the D-CWP pre-step skip-test, the F-FULL `ran M(d)` footprint hint, and the "divergences from Green to not replicate" (type slot indexed because F-MATCH ranges over all slots; real home-document scoping) — all correctly grounded and directly actionable.
