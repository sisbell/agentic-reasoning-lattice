So the items below are elevations, completeness adds, and calibration — most-valuable first.

**1. "Guarantees to uphold": link carry-through (V10) is missing from the list and under-stated as a guarantee.** Refraction — "a link to one version is a link to all versions" — is one of the two pillars of G2 (it is *why* copying is forbidden), yet it appears only as a downstream hand-off and a "zero per-link work" aside. Add it to **hold-by-construction**, and state the three facts a builder needs to model link discovery correctly: (a) carry-through is **arrangement-driven and continuous**, computed at query time from `ran(M(v))=A` via LP12 against the unchanged link store — *not* a fork-time capture; (b) it therefore **covers links created after the fork** (corollary ii) and is **bidirectional** (the shared anchor makes the link discoverable from source and version at once); (c) it is **conditional** — if the version later contracts off the anchor, the link ceases to be discoverable *from the version* while persisting and remaining discoverable from the source (corollary iii). Also tighten "carry-through total" to "total **over content anchors**" (V2b excludes link-subspace anchors). Without this, a builder may either snapshot/migrate links (which the note forbids) or assume carry-through freezes at the fork.
