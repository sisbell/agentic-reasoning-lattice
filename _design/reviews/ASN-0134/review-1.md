## Revision list

**1. [DEFECT] Implementation approaches → "organizing insight": clause 4 is mis-sorted.** The paragraph says that moving to per-home concurrency makes "exactly four things stop being free... 2, 5, 6, 7," and that "Clauses 1/3/4 you then maintain by discipline." That is wrong for clause 4. Clause 4 (per-call snapshot of a *single* bounded access) is the single-access twin of clause 6 (multi-access): both require the bounded read to land on one committed state, and under the per-home concurrency this paragraph describes, *neither is free* — a lone `Observe_K`'s internal sub-reads over `L_K` and the **global** `nullified` slice can straddle a cross-home commit (A3). The note's MIC-minimality is explicit that clause 1's atomicity does **not** deliver clause 4 ("drop 4 and a single logical read's internal accesses... straddle a commit... though clause 1 keeps every step atomic"). Satisfying clause 4 thus needs the *same* snapshot mechanism as clause 6 (free with persistent immutable state; a version-stamp or reader-lock with a mutable store) — which is exactly what the digest's own State-Representation section then says. As written this is an internal contradiction and a misread of clause 4's status: a builder picking a mutable store and following "clause 4 by discipline" would ship torn reads of `A_K`, the precise failure the note's drop-clause-4 counterexample warns against. **Fix:** pull clause 4 out of the "by discipline" bucket and pair it with clause 6 as one snapshot obligation at two access counts — both free under the single loop, both needing a snapshot mechanism under concurrency. Leave only clauses 1 and 3 as genuinely discipline-maintained.

**2. [SHARPENING] "What must be built" → "recovery mechanism," and approaches → "Journal & recovery": attribute it, don't imply note-mandate.** ASN-0134 is scoped to consistency/isolation and says nothing about durability or persistence — it reasons only about the abstract sequence `𝔼`, and a purely in-memory realization satisfies all of MIC. Recovery is therefore not a MIC commitment. The content is sound and well-grounded (the reference's weak durability — evidence Q13/Q17: shutdown-only flush, crash-mid-write divergence, no startup validation — motivates it, and A6's "every prefix is canonical" cleanly licenses "recovery = replay, no consistency check"). Mark it as a cross-cutting builder concern informed by the evidence and A6, not a clause the note forces, so a reader doesn't infer ASN-0134 mandates durability.

**3. [SHARPENING] Generalize or cite the stack/source specifics that no review channel supports.** "the `im` crate" (presumes a Rust stack the note/evidence never establish), "exactly this repo's `links.jsonl` + replay," and "this repo's substrate computes `max+1` under the home's prefix" are grounded in neither the note, the evidence, nor documented Green structure. They may be correct repo facts, but as uncited source/stack specifics they read as unverifiable, and naming `im` dips below design altitude. Generalize ("a persistent immutable-collection library"; "an append-only step journal + replay") or attach a citation.

**4. [SHARPENING] Forced bullet "Three obligations exceed per-home discipline": separate the *axes*.** This lumps clause 5 with clauses 6/7, but they differ on the axis the note cares about. Clause 5 (run contiguity) is per-run yet still *local* — per-`(home, subspace)` in scope, "strictly weaker than any cross-home exclusion" (W2). Only clauses 6 and 7 are *global* and "resist the per-home thesis." Phrase clause 5 as "exceeds per-*step* granularity but stays within per-home scope," distinct from the genuinely global 6/7. The "still-local"/"global" tags get partway there; the umbrella phrase undercuts them.

**5. [SHARPENING] Clause-7 guarantee understates a limit the note stresses.** "idem=⊤ no-duplicate (clause 7)" is correct, but even under full MIC a *lone* `idem=⊤` emit racing a nullify of its coverage-equal incumbent (§4 instance (ii)) has an order-dependent *outcome* — `∅` vs `{A'}`, one step vs two — that clause 7 does not remedy (it serializes only racing *emits*; emit-before-retract is vacuous here). Add a line: MIC delivers serializability, not a unique `idem=⊤` outcome; the emit-vs-nullify order-instability is inherent, not a duplicate.
