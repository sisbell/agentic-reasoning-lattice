# Xanadu Engine — Module Decomposition

## Overview
Three kernel modules sit at the bottom: a pure value-level **address & span algebra** (M1), a **transaction/journal/concurrency kernel** (M2) that makes every change atomic-ordered-durable-recoverable, and a **namespace** (M3) that mints, records, and owns addresses. On that kernel ride the system's two great state families as feature modules — the document substrate (immutable content store M4, mutable arrangement+editing M5, read-only queries M6) and the link/relation subsystem (store+types M7, discovery M8) — capped by a programmable coordination layer (M9), with an external command surface (M10) fronting the whole system. The single organizing idea: separate the **permanent immutable record** from the **mutable presentation**, funnel every mutation through one kernel, and keep every derived index as a recomputable hint over an append-only journal — with the content-provenance relation R the one *derived-looking* relation that is nonetheless authoritative, non-recomputable history (it cannot be rebuilt from the current arrangement), as distinct from the recomputable index hints; the primary content, link, registry, and arrangement stores remain authoritative in their own right — the arrangement (the POOM) is authoritative *mutable* state, recovered by replaying its edit-delta journal, which is distinct from a recomputable hint.

## Modules

### M1 — Address & Span Algebra
- **Responsibility:** The pure value-level calculus of the address space — tumbler identity/order/containment/classification, position arithmetic, and the interval algebra over spans and span-sets — decidable from values alone, consulting no state and doing no I/O.
- **Sources:** ASN-0034 (tumbler value, intrinsic comparator, field parser/validator, action point & `sig`, ⊕/⊖/`inc`, spans, ordinal shift); ASN-0045 (node/account/document/element classifier, `T4-valid`, level vocabulary); ASN-0053 (span & span-set algebra — classify/merge/split/intersect/difference, canonical normalization, coverage).
- **Depends on:** — (foundation)
- **Key components:** tumbler value & lexicographic order; field parser + T4 admission validator; tumbler decomposition (address → parent / depth / ordinal); level classifier (node/account/document/element/invalid); action-point & last-significant-position primitives; ⊕/⊖/`inc`; ordinal-only shift; span & span-set types; coverage; span-set canonical normalization; `origin`/level/subspace projectors.
- **Seams:** Hands tumblers, spans, coverage, and the `origin`/level/subspace projections upward as the universal key/endpoint/classification types; leans only on ℕ.

### M2 — Transaction, Journal & Concurrency Kernel
- **Responsibility:** Turn every state change into one atomic, totally-ordered, durable, recoverable step (and composites thereof), and present the spec's sequential semantics faithfully to concurrent clients.
- **Sources:** ASN-0047 (seven-primitive atomic-step model, composite-transaction executor & boundary, commit-marker durability); ASN-0134 (Minimal Isolation Contract — single linearization point, per-(home,subspace) serialization, snapshot reads, commit-before-acknowledge, the keyed dedup critical section); the pervasive journal+replay+snapshot recovery discipline running through the corpus.
- **Depends on:** — (foundation; generic over caller-supplied opaque keys & payloads)
- **Key components:** append-only journal of record; single-step linearization / serialization point; composite-transaction boundary; per-key serialization (the key supplied by the caller — e.g. per-(home,subspace)) & snapshot reads; generic keyed critical section (key supplied by the calling store); snapshot/checkpoint; replay-driven recovery; commit-before-acknowledge gate.
- **Seams:** Stores register record-types and index-rebuilders and obtain atomic commit + consistent snapshots through it (dependency-inverted); it owns ordering/durability/isolation, never store semantics — store-specific serialization keys (e.g. link de-duplication's coverage-class key) are supplied by the calling store, never computed here, so the kernel needs no address algebra of its own. The three-layer permanence discipline (the append-only existential stores, the arrangement edit-delta journal, and the provenance relation — M3's entity registry, M4's content, M7's link store, and M5's arrangement/edit-delta journal + R) is enforced store-wide; the J-couplings are enforced at **M5's placement composite** (never by this kernel), and they span more than M5's own edits: **J0** (content-allocation ⇒ placement) fires in INSERT (M5) and in M9-triggered predicate-definition creation; **J1★** (placement ⇒ provenance) fires in INSERT, COPY, and VERSION (M5) and in predicate-definition creation; **link** placement (K.μ⁺_L) is deliberately uncoupled from provenance and link allocation (K.λ) from content allocation (ASN-0047 J-LV). Both the permanence discipline and the J-couplings are enforced by the stores at their composite boundaries *through* this kernel, never by it.

### M3 — Namespace: Allocation, Registry & Ownership
- **Responsibility:** Own the authoritative permanent name/entity space — mint fresh globally-unique addresses under the frontier discipline, record what exists, and resolve who owns what by prefix.
- **Sources:** ASN-0040 (baptismal registry, next-address allocator, atomic baptism, B6 gate, per-namespace frontier); ASN-0093 (allocation substrate, K.σ document/entity registration, sub-allocator chains); ASN-0047 (entity-registry membership + parent-exists P8 admission check); ASN-0034 (per-domain allocators, durable monotone frontier); ASN-0042 (principal registry, `owns` predicate, longest-prefix `ω` resolver, delegation gate, fork); ASN-0103 (CREATENEWDOCUMENT); ASN-0123 (version-vs-document frontier separation — an allocator obligation).
- **Depends on:** M1, M2.
- **Key components:** per-(home,subspace) frontier allocator (`inc`-modes, gap-free, monotone), **level-aware** so a document's chain (minted under its account) and a version's chain (minted under the source document) advance on separate frontiers and never re-mint a colliding address (ASN-0123); baptismal/entity registry (append-only existence set; node/account/document); validity/admission gate (B6 well-formedness + parent-exists P8); principal registry + `owns` + effective-owner resolver `ω`; delegation gate; fork allocation; genesis/seed init; CREATENEWDOCUMENT.
- **Seams:** Hands fresh, T4-valid, owned addresses plus "is allocated?"/"who owns?" answers to every store and operation; persists registry + frontier via M2; deliberately decoupled from content (ghost elements). CREATENEWDOCUMENT registers a document in the entity registry but does **not** materialize its (empty) arrangement — that lives in M5 and is left implicit/lazy. M5 reads this registry only to check "is d registered?" as an edit precondition (via `M5 → M3`); the registered-empty (⟨⟩) vs unallocated (fail) *query* distinction lives in M6 and M8 (via `M6 → M3` and `M8 → M3`). This diverges from ASN-0103's eager-`M(d)=∅` recommendation, which would write M5 from M3 and close an `M3 → M5` cycle.

### M4 — Content Store (Istream)
- **Responsibility:** The permanent, append-only, immutable byte store keyed by allocated address — Nelson's permascroll; the never-mutated, never-GC'd half of the two-layer state.
- **Sources:** ASN-0036 (content store C — write-once, origin-identity-not-value, no update/delete); ASN-0093 (the storage half of K.α — value write at an address minted by M3 and orchestrated by M5).
- **Depends on:** M1, M2.
- **Key components:** address→value append-only map; membership & value-at lookup; origin-identity discipline (no value-dedup as identity); the no-mutate/no-delete/no-GC guarantee; replay-recovered index (a hint).
- **Seams:** Content addresses arrive as parameters (minted by M3, orchestrated inside M5's placement composite — M4 itself calls no allocator); durability from M2; hands immutable value lookups to retrieval (M6 — RETRIEVEV, ASN-0115) and to predicate-definition read-back (M9, value-at), and serves as the referential-integrity target for arrangements (M5); never read for bytes by the link layer.

### M5 — Arrangements & Editing (Vstream)
- **Responsibility:** Own each document's mutable V→I arrangement (the POOM) — the only layer where *destructive* change lives — together with the append-only content-provenance relation R, and provide the editing/versioning operations. R and M are two distinct permanence layers (ASN-0047) deliberately co-located: every *content* placement appends R atomically with its M edit (write-locality), while link placements (K.μ⁺_L) are uncoupled from R (ASN-0047 J-LV) and append nothing to it; and the read-side observers that join M and R — SHOWDELETIONS, FINDDOCSCONTAINING (M6) — require a *single consistent (M, R) snapshot* (ASN-0075's atomic root-swap of the M edit and the R append), which co-location makes one commit. (COMPARE, also in M6, needs only a consistent *multi-document M* snapshot — it reads neither R nor content, ASN-0122.) R is authoritative, non-recomputable history, not part of the mutable presentation though housed beside it.
- **Sources:** ASN-0036 (arrangement M, S-invariants); ASN-0058 (POOM mapping block, split/merge/canonicalize, resolve); ASN-0082 (gap open/close displacement); ASN-0084 (cut-point rearrangement primitive); ASN-0116 INSERT; ASN-0117 DELETE; ASN-0118 COPY; ASN-0119 REARRANGE; ASN-0123 CREATENEWVERSION; ASN-0047 (extend/contract/reorder + provenance R; content-allocation⇒placement⇒provenance J-couplings enforced at M5's composite boundary).
- **Depends on:** M1, M2, M3, M4.
- **Key components:** per-document arrangement store (POOM, content + link subspaces) — authoritative, replay-recovered mutable state, not a recomputable hint; arrangement/edit-delta journal — every INSERT/DELETE/COPY/REARRANGE/VERSION mutation of the POOM recorded as a journaled delta via M2's record-type, the authoritative edit log the POOM is replay-recovered from; split / merge / canonicalize; resolve (V→I); displacement (extend/contract/reorder); INSERT / DELETE / COPY / REARRANGE / VERSION; provenance relation R (append-only, authoritative); referential-integrity gate at placement, verified by M5 only on the **content** side (every content-subspace arrangement write targets an address already allocated in M4, checked via M3); the **link** side is discharged by-construction inside M7's MAKELINK, which hands M5 an already-allocated link address through a semantics-blind seating API — so M5 never reads M7 (no M5 → M7 edge); subspace-match invariant — a pure M1 classification of each V-position's address (content vs link subspace) — driving S3★ routing (content V-positions resolve into M4, link V-positions name addresses in M7's store), with M5 enforcing referential integrity on the content side only; non-transclusion guard (no content V-position binds a link address; a link is arranged only in its home document — CL-OWN); subspace confinement & contiguity maintenance.
- **Seams:** Mints content addresses via M3, writes bytes via M4, commits composites via M2; exposes V→I resolution (and the within-document I→V reverse lookup / inverse-arrangement hint that M8's projection consumes) and arrangement-mutation (including a semantics-blind link-seating API for M7); hands R to content query (M6).

### M6 — Content Retrieval & Query
- **Responsibility:** Read-only observers over documents — deliver content, report extents, attribute origin, and answer provenance/version/containment questions; owns no authoritative state.
- **Sources:** ASN-0115 RETRIEVEV; ASN-0112 RETRIEVEDOCVSPAN; ASN-0113 RETRIEVEDOCVSPANSET; ASN-0077 SHOWORIGIN; ASN-0075 SHOWDELETIONS; ASN-0122 COMPARE; ASN-0124 FINDDOCSCONTAINING.
- **Depends on:** M1, M2, M3, M4, M5.
- **Key components:** content delivery (resolve→fetch); document-extent queries (span / span-set); origin projection (SHOWORIGIN); deletion classification (SHOWDELETIONS); version comparison (COMPARE); document-containment oracle (FINDDOCSCONTAINING) + its reverse-index hint over R; document-allocation / registry-membership gate (registered-empty vs unallocated).
- **Seams:** Resolves through M5's arrangements, fetches bytes from M4, reads R for provenance, projects origin via M1, **checks document allocation against M3's entity registry** (the registered-empty → ⟨⟩ vs unallocated → fail distinction that RETRIEVEDOCVSPANSET, RETRIEVEDOCVSPAN, RETRIEVEV, SHOWORIGIN, and COMPARE require), takes snapshots from M2; writes nothing.

### M7 — Link & Relation Store
- **Responsibility:** Own the authoritative link/typed-relation store — create, read, type, retract, and supersede links and typed relations as immutable, address-identified, append-only objects under the shape/admission discipline — and own the spanfilade coverage index that both its own `Observe` and downstream discovery read.
- **Sources:** ASN-0043 (link store, endsets, type-by-coverage, link value, spanfilade); ASN-0093 (K.λ); ASN-0120 MAKELINK; ASN-0111 READLINK; ASN-0114 FOLLOWLINK; ASN-0086 (Emit/Observe/Nullify, active/audit, retraction); ASN-0126 (shape gate); ASN-0128 (type registry, idempotence/dedup, behavior declarations + all four behavior atoms BH1–BH4, shipped types); ASN-0125 (link editing / supersession).
- **Depends on:** M1, M2, M3, M5.
- **Key components:** link store (L) + endset/link value; type-by-coverage matching; MAKELINK / READLINK / FOLLOWLINK; Emit / Observe (served by the M7-owned spanfilade coverage index) / Nullify + active vs audit slices; nullified/tombstone set; spanfilade content-coverage index (per-slot, recomputable hint, shared with M8 discovery); shape gate + type registry (shape/idem/behaviors; immutable, construction-time); de-duplication (serialized via M2's keyed critical section, keyed by a coverage-class computed in M1); behavior *declarations* and all four behavior atoms (BH1 read-filter, BH2 determinate-walk over forward adjacency, BH3 typed-reverse-lookup over the spanfilade, BH4 age-staleness over home-frontier/chain-index); supersession claims.
- **Seams:** Allocates link addresses via M3, resolves V-region endset args & seats home links via M5's arrangement API, commits via M2 (supplying the coverage-class dedup key to M2's keyed critical section) and rebuilds the spanfilade by replay through M2 (a recomputable hint); hands the canonical store, active/audit slices, the spanfilade coverage index, and behavior declarations to discovery (M8) — which executes findlinks/count/window over that shared index — and hands the full PL read surface (Observe plus all four behavior atoms BH1–BH4, all served from M7's own spanfilade so there is no upward dependency on M8) to coordination (M9).

### M8 — Link Query & Discovery
- **Responsibility:** Read-only indexed discovery and resolution over the link store — which links touch a content region, project/resolve/count/paginate them — executing over M7's spanfilade coverage index and owning no authoritative state.
- **Sources:** ASN-0098 (project / coverage / discoverability); ASN-0127 (content-region link query); ASN-0121 (findlinks — four-set match); ASN-0108 (windowed search); ASN-0132 (count); ASN-0131 (RETRIEVEENDSETS); ASN-0117 (pre-edit link-survival check — read-only); ASN-0125 (archival link-discovery in(y)/out(x)).
- **Depends on:** M1, M2, M3, M5, M7.
- **Key components:** project / coverage / discoverability; findlinks (four-set match); count; windowed enumeration (identity cursor); RETRIEVEENDSETS; pre-edit link-survival check (which links a proposed DELETE orphans from a document — findlinks-over-Δ + last-witness, ASN-0117; read-only, never touches M5's edit path); archival link-discovery in(y)/out(x) over supersession/edit lineage (ASN-0125, composing M7's BH3 reverse index); V→I image / resolve (via M5; content-subspace restriction supplied per-caller); document-existence gate (registered-empty → defined/∅ vs unregistered → error, via M3's entity registry).
- **Seams:** Reads the link store, active slice, behavior atoms (including M7's BH3, composed for reverse-discovery), and the spanfilade coverage index from M7 (executing findlinks / count / window over that shared index), resolves content regions to I-addresses via M5's arrangement in the V→I direction (image/resolve) and projects coverage to V-positions via M5's arrangement in the I→V direction (a within-document reverse lookup — a scan of the forward map, or an inverse-arrangement hint that, if materialized, lives in M5), **checks document existence against M3's entity registry** (the registered-empty → ∅ vs unregistered → error distinction that findlinks_V / project / windowed search / RETRIEVEENDSETS require), takes snapshots via M2; hands link-discovery answers to readers; writes nothing authoritative and owns no index.

### M9 — Predicate & Coordination Layer
- **Responsibility:** The substrate's closed predicate/query language, its persistence as first-class content, and the reactive rule engine built on it — the programmable, self-monitoring automation layer with a defined quiescence theory.
- **Sources:** ASN-0129 (PL: closed algebra, evaluator, dynamics/stability analyzer); ASN-0130 (predicate definitions as substrate content — registration, references, versioning); ASN-0133 (quiescence: rule registry, fires, scheduler, termination); consumes ASN-0128's behavior atoms.
- **Depends on:** M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M7.
- **Key components:** PL term representation + type checker + pure evaluator; dynamics/stability classifier; predicate-definition encoding/registration/expansion (defs stored as content); rule registry + trigger evaluation + atomic fires; quiescence detector + fair scheduler.
- **Seams:** Reads structural state entirely via M7 — `Observe` plus the four behavior atoms (BH1–BH4) — and is fenced off from M8's content-region queries (ASN-0129), so it carries no M8 dependency; resolves residence via M3, registers predicate-definitions as content runs through M5's placement composite (M5 mints the run via M3 and writes its bytes via M4, satisfying ASN-0047's J0 content-allocation⇒placement coupling — the def's identity is its content start-address) and reads a registered def back by address via M4 (value-at); **writes into M7** — `register_pred`/`certify_pd_stable` emit `pdef`/`pd_stable` classifier tuples and reactive rule fires emit via `Emit_K`, all through M7's gated write path (the `pdef`/`pd_stable` types must already be present in M7's immutable, construction-time type registry); leans on M2 for snapshot-consistent multi-read verdicts and atomic fires; hands a programmable coordination surface upward (out of corpus).

### M10 — Operation Surface (FEBE Command Layer)
- **Responsibility:** The system's public command surface and request lifecycle — parse and dispatch each FEBE operation, fix its single linearization point, gate its response on commit (commit-before-acknowledge), marshal results, surface typed rejections for precondition / out-of-order failures (never a silent skip), and orchestrate the few composite operations that span more than one store family within one M2 transaction. It owns the request lifecycle and cross-family orchestration — **never the per-store operation logic**, which stays in the store/query modules.
- **Sources:** ASN-0134 (commit-before-acknowledge, the request/response path, the pipelining-vs-sequential-client distinction — re-homed here from the kernel, which keeps only the commit-gate *mechanism*); the *command-surface* aspect of the operation notes — dispatch + lifecycle for the mutators (ASN-0103/0116/0117/0118/0119/0120/0123/0125) and request marshaling for the readers (ASN-0111/0112/0113/0114/0115/0121/0122/0124/0131/0132). Each note's *per-store logic* lives in its owning module (M5/M6/M7/M8); only its lifecycle wrapper is here.
- **Depends on:** M1, M2, M3, M5, M6, M7, M8.
- **Key components:** request parse + dispatch table; per-operation linearization entry (one operation → one M2 step or composite boundary); commit-before-acknowledge sequencing; response marshaling; precondition/rejection surfacing (typed, re-orderable, never silent); cross-family composite orchestration (an operation touching >1 store family, committed as one M2 transaction).
- **Seams:** The front door — external clients enter here. Dispatches each operation down to its owning store/query module (M5/M6/M7/M8), opens and commits its transaction via M2, and returns only after commit. **Parallel to M9, not above or below it:** M9's automation fires directly into M7's gated write path, not through this surface, so no edge joins them. Owns no authoritative state.

## Module DAG
```
M3 → M1, M2
M4 → M1, M2
M5 → M1, M2, M3, M4
M6 → M1, M2, M3, M4, M5
M7 → M1, M2, M3, M5
M8 → M1, M2, M3, M5, M7
M9 → M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M7
M10 → M1, M2, M3, M5, M6, M7, M8
```
M1 and M2 are independent foundations: M2 is generic over caller-supplied opaque keys and payloads and needs no address algebra, so it carries no edge to M1. Acyclic — every edge points to a lower-numbered module. The one seam that could have closed a cycle (M5 owns the arrangement; M7's MAKELINK *writes* its link subspace) is broken by direction: M5 exposes a semantics-blind link-seating API and never interprets link semantics, so **M7 → M5** with no return edge. A second potential cycle — M3 registers documents while M5 owns their arrangements — is avoided by leaving a new document's arrangement implicit/lazy so M3 never writes M5: the only edge is **M5 → M3**. A third — `Observe` (M7) and discovery (M8) both need the coverage index — is resolved by placing the spanfilade in M7 (co-located with the link writer): Observe reads it directly and M8 reads it across the existing **M8 → M7** edge, so no `M7 → M8` edge arises. M9's predicate language reads all its atoms — `Observe` and the four behaviors (BH1–BH4) — from M7, and is fenced off from M8's content-region queries (ASN-0129), so **no M9 → M8 edge arises** despite M8 being lower-numbered. M9 creates predicate-def content through M5's placement composite, giving **M9 → M5**, which stays acyclic since M5 < M9. **M10**, the external command surface, sits at the top: it dispatches operations down to M5/M6/M7/M8 and commits through M2, so every edge points to a lower-numbered module. It is **parallel to M9** — M9's rule fires reach the stores directly via M7's gated write path, not through M10 — so no edge joins them.

**Valid topological build order:** M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8, M9, M10.

## Open partition questions
- **Content store (M4) vs Arrangements (M5).** Split here on change-profile (immutable Istream vs mutable Vstream — the system's architectural spine). But M4 is thin in *mechanism*; the two are "the strand model" of one note (ASN-0036) and could merge into a single "Document Substrate." I kept them apart to make the Istream/Vstream split module-visible.
- **Ownership (ASN-0042) inside M3 vs its own Authority module.** Folded in because principals are a labeling of the registry and delegation *is* a baptism — but authority is *policy* over allocation's *mechanism* (Lampson's separation), and a session/access layer is its natural consumer. The longest-prefix `ω` resolver + delegation + fork could stand alone; the Authority-module split is the live alternative.
- **M7/M8 link boundary.** Type registry, shapes, dedup, behavior *declarations*, all four behavior atoms (read-filter, determinate-walk, typed-reverse-lookup, age), and the **spanfilade coverage index** are in M7; indexed *query execution* — findlinks/count/window — is in M8, reading M7's index across the existing `M8 → M7` edge. Placing BH3 (typed-reverse-lookup) in M7 alongside the reverse index it reads keeps PL's *entire* read surface (Observe + all behaviors) inside M7, so M9 needs no M8 dependency (ASN-0129 fences PL off from M8's content-region queries). The spanfilade is placed in M7 (co-located with the link writer) so that `Observe` (M7) and discovery (M8) share one index — restoring ASN-0086's indexed Observe on the hot PL-polling path, at the cost of putting a discovery-shaped structure in the store module. `Observe` still overlaps `findlinks` (M8) conceptually. Concretely, the per-slot-match + AND-of-ORs *combiner* is implemented twice today (inside Observe in M7, inside findlinks in M8); factoring that combiner into M7 alongside the index it owns — leaving M8 as pure discovery presentation (windowing/cursors, count, pagination, projection, RETRIEVEENDSETS) over M7's matcher — would remove the duplication, and is sound either way. This remains the softest seam in the design; the whole link subsystem could also collapse to one module.
- **M9 as one module vs two.** PL + predicate-defs-as-content (the language and its persistence) is arguably a separate responsibility from the reactive rule engine (ASN-0133) written *in* PL. Bundled as "the programmable layer"; splittable into PL-evaluator and rule-engine.
- **Provenance R: distributed vs its own module.** R's authoritative state is in M5 (written by edits, where the responsibility now brackets it explicitly as authoritative history co-located for write-locality and single-snapshot reads); its query family (SHOWDELETIONS, FINDDOCSCONTAINING) is in M6; SHOWORIGIN's pointwise origin projector is pure M1, but the SHOWORIGIN *operation* itself — whose I-span/V-span resolvers read arrangement state — is correctly in M6. A dedicated "Provenance & Attribution" module (R + those queries) is defensible, since R is non-recomputable authoritative history and shares the spanfilade *shape* with M7's link index.
- **Transaction kernel (M2): module vs cross-cutting discipline.** Treated as a buildable WAL/MVCC/recovery engine that stores plug into. One could instead make only the MIC *contract* shared and let each store own its own journal/recovery — at the cost of duplicating the recovery story the whole corpus leans on.
- **Operation surface (M10): thickness, and its relation to M9.** Added in the operator pass because the review loop checks note-coverage, not missing *layers* — every operation note is placed in the store it touches, so a command surface was never flagged. Kept deliberately thin: it owns the request lifecycle (parse/dispatch/linearize/commit-before-ack/marshal/reject) and only the genuinely cross-*family* composites; single-family operation logic stays in the stores. Open: (a) exactly which composites are cross-family enough to orchestrate here vs leave in M5; (b) whether M9 and M10 stay parallel surfaces (current call) or M9's fires should also route through M10's lifecycle rather than directly into M7. This module is the one part of the decomposition not produced by the convergence loop.
