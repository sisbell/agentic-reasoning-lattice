1. `[SHARPENING]` **State the fold-totality posture for out-of-contract `M5Rec`s.** M2's contract requires `apply` to be total, and §10 asserts `apply_m5` is "pure/total/deterministic," but the behavior of `splice_in`/`remove_range`/`reorder` on a record that violates its staging-time validation (e.g. `ContentPlace.at > total_width + 1`, `ContentRemove` overrunning the list, `ContentReorder` with a malformed cut vector) is never defined. 2. `[SHARPENING]` **Promote the SHOWDELETIONS-difference placement to its own Conflicts entry.** The decomposition lists "deletion classification (SHOWDELETIONS)" under M6, but the design computes the entire `ever_placed ∖ content_image` join inside M5 (`deletions`), leaving M6 gate-and-marshal only. Give this boundary shift the same explicit conflict treatment.

3. `[SHARPENING]` **Note R's duplicate-append growth as an Open decision.** `prov_by_doc` gains one `Span` per placed run forever, including denotational no-ops (repeated COPY of the same region into the same document). Correct under P2, but `docs_containing`/`deletions` scan costs scale with the raw span count; record the choice (accept growth vs. an append-time within-class subsumption check) alongside Open decision #3.

4. `[SHARPENING]` **Split `InsertError::BadPosition` for verdict precision.** The design elsewhere insists on precise typed rejections (the `BadSpan` rationale in Conflicts #7, the explicit `NodeTierCrossOwner`), yet INSERT/COPY lump "wrong subspace" and "ordinal out of range" into one `BadPosition`, where DELETE distinguishes `NotContentSubspace`/`NotArranged`/`OutOfBounds`. Align for M10's benefit.

5. `[SHARPENING]` **Name `ever_placed`'s consumer or demote it.** It is public on the M6 seam, but no listed query consumes it directly (`deletions` covers SHOWDELETIONS; FINDDOCSCONTAINING uses `docs_containing` + `project`). A public raw mixed-length cover with no named consumer invites exactly the `LevelMismatch` misuse the rest of the design works to fence off; either cite the M6 use that needs it or mark it internal.

6. `[SHARPENING]` **Consider per-op trait bounds.** The single `impl` block's union bound (`HasM3 + HasContent`, `From<M3Rec> + From<ContentWrite>`) over-constrains `delete`/`rearrange`/`copy`, which stage only `M5Rec`. The comment acknowledges this; splitting the impl (or stating why the union is kept) would let a minimal test world drive the read-only-upstream ops.
