Reviewing M2 against ASN-0047, ASN-0134, and the decomposition's boundary assignment. This is a careful, largely sound design — the WAL/marker-as-ack discipline, the durable-vs-visible coupling-to-mode via the type system, the merge-at-install footprint contract (read-set vs apply-fold distinction), and the two-pass recovery with the three Seq-regions all hold up under scrutiny. Two material problems and two sharpenings.

---

**1. [DEFECT] §7 (Recovery) never recovers the `Txn` high-water, so cross-session `Txn`-id reuse corrupts the by-`Txn` grouping.** Recovery groups journal frames "by their `txn` (not file position)" to validate each marker's `records_checksum` after a `magic`-resync (§1/§7) — this grouping is correct *only if `Txn` is unique within the scanned region*. But `Txn` is a *separate* "per-kernel-monotone" counter "assigned when `transact` begins staging" (§1), and recovery rebuilds only "the `Seq` high-water (as W)" — `Txn` is unmentioned, so a post-crash session resets it and reuses ids. Since Seqs (and thus `W`) continue across sessions, whenever a session crashes *before checkpointing*, the next recovery loads the older checkpoint and replays a range `(S_load, W]` spanning **two sessions' transactions** — whose reused `Txn` ids now collide. Grouping merges two distinct transactions under one `Txn`; the later marker's `records_checksum` then fails against the merged record set, and the txn is either **discarded as un-committed (committed-data loss)** or surfaced as a **spurious `Corruption{at}` halt**. Fix: either recover the `Txn` high-water at `open` (max `Txn` over the scanned journal + 1), or — simpler and self-healing — **derive `Txn` from the transaction's first `Seq`** (unique and recovered for free), eliminating the separate counter entirely.

**2. [DEFECT] `critical_section` is gated on `M: Concurrent`, which wrongly admits `OptimisticCas` — internally contradictory and functionally incoherent.** The interface/§4 claim that `critical_section` is defined on "the concurrent modes (`M: Concurrent` — `PerKeyMerge` / `OptimisticCas`), where the lock table is live" directly contradicts §8, which states CAS "**holds no locks**" / "holds no keys there is nothing to release." A lock-free CAS has no live lock table to hold keys across the section's *separate* inner commits, and — more fundamentally — it can only abort *itself* on a foreign commit, never *exclude* a foreign same-key writer, so it cannot deliver the run-exclusion `critical_section` exists for. CAS is therefore in exactly the position `SingleApplier` is (atomic one-`transact` runs, no coherent hold-across-commits) — which is *why* the design correctly gates the method off `SingleApplier`. Gate `critical_section` on a `PerKeyMerge`-specific bound (a concrete `impl<W> Kernel<W, PerKeyMerge>`, or a `HasLockTable` marker impl'd only for `PerKeyMerge`), drop `OptimisticCas` from whatever trait backs the method, and correct the "lock table is live under `OptimisticCas`" claim. (No capability is lost: CAS runs are already atomic via one `transact`, over-satisfying clause 5.)

**3. [SHARPENING] §8 should state how `OptimisticCas` delivers clauses 2/7 and why footprint confinement is automatic there.** Under CAS the `keys` argument is inert (no locks held); clauses 2 and 7 are delivered by the whole-root CAS's *abort-on-any-commit* plus caller re-invocation (the re-invoked closure reads the prior winner's deposit and returns Hit / a fresh slot). Make this explicit — a reader currently has to reconstruct it. Relatedly, the whole-root CAS validates the *entire* committed-state read-set by construction, so footprint confinement is **automatic** in v1; the stated "same footprint-confinement obligation as merge-at-install" is forward-looking — it becomes a real store obligation only under the per-frontier-CAS refinement (narrower validation). Say so, lest a builder wrongly conclude an unconfined-read store is unsafe under v1 CAS.

**4. [SHARPENING] Complete (or mark illustrative) the `→_sh` classification behind "Seq refines `idx(σ)`."** §2 / the interface note classify `K.σ`/`K.α`/`K.λ_sh` as `→_sh` and `K.μ⁺`/`K.μ⁻`/`K.ρ` as non-`→_sh`, using ASN-0134's vocabulary — but M2 also journals ASN-0047 records the enumeration omits (`K.δ` for nodes/accounts, `K.λ`, `K.μ⁺_L`). Since M2 acts on no record's `→_sh`-ness, this is harmless, but note `K.δ`-for-documents ≡ ASN-0134's `K.σ` and mark the list non-exhaustive, so a reader isn't left thinking those five are the whole vocabulary.

---

The boundary discipline is clean throughout (J-couplings → M5, frontier/coverage math → M3/M7, lifecycle/ack → M10, permanence → stores, all explicitly disclaimed and correctly assigned), the conflict resolutions (#1 composite-vs-batch, #2 single-writer-vs-per-home, #3 visible-after-durable-vs-orthogonal) are stated and sound, and the durability/poison/truncation failure paths are airtight. Both defects have small, local fixes — but #1 is a silent recovery-correctness bug and #2 is a self-contradiction a builder hits immediately, so this is not yet buildable-as-written.
