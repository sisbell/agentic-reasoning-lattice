Reviewing M2 against ASN-0047 and ASN-0134 for buildability, interface/seam fidelity, and internal consistency. The single-applier and per-key-merge paths are sound and concrete; the problems concentrate in the optional CAS realization and in an overstated durability mechanism.

## Revision list

**1. [DEFECT] Fix or fence `OptimisticCas` + `Durability::Fsync` recovery — as specified it silently corrupts on an ordinary crash.** Under CAS the design states (a) Seqs are assigned at the winning CAS but frames are appended *after* it, so they "land in the journal out of `Seq` order"; (b) each commit advances `Clean{through: last_seq}` with its *own* Seq; and (c) recovery keeps txns with `last_seq ≤ W` and folds "sorted by `Seq`" with **no** contiguity check (the doc forbids one). These combine unsoundly: a higher-Seq winner (seq 6) can durably append its frames + `Clean{through:6}` while a lower-Seq winner (seq 5), whose frames land *later* in the file, has not yet fsynced. A crash loses seq 5; recovery computes `W = 6`, replays `(S_load, 6]`, finds seq 6 but not seq 5, and — since "a merely missing `Seq` is never corruption" — folds seq 6 over a state missing seq 5, leaving a permanent hole in the allocation chain (a **W1** violation) or a wrong non-idempotent fold, *silently*. The root cause: `Clean{through}`'s invariant ("everything ≤ through is durable") fails the moment appends are out of `Seq` order. Pick and state one: (i) restrict CAS to `Durability::None`; or (ii) under CAS advance/derive `W` as the **longest contiguous durable `Seq` prefix**, not the max `Clean` (accepting the coordination this reintroduces); or (iii) demote CAS+Fsync to an explicitly-unresolved open problem rather than a specified realization. The single-applier and per-key paths are unaffected (in-order appends + prefix-flushing fsync keep `Clean{through}` honest).

**2. [SHARPENING] Stop presenting the clean watermark as a v1 necessity, and drop its second fsync on the recommended path.** It is billed as "the marker-ack soundness fix" that "closes the phantom-commit hole," at the cost of a second *ordered* fsync per commit ("fsync records+marker, THEN advance+fsync the clean watermark"). On the single-applier durable-before-visible path it is redundant: a marker that is CRC-valid *at recovery time* is by definition on the disk recovery reads (hence durable); `records_checksum` already rejects a txn whose records didn't all land; truncate-on-failure removes the un-acked tail; and poison-on-truncate-failure + "the failed commit never advanced the high-water" already prevent any colliding successor (there is none to reuse the Seq). The only residual the watermark "fixes" — a durable-but-un-acked marker getting replayed — is exactly the lost-ack case ASN-0134 SAFE(b)(iii) assigns to the *client*, so the watermark buys a guarantee *stronger than the notes require* at 2× hot-path fsync, and its real payoff (one batched ack) only arrives under the **deferred** group-commit. Either fold the ack into the marker (one fsync/commit) for v1 and reintroduce a separate `Clean` with `FsyncBatch`, or keep it but relabel it forward-design + recovery-clarity rather than a correctness necessity.

**3. [SHARPENING] Promote "neighbors expose pure per-step logic + their `Record` variants" from an example to a stated seam contract.** Because `transact` is non-reentrant, every module whose primitive appears as a *step inside another module's composite* (M3 allocation inside M5 placement; M7 seating; M1 math) must expose that primitive as a **pure** function over a supplied `&W` *and* expose its `Record` variant, separately from its standalone `transact`-wrapped form — else the downstream composite is unbuildable without a deadlocking nested `transact`. Today this lives only inside the M5 seam prose; add it as an explicit hard contract in "Dependencies & seams" so M3/M7 are built with the pure surface up front instead of discovering the constraint at wiring time.

**4. [SHARPENING] Give the per-key merge-at-install path the same step-by-step pseudocode §3 gives the single applier.** The scaling path (acquire keys → run `f` against `base` outside the install lock → install section {assign Seqs, re-fold `stg.records` over current root, journal, swap} → release keys → fsync+`Clean` → return) exists only as §8 prose, yet it is precisely where the re-fold-without-re-running-`f`, the exact key-release point (after swap, before fsync), and the visible-before-durable poison interact. A parallel pseudocode block removes the reconstruction burden and pins the lock-hold window.

**5. [SHARPENING] State that footprint confinement for an inner `transact(&[], f)` is discharged by the enclosing `critical_section`'s held keys.** The inner calls hold *no keys of their own* while reading the frontier under the section's `key(d,s_C)`. A builder reading "`f`'s read set must lie under the *txn's held keys*" literally would judge the inner read unconfined and the clause-5 path unsound; say explicitly that keys held by an enclosing section satisfy the contract.

**6. [SHARPENING] Make recovery's two-pass structure and `Corruption{at}` explicit.** Classifying a corrupt run as `≤ S_load` / `(S_load, W]` / `> W` needs `W` *before* the fold — i.e. a first pass scanning (resyncing via `magic`) to find the highest durable `Clean` and the corrupt runs' inferred Seq bounds, then a second pass folding `(S_load, W]`. Say so, and say `Corruption{at}` carries the offending run's inferred upper bound (the next valid frame's `Seq`), so no one attempts single-pass classification.

VERDICT: REVISE
