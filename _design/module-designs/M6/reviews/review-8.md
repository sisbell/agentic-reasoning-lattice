## Revision list

**1. [SHARPENING] Re-characterize the ordinal-level gate on COMPARE / FINDDOCSCONTAINING / SHOWORIGIN_V as a *domain-narrowing*, not "faithful completeness-preservation."** *Conflicts resolved 8* claims rejecting a non-ordinal-level content span is "the faithful behavior" and that "the binding reason to reject … is completeness, not convenience." That overstates it. X12 (content-subspace + T12) and WF_V(iv) genuinely admit such spans, so M6 accepts a *strictly smaller* domain than the notes specify. And rejection is not actually forced: a non-ordinal-level content span like `([s_C,3],[w₁,w₂])` denotes exactly the content positions `{[s_C,k] : k ≥ 3}`, which M6 *could* normalize to the ordinal-level `([s_C,3],[0, n_C−2])` after reading `content_count` and then resolve faithfully. So the honest framing is: "M6 narrows these ops to ordinal-level spans because supporting the wider T12 domain would require normalization machinery M6 chooses not to build; the narrowing is surfaced as a typed rejection." Also soften "Every selection these notes name is in any case expressible as an ordinal-level span" — open-ended selections ("from position 3 to the end") need the count, which is the whole reason a non-ordinal-level span is convenient. The *behavior* (typed `MalformedSpan`) is sound and a builder isn't misled — only the justification needs honesty.

**2. [SHARPENING] Simplify the SHOWDELETIONS exactness argument — `denotes` is exact *unconditionally*, by `difference_sets`' denotational contract.** The section spends a paragraph on prefix-freeness + chain-confinement + a fallback (`ever_placed.denotes ∧ ∉ arranged_content`) to defend against a coalesced cover producing an interval phantom. But M5's `deletions(d) = ever_placed(d) ∖ content_image(d)` is a *set difference*, and `⟦difference_sets(A,B)⟧ = ⟦A⟧ ∖ ⟦B⟧` by definition — a span coalesced across a non-deleted ordinal would *change the denotation*, so it cannot occur in a correct `difference_sets` (the two unit spans straddling a live ordinal are non-adjacent and stay separate). Hence `⟦deletions(d)⟧ = {x : DELETED(x,d)}` exactly, regardless of coalescing. Replace the chain-confinement reasoning with this one-line denotational argument and either drop the `ever_placed` fallback or demote it to a pure defensive-test note; as written it makes a robust path look fragile.

**3. **4. [SHARPENING] Give SHOWORIGIN_V's depth-incompatible case its own error rather than overloading `RangeNotPresent`.** A `#start ≥ 3` span fails WF_V(v) (depth mismatch), while a partially-unbound depth-2 span fails WF_V(vi) (range overrun); both currently surface as `RangeNotPresent` via the `resolved < ordinal(width)` test.
